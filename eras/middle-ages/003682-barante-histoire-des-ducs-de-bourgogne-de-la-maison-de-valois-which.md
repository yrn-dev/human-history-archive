# Histoire des ducs de Bourgogne de la maison de Valois

*Histoire des ducs de Bourgogne de la maison de Valois* is a historical work authored by the French statesman and historian Amable Guillaume Prosper Brugiere, Baron de Barante. The work was published in a series of volumes between 1824 and 1828, although other archive records list the publication dates as 1833–1836 across 13 volumes in octavo format.

## Authorship and Impact
The author, Baron de Barante (1782–1866), was born in Riom on June 16, 1782. A son of an advocate, Barante had a distinguished career in the French civil service and diplomacy, serving as the prefect of Vendee and Nantes, and later as the ambassador to Turin and St Petersburg. He was a prominent Liberal and a peer of France, eventually receiving the grand cross of the Legion of Honour.

The publication of *Histoire des ducs de Bourgogne de la maison de Valois* was a significant milestone in Barante's literary career, as the work procured his immediate admission to the French Academy.

## Critical Reception
The work received a mixed critical reception. It was highly praised by the romantic school for its purity of style and its narrative qualities. However, critics also noted that the work exhibited a lack of scientific scholarship and a lack of critical sense.
