# Lombardus and the Sentences

## Overview of the Sentences
The *Sentences* of Lombardus is a significant theological work that served as a primary subject for scholarly commentary during the medieval period. Its influence is evidenced by the extensive academic efforts to provide expositions of its contents, which were utilized by theologians to refine and communicate church doctrine.

## The Commentary by Bonaventura
Among the various analyses of Lombardus's work, the commentary written by Bonaventura is particularly distinguished. In the Lyons edition of Bonaventura's works—a collection spanning seven folio volumes—his commentary on the *Sentences* occupies two volumes. This specific work is celebrated among medieval theologians as being incomparably the best exposition of the third part of the *Sentences*.

## Context within Medieval Theology
The study of texts like those of Lombardus occurred within a broader medieval theological tradition. During this era, the "Schoolmen" (medieval theologians) utilized the New Testament as a storehouse of proof-texts to support the conclusions of the Church. This scholarly environment also saw the development of Scholasticism, a system that was influenced by earlier figures such as Gregory, the Fourth Doctor of the Latin Church, who bridged the gap between the Latin Fathers and later Scholastic speculation.
