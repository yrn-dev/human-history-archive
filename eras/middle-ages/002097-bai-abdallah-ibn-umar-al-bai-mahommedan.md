# BAI[D.][=A]W[=I] ('Abdallah ibn 'Umar al-Bai[d.][=a]w[=i])

## Biography
'Abdallah ibn 'Umar al-Bai[d.][=a]w[=i] was a Mahommedan critic born in Fars. His father served as the chief judge in that region during the period of the Atabek ruler Abu Bakr ibn Sa'd, who ruled from 1226 to 1260. Following in a judicial capacity, Bai[d.][=a]w[=i] became a judge in Shiraz. He died in Tabriz approximately 1286.

## Major Works and Contributions
The primary work authored by Bai[d.][=a]w[=i] is a commentary on the Koran titled *The Secrets of Revelation and The Secrets of Interpretation* (*Asr[=a]r ut-tanz[=i]l wa Asr[=a]r ut-ta' w[=i]l*). This text functions largely as a digest of the *al-Kashsh[=a]f*, which is the great Mu'tazalite commentary written by Zamakhshar[=i]. In creating his work, Bai[d.][=a]w[=i] utilized the *al-Kashsh[=a]f* while incorporating additional notes and making certain omissions.

In addition to his commentary on the Koran, Bai[d.][=a]w[=i] was the author of several treatises on theology.

## Reception and Scholarly Analysis
Among orthodox Moslems, Bai[d.][=a]w[=i]'s commentary is regarded as the standard work and is viewed as almost holy. However, critical analysis has noted that the work is not always accurate and is not complete in its treatment of the linguistic or theological knowledge it addresses, a point referenced in Th. Noeldeke's *Geschichte des Qorans* (1860).

The work has been the subject of extensive further study, resulting in the creation of many supercommentaries.

## Editions and Publications
Several editions of Bai[d.][=a]w[=i]'s work have been published in the East. In the West, H. O. Fleischer edited a two-volume set in Leipzig between 1846 and 1848, with indices later edited by W. Fell in 1878. Additionally, D. S. Margoliouth edited a selection of the work with numerous notes, published in London in 1894 as *Chrestomathia Beidawiana*.
