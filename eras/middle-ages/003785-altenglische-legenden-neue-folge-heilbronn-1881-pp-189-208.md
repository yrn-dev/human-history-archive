# Altenglische Legenden. Neue Folge (Heilbronn, 1881)

## Overview
*Altenglische Legenden. Neue Folge* is a publication from 1881, printed in Heilbronn. The work is associated with the scholarship of C. Horstmann and specifically contains the text of the legend of St Machor on pages 189 through 208.

## Relationship to the Legends of the Saints
The publication of the legend of St Machor is linked to a broader body of work concerning the *Legends of the Saints*. C. Horstmann, who supports the theory that these legends were authored by Barbour, printed the text of the *Legends of the Saints* in a two-volume set titled *Barbours des schottischen Nationaldichters Legendensammlung nebst den Fragmenten seines Trojanerkrieges*, published in Heilbronn between 1881 and 1882.

## Scholarly Debate and Alternative Editions
The authorship of the *Legends of the Saints* has been a subject of academic dispute. While Horstmann upholds Barbour's authorship, other scholars have challenged this claim. W. M. Metcalfe disputed Barbour's claim and subsequently released a three-volume edition in 1896 titled *Legends of the Saints in the Scottish Dialect of the Fourteenth Century*, published by the Scottish Text Society.

Further academic inquiry into whether the Scottish legends published by Horstmann are indeed the work of Barbour was conducted by P. Buss, who published *Sind die von Horstmann herausgegebenen schottischen Legenden ein Werk Barberes?* in Halle in 1886. This topic was also discussed in the publication *Anglia* (ix. 3, 1886). Additional perspectives on these texts can be found in the works of Skeat and Koeppel.
