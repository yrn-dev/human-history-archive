# Bohemund VI

Bohemund VI was a medieval ruler who served as the prince of Antioch and count of Tripoli. Born in 1237, he was the son of Bohemund V and Luciana, who was the daughter of the count of Segni and a nephew of Innocent III.

## Succession and Early Life
Bohemund VI succeeded his father in 1251. In 1252, he was knighted by St Louis. His early years of rule were marked by the maintenance of a diplomatic connection with Cyprus; his sister, Plaisance, had married Henry I of Cyprus in 1250. This connection had been originally established by the marriage of Bohemund VI's father, Bohemund V, to Alice, the widow of Hugh I of Cyprus.

## Governance and Diplomacy
In 1252, Bohemund VI established himself in Antioch, while leaving Tripoli to its own administration. He was involved in the regional politics of the Levant, and in 1257, he procured the recognition of his nephew, Hugh II (the son of Henry I and Plaisance), as the king of Jerusalem.

Bohemund VI's marriage to Sibylla, the sister of Leo III of Armenia, served as a critical diplomatic tool. This marriage, along with the mediation of St Louis, finally brought peace in 1251 to the hostility between Armenia and the state previously led by his father, Bohemund V.

## Military Conflicts and Later Years
During his reign, Bohemund VI allied himself with the Mongols to oppose the advance of the Egyptian sultan. However, this period was marked by significant territorial loss. In 1268, he lost Antioch to Bibars. By the time of his death in 1275, his holdings had been reduced, and he was only the count of Tripoli.

## Succession of Bohemund VII
Bohemund VI was succeeded in 1275 by his son, Bohemund VII, who was born to him and Sibylla. Upon Bohemund VII's succession, Sibylla served as regent. Bohemund VII's reign was short and troubled, characterized by conflicts with the Templars established in Tripoli. In the year of his death, 1287, he lost Laodicea to the sultan of Egypt. Because Bohemund VII died without issue and Tripoli was captured within two years of his death, the county of Tripoli is considered to have become extinct with him.
