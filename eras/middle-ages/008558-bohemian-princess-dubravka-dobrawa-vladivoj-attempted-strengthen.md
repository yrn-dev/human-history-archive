# Princess Dubravka (Dobrawa)

Princess Dubravka, also referred to as Dobrawa or Bona, was a Bohemian princess who played a significant role in the religious and political transitions of Poland and Bohemia.

## Role in the Christianization of Poland
Princess Dubravka is noted for her influence on the Polish court. She was the wife of Mieszko, who is recognized as the first Christian prince of Poland. Through her, the Polish court was converted from paganism to Catholicism, a process facilitated by her chaplain, Jordan.

## Descendants and the Polish Monarchy
Dubravka was the mother of Boleslaus I, known as "The Great," who succeeded Mieszko in 992. Boleslaus I is described as a born warrior who transformed the struggling Polish principality on the Vistula into a great power. He recognized the essential superiority of Christianity over other religions and deliberately associated himself with the new faith. Under his rule, Poland expanded its territory, seizing Pomerania in 996 to gain a seaboard and occupying the city of Cracow, which had previously been a Czech city, by taking advantage of instability within Bohemia.

## Connection to the Bohemian Throne
The lineage of Princess Dubravka later intersected with the leadership of Bohemia through her son, Vladivoj. Vladivoj was the brother of Boleslaus the Great and a son of Dubravka. 

During a period of instability in Bohemia, the country was plagued by constant civil war resulting from dissensions between Boleslav III and his brothers, Ulrick and Jaromir. Although Boleslav III managed to expel his brothers, his cruelty led the Bohemian people to dethrone him. In his place, they chose the Polish prince Vladivoj as their ruler.

## The Reign of Vladivoj
Upon ascending to power, Vladivoj sought to solidify his authority. He attempted to strengthen his hold over Bohemia by securing assistance from Germany. As part of this effort to maintain his position, he consented to continue the payment of tribute.
