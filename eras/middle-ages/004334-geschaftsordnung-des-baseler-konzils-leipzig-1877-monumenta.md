# Geschaftsordnung des Baseler Konzils and Monumenta

## Die Organisation und Geschaftsordnung des Baseler Konzils
The work titled *Die Organisation und Geschaftsordnung des Baseler Konzils* was authored by O. Richter and published in Leipzig in 1877. This text is cited as an authority regarding the Council of Basel. Other primary and secondary authorities for the study of this council include the works of Mansi (vol. xxix.-xxxi.), Aeneas Sylvius' *De rebus Basileae gestis* (1803), and Hefele's *Conciliengeschichte* (vol. vii., 1874). Additionally, the archive lists J. Haller's *Concilium Basiliense* (vol. i.-v., 1896-1904) and G. Perouse's *Le Cardinal Louis Aleman, president du concile de Bale* (1904) as relevant sources.

## Monumenta Conciliorum generalium seculi xv.
The *Monumenta Conciliorum generalium seculi xv., Scriptorum* is a collection of records published in Vienna between 1857 and 1895. The archive notes the existence of volumes i., ii., and iii. of this work.

## Monumenta Germaniae historica
The *Monumenta Germaniae historica* is a significant historical collection with various sections and volumes published in Hanover and Berlin. 

### Scriptores and Bände
Within the *Scriptores* section of the *Monumenta Germaniae historica*, several historical accounts are preserved:
* **Bände i. and ii. (1826 fol.):** These volumes contain the *Annales Fuldenses*, Nithard's *Historiarum Libri*, the *Vita Hludowici* attributed to Astronomus, Thegan's *Vita Hludowici*, and Ermoldus Nigellus' *In honorem Hludowici imperatoris*.
* **Bände i. and iii. (1826 fol.):** These volumes include the *Annales Bertiniani* and the *Chronica S. Benedicti Casinensis*.
* **Bände vi., xii., and xx. (1826-1892):** These volumes contain the *Vita Norberti archiepiscopi Magdeburgensis*, as well as works by Otto von Freising, including the *Chronicon Annalista Saxo* and the *Narratio de electione Lotharii*.

### Administration and Poetae latini
The direction of the *Monumenta Germaniae historica* involved a revised committee of which Ernst Ludwig Dummler became a member in 1875. Dummler specifically undertook the direction of the *Antiquitates* section and later served as the president of the central board in Berlin in 1888. Between 1881 and 1884, Dummler edited the first and second volumes of the *Poetae latini aevi Carolini* for the *Monumenta Germaniae historica* in Berlin.
