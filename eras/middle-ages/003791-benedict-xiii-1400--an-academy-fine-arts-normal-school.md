# Benedict XIII

## Papal Confirmation of Educational Institutions
In the year 1400, Pope Benedict XIII issued a bull of confirmation for the college of medicine and surgery in Barcelona. This institution is part of a broader history of numerous and important educational establishments in the city.

## Context of Barcelona's Education and Infrastructure
The educational landscape of Barcelona includes various specialized institutions alongside the college confirmed by Benedict XIII. These include:
*   An academy of fine arts
*   A normal school
*   A theological seminary
*   An academy of natural sciences
*   An upper industrial school
*   A school of navigation
*   An institution dedicated to the education of deaf-mutes

Additionally, the city's university (*Universidad Literaria*) was founded later, in 1430, by the city's magistracy and received its own bull of confirmation from Pope Nicholas V in 1450.

Other significant civic and charitable foundations in the city include the hospital general, which dates from 1401, and the Casa de Caridad (house of charity) and the foundling hospital. The city also features notable Gothic and ancient structures, such as the Lonja (1383), the Casa Consistorial (1369-1378), and the Atarazanas (Arsenals), which was finished around 1243.

## Religious and Political Relations
During the period of the Great Schism, Benedict XIII of Avignon was one of the antipopes to whom Naples had previously owed spiritual allegiance. This allegiance was eventually shifted to the Roman obedience under Pope Boniface IX, who served as pope from 1389 to 1404.
