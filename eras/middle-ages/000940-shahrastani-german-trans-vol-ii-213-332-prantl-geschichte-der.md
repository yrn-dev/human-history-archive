## K. Prantl and the History of Logic

K. Prantl is an author associated with the study of logic and the works of various scholars. His work, *Geschichte der Logik*, is referenced in multiple contexts:

*   **Avicenna**: Prantl's *Geschichte der Logik* (volume ii, pages 318-361) is listed as a source for the philosophy of Avicenna.
*   **Other Logical Works**: Prantl's *Geschichte der Logik* (book iv, pages 14-38) is cited in relation to a series of works including *Summula de dialectica* (1487), *Compendium logicae* (1489), *Quaestiones in viii. libros physicorum* (1516), *In Aristotelis Metaphysica* (1518), *Quaestiones in x. libros ethicorum Aristotelis* (1489; 1637), and *Quaestiones in viii. libros politicorum Aristotelis* (1500).

## Other Contributions by Prantl

Beyond the field of logic, Prantl is associated with botanical research. In collaboration with Engler, he authored *Die natuerlichen Pflanzenfamilien*. This work is cited as a general authority on the structure and functions of bacteria, as well as a source for the classification of fungi and the study of the general distribution of fungi.
