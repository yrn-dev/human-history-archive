# Averruncate

The term "averruncate" is derived from the Latin *averruncare*, which means to remove mischief or to ward off. In historical and practical application, the term has evolved to describe both physical agricultural activities and spiritual or moral conduct.

## Arboriculture and Agriculture
In the field of arboriculture, an "averruncator" refers to a specific type of long shears. These tools are utilized for the purpose of "averruncating," which in this context means pruning off the higher branches of trees. 

Over time, the meaning of the word expanded to include "pruning vines" and "weeding the ground." This shift in meaning was based on a supposed derivation from the Latin *ab* (off) and *eruncare* (to weed out). Consequently, the word was sometimes spelled "aberuncate" to align with this perceived origin. However, the *New English Dictionary* considers such a derivation to be impossible.

The "Home of the Hereafter" is specifically assigned to those who do not seek corruption, oppression, or high-handedness on earth, and who do not desire to make mischief or exalt themselves. The texts emphasize that the "good end" and the "best" abode of the hereafter are reserved for those who guard against evil or ward it off.

### Rewards for the Righteous
Those who ward off evil are described as the "righteous." When asked what their Lord has revealed, they respond, "Good." The texts teach that for those who do good in this world, there is a reward, and the home of the Hereafter is even more excellent. This reward consists of Gardens of Eternity (or Gardens of Eden) with rivers flowing beneath them, where the inhabitants will have whatever they wish.

### Perseverance and Trial
The texts acknowledge that individuals will be tried and tested through their personal selves and their possessions, and will hear grieving or annoying talk from polytheists and those who received the Book previously. In these circumstances, the texts teach that if a person perseveres patiently and guards against evil (or wards it off), this becomes a determining factor in all affairs and is a sign of a steadfast heart.
