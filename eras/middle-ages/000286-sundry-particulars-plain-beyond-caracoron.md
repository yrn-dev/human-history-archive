# SUNDRY PARTICULARS OF THE PLAIN BEYOND CARACORON

The region beyond the city of Caracoron, specifically extending toward the north, contains the Plain of Bargu and the territories inhabited by the Mescript people. This area is characterized by its distance from the central Tartar administrative hubs and its distinct environmental and social conditions.

## Geography and Travel
To reach the Plain of Bargu from Caracoron and the Altay—the location where the bodies of the Tartar Sovereigns are buried—one must travel north for a period of forty days. The region is noted for its abundance of lakes, pools, and marshes.

## The Mescript People
The inhabitants of the Plain of Bargu are known as the Mescript. They are described as a very wild race who are subject to the Great Kaan. Their customs are similar to those of the Tartars, though they lack certain staples, as they have neither wine nor corn.

The Mescript economy is primarily based on cattle. Their livestock consists largely of stags, which the people use as mounts for riding. Due to the marshy nature of the landscape, the Mescript also supplement their diet with fish and birds. They specifically target birds during the moulting season; when the birds have cast their feathers and are unable to fly, the Mescript catch them for food.

## Context of the Surrounding Region
The Plain of Bargu lies beyond Caracoron, a city approximately three miles in compass. Caracoron is enclosed by a strong earthen rampart due to the scarcity of stone in the area and features a great citadel containing the Governor's palace. It is recorded as the first city the Tartars possessed after leaving their own country.

The broader ancestral lands of the Tartars, located in the north on the borders of Chorcha, were similarly characterized by extensive plains and excellent pasture-lands with many sheets of water and great rivers. These original lands lacked towns or villages and had no sovereign, though the inhabitants paid a tribute of one beast out of every ten, along with a tithe of their gear, to a prince known in their tongue as Unc Can (referred to as Prester John).
