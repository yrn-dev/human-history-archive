# Otto III

Otto III was a member of the Saxon line of emperors, succeeding Otto II in 983. He reigned until 1002.

## Early Life and Guardianship
In 983, Otto III ascended to power as a youth. During this period, Lothair, the king of France, was chosen to serve as the young emperor's guardian.

## Imperial Residences and Administration
The emperor frequently resided at the imperial stronghold (Kaiserpfalz) located in Dornburg, Germany. This town, situated on a hill above the Saale, served as a bulwark against the Slavs and was a residence for both Otto III and his predecessor, Otto II. 

In the administration of his empire, Otto III employed Leo of Vercelli, who served as his chancellor. During his tenure, Leo of Vercelli protested that a document known as the *Constitutum* was a forgery, although this protest was without effect.

## Relations with Poland
In the year 1000, Otto III visited Gnesen, where he was entertained with great magnificence by Boleslaus I (known as "The Great"), the king of Poland. During this visit, the emperor declared that Boleslaus was too worthy to be merely a *princeps*. Consequently, Otto III conferred the royal crown upon him.

Following the death of Otto III, Boleslaus I took advantage of the situation to invade Germany. Boleslaus penetrated as far as the Elbe, occupying Meissen and Stralsund, and extended his dominions to the Saale and the Elster.
