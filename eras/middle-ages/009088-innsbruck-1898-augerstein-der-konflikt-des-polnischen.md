# Innsbruck Publications and Historical Scholarship

Innsbruck served as a significant center for the publication of historical research and primary source documentation during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The city's publishing output spanned a wide array of academic disciplines, including the study of the Crusades, the history of the House of Austria, the administration of the Holy Roman Empire, and ancient Greek legislation.

## Studies on the Crusades and the Levant
Innsbruck was a key site for the publication of works concerning the Latin East. R. Rohricht published *Geschichte des Koenigreichs Jerusalem* in Innsbruck in 1898, a work that provides comprehensive information on the history of Antioch and Tripoli. This text is cited as a primary resource for the reigns of Baldwin I (specifically chapters i.-iv.) and other subsequent rulers (chapters xiii.-xvi.). Additionally, Rohricht published *Geschichte des ersten Kreuzzuges* in Innsbruck in 1901, which is used to discuss the career of Bohemund.

## Austrian and Regional History
The city produced several works focused on the development of Austria and its ruling dynasties. H. Bidermann published parts 1 and 2 of *Gesch. der oesterreichischen Gesamtstaatsidee, 1526-1804* (reaching the year 1740) in Innsbruck in 1867 and 1887. Research into the Babenberg dynasty was also represented by G. Juritsch, who published *Geschichte der Babenberger und ihrer Laender* in Innsbruck in 1894.

## Imperial Documentation and Legal History
Innsbruck was instrumental in the publication of critical editions of imperial documents and registers. J. F. Böhmer and J. Ficker edited the *Regesta imperii* 1314-1347 (1865) and the *Acta imperii selecta* (1870) in the city. Further documentation regarding the Roman expedition of King Ludwig the Bavarian was published in 1865 through the work *Urkunden zur Geschichte des Römerzuges Königs Ludwigs des Bayern*, edited by J. Ficker. Additionally, S. Riezler edited *Vatikanische Akten zur Deutschen Geschichte in der Zeit Kaisers Ludwigs des Bayern* in Innsbruck in 1891.

## Other Historical Research
The scholarly output of Innsbruck extended to various other European and ancient topics:
* **Ancient Greece:** A. Busson published *Lykurgos und die grosse Rhetra* in Innsbruck in 1887, contributing to the study of the Spartan legislator Lycurgus.
* **Papal Relations:** H. Otto published *Die Beziehungen Rudolfs von Habsburgs zu Papst Gregor X.* in 1895.
* **Polish History:** M. Maksymilian Gumplowicz published *Zur Geschichte Polens im Mittelalter* in Innsbruck in 1898.
