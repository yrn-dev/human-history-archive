# Dalimil

## Overview
Dalimil is the name associated with a rhymed chronicle of considerable historical value. While the work is generally known as the chronicle of Dalimil, this attribution is noted to be incorrect. The text serves as a record of Bohemian history, spanning from the earliest periods up until the reign of King Henry of Carinthia, who was the immediate predecessor to King John.

## Authorship and Period
The author of the chronicle is believed to have lived during the reign of King John, which lasted from 1310 to 1346. The work is categorized as part of Old Czech literature.

## Content and Themes
The chronicle documents the events of Bohemia's past, concluding its historical narrative with the reign of King Henry of Carinthia. A defining characteristic of the text is the presence of a strong feeling of racial antipathy toward the Germans, which pervades the writing.

## Context within Bohemian Literature
The chronicle exists alongside other early records of the Bohemian or Czech language. These early genuine documents include various legends—such as those of St. Dorothy and St. Catherine—and several hymns. Additionally, ancient epic fragments have been preserved, including *Tandarias a Floribella* and the *Alexandreis*. While other documents like the MSS. of Kralodvur (Koniginhof) and Zelena Hora (Gruneberg) exist, they are almost certainly forgeries created in the early 19th century. Since the revival of Bohemian literature in the 19th century, these early writings, including the chronicle, have been printed.
