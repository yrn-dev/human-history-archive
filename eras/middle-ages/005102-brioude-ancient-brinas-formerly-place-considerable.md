# Brioude

Brioude, known in antiquity as *Brinas*, is a town located in central France. It serves as the capital of an arrondissement within the department of Haute-Loire. Geographically, the town is situated on the left bank of the Allier river at an elevation of 1,467 feet above sea level, positioned 47 meters northwest of Le Puy and accessible via the Paris-Lyon railway. As of 1906, the population of the town was 4,581.

## History and Military Conflicts
Formerly a place of considerable importance, Brioude has a history marked by repeated sieges and captures. The town was taken in turn by the Goths in 532, the Burgundians, the Normans, and the Saracens in 732. 

In the late 12th century, the town faced internal conflict; the viscount of Polignac sacked Brioude in 1179. Two years later, in 1181, the viscount made a public apology in front of the church. To ensure the protection of the relics of St Julian, a body of twenty-five knights was established. Later, following 1361, the town served as the headquarters for Bérenger, the lord of Castelnau, who led one of the bands of military adventurers that devastated France during that period.

## Governance and Civic Life
The knights of St Julian—who later became known as canons—held the title of counts of Brioude. For a significant period, these figures opposed the civic liberties of the town's inhabitants. In modern administration, Brioude is the seat of a sub-prefect, as well as the seat for tribunals of commerce and tribunals of first instance.

## Architecture and Urban Layout
Brioude has largely avoided modernization, preserving many of its old fountains and houses. The town's interior is characterized by narrow and irregular streets, though it is encircled by wide, tree-lined boulevards.

The most significant architectural landmark is the church of St Julian, constructed during the 12th and 13th centuries. Built in the Romanesque style of Auvergne, the church is noted for its choir, radiating chapels, apse, and the mosaic ornamentation found on the exterior.

## Economy and Industry
The town is situated on a plain of great fertility. This geography supports a considerable grain trade and market-gardening activities on the outskirts of the town. The industrial sector of Brioude includes the following:
* Antimony mining and founding
* Lace-making
* Saw-milling
* Brewing
