# CONCERNING THE CITY OF COBINAN

## Overview and Geography
Cobinan is described as a large town located beyond the kingdom of Kerman. Travelers reaching the city from Kerman must first traverse a desert extending for four days, during which wild asses may be seen. The city is situated along a route that leads toward the province of Tonocain. 

In historical and geographic analysis, the city is also referred to as Koh-banan, Kuh-Banán, or Cabanant. Some accounts suggest that the modern Kuh-Banán does not fit the original descriptions provided by Marco Polo, and it has been proposed that the actual site may be found among ruins on the stage to Darband.

## Religious and Social Life
The inhabitants of Cobinan worship Mahommet.

## Industry and Production
The city is noted for its production of iron, steel, and *Ondanique*. Artisans in Cobinan manufacture steel mirrors characterized by their beauty and great size. 

The city is also a center for the preparation of *Spodium* and *Tutia*, the latter of which is considered very beneficial for the eyes. The production process for these substances involves a specific type of earth with required qualities. This earth is placed into a large flaming furnace equipped with an iron grating. As the earth is heated, moisture and smoke are expelled and adhere to the iron grating, forming *Tutia*. The remaining slag left after the burning process is identified as *Spodium*.

## Travel and Surrounding Terrain
Departing from Cobinan leads the traveler into a desert of "surpassing aridity" that lasts for approximately eight days. This region is devoid of trees and fruits, and the available water is described as bad and bitter. Because of these conditions, travelers must carry their own food and water, though cattle are forced to drink the bitter water due to extreme thirst.

At the conclusion of this eight-day journey, travelers arrive in the province of Tonocain, which marks the northern extremity of Persia. Tonocain is characterized by a temperate climate and contains many towns and villages. It is also home to an immense plain featuring the *Arbre Sol* (known to Christians as the *Arbre Sec*), a tall, thick tree with green bark on one side and white on the other. This tree produces a rough husk similar to a chestnut but contains nothing inside. The wood is strong and yellow, resembling box. Local tradition holds that the battle between King Darius and Alexander was fought in the vicinity of these trees.
