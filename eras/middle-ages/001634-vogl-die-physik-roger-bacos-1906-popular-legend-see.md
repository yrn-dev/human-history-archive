# S. Vogl, Die Physik Roger Bacos (1906)

## Overview
*Die Physik Roger Bacos*, published in 1906 by S. Vogl, is a scholarly work concerning the physics of Roger Bacon. It is listed within the bibliography of research pertaining to Bacon's life, doctrines, and scientific contributions.

## Context within Bacon Scholarship
The work by S. Vogl exists alongside a variety of other critical studies on Roger Bacon. These include E. Charles's 1861 work, *Roger Bacon, sa vie, ses ouvrages, ses doctrines d'apres des textes inedits*, which is considered perhaps the best work on the subject, though it is noted for a modern interpretation and a somewhat enthusiastic estimate. Other scholars who have provided contrasting or complementary perspectives include:

*   **Schneider**, who wrote *Roger Bacon, Eine Monographie* (1873).
*   **K. Werner**, who authored *Die Psychol. ... des Roger Bacon* and *Die Kosmologie ... des Roger Bacon* (1879).
*   **K. Prantl**, who offered a severe criticism of Bacon's logical doctrines in *Gesch. der Logik*.
*   **Karl Pohl**, who examined the relationship between philosophy and theology in *Das Verhaeltniss d. Philos. zur Theol. bei Roger Bacon* (1893).
*   **A. Parrot**, who wrote *Roger Bacon et ses contemporains* (1894).
*   **E. Fluegel**, who published *Roger Bacons Stellung in d. Gesch. d. Philos.* (1902).

## Related Primary Sources
Research into Bacon's physics, such as that conducted by Vogl, draws upon various manuscripts. Key documents include:

*   ***Communia Naturalium***: An extensive work focusing on the fundamental notions of physics. Copies of this text are held in the British Museum, the Mazarin library at Paris, and the University College and Bodleian libraries at Oxford.
*   ***Baconis Physica***: A manuscript contained within the additional manuscripts of the British Museum.
*   ***De Communibus Mathematicae***: A work on the fundamental notions of mathematics, located in the Bodleian and the Sloane collection.

Other fragments and works attributed to Bacon include the *Compendium Studii Theologiae*, the *Quinta Pars Compendii Theologiae*, and logical fragments such as the *Summulae Dialectices*.

## Distinction from Popular Legend
The academic study of Bacon's physics and philosophy is distinct from the popular legends associated with him. While scholars like Vogl analyze his scientific contributions, popular folklore is preserved in works such as *Famous Historie of Fryer Bacon* (1615), R. Greene's *Friar Bacon and Friar Bungay* (1587 or 1588), and *A Piece of Friar Bacon's Brazen Heade's Prophesie* (1604).
