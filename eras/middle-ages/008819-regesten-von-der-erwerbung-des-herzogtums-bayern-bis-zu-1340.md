# Wittelsbachische Regesten von der Erwerbung des Herzogtums Bayern bis zu 1340

The *Wittelsbachische Regesten von der Erwerbung des Herzogtums Bayern bis zu 1340* is a historical collection of documents edited by the German historian Johann Friedrich Böhmer. Published in Stuttgart in 1854, the work focuses on the regesta—abstracts or summaries of charters and official documents—pertaining to the Wittelsbach dynasty from the time they acquired the Duchy of Bavaria until the year 1340.

## Authorship and Editorial Context
The collection was compiled by Johann Friedrich Böhmer (1795–1863), a historian born in Frankfort-on-Main. Böhmer was educated at the universities of Gottingen and Heidelberg and served in several official capacities in Frankfort, including as the city's librarian and archivist, as well as secretary of the *Gesellschaft fur altere deutsche Geschichtskunde*.

Böhmer's scholarly output was primarily dedicated to the collection and tabulation of imperial documents and charters from the Middle Ages. His methodology involved creating chronological-diplomatic abstracts of these records. The *Wittelsbachische Regesten* is one of several such specialized collections he produced, complementing his broader works on imperial history.

## Relation to Other Works by Böhmer
The *Wittelsbachische Regesten* exists alongside a vast body of Böhmer's research into the diplomatic history of the Holy Roman Empire. His other significant contributions to the field of regesta include:

*   **Regesta chronologico-diplomatica regum atque imperatorum Romanorum 911-1313**: Published in Frankfort in 1831.
*   **Regesta chronologico-diplomatica Karolorum**: A collection of abstracts of all Carolingian documents published in Frankfort in 1833.
*   **Regesta imperii**: A series of volumes covering various periods, including 1314-1347 (1839), 1246-1313 (1844), and 1198-1254 (1849).
*   **Fontes rerum Germanicarum**: A collection of original authorities for German history during the 13th and 14th centuries, published between 1843 and 1868.
*   **Die Reichsgesetze 900-1400**: Published in Frankfort in 1832.
*   **Codex diplomaticus Moeno-Francofurtanus**: An Urkundenbuch of the imperial city of Frankfurt, published in 1836.

## Historical Scope
The specific focus of the *Wittelsbachische Regesten* is the acquisition and administration of the Duchy of Bavaria by the House of Wittelsbach. By tabulating documents up to the year 1340, the work provides a diplomatic record of the dynasty's early territorial gains and political developments. This period overlaps with the broader imperial history Böhmer documented in his *Regesta imperii* and *Fontes rerum Germanicarum*, particularly regarding the 13th and 14th centuries.
