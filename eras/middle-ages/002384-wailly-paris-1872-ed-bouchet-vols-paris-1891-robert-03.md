# Bibliographical Records of N. de Wailly and Related Historical Works

The archive contains several references to the scholarly contributions of N. de Wailly, primarily concerning the publication and editing of historical memoirs and chronicles related to French and Byzantine history.

## Publications by N. de Wailly
N. de Wailly is noted for publishing significant contemporary accounts of Louis IX. Specifically, de Wailly published the Memoirs of the Sire Jean de Joinville for the *Soc. de l'Hist. de France*. This work was released under the title *Histoire de Saint Louis* in Paris in 1868, with a subsequent edition including a translation published in 1874.

Additionally, de Wailly is cited as an editor for the work *La Conquete de Constantinople* by Villehardouin, with an edition published in Paris in 1872.

## Historical Accounts of Constantinople
The records list various authorities regarding the conquest and fall of Constantinople. In addition to the edition of Villehardouin edited by de Wailly, the archive notes the following sources:
* Robert de Clari's *La Prise de Constantinople*, found in Hopf's *Chroniques greco-romaines*.
* The *Chronique* of Ernoul, edited by Mas Latrie (Paris, 1871).
* Works by Nicetas (ed. Bonn, 1835) and George Acropolites (vol. i., ed. Heisenberg, Leipzig, 1903).
* Documents found in Tafel and Thomas's *Urkunden zur aelteren Handels- und Staatsgeschichte der Republik Venedig* (Vienna, 1856).

Modern analyses of the empire of Constantinople under French emperors include Ducange's *Histoire de l'empire de Constantinople sous les empereurs francais* (Paris, 1657), Gibbon's *Decline and Fall* (vol. vi., ed. Bury, 1898), and Gerland's *Geschichte des lateinischen Kaiserreiches von Konstantinopel* (part i., Homburg v. d. Hoehe, 1905). Other relevant works include G. Finlay's *History of Greece* (vol. iv., Oxford, 1877), Pears' *The Fall of Constantinople* (London, 1885), and Hopf's "Griechische Geschichte" in Ersch and Gruber's *Encyklopaedie* (vol. lxxxv., Leipzig, 1870).

## Records of Louis IX and the French Monarchy
Beyond the memoirs published by de Wailly, other primary authorities on Louis IX include William of Nangis' *Gesta Ludovici IX.*, edited by M. Bouquet. Modern scholarship on Louis IX and his era includes:
* Frederick Perry's *Saint Louis, the Most Christian King* (New York, 1901).
* E. J. Davis' *The Invasion of Egypt by Louis IX. of France* (1898).
* H. A. Wallon's *Saint Louis et son temps* (1875).
* A. Lecoy de la Marche's *Saint Louis* (Tours, 1891).
* E. Berger's *Saint Louis et Innocent IV* (Paris, 1893) and *Histoire de Blanche de Castille* (1895).
* Winifred F. Knox's *The Court of a Saint* (1909).
