# The Desert of Eight Days' Journey

The desert encountered upon departing from the City of Cobinan is characterized by its surpassing aridity. This region extends for approximately eight days' journey and is devoid of both trees and fruits. The water available within this desert is described as bitter and bad; however, cattle are forced to drink it due to their extreme thirst. Because of the poor quality of the water and the lack of vegetation, travelers are required to carry their own supplies of both food and water.

## The Province of Tonocain
At the conclusion of the eight-day journey through the desert, travelers arrive at the Province of Tonocain. This region marks the northern extremity of Persia and contains a significant number of villages and towns. The climate of Tonocain is noted for being extremely temperate, remaining neither very hot nor very cold. Consequently, the towns and villages possess a great abundance of everything good. The native population of the province worships Mahommet and is described as a fine-looking people, with the women being surpassingly beautiful.

## The Arbre Sol
Tonocain contains an immense plain featuring a specific type of tree known as the Arbre Sol, referred to by Christians as the *Arbre Sec*. This tree is characterized by the following features:
* **Appearance:** It is a tall and thick tree. Its bark is green on one side and white on the other.
* **Fruit:** It produces a rough husk similar to that of a chestnut, though the husk contains nothing.
* **Wood:** The wood is very strong and yellow in color, resembling box wood.

The Arbre Sol grows in isolation; no other trees are found within a hundred miles of it, except for one side where other trees appear within a distance of about ten miles. According to the people of the country, the battle between King Darius and Alexander was fought in this specific area.
