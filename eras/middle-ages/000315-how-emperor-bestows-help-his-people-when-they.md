# How the Emperor Bestows Help on His People

The methods by which an emperor provides assistance to his subjects vary by region and administration, ranging from the strategic management of food supplies to the provision of diplomatic and material support to neighboring territories.

## Provisions Against Dearth and Murrain

In the administration of the Great Kaan, specific systems were established to protect the population from the afflictions of murrain or dearth. To mitigate the effects of food shortages, the Emperor implemented a system of strategic reserves. When corn was found to be abundant and cheap, the Emperor purchased large quantities of the crop. This corn was then stored in great granaries located across all his provinces. These facilities were managed with such care that the stored corn could be kept for a period of three or four years to be used as help for the people during times of dearth.

## Regional and International Assistance

The influence of the Chinese Emperor extended beyond his immediate borders, providing aid to other lands. In historical correspondence regarding relations with China, it is noted that the relationship between certain entities and the Emperor was viewed as that of a child to a parent. In instances where these external parties were unfortunate, the Emperor provided them with help. This relationship was characterized by a recognition of the Emperor's suzerainty, with the understanding that all lands, both within and without, were subject to China.
