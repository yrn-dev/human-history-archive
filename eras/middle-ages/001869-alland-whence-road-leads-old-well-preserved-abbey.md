# Alland

## Geography and Location
Alland is a location situated in the vicinity of Baden, a town and watering-place in lower Austria. It is located farther up the picturesque valley of the Helenenthal, which is traversed by the Schwechat river.

## Connection to Heiligenkreuz
Alland serves as a point of transit for those visiting the abbey of Heiligenkreuz. A road leads from Alland to this abbey, which is described as being both old and well-preserved. The abbey features a church dating from the 11th century built in the Romanesque style. Notable characteristics of the site include fine cloisters and the tombs of several members of the Babenberg family.

## Regional Context
Alland is part of a region characterized by diverse landmarks and natural features. Nearby, the valley of the Helenenthal is crossed by the magnificent aqueduct of the Vienna waterworks. The area also contains historical ruins, such as the 12th-century castle of Rauheneck and the ruined castle of Rauhenstein, also from the 12th century. Additionally, the region includes the Chateau Weilburg, constructed between 1820 and 1825 by Archduke Charles, the victor of Aspern.

Further within the vicinity is Mayerling, a hunting-lodge where the crown prince Rudolph of Austria was found dead in 1889. The highest geographical point in the neighborhood of Baden is the peak of the Hoher Lindkogel, which reaches an elevation of 2,825 feet and is popularly known as the Eiserne Thor.
