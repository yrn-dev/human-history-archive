# Leipzig

Leipzig is a city that has served as a significant center for the publication of academic, scientific, and historical works across various disciplines. The archive's records indicate a high volume of scholarly output from the city, particularly during the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

## Historical and Political Publications
Leipzig has been a primary location for the publication of texts concerning world history and political structures. In the field of Byzantine history, the city produced George Acropolites' first volume in 1903 and Hopf's "Griechische Geschichte" in 1870, which appeared in Ersch and Gruber's *Encyklopaedie* (vol. lxxxv). 

The city also published works regarding the Reformation and ecclesiastical history. Examples include Myconius' *Historia Reformationis 1517-1542* (1718) and G. Lösche's *Vollständige Reformations-Acta und Documenta*, published between 1720 and 1729. Additionally, the city hosted the publication of the *Realencyklopadie* (vol. iii, 3rd ed., 1897) by Herzog-Hauck.

## Ancient Near East and Classical Studies
A substantial amount of research regarding ancient civilizations has been published in Leipzig. This includes legal and contractual studies of Babylon and Assyria, such as J. Kohler and F. E. Peiser's *Aus dem babylonischen Rechtsleben* (1890 ff.) and *Hammurabis Gesetz* (1904), as well as Br. Meissner's *Beitraege zur altbabylonischen Privatrecht* (1893).

Regarding ancient religion and mythology, Leipzig publications include H. Zimmern's *Beitraege zur Kenntniss der babylonischen Religion* (1901), J. A. Craig's *Assyrian and Babylonian Religious Texts* (1895-1897), K. L. Tallqvist's *Die assyrische Beschwoerungsserie Maql[=u]* (1895), J. A. Knudtzon's *Assyrische Gebete an den Sonnengott* (1893), and Craig's *Astrological-Astronomical Texts* (1892).

In the realm of Classical studies and philosophy, the city produced several works on Democritus, including H. C. Liepmann's *Die Leucipp-Democritischen Atome* (1886), G. Hart's *Zur Seelen- und Erkenntnislehre des Democritus* (1886), and A. Dyroff's *Demokritstudien* (1899). Studies on Homer were also published there, such as Buchholz's *Die homerischen Realien* (1871-1873), W. Helbig's *Das homerische Epos aus den Denkmalern erlautert* (1884, with a second edition in 1887), Frohwein's *Verbum Homericum* (1881), Gehring's *Index Homericus* (1891), and the *Lexicon Homericum* edited by H. Ebeling (1880-1885).

## Science and Economics
Leipzig has been a center for the publication of scientific treatises and economic analyses. In the field of physics and perception, works published in the city include *Grundlinien d. Lehre v. d. Bewegungsempfindungen* (1875), *Die Mechanik in ihrer Entwickelung* (1883, with a revised edition in 1908), *Populärwissenschaftliche Vorlesungen* (3rd ed., 1903), and *Erkenntnis und Irrtum* (1905).
