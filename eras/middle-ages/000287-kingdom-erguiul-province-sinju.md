# THE KINGDOM OF ERGUIUL AND PROVINCE OF SINJU

The Kingdom of Erguiul and the Province of Sinju are territories located within the great Province of Tangut, both of which are subject to the Great Kaan. Erguiul is reached by traveling five days east from Campichu across a tract where many spirits are heard speaking during the night.

## The Kingdom of Erguiul
Erguiul serves as the capital of its kingdom. The population of the region is composed of Nestorian Christians, worshippers of Mahommet, and Idolaters. The people are described as traders and artisans who produce an abundance of corn. The total extent of the province is a journey of 26 days.

The Idolaters of Erguiul are characterized as fat people with black hair and little noses. They generally lack beards, possessing only a few hairs on the upper lip. The women are described as pretty creatures with very smooth and white skin. Socially, the men are noted as being very sensual and are permitted by their religion to marry many wives. Beauty is highly valued in women regardless of their descent; a beautiful woman may marry one of the greatest men in the land, provided the man pays a great sum of money to the woman's parents based on a negotiated bargain.

The local fauna includes pheasants that are nearly as large as peacocks, with tails measuring between 7 and 10 palms in length. Other varieties of pheasants and birds with beautiful variegated plumage are also found in the region.

## The Province of Sinju
Traveling in a south-easterly direction from Erguiul leads into the province of Cathay. Following this road leads to the city of Sinju, which also belongs to Tangut and is subject to the Great Kaan. Sinju oversees many villages and towns. The population consists of Christians, worshippers of Mahommet, and Idolaters.

The region is known for its wild cattle, which are splendid creatures nearly as large as elephants. These animals are covered in shaggy hair approximately four palms long—except on their backs—and are colored partly black and partly white. The hair or wool of these cattle is extremely fine and white, surpassing silk in whiteness and fineness.

Some of these cattle are tamed by being caught when young. Additionally, they are crossed with common cows to produce "wonderful beasts" that are superior to other animals for work. The people commonly use these cattle for general work, as burdens, and in the plough, where they are capable of performing twice the work of any other cattle due to their great strength.

## Geography and Regional Context
Erguiul is situated five days east of Kanchau. From Erguiul, a journey of eight days eastward leads to the province of Egrigaia, another territory belonging to Tangut. Egrigaia contains numerous cities and villages, with its capital at Calachan. While the people there are chiefly Idolaters, the province also contains fine Nestorian Christian churches.
