# Rechtsquellen von Basel and Related Basler Works

The *Rechtsquellen von Basel* is a two-volume work published between 1856 and 1865. It was authored by A. Heusler, who also wrote *Verfassungsgeschichte d. Stadt Basel im Mittelalter* in 1860.

## Historical and Legal Documentation of Basel
The *Rechtsquellen von Basel* exists alongside a variety of other scholarly works documenting the history, law, and administration of the city of Basel. Legal and constitutional history is further explored in Heusler's *Verfassungsgeschichte d. Stadt Basel im Mittelalter*. Other primary source collections include the *Urkundenbuch* for the city (published from 1890) and the *Urkundenbuch d. Landschaft B.* (published from 1881).

## General Historical Works on Basel
The archive contains references to several comprehensive histories of the city. These include:
* *Geschichte von Basel* by H. Boos, of which only volume i. (covering history up to 1501) was published in 1877.
* *Gesch. d. Stadt Basel*, a three-volume work by R. Wackernagel published from 1906 onwards.
* *Bilder aus d. Geschichte von Basel*, a three-volume set by A. Burckhardt published between 1869 and 1882.
* *Basler Chroniken*, which consists of original chronicles published in five volumes in Leipzig from 1872 to 1890.

## Specialized Studies of Basel
Various authors have focused on specific aspects of the city's development:
* **Industry and Trade:** T. Geering published *Handel und Industrie d. Stadt Basel* in 1885.
* **Academic History:** The history of the University of Basel is covered by W. Vischer in *Gesch. d. Universitaet B.* 1460-1529 (1860) and by R. Thommen in *Gesch. d. Universitaet B.* 1532-1632 (1889).
* **Ecclesiastical Power:** L. Stouff wrote a two-volume work titled *Pouvoir temporel des eveques de Bale*, published in Paris in 1891.
* **Biographies and Imagery:** The *Basler Biographien* was published in three volumes from 1900 to 1905, and L. A. Stocker published *Basler Stadtbilder* in 1890.
* **Political History:** K. Weber wrote *Die Revolution im Kanton Basel*, 1830-1833 (1907), and G. Gautherot published *La Republique rauracienne* in 1908.

Additionally, the *Festschrift z. 400ten Jahrestage d. ewig. Bundes zwisch. B. und den Eidgenossen* was published in 1901 to commemorate the 400th anniversary of the eternal alliance between Basel and the Eidgenossen.

## Other Publications in Basel
The city of Basel also served as a publication site for works unrelated to its own local history. For example, E. Dunant's *La Reunion des Grisons a la Suisse* (1798-1799) was published there in 1899. Additionally, the work *Über meine Entlassung* was published in Basel in 1838, and F. D. Gerlach's *Marius und Sulla* was published there in 1856. In the field of legal sources for other regions, R. Wagner and L. R. von Salis published *Rechtsquellen d. Cant. Graubunden* in Basel between 1877 and 1892, and F. Jecklin's *Materialen zur Standes- und Landesgeschichte Gem. iii. Bunde* (Graubunden), 1464-1803 (part i., *Regesten*), was published in Basel in 1907.
