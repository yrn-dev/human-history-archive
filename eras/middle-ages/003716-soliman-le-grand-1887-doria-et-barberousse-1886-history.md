# Soliman le Grand (1887) and Doria et Barberousse (1886)

The works *Soliman le Grand* (specifically titled *Les Corsaires barbaresques et la marine de Soliman le Grand*, 1887) and *Doria et Barberousse* (1886) are treatises written by Ad. Jurien de la Graviere and published in Paris. These texts are noted for being readable and for the author's application of technical knowledge to the subject matter.

## Subject Matter and Scope
The writings of Ad. Jurien de la Graviere focus on the epoch of the beylerbeys and the conduct of regular wars. Specifically, *Les Corsaires barbaresques et la marine de Soliman le Grand* and *Doria et Barberousse* detail the campaigns of the Barbarossas. These works are part of a broader series of treatises by the author that also include *Les Chevaliers de Malte* (1887) and *La Guerre de Chypre* (1888).

## Context within Barbary History
The history of the Barbary states and the Barbarossas has been documented by various authorities. Other sources concerning the Barbarossas include:
*   *The History of the Ottoman Empire* by Joseph von Hammer-Purgstall (French translation by J. J. Hellert, 1835-1843), though this account requires correction by other authorities.
*   *La Fondation de la regence d'Alger, histoire des Barberousse, chronique arabe du XVIeme siecle*, published in Paris (1837) by Sander Rang and Ferdinand Denis, which provides a Moslem version of the story.
*   *Histoire d'Alger* (Paris, 1887) by H. D. de Grammont, which is based on original authorities and collects later evidence.

Additional primary authorities for the early history of the Barbary states include Luis del Marmol Carvajal's *Descripcion de Africa* (1573), Diego de Haedo's *Topographia e Historia General de Argel* (1612), and Pere Pierre Dan's *Histoire de Barbarie et de ses corsaires* (1637).

## Related Maritime and Naval Records
The maritime conflicts of this era are further documented in *The History of the Maritime Wars of the Turks* by Hajji Khalifa (translated by J. Mitchell, 1831). This specific work is said to have been founded on evidence collected by order of the sultan Suleiman. In a different regional context, the history of the British consulate in Algiers is provided in Sir R. L. Playfair's *Scourge of Christendom* (1884), and American naval interactions are covered in Gardner Weld Allen's *Our Navy and the Barbary Corsairs* (1905).

## Biographical Context of Andrea Doria
As *Doria et Barberousse* concerns the figure of Doria, other biographical works on Andrea Doria exist in the archive. These include:
*   An accurate and documented biography by E. Petit (*Andre Doria*, Paris, 1887), though the author is noted as being perhaps unduly harsh in his judgment of the admiral.
*   *Vita di Andrea Doria* by F. D. Guerrazzi (3rd ed., 1874).
*   Earlier works such as L. Cappelloni's *Vita di Andrea Doria* (1863) and V. Sigonius's *Vita Andreae Doriae* (1576).
*   The *Atti della Societa ligure di Storia patria* (vol. viii), which contains "Documenti ispano-genovesi dell'Archivio di Simancas."
