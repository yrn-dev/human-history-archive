# Ch. de Remusat, Bacon, sa vie ... et son influence

## Overview
*Bacon, sa vie ... et son influence* is a work authored by Ch. de Remusat. The text was first published in 1857, with subsequent editions released in 1858 and 1877. It is categorized within the bibliography of philosophy, specifically concerning the life and influence of Francis Bacon.

## Philosophical Contributions and Induction
The work by Remusat is cited in discussions regarding the originality of Francis Bacon's theory of induction. A specific point of contention in philosophical study is whether Bacon's inductive method is merely a version of the Aristotelian *epagoge*. Remusat addresses this subject on pages 310-315 of *Bacon, sa vie ... et son influence*.

This discussion is further contextualized by other scholars, such as Waddington in *Essais de Logique*, who provides a criticism of these views. The broader debate centers on whether Bacon's induction—which he viewed as the process of elimination, exclusion, and rejection—was an original contribution or derived from Greek precedents. While Bacon acknowledged that Plato used a method similar to his own, the essence of Bacon's approach was tied to his doctrine of forms and his specific view of science and its problems.
