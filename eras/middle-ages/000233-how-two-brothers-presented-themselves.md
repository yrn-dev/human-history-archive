# The Presentations of the Polo Brothers

The travels of the two brothers, Messer Nicolo and Messer Maffeo Polo, are characterized by several formal presentations before high-ranking political and religious leaders. These encounters occurred during their journeys between Europe and the court of the Great Kaan.

## Presentation to Barca Kaan
During their early travels, after staying in Soldaia, the two brothers journeyed to the court of a Tartar Prince named Barca Kaan, whose residences were located at Bolgara and Sara. Barca Kaan was regarded as one of the most courteous and liberal Princes among the Tartars. Upon their arrival, the brothers presented the Prince with all the jewels they had brought with them. Barca Kaan accepted these offerings graciously and provided the brothers with a return value at least twice that of the jewels. The brothers remained at his court for a twelvemonth.

## First Presentation to the Great Kaan
Following the counsel of envoys they met in the city of Bocara, the two brothers traveled for a full year, moving northward and north-eastward, before reaching the court of the Great Kaan. The Great Kaan received them with hospitality and great honor, expressing significant pleasure at their visit.

During this presentation, the Great Kaan questioned the brothers extensively on the administration of foreign powers. He inquired about how emperors maintained their dignity, how they administered justice within their dominions, and the manner in which they went to battle. He asked similar questions regarding other potentates, princes, and kings. Furthermore, the Great Kaan sought information regarding the customs of the Latins, the Church, the Pope of Rome, and the general manners of Christians. The two brothers, who were proficient in the Tartar language, provided these answers with order, good sense, and truth.

## Presentation to the New Pope
After being sent as envoys by the Great Kaan to the Pope, the brothers eventually returned to Venice. Later, they departed Venice again, accompanied by Mark, the son of Messer Nicolo. After reaching Acre, the two brothers presented themselves before the new Pope, Pope Gregory of Piacenza (also referred to as Pope Gregory X).

The brothers approached the Pope with humble reverence and paid their respects. The Pope received them with satisfaction and great honor, granting them his blessing. In response to their presentation, the Pope appointed two learned Churchmen from the Order of Preachers—Friar William of Tripoli and Friar Nicolas of Vicenza—to accompany them back to the Great Kaan. The Pope provided the party with proper credentials, letters of reply to the Great Kaan's messages, and various fine crystal vessels as presents.

## Return to the Great Kaan
In a subsequent journey, Messer Nicolo and Messer Maffeo Polo, accompanied by Mark, traveled once more to the court of the Great Kaan. The records indicate that the three of them presented themselves before the Great Kaan together. Following this presentation, the Great Kaan employed Mark on an embassy of his own. Eventually, the two brothers and Mark asked for leave to depart and returned to their own country.
