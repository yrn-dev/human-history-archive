# OF THE PROVINCE OF YARCAN

## Overview and Geography
Yarcan, also referred to as Yarkand or Yarken, is a province described as being five days' journey in extent. There are various theories regarding the derivation of its name. One Chinese traveler suggests the name is composed of *Iar* (earth) and *Kiang* or *Kand* (large, vast), though this is considered doubtful. A more probable derivation is that the name is formed from *Yar* (new) and *Kand*, *Kend*, or *Kent* (city).

## Governance and Political Affiliation
The province is subject to the nephew of the Great Kaan. However, other records indicate that Yarcan belonged to Kaidu. This creates a point of contention regarding boundaries, as the border between the territories of Kaidu and the Great Kaan was situated between Komul and Karashahr, which is located significantly further to the east.

## Population and Culture
The inhabitants of Yarcan are noted for being great craftsmen. The province possesses an abundance of resources, with cotton being particularly plentiful.

The religious landscape of the province is diverse. While the people follow the Law of Mahommet, there are also populations of Jacobite and Nestorian Christians residing there.

## Public Health
A significant proportion of the population in Yarcan suffers from specific physical ailments. These include "great crops at the throat" (identified in archive notes as goître) and swollen legs. These conditions are attributed to a specific quality found in the province's drinking water.
