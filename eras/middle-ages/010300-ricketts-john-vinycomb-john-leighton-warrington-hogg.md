## Modern English Book-plate Design
In recent years, the creation of personal book-tokens, or book-plates, has transitioned from being a task left to the routine skill of heraldic stationers to being recognized as a minor branch of a higher art. This shift has introduced a new class of designs characterized by a wide variety of styles. In these modern designs, the purely heraldic elements tend to become subsidiary, while symbolic or allegorical elements assert themselves more strongly.

Among the modern English artists who have specifically focused on the devising of book-plates and produced admirable designs are Charles Ricketts, John Vinycomb, John Leighton, and Warrington Hogg. Other notable artists in this field include J. Forbes Nixon, Erat Harrison, J.D. Batten, Robert Anning Bell, and G.W. Eve, as well as C.W. Sherborn.

## Production and Popularization
The popularization of the book-plate, which was previously almost exclusively restricted to important collections or ancestral libraries, has been aided by the development of process work. This technology has made the reproduction of elaborate and beautiful designs both cheaper and easier; consequently, the vast majority of modern plates are reproduced by process. Despite this trend, a small number of artists continue to use the graver. In England, the most well-known of these engravers are G.W. Eve and C.W. Sherborn.
