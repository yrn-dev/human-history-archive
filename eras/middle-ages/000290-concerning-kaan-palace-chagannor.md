# CONCERNING THE KAAN’S PALACE OF CHAGANNOR

## Overview and Construction
The palace of Chagannor served as a residence for the Kaan. According to the *Siu t’ung kien*, as quoted by Palladius, the palace in Chagannor was constructed in the year 1280. The location is situated in a region where the Kaan maintained a residence for three months of the year—specifically during June, July, and August. This location was preferred by the Kaan because it is a very cool place and is by no means hot.

## The Cane Palace and Seasonal Use
Within the grounds of the park, the Lord resided alternately in a Marble Palace and a Cane Palace. The use of these residences was seasonal; upon the arrival of the 28th day of the moon of August, the Kaan would take his departure from the site. Following his departure, the Cane Palace was taken to pieces.

## The Valley of the Cators
Near the city, there is a valley where the Emperor established several small houses. These structures were used to keep a huge number of *cators* in mew. The *cators* are identified as the Great Partridge. These birds were maintained in such vast quantities that men were appointed specifically to take charge of them. This arrangement ensured that whenever the Kaan visited the place, he could be furnished with as many *cators* as he desired.

## Associated Imperial Assets
The Kaan maintained an immense stud of white horses and mares at this location, numbering more than 10,000 animals, all of which were pure white without a speck. The milk from these mares was reserved exclusively for the Kaan and his family. The only exception to this rule was a single great tribe known as the Horiad, who were granted the privilege of drinking the milk. This privilege was bestowed upon them by Chinghis Kaan as a reward for their assistance in winning a certain victory long ago.

## Geography and Access
The palace of Chagannor was positioned along the routes between the capital and other imperial sites. According to records from Palladius, the journey from Siuen-te chau to Chagannor took three days, and the journey from Chagannor to Shang-tu also took three days.
