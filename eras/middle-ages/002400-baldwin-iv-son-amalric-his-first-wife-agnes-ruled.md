# Baldwin IV

Baldwin IV was the King of Jerusalem from 1174 to 1183. He was the son of Amalric I and his first wife, Agnes. Educated by William of Tyre, Baldwin IV ascended to the throne at the age of thirteen. Because of his youth and the fact that he was a leper, the kingdom was placed under the regency of Raymund II of Tripoli.

## Political Climate and Saladin
The early years of Baldwin IV's reign were characterized by a period of relative stability for the kingdom, as the attention of Saladin was diverted. For several years, Saladin attempted to seize the inheritance of the sons of Nureddin. This effort saw partial success in 1174, though it was not fully realized until 1183.

## Internal Challenges and Key Figures
The reign of Baldwin IV was primarily troubled by two figures: his sister Sibylla and Raynald of Chatillon.

Sibylla, the heiress to the kingdom, presented a significant political problem regarding her marriage. She was first married to William of Montferrat, with whom she had a son named Baldwin. In 1180, she married Guy of Lusignan. The latter years of Baldwin IV's reign were marked by dissensions between the king and the couple consisting of Sibylla and Guy.

Raynald of Chatillon, the lord of Krak (Kerak) to the east of the Dead Sea since 1176, was a former prince of Antioch through his marriage to Constance (1153-1159) and had spent many years as a captive of the Mahommedans. Raynald utilized the strategic position of his fortress, which sat upon the primary trade route between Egypt and Damascus, to plunder caravans in 1182. These actions helped precipitate an inevitable attack by Saladin.

## Succession and Regency
When Saladin's attack occurred, Baldwin IV appointed Guy of Lusignan as regent. However, Guy declined to engage in battle. As a result, Baldwin IV deposed Guy from both his regency and his right of succession. In 1183, Baldwin IV had his nephew—Sibylla's son by her first husband—crowned as King Baldwin V.

Despite these changes, Baldwin IV remained active for a period. In 1184, he transferred the regency to Raymund of Tripoli. Baldwin IV died in 1185.
