# BUCHAN, EARLS OF

The earldom of Buchan was originally part of the earldom of Mar and Buchan, which was one of the seven original Scottish earldoms. In later history, Buchan was separated from Mar to become its own distinct earldom.

## Early Earls and the Comyn Family
Among the early holders of the title were Alexander Comyn, who died in 1289, and John Comyn, who died around 1313; both men served as constables of Scotland. Henry Beaumont, who died in 1340, also held the earldom after marrying a member of the Comyn family. 

The Comyn family's influence extended to other high-ranking nobles of the era. Marjory, the daughter of Alexander Comyn, earl of Buchan, married Patrick Dunbar, the 1st earl of March and 8th earl of Dunbar. While Patrick Dunbar was favorable to English interests and did homage to Edward I of England, Marjory took the opposing side, holding the castle of Dunbar for Baliol until she was forced to surrender it to Edward in 1296.

Another significant figure was Isabel, the wife of John Comyn and countess of Buchan. In 1306, she crowned Robert the Bruce as king at Scone. Following this event, she was imprisoned at Berwick, though records clarify that she was not held in a cage on the castle wall.

## The Stewart Period
Around 1382, the earldom was acquired by Sir Alexander Stewart (d. c. 1404), known as the "wolf of Badenoch" and a son of King Robert II. The Stewart family maintained control of the earldom for approximately one and a half centuries, although the succession did not follow a direct line from Sir Alexander.

The Stewart era produced several celebrated earls, including Robert, duke of Albany, who served as the Scottish regent. His son, John, also held the title and was appointed constable of France before being killed at the battle of Verneuil in 1424.

## Transition to the Erskine Family
In 1617, the earldom passed to James Erskine, who died in 1640. James was the son of John Erskine, the 2nd (or 7th) earl of Mar. The title came to James through his mother, Mary, who had inherited the earldom from her father, James Douglas, who died in 1601. From 1617 onward, the earldom of Buchan has been retained by the Erskine family.
