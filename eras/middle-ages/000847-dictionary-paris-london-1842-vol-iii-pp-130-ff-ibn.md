# Biographical Dictionary of Ibn Khallikan

The *Biographical Dictionary* is a historical work authored by Ibn Khallikan (also referred to as Ibn Halikan). A significant English translation of this text was produced by McG. de Slane and published in Paris and London in 1842.

## Use as a Biographical Source

The *Biographical Dictionary* serves as a primary reference for the lives of various historical figures. Within the 1842 translation by McG. de Slane, specific biographical accounts are detailed across multiple volumes:

*   **Asma'i**: Information regarding the life of Asma'i is located in volume ii, pages 123-127.
*   **Buhtur**: The biography of the Arabian poet Buhtur (al-Walid ibn 'Ubaid Allah), who lived from 820 to 897, is found in volume iii, starting on page 657.
*   **Other Figures**: The work is also cited as a source for the life of Avicenna, and other biographical details can be found in volume iii, pages 130 and following.

## Historical and Academic Application

Beyond individual biographies, the *Biographical Dictionary* has been utilized by historians to reconstruct military and political events. For example, M. Cardonne used the writings of Ibn Halikan, along with Hidjazi and an anonymous writer, to compile a "pure and simple account" of the losses suffered by the Arabs during their invasion of France and their subsequent defeat by Charles Martel.
