# Verfassungsgeschichte d. Stadt Basel im Mittelalter (1860)

*Verfassungsgeschichte d. Stadt Basel im Mittelalter* is a historical work published in 1860. It was authored by A. Heusler, who also produced the two-volume work *Rechtsquellen von Basel* between 1856 and 1865.

## Context and Related Scholarship
The work exists within a broader body of literature concerning the history and administration of Basel. ## Academic and Institutional History of Basel
The intellectual environment of Basel is reflected in the history of its university. W. Vischer published a history of the University of Basel covering the period from 1460 to 1529 in 1860. R. Thommen later published a history of the university covering the years 1532 to 1632 in 1889.

The city's academic life also included figures such as Jakob Burckhardt (1818–1897). Burckhardt served as a professor of history at the University of Basel during several periods: from 1845 to 1847, 1849 to 1855, and finally from 1858 until 1893. Despite being offered professorships at Tübingen in 1867 and Berlin in 1872 (to fill a vacancy left by Ranke), Burckhardt remained in Basel.
