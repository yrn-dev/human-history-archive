# Regesta chronologico-diplomatica regum atque imperatorum Romanorum

The *Regesta chronologico-diplomatica regum atque imperatorum Romanorum* is a historical abstract focused on the collection and tabulation of imperial documents and charters from the middle ages. It was authored by the German historian Johann Friedrich Bohmer.

## Authorship and Development
Johann Friedrich Bohmer (1795–1863) was a historian born in Frankfort-on-Main. Educated at the universities of Gottingen and Heidelberg, Bohmer served as the librarian and archivist for the city of Frankfort, as well as the secretary of the *Gesellschaft fur altere deutsche Geschichtskunde*. 

Bohmer's scholarly work was primarily dedicated to the organization of imperial documents. The *Regesta chronologico-diplomatica regum atque imperatorum Romanorum* first appeared in 1831 in Frankfort, covering the period from 911 to 1313. This work was later expanded upon through several subsequent publications and supplements:
*   For the period 1246–1313, two supplementary volumes were published in 1844.
*   For the period 1314–1347, three supplementary volumes were published in 1839.
*   Bohmer also edited a section of the *Regesta* covering the period 1198–1254, published in Stuttgart in 1849.

These collections are characterized by the inclusion of explanatory passages and introductions written by Bohmer.

## Related Works by Bohmer
In addition to the *Regesta chronologico-diplomatica regum atque imperatorum Romanorum*, Bohmer produced several other significant collections of historical documents and authorities:
*   **Regesta chronologico-diplomatica Karolorum**: Published in 1833, this work provides short abstracts of the documents of all the Carolingians.
*   **Fontes rerum Germanicarum**: Published between 1843 and 1868 in Stuttgart, this is a collection of original authorities regarding German history during the 14th and 13th centuries. The final volume of this series was edited by A. Huber following Bohmer's death.
*   **Die Reichsgesetze 900-1400**: Published in 1832.
*   **Wittelsbachische Regesten von der Erwerbung des Herzogtums Bayern bis zu 1340**: Published in 1854.
*   **Codex diplomaticus Moeno-Francofurtanus**: An *Urkundenbuch* (document book) of the imperial city of Frankfurt, published in 1836, with a later edition released by F. Law in 1901.

Bohmer also contributed a series of *Regesta imperii*.

## Historical Context of the Author
Bohmer's personal views influenced his professional life; he maintained a great affection for the Roman Catholic Church and Austria, while expressing a strong dislike for the Protestant faith and Prussia. Despite these inclinations, he did not belong to the Roman Catholic Church. He died unmarried in Frankfort on October 22, 1863.
