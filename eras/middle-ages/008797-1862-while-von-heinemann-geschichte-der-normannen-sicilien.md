In the field of geology, 1862 marked the death of Heinrich Georg Bronn, a German geologist born in 1800. Bronn, who died at Heidelberg on July 5, 1862, was a professor of natural history and a specialist in palaeontological studies. His contributions to the field included the editing of the *Jahrbuch für Mineralogie* (and its continuation as *Neues Jahrbuch*) from 1830 until his death.
