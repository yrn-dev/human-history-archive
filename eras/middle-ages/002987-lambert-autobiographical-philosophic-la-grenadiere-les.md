# Louis Lambert, La Grenadiere, and Les Marana

The titles *Louis Lambert*, *La Grenadiere*, and *Les Marana* refer to literary works published in 1832. These works are part of a larger body of writing produced during a prolific period of authorship that included several other notable titles.

## Louis Lambert
Published in 1832, *Louis Lambert* is characterized as being both philosophic and autobiographical in nature.

## La Grenadiere and Les Marana
Also published in 1832, *La Grenadiere* and *Les Marana* appeared alongside *Louis Lambert*. Of these, *Les Marana* was a great favorite of the author.

## Context of Production
These works were written during a period of significant literary output. In the "epoch year" of 1830, other works were produced, including *La Maison du chat qui pelote* and *La Peau de chagrin* (the latter of which was not fully published until 1831). *La Peau de chagrin* is noted for its central idea regarding the Nemesis of accomplished desire, though it is described by some as being full of surplusage and romantic extravagance.

Following the 1832 publications of *Louis Lambert*, *La Grenadiere*, and *Les Marana*, the author produced further works in 1833. These included *Eugenie Grandet*, which is considered a universally admitted masterpiece, and *L'Illustre Gaudissart*, described as very amusing. Other 1833 titles include *Ferragus, chef des devorants* (the first part of *L'Histoire des treize*) and *Le Medecin de campagne*. The latter contains a popular "legend of Napoleon," though the work as a whole is considered a little heavy.
