# Basel: Historical and Bibliographic Records

The historical study of Basel is supported by a wide array of specialized literature covering its biography, chronicles, constitutional history, and the development of its university and industry.

## Historical Documentation and Chronicles
The archival record for Basel includes the *Basler Chroniken*, a five-volume set of original chronicles published in Leipzig between 1872 and 1890. Further biographical data is contained within the three volumes of *Basler Biographien*, published from 1900 to 1905. General histories of the city include the work of H. Boos, *Geschichte von Basel*, though only the first volume (covering the period up to 1501) was published in 1877, and the three-volume *Gesch. d. Stadt Basel* by R. Wackernagel, published from 1906 onwards. A. Burckhardt also contributed to the historical record with *Bilder aus d. Geschichte von Basel*, published in three volumes between 1869 and 1882.

## Governance and Legal History
The legal and constitutional framework of Basel during the Middle Ages is detailed in A. Heusler's *Verfassungsgeschichte d. Stadt Basel im Mittelalter* (1860) and the two-volume *Rechtsquellen von Basel* (1856-1865). The temporal power of the bishops of Basel is specifically addressed in the two-volume work by L. Stouff, *Pouvoir temporel des eveques de Bale*, published in Paris in 1891. 

Additionally, the city's relationship with the Swiss Confederates is documented in the *Festschrift z. 400ten Jahrestage d. ewig. Bundes zwisch. B. und den Eidgenossen* (1901). More recent political upheavals are recorded in K. Weber's *Die Revolution im Kanton Basel, 1830-1833* (1907). Primary source materials are available through the *Urkundenbuch*, with the version for the landscape (*Landschaft B.*) published from 1881 and the version for the city published from 1890.

## Urban Life and Institutions
The visual and physical aspects of the city are captured in L. A. Stocker's *Basler Stadtbilder* (1890). The economic development of the city, specifically its trade and industry, is analyzed in T. Geering's *Handel und Industrie d. Stadt Basel* (1885). 

The history of the University of Basel is documented across two primary scholarly works: W. Vischer's *Gesch. d. Universitaet B.*, which covers the years 1460-1529 (1860), and R. Thommen's *Gesch. d. Universitaet B.*, which covers the period from 1532-1632 (1889).
