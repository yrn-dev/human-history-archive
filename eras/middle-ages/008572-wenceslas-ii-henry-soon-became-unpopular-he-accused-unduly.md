# Wenceslas II

## Early Life and Accession
Wenceslas II was the son of Ottakar. At the time of his father's death, Wenceslas was only seven years of age. During his early years, Bohemia was governed by his nephew, Otto of Brandenburg, who served as the guardian of the young sovereign. Otto's period of rule was marked by significant unpopularity, which led to an insurrection and a period of complete anarchy within Bohemia.

The country was eventually pacified through the intervention of Rudolph of Habsburg. At the age of twelve, Wenceslas became the nominal ruler of Bohemia, although actual power remained in the hands of Zavis of Falkenstein. Zavis was one of the great Bohemian nobles and had married Kunegunda, the mother of the king.

## Independent Rule and Reign
The influence of Zavis of Falkenstein eventually became invidious to Wenceslas. In 1290, the king ordered that Zavis be beheaded. Despite being only nineteen years of age at the time, Wenceslas thereafter governed Bohemia himself. His short reign is described as a period of great happiness for the country.

Under his rule, the influence of Wenceslas expanded beyond Bohemia; Poland accepted his rule, and he was offered the Hungarian crown.

## Conflict and Death
Towards the end of his reign, Wenceslas became involved in a war with Albert, the archduke of Austria (who later became king of the Romans). Wenceslas died suddenly in 1305 while he was preparing to invade Austria.

## Succession and Legacy
Wenceslas was succeeded by his son, Wenceslas III, who was sixteen years old at the time of his father's death. Wenceslas III ruled over Bohemia for only one year. While planning a warlike expedition against Poland—a country over which the Bohemian sovereigns continued to maintain a claim—Wenceslas III was murdered by unknown assassins in 1306. This event marked the end of the Premyslide dynasty's rule over Bohemia.

Wenceslas II also had a daughter, Princess Elizabeth. She married John of Luxemburg, who later became king of Bohemia. Another daughter of Wenceslas II married Henry, duke of Carinthia, who was elected sovereign by the Bohemians after the death of Rudolph.
