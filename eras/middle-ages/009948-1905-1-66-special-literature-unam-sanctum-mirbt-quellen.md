# Carl Theodor Mirbt

## Biography and Academic Position
Carl Theodor Mirbt, D.TH., is a Professor of Church History at the University of Marburg. He is recognized as an author and scholar of ecclesiastical history, having produced works such as *Publizistik im Zeitalter Gregor VII.* and *Quellen zur Geschichte des Papstthums*.

## Contributions to Papal History
Mirbt has contributed extensively to the study of the papacy through the compilation and analysis of primary sources. His work *Quellen zur Geschichte des Papsttums* (2nd edition, Tubingen, 1901) serves as a significant resource for historians. Specifically, this text reprints the most important decrees and letters of Gregory VII (found in Nr. 183 sqq., pp. 100 sqq.).

Additionally, Mirbt's scholarship extends to the analysis of specific papal documents and figures:
* **Boniface VIII:** Mirbt's *Quellen zur Geschichte des Papsttums* is cited as special literature regarding the document *Unam Sanctum* (specifically pages 148 f.).
* **Gregory VII:** Mirbt provided a comprehensive survey of the literature and sources for the history of Gregory VII. This survey is located under the entry "Gregor VII." in the 3rd edition of Herzog-Hauck's *Realencyklopadie* (vol. vii, pp. 96 sqq.).
* **General Papal Studies:** Mirbt is credited with writing an excellent article and providing an exhaustive bibliography on Gregory in Hauck's *Realencyklopadie* (3rd edition).
