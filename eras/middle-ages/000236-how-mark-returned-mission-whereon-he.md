# THE RETURN OF MARCO POLO FROM HIS MISSION

## Presentation to the Emperor
Upon the conclusion of his ambassage, Mark returned and presented himself before the Emperor. His first action upon returning was to provide a formal report regarding the specific business with which he had been charged. He informed the Emperor that this business had been successfully accomplished.

## Account of Travels
Following his official report, Mark provided a detailed account of his experiences. He described the various novelties and strange things he had seen and heard during his mission. This account was delivered in a manner that was characterized as both intelligent and pleasant.

## Reception and Recognition
The narrative provided by Mark had a significant impact on his audience. The Emperor, as well as all others who heard the story, were surprised by the account. This display of ability led the listeners to remark that if the young man continued to live, he would certainly become a person of great worth and ability.

As a result of this successful return and the impression he made upon the Emperor, he was granted a new title. From that time forward, he was always referred to as Messer Marco Polo.

## Nature of the Mission
While the term "ambassage" is used, the nature of Mark's employment was likely that of a commissioner. This role corresponds to the term *Ilchi*, which referred to various classes of individuals employed on government commissions. Such roles were considered confidential and honourable.
