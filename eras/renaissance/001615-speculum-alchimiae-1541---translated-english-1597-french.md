# Speculum Alchimiae

## Overview and Publication
The *Speculum Alchimiae* is a printed work first published in 1541. It is listed among the printed works of Roger Bacon, an English author.

## Translations
Following its initial publication, the text was translated into other languages to reach a wider audience. An English translation of the *Speculum Alchimiae* was produced in 1597. Additionally, a French translation was published by A. Poisson in 1890.

## Context within the Author's Works
The *Speculum Alchimiae* is part of a broader body of literature attributed to Roger Bacon. Other works printed during the 16th and 17th centuries include *De Mirabili Potestate Artis et Naturae* (1542), which saw an English translation in 1659, and the *Libellus de Retardandis Senectutis Accidentibus* (1590), translated by Richard Brown in 1683 as the "Cure of Old Age."

Rogeri Baconis Anglici de Arte Chymiae Scripta*, which contains several small tracts including *Secretum Secretorum*, *Speculum Secretorum*, *Tractatus Trium Verborum*, *Breve Breviarium*, *Verbum Abbreviatum*, and *Excerpta de Libra Avicennae de Anima*.
