## Notable Deaths
Mario di Calasio (1550–1620) died on February 1, 1620. An Italian Minorite friar born in a small town in the Abruzzi, Calasio was a Franciscan who became an authority on Hebrew and devoted himself to Oriental languages. He served as the confessor to Paul V and held the chair of Scripture at Ara Coeli in Rome. Calasio authored the *Dictionarium hebraicum* (1617) and the Hebrew grammar *Canones generates linguae sanctatae* (1616). He spent forty years working on the *Concordantiae sacrorum Bibliorum hebraicorum*, a work based on Nathan's *Hebrew Concordance* (1523) and published posthumously in four volumes in 1622.
