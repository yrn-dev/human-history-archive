# Old Palace (Alte Residenz)

The Old Palace, known as the *Alte Residenz*, is a picturesque building located in the town of Bamberg, an archiepiscopal see in the kingdom of Bavaria, Germany.

## Construction and History
The *Alte Residenz* was constructed in 1591. It was built upon the site of a previous residence that had belonged to the counts of Babenberg. It stands as one of the notable architectural features of Bamberg, alongside the New Palace, which was built between 1698 and 1704 and served as the former residence of the prince-bishops and, later, the deposed King Otto of Greece from 1864 to 1867.

## Location and Urban Context
The Old Palace is situated within Bamberg, a town positioned on an open plain on the river Regnitz, approximately two miles above where the river joins the Main. The town's geography is divided into an upper and lower section. The upper town is characterized by seven hills, each of which is crowned by a church. The lower town is divided into three districts by the Ludwigskanal and the river, and it remains partially enclosed by ditches and walls.

## Related Landmarks in Bamberg
The *Alte Residenz* is part of a city rich in historical and religious monuments:
*   **The Cathedral:** A late Romanesque building founded in 1004 by emperor Henry II and completed in 1012. It contains an equestrian statue of emperor Conrad III and a marble tomb for the empress Cunigunde and Henry II, carved by Tilman Riemenschneider between 1499 and 1513.
*   **Other Churches:** These include the 11th-century Romanesque basilica of Jakobskirche, the St Martinskirche, and the Marienkirche (or Obere Pfarrkirche), built between 1320 and 1387 in a pure Gothic style. The 12th-century Romanesque Michaelskirche sits on the Michaelsberg; it was formerly a Benedictine monastery before being secularized in 1803.
*   **Civic Structures:** The Rathaus, rebuilt between 1744 and 1756, is located on an artificial island halfway across the *Obere Bruecke* (completed 1455). The town also houses a royal lyceum, formerly a Jesuit college, which contains a royal library with over 300,000 volumes.
*   **Monuments:** The Maximilian fountain, erected in 1880, features statues of St Otto (bishop of Bamberg), Conrad III, emperor Henry II and his wife, and Maximilian I of Bavaria.
