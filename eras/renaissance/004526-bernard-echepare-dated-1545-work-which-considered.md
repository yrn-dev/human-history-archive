# Bernard d'Echepare

## Literary Contributions
Bernard d'Echepare was a writer whose work represents a significant milestone in Basque literature. He is the author of the *Linguae Vasconum Primitiae*, a collection of poems. This work is distinguished as the first book printed in the Basque language.

## Chronology and Context
The poems of Bernard d'Echepare are dated to 1545. In the broader context of Basque literary history, the archive notes that folk-tales cannot be considered exclusively Basque and that the general literature of the region is altogether too modern. While d'Echepare provided the first printed work, the standard for the Basque language is considered to be the Protestant translation of the New Testament. This translation was printed in 1571 at La Rochelle by Jean de Licarrague, under the auspices of Jeanne d'Albret.

## Basque Literary Traditions
The Basque literary landscape includes *pastorales*, which are open-air dramas similar to the mysteries and moralities of the middle ages. Although these dramas are derived from French materials, certain elements of their *mise-en-scene*—specifically the invariable introduction of a dancing chorus—suggest the existence of earlier traditions. 

Despite these forms, the archive indicates that the majority of other Basque literature consists of translated and religious works. No discovered manuscript is earlier than the 18th century, and it is only recently that a real literature has been attempted in the Basque language with any success.
