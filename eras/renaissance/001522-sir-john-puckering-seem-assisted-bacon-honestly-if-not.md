## Family and Intellectual Background

Bacon was born into a family of significant culture and political influence. His father, Sir Nicholas Bacon, served as lord keeper of the great seal under Queen Elizabeth and maintained a close friendship with Sir William Cecil. His mother was a daughter of Sir Anthony Cooke and a warm adherent of the Puritan or Reformed Church.

Intellectually, Bacon was dissatisfied with the Aristotelian philosophy taught during his time at Cambridge, viewing it as fruitless and useful only for disputation.
