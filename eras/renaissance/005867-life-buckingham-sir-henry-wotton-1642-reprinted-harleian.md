# Life of Buckingham, by Sir Henry Wotton (1642)

The *Life of Buckingham* is a biographical work written by Sir Henry Wotton in 1642. This text has been preserved and reprinted in the *Harleian Miscellany* (viii. 613). It serves as one of the primary biographical sources concerning the life of George Villiers, the Duke of Buckingham.

## Biographical Details of the Subject
According to the *Life* written by Wotton, the date of Buckingham's birth is recorded as August 28th. However, archival analysis of the text suggests this may be a misprint. Wotton notes that Buckingham died on August 23rd, stating that the "great peer" died in the 36th year of his age "compleat and three days over." Based on this calculation of his age at death, it is probable that the intended birth date was August 20th rather than August 28th.

## The Author and Context
The author, Sir Henry Wotton, belonged to a family associated with Bocton Malherbe. The Wotton lineage produced Nicholas Wotton, who served as a privy councillor to several English monarchs, including Henry VIII, Edward VI, Mary, and Elizabeth. 

In the broader context of English literature, Wotton's own life was later the subject of a biography by Izaak Walton, published in 1651. Walton is regarded as a pioneer of deliberate English biography, having also published a *Life of Donne* in 1640.
