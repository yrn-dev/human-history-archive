## Key Historians and Authoritative Works
The study of the English constitution is supported by a wide array of scholarly texts. Prominent among these are the works of W. Stubbs, whose *Constitutional History of England* is cited across multiple volumes, and H. Hallam, who also authored a *Constitutional History of England*. Other significant contributions include T. ## The Office of the Justiciar
In the broader context of constitutional history, the role of the justiciar is noted for its eventual decline. Hubert is remembered as the last of the great justiciars. During his time, he was viewed as a popular hero; Matthew Paris records that a common smith once refused to put fetters on Hubert, describing him as the man "who restored England to the English." However, the office of the justiciar eventually became too powerful for a subject to hold. Consequently, its most important powers were removed, rendering the position politically insignificant.
