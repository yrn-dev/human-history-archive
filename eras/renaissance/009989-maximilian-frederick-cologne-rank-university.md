# Maximilian Frederick of Cologne and the University

## Elevation of the Academy
In 1786, Archbishop Maximilian Frederick of Cologne raised an existing academy to the rank of a university. This academy had been founded approximately nine years prior to the Archbishop's action. Following its elevation to university status, the institution continued to exercise its functions until 1794, at which point it was dissolved by the last elector.

## The University Building and Facilities
The university occupies a significant portion of the southern frontage of the town. The building currently used by the university was originally the electoral palace, which was constructed around 1717 using materials sourced from old fortifications. After the town came into Prussian possession, the building was remodelled.

The present establishment of the university dates from 1818 and was created by King Frederick William III of Prussia. The institution currently consists of five faculties:
*   Legal
*   Medical
*   Philosophic
*   Roman Catholic theology
*   Protestant theology

The university's library contains more than 230,000 volumes, and its antiquarian museum houses a valuable collection of Roman relics found in the surrounding area. The university is further supported by a laboratory, five clinical departments, and institutes dedicated to chemical, pathological, and physiological studies.

## Associated Institutions and Landmarks
Several other academic and scientific facilities are connected to or located near the university:
*   **Academy of Agriculture:** Located at the palace of Clemensruhe in Poppelsdorf, this academy includes a botanic garden and a natural history museum. It is accessed via a fine avenue roughly one mile long, featuring double rows of chestnut trees on both sides.
*   **Observatory:** A splendid observatory is situated on the south side of the road; it was under the charge of Friedrich Wilhelm Argelander for a long period.
*   **Roman Catholic Archiepiscopal Theological College:** Established in 1892, this college is situated on an eminence that overlooks the Rhine.
