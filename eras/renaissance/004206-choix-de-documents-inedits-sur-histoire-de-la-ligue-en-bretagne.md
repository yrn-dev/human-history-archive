# Choix de documents inedits sur l'histoire de la ligue en Bretagne

## Overview
*Choix de documents inedits sur l'histoire de la ligue en Bretagne* is a historical work published in 1880. The text was authored solely by Anatole Jean-Baptiste Antoine de Barthélemy.

## Authorship
The work was written by Anatole Jean-Baptiste Antoine de Barthélemy (1821–1904), a French numismatist and archaeologist. Born in Reims on July 1, 1821, and passing away in Ville d'Avray on June 27, 1904, Barthélemy was a recognized scholar who was elected as a member of the Académie des Inscriptions et Belles-lettres in 1897.

While *Choix de documents inedits sur l'histoire de la ligue en Bretagne* was a solo effort, Barthélemy also engaged in collaborative historical research. In 1858, he worked with J. Geslin de Bourgogne to publish *Etudes sur la revolution en Bretagne*. Additionally, between 1855 and 1879, he produced an exhaustive six-volume series titled *Anciens eveches de Bretagne; histoire et monuments*.

## Academic Context
Barthélemy is most noted for his reputation in the field of numismatics. His contributions to this discipline include several popular publications, such as the *Nouveau manuel complet de numismatique ancienne* (first published in 1851 with a revised second edition in 1890) and the *Nouveau manuel complet de numismatique du moyen age et moderne* (1853), which was later revised by Adrien Planchet.

His technical expertise is further evidenced by a variety of monographs and articles, including:
* *Numismatique merovingienne* (1865)
* *Essai sur la monnaie parisis* (1874)
* *Note sur l'origine de la monnaie tournoise* (1896)

Furthermore, he edited the volume on *La Numismatique de la France* (1891) for the series of instructions issued by the Comité des travaux historiques et scientifiques.
