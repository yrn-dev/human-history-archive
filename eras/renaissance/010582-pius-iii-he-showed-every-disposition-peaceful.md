# Pius III.

## Election and Character
Francesco Todeschini-Piccolomini was elected as Pope Pius III. during a conclave held in 1503. His election occurred during a period of significant instability in Rome, following the death of the previous pope on August 18, 1503. This death had coincided with a fever that incapacitated Cesare Borgia, an event that proved to be Borgia's ruin as his enemies rose against him. While Cesare had hoped the Spanish cardinals would elect a candidate friendly to his house, the election of Francesco Todeschini-Piccolomini resulted in a pontificate characterized by a peaceful and respectable disposition. Despite these qualities, Pius III. was noted to be old and in poor health.

## Impact on Cesare Borgia
The ascension of Pius III. had an immediate negative impact on the dominions of Cesare Borgia, which began to fall apart. Various lords and dukes regained their territories during this time:
* Guidobaldo, the duke of Urbino, returned to his duchy with the assistance of Venice.
* The lords of Pesaro, Rimini, and Piombino regained their own lands.

The only territory that continued to hold out was Cesena, which was defended by a governor who remained faithful to Cesare.

## Death and Succession
The reign of Pius III. was brief, as he died on October 18, 1503. Following his death, a new conclave was convened. Cesare Borgia, still relying on the support of the Spanish cardinals, attempted to prevent the election of Giuliano della Rovere, who was an enemy of the Borgia house. However, della Rovere's prospects had improved to the point that terms had to be reached with him.

On November 1, Giuliano della Rovere was elected and took the name Julius II. While Julius II did not show ill-will toward Cesare, he declared that the territories Cesare held must be restored to the church, stating, "we desire the honour of recovering what our predecessors have wrongfully alienated." This eventually led to the end of Borgia's power, as Julius II later had him arrested to prevent Venice from establishing a protectorate over the principalities in Romagna.
