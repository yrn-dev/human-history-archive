# Anacanthim and Marine Life of South Asia

## Distribution of the Cod Family
Within the Anacanthim, the cod family, which is well known in Europe, is represented by only one or two species in the seas of south Asia. In contrast, allied fishes and soles are numerous along the coasts of this region.

## Other Fish Families and Species
The seas of south Asia and surrounding areas host a variety of other fish families:

*   **Physostomi:** The siluroids are abundant in muddy waters and estuaries. Certain species exhibit remarkable habits, such as males that carry ova in their mouths until the young hatch.
*   **Scopelidae:** This small family includes the gelatinous *Harpodon*, also known as the bumalo.
*   **Clupeidae:** Herrings are most abundant in these waters. Sardines, or anchovies, are also found in shoals, though they appear at uncertain and irregular intervals.
*   **Other Species:** Flying fishes and gar-fish are numerous and extend into European seas. Marine eels (*Muraenidae*) are found in the Indian seas, though they are more numerous toward the Malay Archipelago. Additionally, pipe-fishes (*Syngnathus*), sea-horses (*Hippocampus*), fife-fishes (*Sclerodermus*), globe-fish, sun-fish, and other allied forms of *Gymnodontes* are not uncommon.

## Cartilaginous Fishes
The Chondropterygii, or cartilaginous fishes, are well-represented in these waters. True sharks and hammer-headed sharks are numerous. The dog-fish is also present, with one species extending from the Indian seas to the Cape of Good Hope. Furthermore, ordinary rays and skates, electrical rays (*Torpedinae*), and saw-fishes (*Pristidae*) are found in considerable numbers.
