# Albert Frederick Pollard

Albert Frederick Pollard, M.A., F.R.Hist.Soc., was a historian and academic who served as the Professor of English History at the University of London. He was a Fellow of All Souls' College, Oxford, and held the position of Assistant Editor of the *Dictionary of National Biography* from 1893 to 1901. His academic achievements include receiving the Lothian prizeman (Oxford) in 1892 and the Arnold prizeman in 1898.
