# February 1672

The month of February 1672 is associated with several significant appointments and deaths across the fields of academia, the arts, and the nobility.

## Academic Appointments
In February 1672, Dr. Barrow was appointed as the master of Trinity College. This appointment was made via a king's patent dated February 13, 1672, and occurred following the promotion of Dr. Pearson to the see of Chester. Dr. Barrow had previously held several academic positions, including the Greek professorship at Cambridge starting in 1660 and a professorship of geometry at Gresham College in 1662. He had also served as the first professor of a mathematical chair at Cambridge founded by Henry Lucas, a position he resigned in 1669 to his pupil, Isaac Newton, in order to pursue the study of divinity. Following his appointment as master of Trinity College, Dr. Barrow was later chosen as the vice-chancellor of the university in 1675.

## Arts and Theatre
The month of February 1672 also marked the death of Madeleine Bejart, who died on February 17, 1672. Bejart was a tall, handsome blonde and an accomplished actress known for her soubrette roles. She had headed a travelling company that included her brothers and her sister, Genevieve, before joining Moliere to form l'Illustre Theatre in 1643. Moliere wrote several roles specifically for her, including Dorine in *Tartuffe*, Lisette in *L'Ecole des maris*, and Marotte in *Les Precieuses ridicules*. Despite a violent and acrimonious temper, Bejart and her family remained faithful to Moliere's fortunes until her death.

## Political and Religious Developments
During 1672, Buckingham, who had been elected chancellor of Cambridge in 1671, was appointed high steward of Oxford university. In the same year, he advised the declaration of indulgence and continued to favor religious toleration.

Additionally, the year 1672 was a period of transition for the nonconformist preacher Bunyan. While his formal pardon is dated September 13, 1672, he had received a royal licence to preach five months prior to that date. For the following three years, he served as the pastor of his nonconformist body, preaching in a barn on the site where the Bunyan Meeting currently stands.

## Scientific Discoveries
In the field of astronomy, 1672 saw the detection of Rhea, a satellite of Saturn. This discovery was made using a telescope with a 34-ft focus created by G. Campani. This occurred during a period of growth for national observatories, following the establishment of the French national observatory in 1671. Olaus Romer began a nine-year tenure at the Paris observatory starting in 1672, during which time he became familiar with the principles of the altazimuth and the "parallactic machine."
