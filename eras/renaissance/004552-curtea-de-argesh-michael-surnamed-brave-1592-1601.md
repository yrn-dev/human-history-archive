# Michael the Brave and Curtea de Argesh

## The Bassarab Dynasty
The Bassarab dynasty was a prominent family in the history of Walachia. Among its most notable members was Mircea (1386-1418), who accepted Turkish suzerainty. Other significant figures included Petru Cercel, recognized for his profound learning and ability to speak twelve languages, which allowed him to maintain friendly correspondence with the poets and scholars of Italy. Petru Cercel was drowned in Constantinople in 1590 due to the intrigues of Mihnea, who succeeded him on the throne of Walachia. 

The legacy of Petru Cercel is preserved in the British Museum, which holds the oldest manuscripts of the Rumanian Gospels once owned by him and bearing his autograph signature. This text was later published by Dr. M. Caster, funded by the Rumanian government.

## Michael the Brave
Michael, surnamed the Brave, was one of the most prominent members of the Bassarab family. He is recorded as having held power from 1592 to 1601.

## Curtea de Argesh
Curtea de Argesh is the site of a famous cathedral. This cathedral was founded by Neagoe, another prominent member of the Bassarab family.

## Later Dynasty Developments
Following the era of Michael the Brave, Mateiu Bassarab (1633-1654) contributed to the cultural and legal development of the region. He established the first printing-press in Rumania. Under his influence, the first code of laws was compiled and published in Bucharest in 1654. The Bassarab dynasty eventually became extinct with Constantine Sherban in 1658.
