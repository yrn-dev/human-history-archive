# Queen Elizabeth

## Reign and Administration
Queen Elizabeth reigned for forty years, beginning in 1558. During this period, her biography and the history of England were closely linked with that of Cecil. Cecil served as a principal adviser to the queen; he was created M.A. of Cambridge University during a visit by Elizabeth in 1564 and M.A. of Oxford during a similar visit in 1566. Another figure in the royal administration was Sir William Killigrew, who served as an officer in Queen Elizabeth's household and was a member of parliament.

## Diplomatic Relations and Foreign Affairs
Elizabeth's reign involved significant diplomatic engagement with European powers. Castelnau was employed on various missions, including an initial mission to negotiate peace with the queen. Later, Henry III appointed Castelnau as ambassador to Queen Elizabeth, a post he held for ten years. During his tenure at the English court, Castelnau attempted to use his influence to arrange a marriage between the queen and the duke of Alencon to maintain and strengthen the alliance between their two countries. However, Elizabeth frequently made promises only to break them, eventually leading Castelnau to stop communicating such promises to his government.

In the Netherlands, the town of Brielle (also known as "The Brill") was one of four Dutch towns handed over to Queen Elizabeth in 1584. This transfer served as security for expenses the English had incurred while aiding the Dutch.

## Domestic Influence and Society
The queen's influence extended to the religious and economic landscape of England. In the town of Honiton, a lace industry was established by refugees from Flanders under Queen Elizabeth. 

The religious climate of her reign saw the rise of figures such as Richard Bancroft. Bancroft, who later became the bishop of London and virtually the primate in charge of ecclesiastical affairs, preached a sermon at Paul's Cross in 1589 that attacked Puritans and defended the divine right of bishops. This sermon was delivered in such strong language that one of the queen's councillors considered it a threat against the supremacy of the crown. Bancroft was present at the death of Queen Elizabeth.

## Associated Figures
The queen's circle included various professionals and courtiers. Her physician, Marwood, who lived to the age of 105, is commemorated by a black marble tomb in the restored church of St Michael in Honiton.
