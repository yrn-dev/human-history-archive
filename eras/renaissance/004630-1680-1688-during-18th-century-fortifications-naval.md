# Events and Developments: 1680–1688

The period between 1680 and 1688 was marked by significant developments in military engineering, the arts, regional conflicts, and the establishment of financial institutions across Europe.

## Military Fortifications and Naval Development
In France, the town of Brest saw a major advancement in its defensive capabilities. Following earlier improvements by Richelieu, who constructed a harbor with wooden wharves in 1631, and Colbert, who replaced those wharves with masonry, the engineer Vauban constructed fortifications in Brest between 1680 and 1688. These efforts contributed to the town's growing naval importance, a trend that continued to develop throughout the 18th century.

## Regional Conflicts and Political Shifts
Various regions experienced instability and conflict during this timeframe:

*   **Ayrshire:** Between 1660 and 1688, the sympathies of Ayrshire were almost entirely with the Covenanters. This period was characterized by the ravaging of the Highland host and the dragooning of the county. A significant reverse for the Covenanters occurred at Airds Moss, where their leader, Richard Cameron, was killed on July 20, 1680.
*   **Banjaluka:** In the Balkans, the city of Banjaluka—possibly the Roman fort known as *Castra*—was the site of numerous encounters between Turks and Austrians. One of these notable encounters took place in 1688.
*   **English Peerage:** In 1680, William Howard, who had been granted the barony of Stafford in 1640 and created Viscount Stafford, was beheaded. Subsequently, in 1688, his son was created earl of Stafford.

## Arts and Music
The late 17th century was a period of transition for musical instruments. In England, the French hoboy—characterized by two keys and a construction of four parts—was known prior to 1688. Similarly, the *cor anglais* (also known as the *haute-contre de hautbois*) was being developed and improved with key-work during the 17th century. In France, where the *hautbois* was an important element of court music, the *haute-contre de hautbois* was likely provided with keys. By the end of the 17th century, the musicians of the Grande Ecurie du Roi included two players of this instrument.

## Literature and Finance
The year 1680 marked the birth of the German poet Barthold Heinrich Brockes, born in Hamburg on September 22. Brockes would later become a pioneer in German literature by introducing a clear and simple diction and directing attention toward the poetry of nature originating in England.

In the financial sector, the year 1688 is associated with the founding of Smiths' of Nottingham. This provincial bank claims to have been established before the Bank of England.
