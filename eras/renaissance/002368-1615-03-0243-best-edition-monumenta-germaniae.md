# Monumenta Germaniae Historica

The *Monumenta Germaniae Historica* is a comprehensive collection of historical texts and editions, including a section titled *Scriptores*. It serves as a primary resource for the study of various historical figures, periods, and regions, particularly concerning the history of Germany and France.

## Editorial History and Leadership
The *Monumenta Germaniae Historica* has been managed by various committees and historians. Ernst Ludwig Dummler (1830–1902), a prominent German historian and son of the Berlin bookseller Ferdinand Dummler, played a significant role in its direction. In 1875, Dummler became a member of the revised committee overseeing the project, specifically undertaking the direction of the *Antiquitates* section. By 1888, he attained the position of president of the central board in Berlin, a role that served as official recognition of his leading status among German historians.

Dummler's contributions to the *Monumenta Germaniae Historica* include editing the first and second volumes of the *Poetae latini aevi Carolini* between 1881 and 1884.

## Historical Works and Editions
The archive contains a vast array of critical editions of texts. Notable inclusions in the *Scriptores* section include:

*   **Regional and Ecclesiastical Histories:** The *Gesta episcoporum Cambracensium*, a chronicle of the bishops of Cambrai written in the 11th century, is featured in *Scriptores*, Bd. vii. (Hanover and Berlin, 1826–1892). This work, which is considered of considerable importance for the history of northern France during the 11th century, includes an introduction by L. C. Bethmann. While once attributed to Balderic, archbishop of Noyon, it is now believed to have been written by an anonymous canon of Cambrai.
*   **Carolingian and Imperial Sources:** The *Monumenta* includes the *Annales Fuldenses* and Nithard's *Historiarum Libri* (Bände i. and ii., 1826 fol.), as well as the *Annales Bertiniani* and *Chronica S. Benedicti Casinensis* (Bände i. and iii., 1826 fol.).
*   **Biographical and Reign Records:** The life and reign of Lothair are documented through several authorities found in *Scriptores* Bände vi., xii., and xx. (1826–1892), including the "Vita Norberti archiepiscopi Magdeburgensis," Otto von Freising, "Chronicon Annalista Saxo," and "Narratio de electione Lotharii."
*   **Papal and Political Conflict:** The *Monumenta* preserves the works of historians such as Lambert von Hersfeld, Bernold, Berthold, Bruno, Leo of Ostia, Sigebert of Gembloux, Marianus Scotus, Peter of Marte Cassino, Hugo of Flavigny, Donizo, and Arnulph and Landulf of Milan in *Scriptores* vols. v., vi., vii., viii., and xii. Additionally, the *Libelli de lite imperatorum et pontificum saeculis XI. et XII. conscripti* (tomes i., ii., and iii., published 1891–1897) collects pamphlet literature regarding the struggles under Gregory VII.

## Literary and Individual Contributions
The *Monumenta Germaniae Historica* also publishes works of a literary or personal nature:

*   **Boniface:** The *Monumenta* contains an 1892 edition of the letters (*Epistolae*) of Boniface, edited by Dummler. Dummler also edited Boniface's poems (*Aenigmata*) in the *Poetae latini aevi Carolini*, i. (1881).
*   **Ennodius:** An edition of Ennodius's work was published in vol. vii. of the *Monumenta Germaniae historica* in 1885, edited by F. Vogel and featuring exhaustive prolegomena.
*   **Other Texts:** The collection includes the *Johannis* as edited by J. Partsch in 1879, which contains valuable prolegomena. Additionally, Pertz's *Monumenta Germaniae Historica, Scriptores*, vol. xxvii. (pp. 82, 83) is cited as a source for historical research.
