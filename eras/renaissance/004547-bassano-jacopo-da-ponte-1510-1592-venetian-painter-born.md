# BASSANO, JACOPO DA PONTE (1510-1592)

Jacopo da Ponte, known as Bassano, was a Venetian painter who lived from 1510 to 1592. He was born in Bassano, a city in the province of Vicenza, Italy, located at the foot of the Venetian Alps upon the Brenta river.

## Education and Career
Jacopo received his initial education from his father, who was also an artist. Following this period of study, he completed his education in Venice. Upon the death of his father, Jacopo returned to his birthplace of Bassano, where he settled. 

Throughout his life, Bassano's artistic style underwent considerable variation. In his early years, he functioned as a copier of his father's work, though productions from this period are not considered to be of great value. He later experienced a strong attraction to the beautiful colouring and lightness associated with Titian. Eventually, he adopted the distinct style that is recognized as his own.

## Artistic Style and Subjects
Bassano preferred humble subjects over great pictures. His work generally focused on landscapes, cattle, villagers, and peasants, though he also produced historical designs and portraits. 

His work is characterized by several technical strengths:
* **Design and Nature:** His figures are well designed, and his landscapes and animals possess an agreeable air of simple nature.
* **Technical Execution:** His perspective and chiaroscuro are described as unexceptionable. He observed local colours well and utilized fresh, brilliant flesh-tints.
* **Composition:** While his compositions lack grandeur or much eloquence, they are noted for having abundance of truth and force.

Among his works, his altar-piece of the Nativity at Bassano is highly estimated by the best judges; according to Lanzi, it is the finest work of its class.

## Legacy and Authenticity
It is said that Jacopo Bassano finished a great number of pictures. However, his genuine works are relatively rare and valuable. This scarcity is due to the fact that many paintings attributed as originals are actually copies produced by others or by the sons of Bassano. 

The da Ponte family, who were surnamed Bassano after their place of birth, were active in the 16th and early 17th centuries. Jacopo is regarded as the most eminent member of this family. Today, pictures by the da Ponte family are contained within the cathedral, the museum, and some other churches in the city of Bassano.
