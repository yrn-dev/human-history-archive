# Maximilian of Bavaria and the Reconquest of Bohemia

## Military Alliance with Ferdinand
In the early 17th century, the powerful duke Maximilian of Bavaria entered into a military alliance with Ferdinand, who had succeeded Matthias as emperor. Ferdinand was determined to reconquer Bohemia following a period of political instability. This instability had peaked in 1619 when the Bohemian crown became vacant due to the death of Matthias. On July 31 of that year, the Bohemian estates formally deposed Ferdinand, and on August 26, they elected Frederick, the elector palatine, as their king. King Frederick and his queen, Elizabeth of England, arrived in Bohemia in October and were subsequently crowned at St Vitus's cathedral in Prague.

While warfare with Austria continued throughout 1619—during which Thurn occupied Moravia and advanced on Vienna before being forced to retreat—the situation shifted in the following year. Duke Maximilian of Bavaria joined his forces with those of Ferdinand to reclaim the territory. Ferdinand's cause was further supported by aid from Poland, Spain, several Italian states, and the Lutheran elector of Saxony.

## The Campaign of 1620
In September 1620, a large imperialist army entered Bohemia. This force was under the command of Tilly, Bouquoi, and the duke of Bavaria. Following a series of skirmishes in which the Bohemians were defeated, the imperial forces reached the outskirts of Prague on the evening of November 7.

On the morning of November 8, the imperialists attacked the Bohemian army, which held a slightly fortified position on a plateau known as "Bila Hora" (White Hill). The struggle lasted only a few hours, resulting in a Bohemian defeat. By the evening of the battle, the imperialists had occupied the port of Prague on the left bank of the Vltava (Moldau). Following this loss, King Frederick lost all courage and hurriedly fled Prague the next morning.

## Other Bavarian Figures and Orders
The archive contains records of other individuals and institutions associated with Bavaria and the name Maximilian:

*   **Maximilian Emanuel:** The elector of Bavaria who was nominated by the Spanish king to serve as governor-general of the Belgic provinces starting in 1692. He sought to restore prosperity to the region through new customs laws and the construction of canals.
*   **Maximilian I, King of Bavaria:** Involved in a 1816 treaty with Austria regarding the succession to the Baden Palatinate.
*   **Maximilian Joseph:** The first king of Bavaria, who in 1808 gave the final form to the constitution of the Order of St Hubert. He is also mentioned as Maximilian Joseph IV in relation to the confirmation of the Order of St George in 1778.
*   **The Order of St Hubert:** A knightly order founded in 1444 by duke Gerhard V. of Jülich-Berg. It was revived in 1708 by the elector palatine John William of Neuberg and later refined by Maximilian Joseph.
*   **The Order of St George:** Historically instituted in 1729 by the elector Charles Albert (later emperor Charles VII) and confirmed by the elector Charles Theodore and Maximilian Joseph IV.
