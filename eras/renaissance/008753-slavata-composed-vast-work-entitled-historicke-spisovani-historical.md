# Historicke Spisovani

## Overview
*Historicke Spisovani* (translated as historical works) is a vast historical composition authored by William Count Slavata (1572–1652). The work consists of fourteen large manuscript volumes. It was composed during the leisure of the final years of Slavata's long life.

## Content and Structure
The *Historicke Spisovani* is a comprehensive collection that incorporates earlier writings by the author. Specifically, two of the fourteen volumes contain Slavata's *Pamety* (memoirs). These memoirs focus exclusively on the events of 1618 and 1619, a period in which Slavata played a significant role.

## Author Background
William Count Slavata was a contemporary and political opponent of the historian Paul Skala ze Zhore. A faithful servant of the house of Habsburg, Slavata served as a government official. In 1618, at the start of the Bohemian uprising, he was among the officials who were thrown from the windows of the Hradcany palace. He published his *Pamety* in 1637.

## Historical Context and Publication
The creation of *Historicke Spisovani* occurred during a period of decline for the Bohemian national tongue. Following the downfall of Bohemian independence in 1620, the language faced a period of decay, becoming almost extinct as a written language by the 18th century. The new Austrian government actively sought to discourage the use of the language. 

Despite this environment, Slavata and Paul Skala ze Zhore stand out as historians whose works date later than 1620. While Skala was an adherent of the nationalist party and an official for Frederick of the Palatinate, Slavata represented the imperialist party.

The two volumes of the *Historicke Spisovani* containing the *Pamety* have been edited and published by Dr. Jos. Jirecek.
