# Chamber Music of Johann Sebastian Bach

The chamber music of Johann Sebastian Bach consists of a diverse array of compositions for various instrumental combinations, ranging from solo works to ensembles with figured bass.

## Works for Clavier and Other Instruments
Bach composed several sonatas and suites featuring the clavier paired with other melodic instruments. These include:
*   **Clavier and Flute:** 3 sonatas.
*   **Clavier and Violin:** A suite and 6 sonatas.
*   **Clavier and Viola da Gamba:** 3 sonatas.

Additionally, his output in this category includes two trios with figured bass, two flute-sonatas, and a violin suite with figured bass.

## Solo Instrumental Works
Bach wrote extensively for solo instruments without accompaniment. His archive includes:
*   **Violin:** 6 sonatas, which are categorized as 3 sonatas and 3 partitas.
*   **Violoncello:** 6 suites.

## Attribution and Authenticity
The identification of Bach's works is occasionally complicated by the nature of his autographs, as he typically placed the composer's name only on the outside sheet. Because Bach frequently made copies of music that interested him, the loss of an outside sheet often means the music itself must be used to determine authorship.

This issue of attribution extends to the sonatas for clavier and flute. While three are listed, the second of these sonatas is described as being extremely suggestive of Bach's sons. Although Philipp Emanuel Bach ascribes the work to his father, there is a possibility that he docketed it incorrectly while arranging copies. Furthermore, a "twin brother" of this piece exists in the *B.-G.* ix. Anhang ii., for which Philipp Emanuel has not vouched.

## Related Instrumental and Clavier Works
Beyond his chamber music, Bach's instrumental output includes orchestral works such as the 6 Brandenburg concertos, 2 violin concertos, and 4 orchestral suites. He also produced a concerto for two violins and a large concerted violin-movement (a *torso*) that served as a prelude to a lost church cantata.

His clavier and organ collections are extensive. Notable clavier works include *Das wohltemperirte Klavier* (two books of 24 preludes and fugues each), the *Klavier-Uebung* (containing 15 two-part inventions, 15 three-part symphonies, 6 partitas, and the "Goldberg" variations), and the 6 "French" and 6 "English" suites. His organ music includes 6 sonatas, 4 groups of 6 preludes and fugues, *Das Orgelbuechlein*, 18 larger chorale-preludes, and the 6 "Schuebler" chorales.

Two unclassified instrumental works of significant value are *Das musikalische Opfer*—which includes two great fugues, puzzle-canons, and a trio for flute, violin, and figured bass—and *Die Kunst der Fuge*, a series of fugues written as abstract studies.
