# Gavin Douglas

Gavin Douglas is a figure of significance in Scottish literature. His collected works were published in a four-volume edition in 1874, edited by John Small, which remains the only collected edition of his writings. 

## Literary Influence and Analysis
The works of Gavin Douglas have been the subject of various academic analyses regarding his style and influences. P. Gregory Smith's *Specimens of Middle Scots* (1902) serves as a resource.
