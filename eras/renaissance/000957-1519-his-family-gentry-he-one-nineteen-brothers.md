# Pedro Menendez de Aviles

## Early Life and Career
Pedro Menendez de Aviles was a Spanish seaman born on February 15, 1519, in Aviles, Asturias. He was born into a family of gentry and was one of nineteen siblings, including both brothers and sisters. At the age of fourteen, Aviles ran away to sea, spending the next sixteen years engaged in a life of adventure as a corsair.

## Imperial Service and Naval Commands
In 1549, during a period of peace between Spain and France, Emperor Charles V commissioned Aviles to clear the Canaries and the north coast of Spain of French pirates. By 1554, he was appointed captain-general of the "flota," the convoy responsible for trade between America and Spain. This appointment was made by the emperor despite the opposition and will of the Casa de Contratacion, the governing board of American trade.

Prior to sailing to America in 1554, Aviles accompanied the prince of Spain (the future Philip II) to England for the prince's marriage to Queen Mary. As the commander of the flota, Aviles achieved success in returning treasure, which earned him the hearty approval of the emperor. However, his professional conduct created friction with other parties; his high-handed manner offended the Casa de Contratacion, and his refusal to accept bribes for permitting breaches of regulations made him unpopular with merchants.

## European Conflicts and Later Commands
Due to the hostility of the Casa de Contratacion, Aviles applied for service elsewhere after being reappointed commander in 1557. During the war between France and the allied forces of England and Spain, Aviles spent the period until the end of 1559 transporting recruits and money from Spain to Flanders. Following the restoration of peace, he commanded the fleet that transported Philip II from the Low Countries back to Spain.

Aviles returned to the command of the flota in 1560, completing successful voyages to America and back in 1560 and 1561. Despite these successes, his relationship with the Casa de Contratacion remained strained. Upon returning from a voyage in 1563, he was arrested by order of the Casa and imprisoned for twenty months. While the specific charges against him are unknown, Aviles wrote a letter to the king avowing his innocence.

## Legacy
Pedro Menendez de Aviles is recognized as the founder of St Augustine, Florida. He died in 1574.
