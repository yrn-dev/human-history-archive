# Events of January 1642

The month of January 1642 was marked by significant political instability and failed strategic maneuvers during the escalating tensions between King Charles and the English Parliament.

## The Attempted Arrest of the Five Members
In early 1642, an advisor to King Charles—described as the king's "evil genius"—urged the monarch to attempt the arrest of five members of Parliament on the 4th of January 1642. This advisor had previously suggested the impeachment of these members and had gained influence over Charles, who followed his advice over that of men such as Falkland and Hyde.

Despite urging the attempt, the advisor failed to perform his role in the House of Lords regarding the security of the arrest of Lord Mandeville. To Mandeville, the advisor declared that the king was being "very mischievously advised." According to Clarendon, this imprudence led to the betrayal of the king's plan. Following the failure of the 4th of January, the advisor suggested that the king attempt to seize the members by force within the city the following day.

## Military Actions and Other Events
Concurrent with the political turmoil in London, other royalist efforts were underway. William Legge, who had provided useful assistance to King Charles in the months preceding the outbreak of the Civil War, attempted to seize Hull in January 1642, but this effort failed.

## Aftermath for the King's Advisor
The advisor who had urged the January 4th arrests later faced severe legal consequences. In the same month, he was ordered to appear before the House of Lords to answer a charge of high treason related to a supposed armed attempt at Kingston. He avoided this by fleeing to Holland to join the queen, and he was subsequently impeached on the 26th of February.

Though he later returned to England in disguise as a Frenchman to visit Charles at York, he was captured and taken to Hull. He eventually managed to escape after cajoling Sir John Hotham. He later attempted a second visit to Hull to persuade Hotham to surrender the location to Charles, but this project also failed.
