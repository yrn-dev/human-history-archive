# Histoire cirkevni

*Histoire cirkevni* (history of the church) is a vast historical work authored by Paul Skala ze Zhore. The text is composed of ten enormous manuscript volumes. Despite the title's focus on the church, the content of the work addresses ecclesiastical matters as well as political ones.

## Author and Context
Paul Skala ze Zhore (1582–c. 1640) was a historian and an official who served Frederick of the Palatinate, known as the "winter king." Skala was an adherent of the nationalist party. Following his sovereign into exile, he spent the final years of his life in Freiberg, Saxony. It was during this period, after his political activity had ended, that he produced his historical writings. 

Skala's literary output began with a short book consisting of a series of chronological tables; the *Histoire cirkevni* was undertaken somewhat later.

## Content and Publication
The *Histoire cirkevni* is noted for its scale and its dual focus on politics and the church. A specific portion of the work is considered the most valuable: the section dealing with events from 1602 to 1623. In these accounts, Skala writes as a contemporary and frequently as an eye-witness to the events described. This particular section has been edited and published by Prof. Tieftrunk.

## Historical Period
The work emerged during a period of decline for the Bohemian national tongue. Following the downfall of Bohemian independence in 1620, the language began to decline, eventually becoming almost extinct as a written language by the 18th century. Skala is identified as one of only two historians whose works date from after 1620, the other being his political opponent, William Count Slavata.
