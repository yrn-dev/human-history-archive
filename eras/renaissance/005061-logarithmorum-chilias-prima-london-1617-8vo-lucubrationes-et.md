# Logarithmorum Chilias prima and Lucubrationes et Annotationes

The *Logarithmorum Chilias prima* and *Lucubrationes et Annotationes in opera posthuma J. Neperi* are mathematical works published in the early 17th century. These texts are part of a broader body of scientific literature focused on navigation, geometry, and the development of logarithms.

## Logarithmorum Chilias prima
Published in London in 1617 as an octavo (8vo) volume, *Logarithmorum Chilias prima* is one of several technical works produced during this period. It exists alongside other related mathematical and navigational publications, such as the *Arithmetica Logarithmica* (published in London in 1624 as a folio) and the *Trigonometria Britannica* (published in Goudae in 1663 as a folio).

## Lucubrationes et Annotationes in opera posthuma J. Neperi
The *Lucubrationes et Annotationes in opera posthuma J. Neperi* was published in Edinburgh in 1619 as a quarto (4to) volume. This work serves as a commentary or set of annotations on the posthumous works of J. Neper.

## Context of Logarithmic Development
The invention of logarithms was a significant advancement that was eagerly welcomed by contemporary scientists. Johannes Kepler, for example, utilized logarithms for the first time in his 1620 ephemerides, which were dedicated to Baron Napier. 

Kepler also authored a treatise entitled *Chilias Logarithmorum*, which was printed in 1624. However, this specific work had been circulated in manuscript form three years prior to its official printing. The circulation of this treatise contributed significantly to the general adoption of the logarithmic method within Germany.
