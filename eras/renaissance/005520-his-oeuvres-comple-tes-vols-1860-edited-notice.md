# Oeuvres complètes (1860)

The *Oeuvres complètes* published in 1860 in two volumes was edited by Saint-René Taillandier, who provided a notice of the author. This work was followed by another edition released between 1880 and 1884, which consisted of four volumes. For those seeking a comprehensive list of articles regarding the author's work, an exhaustive monograph titled *Brizeux; sa vie et ses oeuvres* was published in 1898 by the abbé C. Lecigne.

## Related Literary Editions of 1860

The year 1860 saw the publication of several other significant literary editions and notices within the archive's records. One such publication was a reprint by Garnier *freres* of the works of Boileau. This edition was based on the critical text established by Berriat Saint-Prix in *Oeuvres de Boileau* (4 vols., 1830-1837), which utilized approximately 350 editions. The 1860 version included notes by Paul Cheron, the *Boloeana* of 1740, and an essay written by Sainte-Beuve.

Additionally, in 1860, Sainte-Beuve authored a notice regarding the Oxford edition of the *Ephemerides* as part of the *Causeries* on July 30th, focusing on the subject of Casaubon.

### Philosophical and Academic Works
The collected works of Descartes have a extensive publication history. Latin editions were published in Amsterdam (8 vols., 1670-1683), Frankfort (7 vols., 1697), and by Elzevir (9 vols., 1713). French editions include a 13-volume set from Paris (1724-1729), an 11-volume republication by Victor Cousin (1824-1826), and a later version under the authority of the minister of public instruction edited by P. Tannery and C. Adam (1897 foll.). Other specific Descartes publications include the *Opera philosophica* (with editions in Amsterdam between 1650 and 1678 and a two-volume Leipzig edition in 1843), and the *Oeuvres philosophiques* by L. Aimé-Martin (1838) and A. Garnier (3 vols., 1834-1835).

### Poetic and Vernacular Works
Jean Daurat's *Oeuvres poetiques* in the vernacular were edited in 1875 by Ch. Marty-Laveaux in the *Pleiade francaise*, featuring a bibliography and biographical notice. Clément Marot's *Oeuvres* saw early important editions in Lyons in 1538 and 1544, with the latter establishing the arrangement of poems used in later issues. Subsequent editions include one by François Mizière (1596), N. Lenglet du Fresnoy (1731), and P. Jannet (1868-1872; new ed. 1873-1876).

### Other Collected Volumes
*   **Barthelemy**: His *Oeuvres completes* (4 vols., 1821) includes a notice by Villenave.
*   **Eustache Deschamps**: His *OEuvres complètes* were edited for the *Société des anciens textes français* by Gaston Raynaud and Queux de Saint-Hilaire (10 vols., 1878-1901), supplemented by an introduction by G. Raynaud.
*   **Balzac**: His works were issued in various forms, including the *Comedie humaine* (1842-1846 in sixteen volumes) and an *Edition definitive* (1869 onwards). A 55-volume set of novels was also produced by Michel (Calmann) Levy. Later additions include the *Repertoire des oeuvres de Balzac* (1893) and *Oeuvres posthumes* (beginning in 1899).
