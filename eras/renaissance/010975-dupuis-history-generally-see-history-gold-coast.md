# Dupuis

The name Dupuis is associated with several distinct historical figures across different fields, including diplomacy, science, politics, and scholarship.

## Joseph Dupuis
Joseph Dupuis served as a British consul, previously stationed at Mogador. In January 1819, he arrived at Cape Coast after being sent by the British government to Kumasi to act as consul and cultivate relations with the powerful African kingdom of Ashanti. 

Dupuis departed for Kumasi on February 9, 1820, arriving on February 28. Following several meetings with the king, he drew up a treaty. This agreement acknowledged the sovereignty of Ashanti over the territory of the Fanti and left the natives of Cape Coast to the mercy of their enemies. This treaty was subsequently disowned by the governor of Cape Coast, Mr. J. Hope Smith, who viewed it as a betrayal of the natives under British protection. The government in London supported Hope Smith, leading to the crown assuming direct control of the British settlements in 1821. Dupuis is also the author of the 1824 work, *Journal of a Residence in Ashantee*.

## Charles Francois Dupuis (1742–1809)
Charles Francois Dupuis was a French politician and scientific writer born on October 26, 1742, at Trye-Chateau. Educated in mathematics and land-surveying by his father, he later received a bursary to the college of Harcourt from the duc de la Rochefoucauld. He served as a professor of rhetoric at the college of Lisieux and became an advocate in 1770 after abandoning a clerical career.

Dupuis was deeply interested in mathematics and astronomy, maintaining a friendship with Lalande. In 1778, he constructed a telegraph based on a principle suggested by Guillaume Amontons to correspond with Jean Fortin in Bagneux; however, he had to destroy the machine during the Revolution to avoid suspicion. He also developed a theory regarding the origin of Greek months, proposing that the zodiac served as an astronomical and rural calendar for its inventors and suggesting that this system originated in Upper Egypt.

His academic and political career included:
* Appointment as the chair of humanity in the College of France.
* Membership in the Academy of Inscriptions (1788).
* Service as one of four commissioners of public instruction for the department of Paris.
* Election as a member of the National Convention by the department of Seine-et-Oise, where he was noted for his moderation.
* Election as secretary to the Assembly in the third year of the republic and a member of the Council of Five Hundred in the fourth.
* Presidency of the Legislative Body following Bonaparte's *coup d'etat* of the 18th Brumaire.

## Charles Alexandre Dupuy (1851– )
Charles Alexandre Dupuy was a French statesman born on November 5, 1851, at Le Puy. He began his career as a professor of philosophy in the provinces and later served as a school inspector. In 1885, he was elected to the chamber as an Opportunist Republican.

Dupuy held several high-ranking government positions:
* Minister for the department of public instruction in M. Ribot's cabinet (1892).
* Minister of the Interior and premier in a ministry he formed in April 1893.
* President of the chamber, elected on December 5, 1893. During his first week in this role, he gained credit for his composure after an anarchist named Vaillant threw a bomb at him, to which Dupuy responded, "Messieurs, la seance continue."
* Premier and minister of the interior again in May 1894.

During his tenure, Captain Dreyfus was arrested and condemned on December 23, 1894. In November 1898, Dupuy formed a cabinet of Republican concentration. Despite expectations that his cabinet would reconcile public opinion to a decision in favor of Dreyfus by the court of cassation, Dupuy proposed a law transferring the decision to a full court of all the divisions of the court of cassation.

## Pierre Dupuy (1582–1651)
Pierre Dupuy, also known as Puteanus, was a French scholar born on November 27, 1582, at Agen. In 1615, he was commissioned by Mathieu Mole to create an inventory of the documents in the *Tresor des chartes*, a task that took eleven years. He and his brother Jacques also published the *History* of Aug. de Thou and served as keepers of the king's library, for which they drew up a catalogue.

Dupuy authored several works based on unpublished documents, focusing on the relations between church and state in the middle ages from a Gallican standpoint (supporting the rights of the crown in political and temporal matters). His works include:
* *Traite des droits et des libertes de l'eglise gallicane, avec les preuves* (1639).
* *Histoire de l'ordre militaire des Templiers* (1654).
* *Histoire generale du schisme qui a ete dans l'eglise depuis 1378 jusqu'a 1428* (1654).
* *Histoire du differend entre le pape Boniface VIII et le roi Philippe le Bel* (1655).
* *Traite des regences et des majorites des rois de France* (1655).
* *Recueil des droits du roi* (1658).
