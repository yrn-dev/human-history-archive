# Historiae de rebus a Carolo VII. et Ludovico XI. Francorum regibus eorumque in tempore in Gallia gestis

The *Historiae de rebus a Carolo VII. et Ludovico XI. Francorum regibus eorumque in tempore in Gallia gestis* is a historical work of considerable value. It focuses on the reigns and the events that took place in Gaul during the time of the French kings Charles VII and Louis XI.

## Authorship and Reception
For a period, the work was believed to have been written by a priest from Liege named Amelgard. However, it is now considered practically certain that the author was Basin. The text is noted for being marred to some extent by the author's personal dislike for Louis XI.

## Related Works by Basin
In addition to the *Historiae*, Basin authored several other texts:
*   **Libellus de optimo ordine forenses lites audiendi et deferendi**: A work containing suggestions for the reform of the administration of justice.
*   **Apologia**: A piece written to respond to charges brought against him by Louis XI.
*   **Breviloquium**: An allegorical account detailing his own misfortunes.
*   **Peregrinatio**: A travel-related writing.
*   **Opinio et consilium super processu et condemnatione Johanne, dicte Puelle**: A defense of Joan of Arc.
*   **Advis de Monseigneur de Lysieux au roi**: A work written in French, published in Paris in 1677.

## Editions and Scholarship
The *Historiae* has been the subject of later scholarly editions and analysis. J. E. J. Quicherat published an edition of the work in Paris between 1855 and 1859. Additionally, G. du F. de Beaucourt published *Charles VII et Louis XI d'apres Thomas Basin* in Paris in 1858.
