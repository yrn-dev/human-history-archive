# Baldovinetti

## Artistic Style and Characteristics
The works of Baldovinetti are characterized by a determined precision and patience in execution. His output often exhibits a quality of grave majesty, a trait he shares with other artists such as Piero della Francesca and those who followed Domenico Veneziano. In several of his works, this majesty is complemented by a peculiar and delicate charm.

Baldovinetti's technical approach evolved over time. In his earlier preserved works, likely dating between 1460 and 1465, his execution is marked by precise and laborious finish. However, in his later works, while the laborious finish remains, there is a noticeable emergence of commonness and harshness of type. During this later period, a lack of care for beauty in composition becomes evident, suggesting that Baldovinetti's identity as a technical and scientific searcher began to predominate over his role as an artist.

## Attributed Works
Several pieces are attributed to Baldovinetti based on sufficient internal evidence, including works that were previously credited to other artists.

### Panels and Religious Works
A small panel located in the Florence Academy depicts three subjects: the Transfiguration, the Marriage of Cana, and the Baptism. Although this piece was long attributed to Fra Angelico, it is now considered to be an early work of Baldovinetti. Additionally, an Annunciation currently in the Uffizi (formerly housed in the church of S. Giorgio) is identified as being unmistakably by Baldovinetti's hand, despite Vasari having attributed it to Peselino.

### Madonnas
Baldovinetti is credited with several Madonnas of a characteristic and fine quality:
* One located in the collection of Madame Andre in Paris, which was acquired directly from the painter's descendants.
* One currently in the Louvre, which was previously part of the Duchatel collection.
* One held by Mr. Berenson in Florence.

The examples found in the Andre collection and the Louvre are further distinguished by the inclusion of beautiful landscape backgrounds.
