## Trade and International Relations in Japan
The year 1600 marks the beginning of Dutch trade in Japan. During this era, the Dutch and English engaged in intrigues against the Portuguese and Spaniards, while the Spanish conducted intrigues against the Dutch. The Dutch eventually established headquarters at Hirado and later at Deshima, and they provided aid in the reduction of the Christian revolt in Shimabara. Their presence in Japan included the introduction of Western learning, the provision of a steamship, and the employment of teachers of military science.
