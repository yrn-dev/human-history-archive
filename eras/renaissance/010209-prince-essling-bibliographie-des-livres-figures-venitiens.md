# Bibliographie des livres a figures venitiens

The *Bibliographie des livres a figures venitiens 1469-1525* is a specialized bibliographical work authored by the duc de Rivoli, also known as the Prince d'Essling. First published in 1892, the work was later released in a new edition in 1906.

## Scope and Subject Matter
The text focuses on "livres a figures" (illustrated books) produced in Venice between the years 1469 and 1525. It is categorized as one of the primary resources for the study of illustrated books from Italy during this period.

## Context within Bibliographical Literature
The *Bibliographie des livres a figures venitiens* represents a shift in the nature of bibliographical literature. While older collectors relied on general manuals—such as W.T. Lowndes' *Bibliographer's Manual of English Literature* (1834) or Brunet's *Manuel du libraire* (1810)—the era of the "omnivorous collector" has passed. In its place, the archive notes a rise in more detailed handbooks and bibliographies dedicated to special books.
