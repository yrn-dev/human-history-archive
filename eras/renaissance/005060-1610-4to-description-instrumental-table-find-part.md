# A Description of an Instrumental Table to find the part proportional

## Overview and Publication
*A Description of an Instrumental Table to find the part proportional* is a work devised by Mr. Edward Wright. The text was published in London in two separate editions, appearing in 1616 and 1618. These editions were printed in 12mo format.

## Context within the Author's Works
The publication of the *Instrumental Table* is part of a broader body of mathematical and navigational works. Other contributions by the same author include:

*   **Navigational and Astronomical Tables:** In 1602, a 4to work titled *A Table to find the Height of the Pole, the Magnetical Declination being given* was published in London. Additionally, "Tables for the Improvement of Navigation" were included in the second edition of Edward Wright's treatise, *Certain Errors in Navigation detected and corrected*, published in London in 1610 (4to).
*   **Logarithmic and Mathematical Studies:** The author produced *Logarithmorum Chilias prima* (London, 1617, 8vo), *Lucubrationes et Annotationes in opera posthuma J. Neperi* (Edinburgh, 1619, 4to), and *Arithmetica Logarithmica* (London, 1624, folio).
*   **Geometry and Trigonometry:** Other works include *Euclidis Elementorum VI. libri priores* (London, 1620, folio) and *Trigonometria Britannica* (Goudae, 1663, folio).
*   **Geographical Treatises:** A 4to work titled *A Treatise on the North-West Passage to the South Sea* was published in London in 1622 and later reprinted in volume iii, page 852 of Purchas's *Pilgrims*.

The author also wrote *Mathematica ab Antiquis minus cognita* and two letters addressed to Archbishop Usher. Some of the author's works remained unpublished, specifically the *Remarks on the Treatise of Longomontanus respecting the Quadrature of the Circle* and the *Commentaries on the Geometry of Peter Ramus*.
