# Elementorum VI. libri priores

## Overview
*Euclidis Elementorum VI. libri priores* is a work published in London in 1620 in folio format. It is listed among the mathematical and scientific contributions of an author whose body of work focused heavily on navigation, logarithms, and trigonometry.

## Context within the Author's Works
The publication of *Euclidis Elementorum VI. libri priores* in 1620 occurred during a prolific period of writing for the author. Prior to this work, the author produced several texts related to navigation and mathematical tables, including:
* *A Table to find the Height of the Pole, the Magnetical Declination being given* (London, 1602, 4to).
* "Tables for the Improvement of Navigation," which appeared in the second edition of Edward Wright's *Certain Errors in Navigation detected and corrected* (London, 1610, 4to).
* *A Description of an Instrumental Table to find the part proportional, devised by Mr Edward Wright* (London, 1616 and 1618, 12mo).
* *Logarithmorum Chilias prima* (London, 1617, 8vo).
* *Lucubrationes et Annotationes in opera posthuma J. Neperi* (Edinburgh, 1619, 4to).

Following the 1620 publication of the *Elementorum* text, the author continued to publish scientific treatises, such as *A Treatise on the North-West Passage to the South Sea* (London, 1622, 4to), which was later reprinted in volume iii, page 852 of Purchas's *Pilgrims*. Other later works include *Arithmetica Logarithmica* (London, 1624, folio) and *Trigonometria Britannica* (Goudae, 1663, folio).
