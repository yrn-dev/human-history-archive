# Athenae Oxon (Bliss) and Biographia Britannica

The *Athenae Oxonienses* (specifically the edition by Bliss) and the *Biographia Britannica* are significant biographical and historical reference works cited across various archival records for their accounts of English figures, academics, and nobility.

## Biographia Britannica
The *Biographia Britannica* is a voluminous biographical collection. A major edition of the work was produced by Kippis, who, with the assistance of Dr. Towers, published five folio volumes between 1778 and 1793. Kippis was a prolific writer who also contributed to *The Library*, *The Monthly Review*, and *The Gentleman's Magazine*, and was involved in the establishment of *The New Annual Register*.

The *Biographia Britannica* serves as a source for a wide array of historical figures:
* **Academic and Scientific Figures:** It contains entries on the English astronomer John Bainbridge (i. 419) and the complaints of Roger Bacon (vol. i. p. 418 in Kippis’s edition).
* **Clerical and Literary Figures:** Volume iii. (1750) provides an elaborate account of the life and writings of Thomas Fuller, drawing from Fuller's own works and an anonymous 1661 biography.
* **Political and Noble Figures:** The work includes biographies of figures such as Bristol (v. 199) and Adrian IV.
* **General Biography:** Other entries include those found in volume v. (pp. 210-238).

## Athenae Oxonienses (Bliss)
The *Athenae Oxonienses*, edited by Bliss, is frequently cited as a primary authority for the lives of individuals associated with Oxford. The archive's sources reference several specific volumes and pages of this work to document various historical persons:
* **Volume II:** Page 522 contains information regarding Sir G. Calvert.
* **Volume III:** This volume is cited for entries on Bristol (p. 338), John Bainbridge (p. 67), Edmund Bolton (p. 36), and other figures (pp. 1100-1105).
* **Volume IV:** Page 207 is listed as a source for the biography of the 2nd Duke of Buckingham.

## Application in Historical Research
These texts are often used in conjunction with other historical authorities to trace the lives of prominent figures. For example, the life of George Villiers, 2nd Duke of Buckingham, is traced through a combination of *Athenae Oxon* (Bliss), *Biographia Britannica*, and works by C.H. Firth and Lady Burghclere. Similarly, the career of the diplomat Bristol is documented using *Biographia Britannica* (Kippis), *Athenae Oxon* (Bliss), and the writings of S.R. Gardiner and Clarendon.

In the case of Edmund Bolton, an English historian and poet who was a retainer of the Duke of Buckingham, *Athenae Oxon* (Bliss) is cited regarding the substitution of his *Life of King Henry II* with a version by Dr. John Barcham due to Bolton's Catholic sympathies.
