## Political and Noble Appointments
Several individuals received titles or offices during this year. William Feilding, the 1st Earl of Denbigh—son of Elizabeth and Basil Feilding—was appointed as the master of the great wardrobe and the *custos rotulorum* of Warwickshire in 1622. Additionally, on September 14, 1622, he was created the earl of Denbigh.

In Scotland, the year marked the end of the life of Alexander Seton, 1st Earl of Dunfermline. Seton had served as the chancellor of Scotland and had been entrusted with the care of Prince Charles after the king departed for England. He also played a role in arranging the union between England and Scotland. Seton died on June 16, 1622, at Pinkie House, located near Musselburgh.

## Administrative and Civic Changes
In the town of Basingstoke, a charter issued by James I in 1622 established a new local government structure. This charter instituted a high steward and under steward, two serjeants-at-mace, four justices of the peace, fourteen capital burgesses, and two bailiffs. It also established a court of record and confirmed a fair to be held at the feast of St Michael the Archangel.
