# De Americaensche Zee Roovers

*De Americaensche Zee Roovers* is a work published in Amsterdam in 1678. The text was written by Exquemelin, who was a buccaneer.

## Translations and Editions
Following its initial publication, the work was translated into several European languages. During the translation process, different translators added material to the original text. Notable versions include:

*   **French Edition:** Translated by Frontignières and titled *Histoire des avanturiers qui se sont signalez dans les Indes*.
*   **English Edition:** Titled *The Bucaniers of America*.

## Historical Context and Related Works
The account provided by Exquemelin is categorized among several other works concerning the history of the West Indies and the activities of buccaneers. Other related texts include:

*   *History of the Settlements and Trade of the Europeans in the East and West Indies* (Book X), written by Raynal and translated into English in 1782.
*   *Voyages* by Dampier.
*   *Monarchs of the Main, &c.* (1855) by Geo. W. Thornbury.
*   *Voyage and Description of the Isthmus of America* (1699) by Lionel Wafer.
*   Works by Père Charlevoix, specifically *Histoire de l'isle Espagnole, &c.* and *Histoire et description générale de la Nouvelle France*.

## Reliability and Authenticity
The archive notes that statements found within *De Americaensche Zee Roovers* and the other works listed above should be received with caution. In contrast, the *History of the Buccaneers of America*, published in London in 1816 by Captain James Burney, is described as a really authentic narrative. Additionally, the *Calendar of State Papers*, Colonial Series (London, 1860 et seq.), provides significant evidence regarding the history of buccaneers in the West Indies.
