# Robert Greville, 2nd Lord Brooke

Robert Greville (c. 1608–1643) was an English nobleman and a member of the parliamentary party during the English Civil War. He succeeded to the barony of Brooke as the 2nd Lord Brooke after the death of his cousin, who had left no sons.

## Political Career and Imprisonment
Greville's involvement in the political conflicts of his era became prominent during the reign of Charles I. In 1639, he was imprisoned at York by the king. This imprisonment was the result of Greville's refusal to take the oath to fight for the king. Following this period, he became an active participant in the parliamentary party.

## Military Actions in the Civil War
During the English Civil War, Robert Greville took an active military role against the Royalists. In August 1642, he led forces in a skirmish at Kineton, where he defeated the Royalists. Due to his activities, he was subsequently granted a command in the midland counties. His military career ended in 1643; after seizing Lichfield, he was killed there on March 2nd of that year.

## Family and Legacy
Robert Greville was the cousin and heir of the 1st Lord Brooke. The 1st Lord Brooke was a writer known for his biography of Sir Philip Sidney, titled *The Life of the Renowned Sr. Philip Sidney. With the true Interest of England as it then stood in relation to all Forrain Princes: And particularly for suppressing the power of Spain Stated by Him: His principall Actions, Counsels, Designes, and Death. Together with a short account of the Maximes and Policies used by Queen Elizabeth in her Government*. The 1st Lord Brooke also wrote on theological, philosophical, and current political topics and was described by Milton as a friend of toleration. He had intended to write a history of England under the Tudors, though Robert Cecil denied him the state papers required for the work.

The lineage of the barony continued after Robert Greville. A descendant, Francis Greville, the 8th baron (1710–1773), was created earl of Warwick in 1746, a title that remained in his family.
