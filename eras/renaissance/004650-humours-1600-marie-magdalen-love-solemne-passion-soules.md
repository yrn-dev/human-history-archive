## Marie Magdalen's Love: a Solemne Passion of the Soules Love
Published in 1595, *Marie Magdalen's Love: a Solemne Passion of the Soules Love* is a work by Breton consisting of two distinct parts. The first part is a prose treatise, which is likely written by a hand other than Breton's. The second part is a poem written in six-lined stanzas, which is certainly the work of Breton. 

This work is distinct from other similarly titled pieces of the era. For instance, *Mary Magdalen's Lamentations* (1604) is a work that has been erroneously ascribed to Breton.
