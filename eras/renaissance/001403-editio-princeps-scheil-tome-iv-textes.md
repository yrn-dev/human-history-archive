# Editio Princeps by V. Scheil

The *editio princeps* of "The Code of Hammurabi" was produced by V. Scheil. This work was published in 1902 in Paris as part of tome iv. of the *Textes Elamites-Semitiques*, which is contained within the *Memoires de la delegation en Perse*.

## Context and Related Works
The publication of the Code of Hammurabi by V. Scheil is situated among various scholarly efforts to document and analyze ancient laws and contracts. Other significant works in the field of Babylonian and Assyrian legal documents include:
*   *Documents juridiques de l'Assyrie et de la Chaldee* by Oppert and Menant (1877).
*   *Aus dem babylonischen Rechtsleben* by J. Kohler and F. E. Peiser (1890 ff.).
*   *Babylonische Vertrage* (1890) and *Keilinschriftliche Actenstuecke* (1889), both by F. E. Peiser.
*   *Beitraege zur altbabylonischen Privatrecht* by Br. Meissner (1893).
*   *Texte juristischen und geschaeftlichen Inhalts* by F. E. Peiser, found in vol. iv. of Schrader's *Keilinschriftliche Bibliothek* (1896).
*   *Assyrian Deeds and Documents relating to the Transfer of Property* (3 vols., 1898) and *Babylonian and Assyrian Laws, Contracts and Letters* (1904), both by C. H. W. Johns.
*   *Early Babylonian History* by H. Radau (1900).

## Subsequent Editions of the Code
Following Scheil's *editio princeps*, several other editions and studies of the laws of Hammurabi, King of Babylon (dated to approximately 2250 B.C.), were published:
*   H. Winckler published "Die Gesetze Hammurabis Koenigs von Babylon um 2250 v. Chr." in *Der alte Orient* (iv. Jahrgang, Heft 4).
*   D. H. Mueller published *Die Gesetze Hammurabis* in Vienna (1903).
*   J. Kohler and F. E. Peiser produced *Hammurabis Gesetz* in Leipzig (1904).
*   R. F. Harper published *The Code of Hammurabi, King of Babylon about 2250 B.C.* in Chicago (1904).
*   S. A. Cook authored *The Laws of Moses and the Code of Hammurabi* in London (1903).
