## Demographic Trends in England and Wales
Records regarding illegitimate births in England and Wales from 1860 to 1907 show a general decline in both absolute numbers and rates. In 1860, there were 43,693 illegitimate births, with a proportion of 2.2 per 1,000 of the population. By 1907, this number had fallen to 36,189, with the proportion dropping to 1.0 per 1,000.

Further analysis of the illegitimate birth rate calculated on the unmarried and widowed female population (aged 15-45) shows a steady decrease. The rate per 1,000 was 14.4 during 1876-1880 and declined to 7.8 by 1907.

## Distribution of Religious Texts
A society dedicated to the distribution of the Scriptures maintains a global presence with secretaries in twenty-seven chief cities and depots in 200 other centers. The organization employs 670 native Christian Bible-women in the East and 930 Christian colporteurs abroad; the latter sold over 2,250,000 volumes between 1905 and 1906.

Financial records for the 1905-1906 period show an expenditure of L238,632 against an income of L231,964, with L98,204 of that income coming from sales. The society also provides L10,000 annually in grants to philanthropic and religious agencies within the United Kingdom. As of March 31, 1906, the society had issued 198,515,199 copies of the Scriptures, more than 78,000,000 of which were in English. The total expenditure up to that date was L14,686,072.
