# Nampa and Regional Connectivity

## Overview and Location
Nampa is a location in the state of Idaho, U.S.A., situated approximately 20 miles west of Boise. It serves as a critical transit point for the region, specifically acting as the connection point where the main line of the Oregon Short Line railway meets a branch line that terminates in Boise.

## Transportation and Infrastructure
The connectivity between Nampa and neighboring cities is facilitated by both rail and electric transport. In addition to the Oregon Short Line railway, Nampa is connected to Boise via electric lines. These same electric lines also provide a connection between Boise and Caldwell.

## Regional Context
Nampa is part of a broader network of settlements in southern Idaho. To its east lies Boise, the capital of the state and the county-seat of Ada county. Boise is located on the north side of the Boise river at an altitude of about 2700 ft. The region is characterized by the immense Boise-Payette irrigation system, which draws water from the Boise river 8 miles above the city of Boise. This system has contributed to rapid growth in the area after 1900.

The surrounding territory is a productive agricultural zone, particularly noted as an excellent country for the cultivation of prunes, apples, and other fruits. Consequently, Boise serves as the trade center for this agricultural and fruit-growing country, as well as for the surrounding mining country.

## Neighboring Cities
Caldwell is another significant nearby location, situated in Canyon county. It is connected to Boise by electric lines and is the site of the College of Caldwell, a Presbyterian institution that opened in 1891.
