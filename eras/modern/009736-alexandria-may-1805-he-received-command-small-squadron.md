# Alexandria

Alexandria is a coastal city that has served as a significant strategic point for naval operations and diplomatic encounters across different historical eras.

## Naval Blockades and Early 19th Century Operations
Following the battle of the Nile, the British Royal Navy maintained a presence off the coast of Egypt. Sir Samuel Hood, a British vice-admiral, was tasked with commanding the blockading force situated off Alexandria and Rosetta after Admiral Nelson departed the coast of Egypt.

In 1805, the city served as a site for diplomatic attempts between members of the French imperial family. Jerome, the brother of Napoleon, sought to bend the emperor's will during an interview held at Alexandria. This meeting occurred during a period of tension regarding Jerome's marriage to Elizabeth Patterson, a union that had annoyed Napoleon and led the emperor to order the exclusion of Elizabeth from his states.

## 20th Century Strategic Importance
During the early 20th century, Alexandria remained a critical location for the defense of the Suez Canal. In the period preceding a projected Turkish attack upon Egypt, the British First Cruiser Squadron—which included the vessels *Warrior*, *Duke of Edinburgh*, and *Black Prince*—was utilized for guard duties at Port Said and Alexandria, as well as for escort duties at sea.

Due to an increasing strain on resources, these larger ships were eventually replaced by smaller, older vessels. This replacement was necessary because the *Black Prince*, *Duke of Edinburgh*, and *Warrior* were urgently required to form a combat squadron near the Cap de Verde Islands to participate in a general combination against von Spee, and were also promised to the Commander-in-Chief for the Grand Fleet.

## Regional Connectivity
Historically, Alexandria served as a primary terminus for regional travel and military movement. The town of Derna, located on the north coast of Africa, functioned as a station on the road connecting Alexandria to Kairawan during early Moslem times. Additionally, Derna served as a base for Egyptian attacks directed toward Tripolitana and Cyrenaica.
