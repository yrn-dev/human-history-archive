## Technical Transcription Notes
The production process included specific transcription standards to maintain the integrity of the original printed texts. These notes, provided by the transcribers, detail how original formatting was handled in the digital versions:

* **Subscripts and Superscripts**: Numbers following letters without a space (such as C2) were originally printed in subscript; in the eBooks, letter subscripts are preceded by an underscore (e.g., C_n). Characters following a carat (^) were originally printed in superscript.
* **Structural Adjustments**: In several volumes, side-notes were relocated to serve as titles for their respective paragraphs.
* **Corrections and Omissions**: In the "Demijohn" to "Destructors" volume, a few typographical errors were corrected and underlined in the HTML version. In the "Dagupan" to "David" and "Bohemia" to "Borgia, Francis" volumes, dots below letters as well as macrons and breves above letters were not inserted.
