# Wright and Related Historical Works

The name Wright appears in the archive's records in connection with various scholarly contributions across different fields, including colonial descriptions, philosophical editing, and mathematical navigation.

## Colonial Descriptions of British Honduras
In the study of British Honduras, P.B. Wright co-authored the *Handbook of British Honduras*, published in Edinburgh in 1892 alongside L.W. Bristowe. This work is cited as providing a good general description of the colony. Other sources for the region include annual reports to the British Colonial Office in London for statistical data, and works by H. Fowler (1879) and J. Bellamy (1889) regarding exploration. The local history of British Honduras is further detailed in publications by A.R. Gibbs (1883), E.J. Squier (1855), and Chief Justice Temple (1847).

## Philosophical Editions of Francis Bacon
W. Aldis Wright is noted for his editorial work on the writings of Francis Bacon. He produced an edition of Bacon's *Essays* in London in 1862. Additionally, Wright edited the *Advancement of Learning*, with a Cambridge edition published in 1866 and a fifth edition released in 1900.

## Mathematical and Navigational Works
The archive records the contributions of Edward Wright to the field of navigation. His treatise, *Certain Errors in Navigation detected and corrected*, was published in London in 1610; the second edition of this work included "Tables for the Improvement of Navigation." Furthermore, a description of an instrumental table devised by Edward Wright was published in London in 1616 and 1618.
