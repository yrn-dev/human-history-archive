# Temperature Variations in Siberia

## Monthly Mean Temperature Differences
Siberia is characterized by very great variations in temperature. The difference between the mean of the hottest month and the mean of the coldest month is hardly anywhere less than 60 deg. Fahr. In areas near the coast, this variation between the monthly means amounts to more than 100 deg. Fahr.

## Comparison with Other Regions
The temperature ranges in Siberia are significantly more extreme than those found in southern Asia. Under the equator, the variation between the hottest and coldest monthly means is reduced to approximately 5 deg. In northern India, the greatest difference does not exceed 40 deg., falling further to about 15 deg. at Calcutta and 10 to 12 deg. at Bombay and Madras. Other regional differences between the means of the hottest and coldest months include:
* **Sea of Aral:** 80 deg. Fahr.
* **Peking (northern China):** 55 deg. Fahr.
* **Astrakhan (on the Caspian):** more than 50 deg. Fahr.
* **Tiflis:** 45 deg. Fahr.
* **Canton:** 30 deg. Fahr.
* **Manila:** 20 deg. Fahr.
* **Singapore, Batavia (Java), and Galle (Ceylon):** less than 5 deg.

## Extreme Temperatures
Beyond the monthly means, the difference between the extreme temperatures of the hottest and coldest months in Siberia is even greater. The extreme temperatures in the region are considered to range from maxima of 80 deg. to 90 deg. Fahr. to minima between -40 deg. and -70 deg. Fahr. 

For comparison, the absolute minimum in northern India (at latitude 30 deg.) hardly drops below 32 deg., and in southern Asia, a maximum of 110 deg. or 112 deg. Fahr. is hardly ever exceeded.
