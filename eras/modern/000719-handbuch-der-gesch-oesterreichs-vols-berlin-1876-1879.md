# Handbuch der Gesch. Oesterreichs

The *Handbuch der Gesch. Oesterreichs* is a historical work authored by Franz X. Krones, who is also identified as Ritter von Marchland. Published in Berlin between 1876 and 1879, the work consists of five volumes.

## Characteristics and Perspective
The *Handbuch der Gesch. Oesterreichs* is noted for containing copious references. In terms of its ideological framing, the work is written from a German-liberal point of view.

## Related Works by the Author
Franz X. Krones produced other historical texts in addition to the *Handbuch*. One such work is *Gesch. der Neuzeit Oesterreichs vom 18ten Jahrhundert bis auf die Gegenwart*, which was also published in Berlin in 1879.

## Context within Austrian Historiography
The *Handbuch der Gesch. Oesterreichs* exists among a wide array of general histories of Austria. Other significant works in the field include:
* **English Language Works:** Archdeacon William Coxe's *History of the House of Austria, 1218-1792* (3 vols., 1817), which was continued by W. Kelly (1853; new edition 1873).
* **German Language Works:** J. Majlath's *Geschichte des oesterreichischen Kaiserstaates* (5 vols., 1834-1850) and A. von Arneth's 10-volume *Geschichte Maria Theresias* (1863-1879).
* **Specialized Histories:** Count F. von Hartig's *Genesis der Revolution in Oesterreich im Jahre 1848* (1851), W. H. Stiles' eye-witness account *Austria in 1848-1849* (1852), and M. Buedinger's *Oesterreichische Gesch. bis zum Ausgange des dreizehnten Jahrhunderts* (1858).
* **Other Series and Perspectives:** The 17-volume series *Oesterreichische Gesch. fuer das Volk* (1864) and W. Rogge's works, including *Oesterreich von Vilagos bis zur Gegenwart* (1872, 1873) and *Oesterreich seit der Katastrophe Hohenwart-Beust* (1879), the latter of which is described as being written from a somewhat violent German standpoint.
