# Battle of Roucoux (Raucourt)

The Battle of Roucoux, also referred to as the Battle of Raucourt, was a military engagement fought on October 11, 1746, during the war in the Netherlands. The conflict took place near Liege and involved the forces of the French against a coalition of allies.

## Combatants and Command
The battle was fought between the French army, commanded by Marshal Saxe, and the allied forces, which were under the leadership of Prince Charles of Lorraine. The allies in this theater included the Dutch and the Austrians.

## Strategic Context
The engagement occurred during the later campaigns of the war in the Netherlands, a period characterized by the developed genius of Marshal Saxe. Following the battle of Fontenoy, the French had maintained a strong offensive. The position of the allies had become increasingly helpless due to the withdrawal of most English forces, who had returned home to assist in suppressing the "Forty-Five rebellion."

By 1746, the French had successfully driven the Austrians and the Dutch back toward the line of the Meuse. During this progression, the French captured most of the important fortresses in the region.

## Outcome and Aftermath
The battle resulted in a victory for the French under Marshal Saxe. This victory placed Holland in immediate danger. By April 1747, Saxe's army had conquered the Austrian Netherlands up to the Meuse and turned its attention toward the United Provinces. The frontier fortresses of the United Provinces offered only slight resistance to the French advance.

The momentum established by the French continued into July 1747, when the duke of Cumberland and the prince of Orange suffered a severe defeat at Lauffeld (also known as Lawfeld or Val) on July 2. Following this victory, Saxe secretly dispatched a corps under Marshal Loewendahl to besiege Bergen-op-Zoom, which the French stormed on September 18.

In the final year of the war, the combined forces of Loewendahl and Saxe attacked Maestricht, which surrendered on May 7, 1748. Although a large Russian army—which had allied with Austria in 1746—arrived on the Meuse to support the allies, they arrived too late to be of use. The general peace of Aix-la-Chapelle (Aachen) was eventually signed on October 18, 1748.
