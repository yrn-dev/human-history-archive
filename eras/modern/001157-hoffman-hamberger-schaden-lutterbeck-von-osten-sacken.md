# Hoffman, J. Hamberger, E. v. Schaden, Lutterbeck, von Osten-Sacken

The names Hoffman, J. Hamberger, E. v. Schaden, Lutterbeck, and von Osten-Sacken appear in the archive's records primarily as adherents and editors associated with the works of Baader.

## Editors of Baader's Works
F. Hoffman, J. Hamberger, E. v. Schaden, Lutterbeck, von Osten-Sacken, and Schlueter were adherents who collected and published the works of Baader in a 16-volume set titled *Baader's saemmtliche Werke*, published between 1851 and 1860. The editors provided valuable introductions to several of these volumes. The collection includes a full biography in Volume XV and an index in Volume XVI, which also contains a sketch of the entire system written by Lutterbeck.

## Related Publications and Authors
Several of these individuals authored specific works regarding Baader's philosophy:

*   **F. Hoffmann:** Published *Vorhalle zur spekulativen Lehre Baader's* (1836), *Grundzuege der Societaets-Philosophie Franz Baader's* (1837), *Die Weltalter* (1868), and *Biographie und Briefwechsel* (Leipzig, 1887). He also produced a three-volume set of *Philosophische Schriften* between 1868 and 1872.
*   **J. Hamberger:** Authored *Cardinalpunkte der Baaderschen Philosophie* (1855) and *Fundamentalbegriffe von F. B.'s Ethik, Politik, u. Religions-Philosophie* (1858).
*   **J. A. B. Lutterbeck:** Wrote *Philosophische Standpunkte Baaders* (1854) and *Baaders Lehre vom Weltgebaeude* (1866).
