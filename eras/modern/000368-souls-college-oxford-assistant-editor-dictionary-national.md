# All Souls' College, Oxford

All Souls' College is an institution at the University of Oxford associated with several scholars and academics.

## Associated Fellows
The college has served as a fellowship for various individuals across different disciplines. Albert Frederick Pollard, a Professor of English History at the University of London, is a Fellow of All Souls' College. He is also an author of works including *Henry VIII.*, *Life of Thomas Cranmer*, and *England under the Protector Somerset*.

In the field of theology and church leadership, Herbert Hensley Henson, a Canon of Westminster Abbey and Rector of St Margaret's, Westminster, was formerly a Fellow of All Souls' College. Henson is the author of *The National Church*, *Christ and the Nation*, *Moral Discipline in the Christian Church*, and *Apostolic Christianity*.

Additionally, Henry William Carless Davis, who served as a Fellow and Tutor of Balliol College, Oxford, held a fellowship at All Souls' College from 1895 to 1902. Davis is the author of *Charlemagne* and *England under the Normans and Angevins, 1066-1272*.
