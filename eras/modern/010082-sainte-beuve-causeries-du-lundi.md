# Sainte-Beuve's Causeries du Lundi

The *Causeries du lundi* is a multi-volume work by Sainte-Beuve that serves as a significant reference for literary history, biography, and the study of historical figures. The work is organized into numerous volumes and is frequently cited in relation to a wide array of French writers, historical memoirs, and political eras.

## Literary and Biographical Analysis
Sainte-Beuve utilizes the *Causeries du lundi* to provide critical notices and analyses of various authors and their works. In volume vi, the author provides a long and careful notice regarding the *Mémoires* of Marmont, a work of military history that serves as a personal defense of Marmont's services and glory. Volume vi is also cited in relation to the works of Ducis, whose tragedies—including *Hamlet*, *Macbeth*, *Oedipe chez Admete*, and *Abufar*—were reprinted in Didot's *Chefs-d'oeuvre tragiques*. Other volumes of the *Causeries* focus on different literary figures and periods. Volume ii is associated with the study of the *Memoires* and the youth and final years of Mme d'Epinay. ## Historical Applications
Beyond individual literary criticism, the *Causeries du lundi* is used as a source for understanding broader historical periods. Specifically, the work is cited as a resource for those studying the reign of Louis XIV. This era is noted for its richness in memoirs describing court life, including the letters of Madame de Sévigné and the memoirs of Saint-Simon. Sainte-Beuve's work complements other historical accounts of this period, such as those by Voltaire in *Siècle de Louis XIV* and P. Clément's history of Colbert's administration.
