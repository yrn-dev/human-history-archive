# Chicago & North-Western Railway (Construction and Repair Shops)

The Chicago & North-Western railway maintains specialized industrial facilities for the construction and repair of railway equipment in several locations across the United States, specifically in the states of Iowa and Wisconsin.

## Facilities in Iowa
In the city of Boone, which serves as the county-seat of Boone county, the Chicago & North-Western railway operates construction and repair shops. Boone is located near the center of the state, a short distance from the Des Moines river. The city, which was laid out in 1865 and chartered as a city in 1868, is an important center for coal and the manufacture of bricks and tiles. In addition to the Chicago & North-Western, the city is served by the Chicago, Milwaukee & St Paul railways and the Fort Dodge, Des Moines & Southern inter-urban railway.

## Facilities in Wisconsin
The Chicago & North-Western railway also maintains railway repair shops in Ashland, a city located in the northern part of Wisconsin at the head of Chequamegon Bay. Ashland serves as the county-seat of Ashland county and is situated approximately 70 miles east of Duluth and Superior, and 315 miles northwest of Milwaukee. 

The city is a significant port for the shipment of iron ore from the Gogebic Range, with annual shipments valued at $12,000,000 and approximating 3,500,000 tons. Along with the railway repair shops, Ashland's industrial base includes foundries, machine shops, iron and steel rolling mills, large saw-mills, and manufactories producing charcoal, wood-alcohol, sulphite fibre, and dynamite. The city is served by several other railways, including the Northern Pacific, the Wisconsin Central, and the Chicago, St Paul, Minneapolis & Omaha.
