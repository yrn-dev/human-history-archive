# Mme du Deffand and the Abbé Barthelemy

The relationship between Mme du Deffand and the abbé Barthelemy is documented through their personal communications, which highlight a connection characterized by intimacy.

## The Correspondance de Mme du Deffand
The letters exchanged between Mme du Deffand and the abbé Barthelemy are preserved in a three-volume collection published in 1866. This work, titled *Correspondance complete de Mme du Deffand avec la duchesse de Choiseul, l'abbe Barthelemy et M. Craufurt*, was edited by the marquis de Sainte-Aulaire. These documents serve as the primary record of the intimate terms on which the two individuals lived.

## The Abbé Barthelemy
The abbé Barthelemy was a figure involved in extensive correspondence with various individuals. In addition to his letters to Mme du Deffand, his writings include:
*   **Correspondence with Paolo Paciaudi:** These letters focused primarily on antiquarian subjects and were edited by Ch. Nisard in 1877, appearing alongside the *Correspondance inedite du comte de Caylus*.
*   **Letters to the Comte de Caylus:** These were published in 1801 by Antoine Serieys under the title *Voyage en Italie*.

Further biographical and professional records of Barthelemy include his own writings, *Memoires sur la vie de l'abbe Barthelemy, ecrits par lui-meme* (1824), which features a notice by Lalande, and his *Oeuvres completes* (1821), a four-volume set containing a notice by Villenave.
