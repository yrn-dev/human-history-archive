# Otto von Bohtlingk

Otto von Bohtlingk (1815–1904) was a German Sanskrit scholar recognized as one of the most distinguished scholars of the 19th century. His academic contributions are considered to be of pre-eminent value within the fields of comparative philology and Indian philology.

## Early Life and Education
Bohtlingk was born in St Petersburg on May 30 (June 11 O.S.), 1815. Between 1833 and 1835, he studied Oriental languages at the university of St Petersburg, focusing particularly on Sanskrit, Persian, and Arabic. He later extended his studies in Germany, spending time first in Berlin and subsequently in Bonn from 1839 to 1842.

## Career and Academic Appointments
Upon returning to St Petersburg in 1842, Bohtlingk became attached to the Royal Academy of Sciences. He was elected as an ordinary member of the society in 1855. His professional standing was further recognized through state appointments; he was named a "Russian state councillor" in 1860 and later received the title of nobility as a "privy councillor."

In the latter part of his life, Bohtlingk moved to Germany. He settled in Jena in 1868 and relocated to Leipzig in 1885. He resided in Leipzig until his death on April 1, 1904.

## Major Works and Contributions
Bohtlingk's scholarly output spanned several decades and included critical editions, translations, and linguistic treatises.

### Philological and Grammatical Works
His first major contribution was an edition of Panini's *Acht Bucher grammatischer Regeln*, published in Bonn between 1839 and 1840. This work served as a criticism of the philological methods employed by Franz Bopp. Forty-seven years later, in 1887, Bohtlingk revisited this work, republishing it in Leipzig with a complete translation under the title *Paninis Grammatik mit Ubersetzung*.

Other significant linguistic publications include:
* *Vopadevas Grammatik* (St Petersburg, 1847)
* *Uber die Sprache der Jakuten* (St Petersburg, 1851)
* *Uber den Accent im Sanskrit* (1843), a treatise on Sanskrit accents.

### Translations and Collections
Bohtlingk produced a critical examination and translation of the *Chhandogya-upanishad* (1889) and a translation of the *Brihadaranyaka-upanishad* (1889), both published in St Petersburg. He also authored *Indische Spruche*, the second edition of which was published in three parts in St Petersburg between 1870 and 1873; an index for this work was later published by Blau in Leipzig in 1893.

### The Sanskrit-Worterbuch
Bohtlingk's *magnum opus* is the *Sanskrit-Worterbuch*, a massive Sanskrit dictionary. The work was completed over a period of twenty-three years with the assistance of two friends, Albrecht Weber (born 1825) and Rudolf Roth (1821–1895). The dictionary was first published in seven volumes in St Petersburg between 1853 and 1875, with a new seven-volume edition released between 1879 and 1889.
