# LIMITED RIGHT OF REPLACEMENT OR REFUND

## Overview of Refund Eligibility
Under the terms specified in paragraph 1.F.3, individuals who discover a defect in an electronic work are eligible for a refund of any money paid for the work. This right is subject to a time limit, as the defect must be discovered within 90 days of receiving the work. To initiate this process, the recipient must send a written explanation to the person from whom the work was received.

## Requirements for Physical and Electronic Media
The procedure for requesting a refund varies depending on the medium used to deliver the work:
*   **Physical Medium:** If the work was received on a physical medium, the recipient is required to return that medium along with their written explanation.
*   **Electronic Delivery:** If the work was received electronically, the providing person or entity may choose to offer a second opportunity to receive the work electronically instead of issuing a refund.

## Replacement Options and Final Demands
The person or entity that provided the defective work maintains the right to elect to provide a replacement copy in lieu of a monetary refund. In cases where a second electronic copy is provided and that copy is also found to be defective, the recipient may demand a refund in writing. In such an instance, the refund may be demanded without any further opportunities for the provider to fix the problem.

## Warranty Limitations
According to paragraph 1.F.4, the limited right of replacement or refund described in paragraph 1.F.3 is the only such right provided. Outside of these specific terms, the work is provided "AS-IS." There are no other warranties of any kind, whether express or implied. This exclusion of warranties specifically includes, but is not limited to, warranties of fitness for any purpose or merchantability.
