# Briefwechsel der Konigin Katharina und des Konigs Jerome von Westfalen

The *Briefwechsel der Konigin Katharina und des Konigs Jerome von Westfalen mit Konig Friedrich von Wurttemberg* is a published collection of correspondence involving Queen Catherine and King Jerome of Westphalia and King Friedrich of Wurttemberg. The work was published in Stuttgart between 1886 and 1887 by August von Schlossberger.

## Related Documentation and Supplements
The correspondence documented by Schlossberger has been supplemented by further materials. Specifically, the author Baron Albert du Casse provided additional information in the work *Corresp. inedite de la reine Catherine de Westphalie*, which was published in Paris between 1888 and 1893.
