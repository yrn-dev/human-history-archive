# Lillie Road and the West Brompton Railway Stations

## Location and Connectivity
Lillie Road is a thoroughfare located in London, England. It serves as a connecting route between South Kensington and Fulham Palace Road. In the western district of Brompton—situated in the south-east of the metropolitan borough of Kensington—the road is joined by Richmond Road and Old Brompton Road, the latter of which continues from Brompton Road leading south-west from Knightsbridge.

## Railway Infrastructure
At the point where Lillie Road joins with Richmond Road and Old Brompton Road, the District and West London railway stations of West Brompton are located.

## Surrounding District
The area around these railway stations is part of the Brompton district. This region is primarily occupied by residences of the better class. Notable landmarks and institutions within this district include:
* The Oratory of St Philip Neri (commonly known as the Brompton Oratory), which is located close to the Victoria and Albert Museum.
* The Brompton consumption hospital.
* The Brompton cemetery, also referred to as the West London cemetery.

## Regional Context
Lillie Road extends into the western metropolitan borough of Fulham. This borough is bounded by Kensington to the north-east, Hammersmith to the north-west, Chelsea to the east, and the river Thames to the south, south-east, and south-west. Other principal thoroughfares in this borough include the King's Road, Fulham Road, Fulham Palace Road, North End Road, and Wandsworth Bridge Road.
