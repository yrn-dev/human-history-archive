## Academic and Professional Developments
The year 1863 marked a significant return for the German historian and publicist Friedrich Karl Biedermann. After a period of retirement in Weimar—where he had edited the *Weimarische Zeitung* following the loss of his professorship—Biedermann returned to Leipzig in 1863. Upon his return, he took on the role of editor for the *Deutsche Allgemeine Zeitung*. Biedermann, who had previously been a member of the national parliament in Frankfort in 1848 and an advocate for German unity under Prussian leadership, would eventually regain his professorship in 1865.
