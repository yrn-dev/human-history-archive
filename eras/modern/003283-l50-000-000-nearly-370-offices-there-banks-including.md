## Other National Banking Institutions
### The Austro-Hungarian Bank
Founded in 1816 as the Austrian National Bank and renamed in 1878, the Austro-Hungarian Bank holds the exclusive legal right to issue bank-notes for the two states of the monarchy. Under a 1899 statute, it became a joint-stock company with a stock of L8,780,000. Its notes must be covered by legal specie (current silver and gold) to the extent of two-fifths. At the end of 1900, the bank reported capital of L8,750,000, a reserve fund of L428,250, cash of L50,754,000, and a note circulation of L62,251,000. By 1907, the reserve fund grew to L548,041, cash to L60,036,625, and note circulation to L84,501,000.
