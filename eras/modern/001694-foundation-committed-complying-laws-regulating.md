# Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation

## Mission and Objectives
The Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation operates with the mission of increasing the availability of licensed works and those in the public domain. The Foundation aims to ensure these works can be freely distributed in a machine-readable format. A key component of this objective is ensuring that these materials are accessible via the widest possible array of equipment, which specifically includes outdated equipment.

## Organizational Support and Funding
The survival of Project Gutenberg™ is dependent upon widespread public support and donations. These contributions are essential for the Foundation to carry out its stated mission. The archive notes that small donations, ranging from $1 to $5,000, are particularly important for the purpose of maintaining the organization's tax exempt status with the IRS.

## Legal Compliance and Charitable Regulations
The Foundation is committed to complying with the laws that regulate charitable donations and charities across all 50 states of the United States.
