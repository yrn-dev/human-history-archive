# Hardwicke in Add. MSS. Brit. Museum

The records pertaining to Lord Chancellor Hardwicke within the Additional Manuscripts (Add. MSS.) of the British Museum consist primarily of letters addressed to him. These documents are a significant component of the archival material used to study the life and correspondence of historical figures, such as Bolingbroke.

## Content and Transcription
The collection of letters to Lord Chancellor Hardwicke held in the Add. MSS. of the British Museum is largely transcribed by W. Sichel. Researchers seeking these specific documents are directed to the Index for the years 1894-1899.

## Relation to Bolingbroke
These manuscripts serve as a bibliographic source for the study of Bolingbroke. They are listed alongside other primary materials used to document his life and political activities, including the *Hardwicke State Papers* (specifically volume ii), the *Marchmont Papers* (edited by Sir G.H. Rose in 1831), and the *Hist. MSS. Comm.* regarding the manuscripts of the Duke of Portland at Welbeck and the Marquis of Bath. Additionally, the British Museum holds a separate collection of Bolingbroke's letters specifically relating to the treaty of Utrecht.
