# Vienna

## Geography and Location
Vienna is the capital of Lower Austria. The city is situated along the Danube, which forms arms and islands beyond the city. The Danube is one of the navigable rivers of the province, and the stretch of the river between Vienna and Linz is noted for its picturesque beauty and the presence of various medieval and modern buildings of historical and archaeological interest.

## Economic and Industrial Role
Vienna serves as the center of the very extensive commerce of Lower Austria. Along with Bohemia and Moravia, Lower Austria is considered to be in the front rank of the Austrian provinces from an industrial perspective. Vienna is the center of this great industrial activity. 

While the city is the primary hub, factories are also spread throughout the region of the Wiener Wald up to the Semmering, utilizing the area's many waters for motive power. The principal industries of the province include:
* Metallurgic and textile industries in all branches
* Milling, brewing, and chemicals
* Paper, leather, and silk
* Cloth, millinery, and *objets de luxe*
* Physical and musical instruments
* Foodstuffs, tobacco, and sugar factories

## Administrative Context
Vienna is located within Lower Austria, a province that is characterized by a high population density. In terms of population density, Lower Austria (which contains Vienna) and Silesia stand higher than Bohemia. 

In the broader political structure of the Austrian provinces, Vienna is the location of the Reichsrat. This legislative body receives deputies from various provinces; for example, Bohemia sends 130 deputies to the Reichsrat at Vienna, and Carniola sends eleven.
