# Schwarzwald

The *Schwarzwald*, or Black Forest, is a mountainous region located within the Grand Duchy of Baden, a sovereign state in the south-west corner of the German empire. The region extends from the river Neckar in the north to the Lake of Constance in the south.

## Geography and Topography
The *Schwarzwald* is characterized by its significant elevation and is divided into two distinct districts by the valley of the Kinzig. These two districts differ in their average heights:

*   **Southern District:** Located to the south of the Kinzig, this area has a mean height of 3,100 ft. The highest summit in this district is the Feldberg, which reaches approximately 4,898 ft.
*   **Northern District:** Located to the north of the Kinzig, this area is lower, with a mean height of only 2,100 ft. The culminating point of this section is the Belchen, which does not exceed 4,480 ft.

## Regional Context
The *Schwarzwald* forms a major part of the mountainous terrain of the Grand Duchy of Baden. This mountainous landscape is the most extensive part of the state, accounting for nearly 80% of its total area of 5,823 sq. m. 

The region is situated among other geographical features of the state. To the north of the Neckar lies the Odenwald Range, which has a mean elevation of 1,440 ft. and reaches a peak of 1,980 ft. at the Katzenbuckel. Additionally, the Kaiserstuhl—an independent volcanic group measuring nearly 10 m. in length and 5 m. in breadth with a high point of 1,760 ft.—lies between the Dreisam and the Rhine.

## Hydrography
The drainage of the *Schwarzwald* is split between two major basins. The greater part of the Grand Duchy of Baden belongs to the basin of the Rhine, which is fed by more than twenty tributaries from the highlands. However, a portion of the eastern slope of the Black Forest belongs to the basin of the Danube, where the river originates from a number of mountain streams.

The duchy contains several lakes, including the Eichener, Mummel, Schluch, and Wilder, though none are considered large. The Lake of Constance (*Boden-See*) is shared between Switzerland and Bavaria.
