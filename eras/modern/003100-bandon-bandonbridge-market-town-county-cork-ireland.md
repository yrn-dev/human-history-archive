# BANDON

Bandon, also known as Bandonbridge, is a market town located in County Cork, Ireland. It is situated within the south-east parliamentary division of the county, positioned picturesquely in a broad, open valley on both sides of the river Bandon. As of 1901, the population of the town was 2,830.

## History and Governance
The town was founded early in the 17th century by Richard Boyle, the earl of Cork, and was subsequently incorporated by James I. Through the founding or rebuilding of Bandon and other towns, the Boyle family established nearly the entire political power of the county. 

In terms of political representation, Bandon originally returned two members to the Irish parliament. Following this, it returned one member to the Imperial parliament until 1885. The town's history includes a period of conflict in 1689 when the Irish destroyed its walls; following this event, Bandon resisted the admission of Catholic inhabitants for a long period.

## Geography and Communications
Bandon is located 20 miles south-west of the city of Cork. It is served by the Cork, Bandon & South Coast railway. The river Bandon, which rises in the Cullinagh Lough and flows generally eastward, is a central feature of the town's geography. Two miles below Bandon on this river lies Innishannon, which serves as the head of navigation. The river Bandon eventually forms an estuary known as Kinsale Harbour.

## Economy and Industry
The town serves as an important agricultural centre. Its industrial infrastructure includes flour-mills, breweries, and distilleries. Additionally, the Bandon district is noted for its salmon fishing. The river Bandon is regarded as a good location for rod-fishing for salmon, and the town is also cited as a good station for trout fishing in the loughs and upper waters of the river and its tributaries.

## Local Landmarks and Administration
An attractive feature of the town is the open park of Castle Bernard, the seat of the earl of Bandon, which is located on the riverside. 

Administratively, Bandon is located in the west riding of County Cork. While assizes for the county are held at Cork, quarter-sessions are held at Bandon, along with other locations such as Bantry, Clonakilty, Macroom, and Skibbereen.
