# Catherine (7 vols., Paris, 1861-1866) and Les Rois freres de Napoleon

The works *Memoires et correspondance du roi Jerome et de la reine Catherine* and *Les Rois freres de Napoleon* are historical texts authored by Baron Albert du Casse. These publications serve as primary bibliographic sources for the study of the Bonaparte family, specifically focusing on the lives and political roles of Napoleon's brothers and their spouses.

## Memoires et correspondance du roi Jerome et de la reine Catherine
Published in Paris between 1861 and 1866, this work spans seven volumes. It is cited as one of the chief works relating to Jerome Bonaparte. The text focuses on the memoirs and correspondence of King Jerome and Queen Catherine. 

The study of Queen Catherine is further supplemented by another work by Baron Albert du Casse titled *Corresp. inedite de la reine Catherine de Westphalie*, published in Paris between 1888 and 1893. Additionally, the correspondence between King Jerome of Westphalia and Queen Catherine with King Friedrich of Wurttemberg was published in Stuttgart (1886-1887) by August von Schlossberger, a collection which was later supplemented by du Casse.

## Les Rois freres de Napoleon
Published in 1883, *Les Rois freres de Napoleon* is a significant work by Baron Albert du Casse that addresses multiple members of the Bonaparte family. Because it examines the "brother kings" of Napoleon, it is listed as a key reference for the biographies of several different individuals:

*   **Joseph Bonaparte:** The work is cited among the many texts dealing with Joseph Bonaparte, alongside du Casse's ten-volume *Memoires et correspondance politique et militaire du roi Joseph* (Paris, 1854).
*   **Louis Bonaparte:** The text is listed as a chief work regarding the life and reign of Louis, the King of Holland.
*   **Jerome Bonaparte:** The work is categorized as one of the primary sources relating to Jerome Bonaparte.

## Context within Bonaparte Scholarship
The writings of Baron Albert du Casse exist alongside a broader body of literature concerning the Bonaparte family. Other notable works on Jerome Bonaparte include *Konig Jerome Napoleon* by M.M. Kaisenberg, *The Bonaparte-Patterson Marriage* by W.T.R. Saffell, *Jerome Napoleon, roi de Westfalie* (1902) by A. Martinet, and *The Burlesque Napoleon* (1905) by P.W. Sergeant. 

General family histories, such as the four-volume *Napoleon et sa famille* (published in Paris between 1889 and 1900) by F. Masson, provide further context to the specific accounts found in du Casse's volumes. The fortunes of the family are also tracked through the biographies of the descendants of Jerome and Lucien Bonaparte.
