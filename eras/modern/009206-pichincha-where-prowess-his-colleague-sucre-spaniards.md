# The Battle of Pichincha and the Liberation of Ecuador

## The Campaign for Quito
Following the expulsion of Spanish forces from Colombia, the neighboring provinces of Peru and Ecuador remained under Spanish possession. To complete the liberation of the entire region, Bolivar placed himself at the head of the army and marched toward Quito, located in Ecuador.

## The Battle of Pichincha
A severe battle took place at Pichincha. Through the prowess of Bolivar's colleague, Sucre, the Spanish forces were routed. This victory allowed the republicans to enter Quito in June 1822.

## Subsequent Campaigns in Peru
Following the success at Pichincha, Bolivar marched upon Lima. The royalists evacuated the city upon his approach, and Bolivar entered the capital in triumph. Upon entering, he was authorized to utilize all the resources of the country and was invested with absolute power as dictator.

However, this position was complicated by the intrigues of republican factions within Peru, which forced Bolivar to withdraw to Truxillo. This left the capital vulnerable to the Spaniards under Canterac, who immediately occupied the city.

## Final Defeat of Spanish Forces
The setback in Lima proved temporary. By June 1824, the liberating army had been completely organized and subsequently routed the vanguard of the enemy. On August 6, Bolivar defeated Canterac on the plains of Junin. 

While Bolivar returned to Lima, Sucre was tasked with following the royalists in their retreat to Upper Peru. Sucre executed this mission with success and ability, achieving a decisive victory at Ayacucho. This victory completed the dispersion of the Spanish force, leaving their possessions in Peru confined to the castles of Callao. These castles were maintained by Rodil for more than a year despite efforts to reduce them.

## The Formation of Bolivia
In June 1825, Bolivar visited Upper Peru. This region had detached itself from the government of Buenos Aires and was formed into a separate state named Bolivia in honor of the liberator. The first congress of this new republic assembled in August 1825. During this assembly, Bolivar was requested to prepare a constitution of government and was declared the perpetual protector.
