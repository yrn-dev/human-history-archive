# Average Circulation in 1906

The year 1906 provides a significant data point for analyzing the circulation of currency and publications across various national banking systems and religious societies. These figures reflect the operational scale of central banks, the regional distribution of private notes, and the global reach of scriptural distribution.

## United Kingdom Note Circulation
In 1906, the total average circulation of notes across the United Kingdom was £43,372,000. This total was distributed among three primary regions:
*   **England:** £29,443,000
*   **Scotland:** £7,477,000
*   **Ireland:** £6,452,000

Within England, the vast majority of the circulation was held by the Bank of England, which averaged £28,890,000. In contrast, joint-stock banks accounted for £429,000 and private banks for £124,000. 

The increase in the Bank of England's circulation since 1844 is attributed in part to the growth of banking offices; while there were 976 offices in England and Wales in 1844, this number rose to more than 5,880 by 1906. Because these offices must hold till-money, which almost always includes Bank of England notes, it is probable that a large portion of the increase is held in these tills rather than representing a significant increase in active circulation.

In Scotland, the banking landscape by 1906 had consolidated into ten banks of issue that carried on nearly all of the country's business, operating through 1,180 branches.

## Continental European Bank Circulation
### The Bank of France
The Bank of France operated primarily through a massive note circulation, which averaged £186,300,000 in 1906. This circulation served as the primary means for conducting most business transactions within France. The limit of this circulation had been extended over several decades, rising from 350 million francs in March 1848 to 5,800 million francs (converted to £232,000,000) by 1906.

### The Reichsbank
In Germany, the notes issued by the Reichsbank averaged £69,000,000 for the year 1906. The bank's circulation was governed by laws from 1875 and 1899. To manage these issues, the bank held bills amounting to £67,000,000 and loans and advances totaling £14,200,000 in its 1906 balance sheet. As of December 30, 1906, the gold held by the Reichsbank was £24,069,000.

## Religious Text Circulation
Outside of financial currency, the circulation of sacred texts reached significant milestones during the 1905-1906 period. A specific society reported an unprecedented total circulation of 5,977,453 copies. This total included 1,326,475 Testaments and 968,683 Bibles.

Of the total volumes, 1,921,000 were issued from the Bible House in London. Distribution was global, with 1,331,000 copies in Welsh or English circulating mainly in the British colonies and England. Other major areas of distribution included:
*   **China:** 1,075,000 copies (mostly separate gospels)
*   **India, Burma, and Ceylon:** 768,000 copies
*   **Central Europe:** 679,000 copies
*   **The Russian Empire:** 595,000 copies
*   **Japan:** 286,000 copies
*   **France:** 203,000 copies
*   **Spain and Portugal:** 120,000 copies
*   **Italy:** 117,000 copies
