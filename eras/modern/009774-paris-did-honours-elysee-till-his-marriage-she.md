# Mathilde Letitia Wilhelmine

## Early Life and Marriage
Mathilde Letitia Wilhelmine was born on May 20, 1820, in Trieste. She was the daughter of Jerome and the sister of Prince Napoleon (XI.). In 1840, she was married to Prince Anatole Demidov, following a period where she had been almost betrothed to her cousin, Louis Napoleon. This marriage ended in separation within five years due to the conduct of Prince Anatole. Following this, Tsar Nicholas compelled Prince Anatole to provide Princess Mathilde with a handsome allowance.

## Residence in Paris and the Elysée
Following the election of Louis Napoleon to the presidency of the republic, Mathilde took up residence in Paris. During this period, she performed the duties of doing the honours of the Elysée until the time of Louis Napoleon's marriage.

## Influence and Later Years
Mathilde continued her life in Paris, where she established herself as a patron and friend to men of letters and art, wielding great influence in these circles. She remained in Paris until her death, which occurred on January 2, 1904.

## Family Relations
Mathilde was the sister of Prince Napoleon (XI.), whose eldest son, Prince Napoleon Victor Jerome Frederic (born 1862), became the recognized head of the French Bonapartist party upon his father's death. Her nephew, Prince Louis Napoleon, served as an officer in the Russian army and was appointed governor of the Caucasus in 1906.
