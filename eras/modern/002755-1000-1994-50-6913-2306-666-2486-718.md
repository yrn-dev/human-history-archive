## Income Tax and Revenue
Prior to 1909, income tax rates fluctuated, reaching a high of 16d. between 1855 and 1857, and a low of 2d. between 1874 and 1876. In the period of 1906-1907, it was estimated that each penny of the tax produced a revenue of £2,666,867.

Various committees investigated the tax system, including select committees in 1851-1852 and 1861, and a Departmental Committee in 1905. A 1906 select committee examined the practicability of differentiating between precarious and permanent incomes and graduating the tax. The committee concluded that graduation via the extension of the existing system of abatements was practicable. They suggested that the limits of prudent extension would not be exceeded by raising the amount of income allowed for an abatement to £1000 or more.
