# Bozena Nemceva

## Overview
Bozena Nemceva is recognized as one of the older writers of Bohemian literature. She is best known for her work *Babicka*, a piece of literature that has achieved international reach and has been translated into many languages.

## Literary Context in Bohemia
Nemceva's contributions are situated within a broader development of Bohemian literature that expanded significantly during the latter part of the 19th century. This growth was particularly notable following the establishment of the national university in 1882. 

Nemceva is categorized among the older writers of this tradition, alongside other figures such as Benes Trebizky, who was known for authoring numerous historical novels. Her work exists as part of a diverse literary landscape that includes the poetry and prose of John Neruda—specifically his philosophical epic *Kosmicke basne*—and the works of Julius Zeyer, whose novel *Jan Maria Plojhar* and epic poem *Vysehrad* are noted for their value and success.

## Contemporary Bohemian Authors
The era of Bohemian literature following the older writers like Nemceva saw the rise of various poets and novelists. Notable poets include Jaroslav Vrchlicky (who wrote under the pseudonym Emil Frida), Svatopluk Cech, and Adolf Heyduk. Vrchlicky is recognized as a brilliant dramatist and poet, producing works such as *Rok na jihu* and *Sonety Samotare*. Svatopluk Cech's work was characterized by national enthusiasm, exemplified in *Basne otroka* and *Vaclav z Michalovic, Lesetinsky Kovar*.

In the realm of the novel and drama, the archive notes the contributions of Alois Jirasek, Jacob Arbes (author of *Romanetta*), and Vaclav Hladik. Hladik's work *Evzen Voldan* is cited as a striking representation of life in modern Prague.
