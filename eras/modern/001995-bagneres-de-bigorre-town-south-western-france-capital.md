# BAGNERES-DE-BIGORRE

Bagneres-de-Bigorre is a town located in south-western France. It serves as the capital of an arrondissement within the department of Hautes-Pyrenees. Geographically, the town is situated on the left bank of the Adour at the northern end of the valley of Campan, approximately 13 miles south-south-east of Tarbes. It is accessible via a branch line of the Southern railway. As of 1906, the population of the town was 6,661.

## Geography and Appearance
The town is noted for its beautiful setting, surrounded by picturesque mountain scenery. It is described as being remarkably clean and neat, with many of its houses featuring marble ornamentation or construction.

## Thermal Springs and Tourism
Bagneres-de-Bigorre is one of the primary watering-places in France. The town possesses some fifty mineral springs, which are primarily characterized by the presence of iron or sulphate of lime. The temperatures of these springs range approximately from 59° to 122° Fahr. These waters are considered efficacious for the treatment of various maladies, including indigestion, nervous affections, and rheumatism.

The tourist season typically begins in May and ends around the close of October. During this period, the population of the town more than doubles. The center of social life in Bagneres is the Promenade des Coustous. The town's principal bathing establishments are town property: the Thermes, which dates from 1824 and contains a good library, and the Neothermes, which occupies a portion of the casino.

## Architecture and Landmarks
The town contains several notable buildings and historical sites:
* **Religious Sites:** The church of St Vincent dates from the 14th and 15th centuries. The town also contains the Carmelite church and the remains of the old church of St Jean.
* **Historical Remains:** The old quarter of the town features several old houses and the remains of a Jacobin monastery, which includes a graceful octagonal tower from the 15th century.
* **Civic Buildings:** Other chief buildings include the town-hall and a museum.

## Industry and Administration
Bagneres-de-Bigorre is the seat of tribunals of commerce and of first instance, and it possesses a communal college. The local economy is supported by several industries:
* **Textiles:** The town produces *barege* (a light fabric made of wool and silk), as well as knitting and weaving of woollen goods.
* **Crafts and Mining:** Local industries include wood-turning and the working of marble, which is both imported and found in the neighborhood. There are also slate quarries in the area.

## History
In antiquity, the town was frequently visited by the Romans, who called it *Vicus Aquensis*, though it later lost its renown. It reappeared in historical records during the 12th century when Centulle III, the count of Bigorre, granted the town a liberal charter. The thermal baths regained permanent importance in the 16th century, a period during which they were visited by Jeanne d'Albret.
