# Puerto Militar and Bahia Blanca

## Location and Geography
Bahia Blanca is a city and port located in Argentina on the Naposta river, situated approximately 3 miles from the river's outlet into a deep and well-sheltered bay of the same name. The city is positioned in the extreme southern region of the province of Buenos Aires, 447 miles southwest of the national capital when traveling by rail. While the immediate vicinity of the city is characterized as low, swampy, and decidedly unhealthy with brackish water, the surrounding region of southern Buenos Aires contains mountainous sections. To address health and infrastructure issues, a project was initiated in 1906 to provide a water supply from the Sauce Grande, located 50 miles away, alongside improvements in street paving and drainage.

## Puerto Militar and Naval Facilities
The Argentine government has established its primary naval station at Puerto Militar. This station is strategically located between the city of Bahia Blanca and the entrance to the bay.

## Port Infrastructure and Trade
The bay of Bahia Blanca is recognized as one of the finest on the Argentine coast. When the channel is properly dredged, it can accommodate steamers with a draught of 30 feet at low-water. The port's facilities include a shipping pier measuring 1640 feet in length, several miles of railway sidings, and spacious warehouses.

The port serves as the natural shipping hub for the southern districts of the province of Buenos Aires, as well as the national territories of Neuquen and La Pampa, the opening of which contributed significantly to the growth of the city. Large quantities of wool and wheat are exported from this location. Trade is increasing rapidly, supported by five or six lines of railway and their associated branches that connect the port to interior producing districts.

## History and Development
Bahia Blanca was established in 1828 with the location of a trading post and a fort. However, its emergence as a commercial center did not begin until 1885 with the opening of its first railway line. Further expansion of transport occurred in 1908, when direct railway communication was established with San Juan and Mendoza.

## City Amenities and Climate
As of an estimated population count in 1903, the city had 11,600 residents. The urban area is equipped with telephone service, electric lights, and street cars. The local climate is characterized by an average annual rainfall of 19 inches and a mean annual temperature of 60 degrees.
