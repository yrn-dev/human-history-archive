# Das Alte Testament im Lichte des Alten Orients

*Das Alte Testament im Lichte des Alten Orients* is a scholarly work authored by A. Jeremias. The text is cited across various academic contexts within the archive, including discussions on biblical geography, ancient religious apparel, and the narratives of Genesis.

## Geographical Interpretations
In the analysis of the biblical account regarding Balak, the king of Moab, and the prophet Balaam, A. Jeremias addresses the location of Pethor. While it has been common to interpret the "River" (*nahar*) mentioned in the text as the Euphrates—identifying Pethor with a *Pitru* found on a tributary of the Euphrates in an inscription of Shalmaneser II—Jeremias adopts a different perspective. He supports the view held by Marquart that the "River" (*nahar*) is actually the "Ravine" (*nahal*) of Egypt or Musri, located on the southern frontier of Judea.
