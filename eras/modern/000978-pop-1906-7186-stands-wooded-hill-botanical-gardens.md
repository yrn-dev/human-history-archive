# Avranches

## Geography and Layout
Avranches is a town located in north-western France, serving as the capital of an arrondissement within the department of Manche. It is situated 87 miles south of Cherbourg via the Western railway. The town is positioned on a wooded hill, and its botanical gardens provide a fine westward view of the rock of St Michel and the bay. At the base of this hill flows the river See, which is navigable from the sea during high tide. The town is encircled by avenues that were built upon the site of ancient ramparts, the remains of which can still be observed on the north side of the town.

## History and Administration
From the year 511 until 1790, Avranches served as a bishop's see. At the end of the 17th century, this see was held by the scholar Daniel Huet. The town's cathedral was once considered the finest in Normandy, but it was destroyed during the first French Revolution because it was deemed insecure. The site where the cathedral once stood is now an open square; a single stone remains there to mark the location where Henry II of England received absolution for the murder of Thomas Becket.

In terms of current administration, Avranches is the seat of a sub-prefect. It also houses a communal college and a tribunal of first instance.

## Architecture and Public Institutions
The town features several notable buildings and monuments. The churches of St Saturnin and Notre-Dame des Champs are modern structures built in the Gothic style. The ancient episcopal palace has been repurposed for use as a court of justice, and the hotel de ville houses a public library. In the public gardens, there is a statue dedicated to General Jean Marie Valhubert, who was killed at Austerlitz.

## Economy and Industry
The primary industry in Avranches is leather-dressing. Other industrial activities carried on in the town include dyeing, brewing, and steam-sawing. In the surrounding environs, horticulture flourishes. The town's trade is centered on the following goods:
* Flowers and fruit
* Butter
* Cattle
* Cider

Additionally, the area supports other fisheries as well as salmon fisheries.

## Demographics
