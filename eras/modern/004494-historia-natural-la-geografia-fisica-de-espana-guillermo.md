# Introduccion a la Historia Natural, y a la Geografia Fisica de Espana

The *Introduccion a la Historia Natural, y a la Geografia Fisica de Espana* is a work authored by D. Guillermo Bowles. Published in Madrid in 1775, the text contributes to the historical and geographical documentation of the Spanish territory.

## Content and Focus
The work is noted for containing excellent chapters specifically focused on the Basque Provinces. Within the broader context of literature concerning this region, it serves as a resource for those studying the natural history and physical geography of Spain during the late 18th century.

## Context within Basque Historiography
The *Introduccion a la Historia Natural, y a la Geografia Fisica de Espana* exists alongside a variety of other indispensable and valuable works regarding the Basque region. While Bowles' work provides geographical and natural history, other contemporary and later sources provide different specialized perspectives:

*   **Manners and Customs:** The *Corografia de Guipuzcoa* by R. P. M. de Larramendi, S.J., written around 1750, is considered indispensable for understanding the customs of the people.
*   **Civic Life:** *El Guipuzcoano instruido* (1780) utilizes a dictionary format to detail the obligations, duties, rights, and life of a Basque citizen.
*   **Local Information:** The *Diccionario Geografico-Historico de Espana* (1802), specifically the volumes covering the Kingdom of Navarra and the provinces of Alava, Guipuzcoa, and the Lordship of Vizcaya, provides extensive local data, though it is noted for a strong bias toward the central government.
*   **Legislation:** The *fueros* (local laws) are detailed in works such as the *Historia de la Legislacion... civil de Espana* by Cayetano Manrique and A. Marichalar (Marques de Montesa), as well as the *Fueros de Navarra Vizcaya, Guipuzcoa y Alava* (1868).

## Related Regional Studies
The study of the regions covered by Bowles has been further expanded by later historians. D. Carmelo de Echegaray, serving as the Cronista de las Provincias Vascongadas, along with F. Soraluce and D. Serapio Mugica, performed critical work in indexing and cataloguing municipal archives across all towns. This archival effort led to the discovery of important manuscripts and missing documents. 

Additionally, specific industrial developments in the region, such as the Basque mining industry, are detailed in specialized texts like *Las Minas de hierro de la provincia de Vizcaya*, which covers progress made between 1870 and 1899.
