# Credit Foncier de France

## Overview and Function
The *Credit Foncier de France* is a financial institution in France characterized by a very considerable capital. While it carries on the business of banking to some measure, it is distinguished from standard banks because its operations consist so largely of mortgages.

## The French Banking Landscape

The Bank of France maintains a steady rate of discount, aided by a large reserve and the fact that trade activity and enterprise among the French people are less intense than those found in Germany or the United Kingdom. ## Provincial Banking
Beyond these major joint-stock institutions, France maintains a large number of banks primarily located in the provinces. These firms are noted for conducting their business with discretion and great prudence. As of 1898, 182 of these firms were members of the French Country Bankers' Association. These banks operate in 66 of the 86 departments of France. Some of these provincial banks utilize a system of small branches similar to the English method; one such bank possesses 18 offices, including its head office, with branches situated in nearby small towns. The French Country Bankers' Association holds its meetings in Paris to discuss matters of interest to the banking profession.
