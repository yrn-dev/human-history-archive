# Louis George Oudard Feudrix de Bréquigny

Louis George Oudard Feudrix de Bréquigny was a scholar and administrator of historical documents who contributed extensively to the study of French history and international records.

## Contributions to French History
Bréquigny was involved in several significant scholarly projects concerning the history of France. He contributed three volumes to the *Table chronologique des diplômes, chartes, lettres, et actes imprimés concernant l'histoire de France* between 1769 and 1783, working in collaboration with Mouchet. Additionally, he authored the preface to volume xi of a work containing important researches into French communes.

He was also tasked with the supervision of a large collection of documents related to French history, a project described as being analogous to Rymer's *Foedera*. As part of this effort, he published the first volume, titled *Diplomatat. Chartae*, &c., in 1791.

## Work on Chinese History and the Revolution
In 1776, at the instance of the minister Bertin, Bréquigny began the collection of *Mémoires concernant l'histoire, les sciences, les lettres, et les arts des Chinois*. This project proceeded until fifteen volumes had appeared; however, the Revolution interrupted his work on this collection.
