# Biblical Repository, Nos. 36 and 38 (1852, 1853) and Neudecker

The *Bibliotheca Sacra and American Biblical Repository*, specifically issues Nos. 36 and 38 published in 1852 and 1853, served as the publication venue for a translation of the autobiography of K.G. Bretschneider. This translation was accompanied by notes and was prepared by Professor George E. Day. The original work by Bretschneider, titled *Aus meinem Leben: Selbstbiographie von K.G. Bretschneider*, was published in Gotha in 1851.
