# Hampshire from 1660 to 1675

## Political Representation and Governance
During the period between 1660 and 1675, political representation for Hampshire and its constituent areas was held by members of the Powlett (or Paulet) family. Charles Powlett, who would later become the 1st duke of Bolton, served as a member of parliament for the county of Hampshire from 1660 to 1675. Prior to his tenure representing the county, he had served as a member of parliament for Winchester. 

The administrative and social hierarchy of the region was closely tied to these parliamentary roles. Charles Powlett eventually succeeded his father as the 6th marquess of Winchester upon the latter's death in 1675.
