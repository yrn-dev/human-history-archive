### Maurice de Saxe
Maurice de Saxe, the subject of the 1891 work *Maurice de Saxe et le marquis d'Argenson*, was a famous marshal. He was born in October 1696 to Augustus the Strong, the elector of Saxony and king of Poland, and Maria Aurora, Countess of Königsmark. His mother, a member of a noble Swedish family, was the mistress of Augustus the Strong.
