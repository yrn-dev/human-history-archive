# Materialism and Related Philosophical Literature

The study of materialism in the 19th century is documented through several critical and historical texts. Among the primary references for this subject are the works of Frauenstädt and Janet. Frauenstädt published *Der Materialismus* in Leipzig in 1856. Additionally, Janet authored *The Materialism of the Present Day: A Criticism of Dr Büchner's System*, which was translated by Masson and published in London in 1867.

## Historical and Philosophical Contexts of Materialism

The broader history of materialistic thought, particularly in relation to ancient philosophy, is addressed in various scholarly works. F. A. Lange wrote *Geschichte des Materialismus*, which was translated into English by E. C. Thomas in 1877. 

Research into the atomistic philosophy of Democritus also intersects with the study of materialism. Key texts on this subject include:
* *Dissertation sur la philosophie atomistique* (1833) by P. Lafaist (Lafaye).
* *De Democrito philosopho* (1873), published in Paris by L. Liard.
* *Die Leucipp-Democritischen Atome* (1886) by H. C. Liepmann, published in Leipzig.
* *Zur Seelen- und Erkenntnislehre des Democritus* (1886) by G. Hart, published in Leipzig.
* *Die Ethika des Demokritos* (1893) by P. Natorp, published in Marburg.
* *Demokritstudien* (1899) by A. Dyroff, published in Leipzig.

Furthermore, general works on Greek philosophy provide context for these developments, such as C. A. Brandis' *Gesch. d. Entwickelungen d. griech. Philosophie* (Bonn, 1862-1864) and Ed. Zeller's *Pre-Socratic Philosophy* (London, 1881).
