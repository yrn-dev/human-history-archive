# Jacqueline and Napoleon Charles Gregoire

## Auguste Amelie Maximilienne Jacqueline
Auguste Amelie Maximilienne Jacqueline was a member of the Bonaparte family. She was the daughter of Charles Lucien Jules Laurent, the prince of Canino, and his cousin Zenaide Bonaparte, who was the daughter of Joseph. Jacqueline married Count Gabrielli.

## Napoleon Charles Gregoire Jacques Philippe
Napoleon Charles Gregoire Jacques Philippe was born in 1839. He was the son of Charles Lucien Jules Laurent, prince of Canino, and Zenaide Bonaparte. He married the princess Ruspoli, with whom he had two daughters.

## Family Background: Charles Lucien Jules Laurent
The father of both Jacqueline and Napoleon Charles Gregoire, Charles Lucien Jules Laurent (1803–1857), was the prince of Canino and the son of Lucien Bonaparte. Rather than pursuing politics, he was primarily a scientist. Between 1825 and 1833, he published a four-volume work titled *American Ornithology* in Philadelphia, which established his scientific reputation. He authored several other zoological works, including:
* *Iconographia della fauna Italica* (1832–1841)
* *Catalogo metodico degli uccelli europei* (1842)
* *Catalogo metodico dei pesci europei* (1845)
* *Catalogo metodico dei mammiferi europei* (1845)
* *Telachorum tabula analytica* (1838)

His later scientific contributions included *Conspectus systematis ornithologiae, mastozologiae, erpetologiae et amphibologiae, Ichthyologiae* (1850), *Tableau des oiseaux-mouches* (1854), and *Ornithologie fossile* (1858).

Due to his scientific achievements, he was elected as a correspondent of the Institute of France in 1844 and an honorary member of the academies of Upsala (1833) and Berlin (1843).

## Political Activity of the Prince of Canino
Around 1847, Charles Lucien Jules Laurent became involved in Italian political agitation. He presided over scientific congresses, most notably in Venice, where he expressed support for the expulsion of the Austrians and the independence of Italy. In 1848, he joined the Junto of Rome and was elected as a deputy to the national assembly by Viterbo. Following the failure of the revolution, he was forced to leave Italy in July 1849, traveling first to Holland and then to France, where he returned to his scientific pursuits.

## Siblings of Jacqueline and Napoleon Charles Gregoire
Jacqueline and Napoleon Charles Gregoire were among eight children who survived their father. Their siblings included:
* Joseph Lucien Charles Napoleon, prince of Canino (1824–1865), who died without heirs.
* Lucien Louis Joseph Napoleon (born 1828), who entered holy orders in 1853 and became a cardinal in 1868.
* Julie Charlotte Zenaide Pauline Laetitia Desiree Bartholomee, who married the marquis of Roccagiovine.
* Charlotte Honorine Josephine, who married Count Primoli.
* Marie Desiree Eugenie Josephine Philomene, who married the count Campello.
