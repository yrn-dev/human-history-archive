# Bolivia: Historical and Geographical Bibliography

The study of Bolivia's geography, industry, and history is supported by a diverse range of authorities, including statistical summaries, geographical dictionaries, and travel narratives produced between the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

## Industrial and Statistical Records
Significant data regarding the republic's industry and demographics are found in the works of M.V. Ballivian. His contributions include *Apuntes sobre la industria de goma elastica, &c.* (1896), which focuses on the elastic gum industry, and the *Noticia Politica, Geografica, Industrial, y Estadistica de Bolivia* (1900). Ballivian also authored *Breves Indicaciones para el Inmigrante y el Viajero a Bolivia* (1898) and collaborated with Eduardo Idiaquez on the *Diccionario Geografico de la Republica de Bolivia* (1900). 

Further industrial insights are provided in the three-part *Monografias de la Industria Minera en Bolivia* (1899-1900). Official government and institutional records also contribute to the archive, such as the *Relaciones Geograficas de Bolivia* from the Archivo de la Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion (1898), the *Boletin de la Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion, Estadistica y Propaganda Geografica*, and the three-volume *Sinopsis estadistica y geografica de la Republica de Bolivia* (1902-1904). Additionally, the International Bureau of the American Republics published *Bolivia, Geographical Sketch, Natural Resources, &c.* in 1904.

## Geographical and Exploratory Works
The geography of Bolivia has been documented through various regional studies and explorations. J.L. Moreno published *Nociones de geografia de Bolivia* in 1889, and M. Dalence produced the *Bosquejo estadistico de Bolivia* in 1878. 

Exploratory accounts provide detailed observations of the terrain and waterways. Enrique Bolland documented explorations in the Laguna Gaiba and the Alto Paraguay in his 1901 work. G.E. Church wrote about routes to Bolivia via the Rio de la Plata (1902) and the River Amazon (1877). Other notable travel and exploration accounts include:
* Andre Bresson's *Sept annees d'explorations, de voyages et de sejours dans l'Amerique australe* (1886).
* Sir W.M. Conway's *Climbing and Exploration in the Bolivian Andes* (1903).
* Edward D. Mathews' *Up the Amazon and Madeira Rivers, through Bolivia and Peru* (1879).
* A. Petrocokino's *Along the Andes, in Bolivia, Peru, and Ecuador* (1903).
* Comte C. d'Ursel's *Sud Amerique: Sejours et voyages au Bresil, en Bolivie, &c.* (1879).
* G. de Crequi-Montfort's "Exploration en Bolivie" in *La Geographie*.

## Historical and Cultural Perspectives
Historical and cultural analyses of the region are represented by works such as Carlos Matzenauer's *Bolivia in historischer, geographischer und cultureller Hinsicht*, published in Vienna in 1897. The intersection of regional conflict and history is captured in M.F. Soldan's *Narracion de Guerra de Chile contra Peru y Bolivia* (1884). General South American geographical and commercial contexts are provided by C.B. Cisneros and R.E. Garcia in *Geografia Comercial de la America del Sur* (1898), as well as Charles Wiener's *Perou et Bolivie* (1880) and C.M. Pepper's *Panama to Patagonia* (1906).
