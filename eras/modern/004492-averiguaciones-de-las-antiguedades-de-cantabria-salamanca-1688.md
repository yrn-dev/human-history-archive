# Averiguaciones de las Antiguedades de Cantabria

## Overview
*Averiguaciones de las Antiguedades de Cantabria* is a historical work authored by R. P. Henao. Published in Salamanca in 1688, the text is categorized among older works on its subject matter. While it is described as often uncritical, it is still considered valuable.

## Publication History
The work was first released in 1688 in the city of Salamanca. A subsequent new edition of the text was published in 1894.
