# Testament politique de M. de Vauban

The *Testament politique de M. de Vauban* is a work that appeared in 1712. According to archival records, this text is simply the *Detail de la France* written by Boisguilbert.

## Attribution and Related Works
The *Testament politique de M. de Vauban* is identified as the work of Boisguilbert. In related bibliographic matters, the text *Dime royale* was previously attributed to Vauban in error. The collected works of Boisguilbert were compiled by Daire and can be found in the first volume of the *Collection des grands economistes*. Additionally, the correspondence of the *controleurs generaux* (vol. i.), published by M. de Boislisle, contains letters from Boisguilbert.

## Context of Vauban
While the *Testament politique* is attributed to Boisguilbert, Vauban himself was a figure of significant authority in military engineering. His influence was such that members of the *corps du genie* maintained an unquestioning reverence for his authority, and his system of fortification was the standard for the corps. 

Vauban's practical expertise was demonstrated during the campaign of 1677 in the Netherlands. During the siege of Valenciennes, King Louis followed Vauban's plans implicitly, even when those plans involved ordering an assault *de vive force* against the unanimous opinion of five marshals of France. This action succeeded beyond Vauban's own expectations, as picked troops forced their way into the town on March 17th.

The legacy of Vauban's engineering is visible in various structures, such as Forts Moselle and Bellecroix, which remain in existence. In Longwy, the upper portion of the town is situated within a Vauban fort, which features a memorial to him and a system of galleries and casemates in the counterscarp.
