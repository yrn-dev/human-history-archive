## Academic and Literary Publications
Several significant works were published or revised in 1920:

*   **The Outline of History**: Written by H. G. Wells with editorial assistance from Professor Gilbert Murray, Sir E. Ray Lankester, Sir H. H. Johnston, and Mr. Ernest Barker, and illustrated by J. F. Horrabin. Volume I and Volume II were published by The Macmillan Company in New York in November 1920. The work includes a time-chart covering A.D. 1220 to A.D. 1920 and discusses the "state of men's minds in 1920."
*   **A Short History of Belgium**: Written by Léon van der Essen, a Professor of History at the University of Louvain. The second edition of this work, which included a special chapter on Belgium during the Great War, was published by The University of Chicago Press in January 1920, with a second impression following in July 1920.
