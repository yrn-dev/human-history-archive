# ASBURY PARK

## Geography and Location
Asbury Park is a city located in Monmouth County, New Jersey, U.S.A. It is situated on the Atlantic Ocean, approximately 35 miles south of New York City, or 50 miles when traveling by rail. The city's boundaries to the north and south are formed by fresh-water lakes, including Deal Lake, which extends into the wooded country.

## History and Governance
Founded in 1869, the city was named in honor of the Rev. Francis Asbury. Its administrative evolution progressed from its founding to being incorporated as a borough in 1874, and subsequently being chartered as a city in 1897. In 1906, the city expanded its borders by annexing territory to the west, which had an estimated population of 6,000.

The municipality maintains control over its own water-works, obtaining its water supply from artesian wells. Additionally, the municipality owns and controls the beach.

## Tourism and Infrastructure
Asbury Park is recognized as one of the most popular seaside resorts on the Atlantic coast. The city features an excellent beach with a boardwalk extending approximately 1 mile in length. Due to its numerous cottages and hotels, the city accommodates a summer population that reaches approximately 50,000, as well as a large population of transients during the winter and autumn months.

The city is accessible via several transportation networks:
* **Railways:** It is served by the Pennsylvania and Central of New Jersey railways.
* **Electric Railways:** Electric lines connect Asbury Park with other New Jersey coast resorts located both to the south and north.
