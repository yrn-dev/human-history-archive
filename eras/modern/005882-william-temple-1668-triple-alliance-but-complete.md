# Sir William Temple and the Triple Alliance

## Diplomatic Role and the Triple Alliance
Sir William Temple served as a diplomat in Holland. During his tenure, he was associated with the policy of the Triple Alliance. By 1671, Temple was replaced in his post by Downing. The objective of Downing's appointment was to incite another war between England and Holland in alignment with French policy and to break up the policy of the Triple Alliance. However, Downing's efforts were unsuccessful due to extreme unpopularity in Holland, which forced him to flee to England after three months of residence.
