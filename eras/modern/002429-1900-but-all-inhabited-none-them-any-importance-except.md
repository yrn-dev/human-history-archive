# Global Demographic and Economic Data (1900)

The turn of the 20th century provides a diverse snapshot of population distributions, regional economies, and trade patterns across various continents, from the archipelagos of the Mediterranean to the plains of Russia and the districts of India.

## European Populations and Regions

In Spain, the Balearic Islands—an archipelago consisting of four large and eleven small islands—had a population of 311,649 in 1900. The archipelago is divided into the eastern group (Majorca, Minorca, and seven islets) and the western group (Iviza, Formentera, and four islets). While the eleven islets were all inhabited in 1900, their combined population was only 171. Among these, Cabrera is noted for its caverns and its former use as a place of banishment; in 1808, Spanish captors landed a large body of Frenchmen there, who nearly perished from starvation.

In western Spain, the province of Cáceres, formed in 1833, recorded a population of 362,164 in 1900. As one of the most thinly peopled Spanish provinces, its population had been steadily increasing. By 1900, its flocks and herds exceeded 1,000,000 head, with the province ranking second only to Badajoz in stock-breeding.

In Hungary, the Banat district—comprising the counties of Krasso-Szoreny, Temes, and Torontal—had a population of 1,431,329 in 1900. The population was divided by nationality into 578,789 Rumanians, 362,487 Germans, 251,938 Servians, and 170,124 Magyars. The chief town of the region was Temesvar, with a population of 53,033.

In Portugal, the region of Beira recorded several town populations in 1900, including Coimbra (18,144), Covilha (15,469), Ilhavo (12,617), Ovar (10,462), Lamego (9,471), Murtosa (9,737), Vizeu (8,057), Castello Branco (7,288), Figueira da Foz (6,221), and Guarda (6,124).

## Russian Urban Centers

In southern Russia, the town of Lutsk, located in the government of Volhynia on the Styr, had a population of 17,701 in 1900. The town's inhabitants, many of whom were Israelites, primarily earned their living by shipping goods on the Styr.

Further south, the Russian seaport of Mariupol, situated on the north shore of the Sea of Azov, had a population of 52,770 in 1900, a figure that included the inhabitants of the Kara-su and Mariinsk suburbs. The town was established in 1779 by Greek emigrants from the Crimea.

## North American Demographics

## International Trade and Agriculture

The United Kingdom's dairy imports showed significant growth between 1891 and 1900. Total butter imports rose from 102,500 tons in 1891 to 170,700 tons by the year ending June 30, 1900. While foreign imports decreased by over 8,000 tons in 1900, colonial supplies increased by 15,000 tons over the previous year. Over the decade, Denmark showed the greatest development in foreign supply, followed by Holland and Russia. Conversely, Sweden's exports to the UK declined, sending 1,400 tons less in 1900 than it had in 1891.

## Regional Hardships and Environmental Factors

In India, the region of Khandesh experienced severe famine during 1899-1900, following a less severe famine in 1896-1897. The region is characterized by the Tapti river and is inhabited in the rugged north and west by the Bhils, an aboriginal tribe numbering 167,000.
