## Political and National Suppressions
In the lands ruled by the house of Habsburg, the national aspirations of Bohemia were long-suppressed. Following the re-establishment of European peace in 1815, these aspirations began to revive, initially through Bohemian literature. Political action was rendered impossible by the absolutist and arbitrary government of Prince Metternich. By 1845, the estates of Bohemia began opposing the government of Vienna, asserting their right to vote on taxes based on the constitution of 1627. By 1847, they sought to introduce the Bohemian language into higher schools and increase town representation at the diet, though the revolutionary outbreak of 1848 prevented the meeting of the estates.

## Medical Suppression
In medical terms, the suppression of urine is categorized into two forms. Obstructive suppression occurs when a morbid process or stone blocks the ureter. Non-obstructive suppression typically occurs during advanced kidney diseases. Additionally, complete suppression of urine can result from injuries to distant parts of the body, such as following severe surgical operations.
