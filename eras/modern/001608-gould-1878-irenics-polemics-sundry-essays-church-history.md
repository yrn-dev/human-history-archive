## Author Biography
Leonard Woolsey Bacon (1830–1907) was the son of Leonard Bacon. He graduated from Yale in 1850 and served as a pastor for various Presbyterian and Congregational churches. Throughout his career, he published several works focused on church history and ecclesiastical matters.
