# Le Requisitionnaire, Les Proscrits, and Le Chef-d'oeuvre inconnu

## Overview and Publication
*Le Requisitionnaire*, *Les Proscrits*, and *Le Chef-d'oeuvre inconnu* are literary works published in 1831. They appeared alongside other titles, including *Maitre Cornelius* and *Jesus Christ en Flandre*. These works were released in the same year that *La Peau de chagrin* was published in its complete form.

## Critical Reception of Le Chef-d'oeuvre inconnu
Among these titles, *Le Chef-d'oeuvre inconnu* is specifically noted as a masterpiece that was fortunately not unrecognized.
