# Automatic Writing

Automatic writing is a phenomenon that often displays indications of telepathy and provides a means of communicating with subconscious strata of the personality. It is characterized by the production of text that may diverge markedly from the normal consciousness of the individual.

## Characteristics and Manifestations
For the most part, automatic writing is believed to bring to the surface half-formed impressions and the debris of lapsed memories that have never reached the focus of consciousness, described as the "stuff that dreams are made of." However, some spontaneous instances produce more complex outputs, including puns, nonsense verses, anagrams, and occasional obscenities or blasphemies.

The physical act of automatic writing can vary significantly from standard writing:
* **Handwriting:** While the writing generally resembles the agent's ordinary handwriting, there are sometimes marked differences. A single automatist may employ two or three distinct handwritings, and in some cases, imitations of the handwriting of other people—whether living or dead—are produced.
* **Mirror Writing:** Writing is not infrequently reversed (*Spiegelschrift*), requiring a looking-glass to be read. This ability is often associated with a liability to spontaneous somnambulism.
* **Physical Sensation:** During the act of writing, the arm and hand are often insensible. Some recorded cases suggest the automatist guides the hand through a special extension of the muscular sense rather than by sight.

## Psychological and Medical Applications
The ability to communicate with subconscious strata of the personality has been utilized by Pierre Janet and others to treat cases of dissociation of consciousness and hystero-epilepsy. In instances where a patient experiencing hysterical convulsions cannot respond to oral appeals, they can sometimes be induced to answer questions addressed to the hand in writing. This method is used to accept therapeutic suggestions or to reveal the secret of the malady.

## Notable Cases and Interpretations
Various researchers and mediums have documented the effects of automatic writing. The American medium Mrs. Piper, while in a state of trance, produced a remarkable series of automatic writings. These are thought by some to indicate communication with the spirits of the dead and appear to exhibit remarkable telepathic powers.

Another well-known case was recorded by Th. Flournoy in *Des Indes a la planete Mars*, in which an automatist produced writing in an unknown character that claimed to be the Martian language.
