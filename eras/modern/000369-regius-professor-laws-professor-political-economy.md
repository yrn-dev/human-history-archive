# Regius Professor of Laws and Professor of Political Economy in the University of Dublin

## Charles Francis Bastable
Charles Francis Bastable, M.A., LL.D., served as both the Regius Professor of Laws and the Professor of Political Economy in the University of Dublin. He is recognized as an author of several academic works, including *Public Finance*, *Commerce of Nations*, and *Theory of International Trade*.
