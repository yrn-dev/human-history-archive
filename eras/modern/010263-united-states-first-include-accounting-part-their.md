# Accounting in University Curricula

## Academic Integration in the United States
In the field of education, the scientific study of book-keeping is primarily the domain of educational institutions, as there are no other notable organizations devoted to this study aside from professional accountants. Among the various universities globally, those located in the United States were the first to integrate accounting as a part of their curriculum.

## International Developments in Accounting Education
Following the lead of United States universities, other nations have begun to treat the subject with similar academic rigor. In Great Britain, the subject has been treated seriously and upon adequate lines by a limited number of institutions, specifically the University of Birmingham, the Victoria University of Manchester, and the London School of Economics (University of London). More recently, Japan has also initiated a movement toward including accounting in its educational systems, a trend that is expected to be followed by other countries.

## Vocational and Professional Training in England
Outside of the university system in England, several bodies have provided certifications in book-keeping for a number of years. These include Owens College, Manchester, the London Chamber of Commerce, and the Society of Arts, all of which grant diplomas to candidates who successfully pass their examinations. Additionally, instruction in book-keeping is provided by most technical schools and polytechnics; however, these institutions generally view the subject as merely a "craft" rather than a scientific academic study.
