# BADENWEILER

Badenweiler is a watering place and health resort located in Germany within the grand-duchy of Baden. It is situated at the western edge of the Black Forest, 28 miles north by east of Basel by rail.

## Geography and Climate
The resort is sheltered by the Blauen, which reaches an elevation of 3,820 feet. The area is characterized by picturesque surroundings and an excellent, equable climate.

## Health and Tourism
Badenweiler attracts 5000 visitors annually. These visitors come to the resort for several reasons:
*   **Mineral Springs:** The location features warm mineral springs with a temperature of 70 degrees Fahrenheit.
*   **Whey Cure:** The resort is visited for its whey cure.
*   **Environment:** The equable climate and the scenery of the area are also primary draws for visitors.

## Landmarks and Architecture
The resort contains several notable structures and sites:
*   **The Kurhaus:** A building constructed in 1853.
*   **Grand-Ducal Castle:** A castle that underwent refitting between 1887 and 1888.
*   **Parish Church:** An Evangelical church built in 1897. It is located at the foot of an 11th-century castle that formerly belonged to the margraves of Baden; this older castle was destroyed by the French during the wars of Louis XV.
*   **Park:** The resort features a park covering 15 acres.
*   **Roman Baths:** Well-preserved Roman baths were discovered at the site in 1784.

## Population
The permanent population of Badenweiler is approximately 600.
