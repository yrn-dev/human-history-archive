# Keeper of the National Museum of Antiquities, Edinburgh, and Assistant

## Joseph Anderson
Joseph Anderson, LL.D., serves as the Keeper of the National Museum of Antiquities in Edinburgh. In addition to his role at the museum, he holds the position of Assistant Secretary of the Society of Antiquaries of Scotland. He is also an Honorary Professor of Antiquities to the Royal Scottish Academy. Anderson is the author of the work titled *Scotland in Early Christian and Pagan Times*.

## Related Museum Roles and Assistants
While Joseph Anderson manages the National Museum of Antiquities in Edinburgh, other institutions maintain similar roles of keepers and assistants. At the British Museum, various specialists hold these titles across different departments:

*   **Egyptian and Assyrian Antiquities:** Leonard William King, M.A., F.S.A., serves as the Assistant to the Keeper of Egyptian and Assyrian Antiquities. King is also a Lecturer in Assyrian at King's College, London, and has conducted excavations for the British Museum at Kuyunjik (Nineveh). His published works include *Assyrian Chrestomathy*, *Annals of the Kings of Assyria*, *Studies in Eastern History*, and *Babylonian Magic and Sorcery*.
*   **Manuscripts:** Sir Edward Augustus Bond (1815–1898) rose through the ranks of the British Museum's manuscript department. After starting as an assistant in 1838, he became the Egerton librarian in 1852, assistant keeper of MSS. in 1856, and keeper in 1867. He eventually became the principal librarian in 1878. Bond is credited with reorganizing the department, creating a classified catalogue of MSS., and founding the Palaeographical Society alongside Sir E. Maunde Thompson.
*   **Printed Books:** Alfred William Pollard, M.A., serves as the Assistant Keeper of Printed Books at the British Museum. He is a Fellow of King's College, London, the Hon. Secretary of the Bibliographical Society, and the Chief Editor of the "Globe" *Chaucer*.
*   **Natural History and Geology:** Karl Dietrich Eberhard Koenig (1774–1851), a German palaeontologist, served as assistant keeper in 1807 before being appointed keeper of the department of natural history in 1813. He later oversaw geology and mineralogy.

## Other Notable Keepers and Curators
The archive records several other individuals who held keeper or curator positions in various national and royal collections:

*   **Royal Collection of Medals:** Jean Jacques Barthelemy (1716–1795), a French numismatist, became the assistant to M. Gros de Boze, the keeper of the royal collection of medals. Upon de Boze's death in 1753, Barthelemy became the keeper of the medals. Although he was nominated as librarian of the Bibliotheque Nationale in 1793, he refused the post to resume his functions as keeper of medals.
*   **British Museum (Manuscripts):** Francis Douce (1757–1834), an English antiquary, held the post of keeper of manuscripts in the British Museum for a period, though he was forced to resign following a quarrel with a trustee.
*   **Royal College of Surgeons, Edinburgh:** William Macgillivray (1796–1852), a Scottish naturalist, was appointed curator of the museum of the Royal College of Surgeons in Edinburgh in 1831, a position he held until 1841.
