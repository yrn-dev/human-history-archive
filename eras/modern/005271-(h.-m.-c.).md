# H. M. C.

The designation "H. M. C." appears in the archive's records primarily as an abbreviation for official appointments within the British administrative and diplomatic service.

## Official Appointments
In the context of frontier and boundary administration, the abbreviation "H. M. Commissioner" is used. Sir Thomas Hungerford Holdich, a Colonel in the Royal Engineers and a Gold Medallist of the R.G.S. (London), 1887, served as the H. M. Commissioner for the Persa-Beluch Boundary in 1896. Sir Thomas Hungerford Holdich also served as the Superintendent of Frontier Surveys in India from 1892 to 1898 and authored works including *The Gates of India* and *The Indian Borderland*.
