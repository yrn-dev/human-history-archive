# Bachgesellschaft

The *Bachgesellschaft* refers to a body associated with the publication of the works of Johann Sebastian Bach. Its outputs have served as a basis for authoritative musical series and have been the subject of critical evaluation regarding editorial accuracy.

## Publications and Editorial Standards
The *Bachgesellschaft* is associated with a complete series of publications issued by Breitkopf and Haertel. While this series has been accepted as authoritative, some critics have noted deficiencies in its editorial approach. Specifically, it has been remarked that the publications of the *Neue Bachgesellschaft* fail to distinguish the original text of Bach from the personal taste of the editor. 

In comparison to the *Bachgesellschaft* series, older vocal scores of cantatas found in the *Edition Peters* are considered far better, despite the fact that they represent only a selection of works rather than a complete series.

## The Neue Bachgesellschaft
The *Neue Bachgesellschaft* is a society established with the specific objective of making the completed results of the first *Bachgesellschaft* more generally accessible. To achieve this goal, the society engages in two primary activities:
* The organization and holding of frequent Bach festivals.
* The issuance of practical and inexpensive editions of the music.

The activities of the *Neue Bachgesellschaft*, combined with a movement aimed at restoring the vocal music of Bach to its proper place within the Lutheran Church, are viewed as having a salutary effect on the future of music.

## Scholarly Contributions and Prefaces
The works associated with the *Bachgesellschaft* (abbreviated in some records as *B.-G.*) have been utilized in scholarly debates and biographical corrections. For instance, the prefaces of the *B. G.* provided a basis for corrections to P. Spitta's biography of Bach. 

Additionally, the prefaces of the *Bachgesellschaft* volumes have been the site of academic disagreement. One example involves the editor of the Four Short Masses, who offered conjectures regarding the "displacing" of structure in the *kyrie* of the G minor Mass (found in the preface of *Jahr. viii.*), a point to which Rust provided an answer in the preface to *Jahr. xxiii.*
