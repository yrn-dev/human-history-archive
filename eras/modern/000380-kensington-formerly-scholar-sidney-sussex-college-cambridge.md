# South Kensington

South Kensington is a location associated with several prominent academic and scientific institutions, including the Natural History Museum and the City and Guilds of London Institute Central Technical College.

## Scientific and Academic Institutions

The Natural History Museum in South Kensington houses the Department of Mineralogy. Leonard James Spencer, M.A., F.G.S., serves as an Assistant within this department. Spencer, who is also the Editor of the *Mineralogical Magazine*, was previously a Harkness Scholar and a Scholar of Sidney Sussex College, Cambridge. His contributions to the archive include work on various minerals, such as autunite, axinite, azurite, barytes, bauxite, biotite, manganite, and marcasite.

Additionally, the City and Guilds of London Institute Central Technical College is located in South Kensington. William Ernest Dalby, M.A., M.Inst.C.E., M.I.M.E., serves as the Professor of Civil and Mechanical Engineering at this institution. Dalby is an Associate Member of the Institute of Naval Architects and has authored works including *The Balancing of Engines* and *Valves and Valve Gear Mechanisms*. He previously served as a University Demonstrator in the Engineering Department at Cambridge.

## Other Notable Figures in South Kensington

The Victoria and Albert Museum, also situated in South Kensington, employs Edward Fairbrother Strange as an Assistant-Keeper. Strange is a Member of the Council of the Japan Society and has authored numerous works on art subjects. He is also a joint-editor of Bell's "Cathedral" Series and has contributed information regarding Aubrey Vincent Beardsley.
