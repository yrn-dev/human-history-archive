# Bukovina

## Geography and Boundaries
Bukovina is a crownland and duchy of Austria. It is bounded to the north by Galicia, to the west by Hungary and Transylvania, to the south by Rumania, and to the east by Rumania and Russia. The total area of the region is 4035 sq. m. 

The topography of the country is characterized by the offshoots of the Carpathians, which are particularly prominent in the southern parts of the territory. Within these mountains, the Giumaleu reaches an altitude of 6100 ft. The Borgo Pass and the Radna Pass serve as the principal passes of the region.

## Hydrography and Climate
With the exception of the Dniester river, which runs along the northern border, Bukovina is part of the Danube watershed. The primary rivers in the region include the Pruth and the Sereth; the latter is joined by several affluents, specifically the Bistritza, the Moldava, and the Suczawa.

The climate is described as healthy but severe, particularly during the winter months. However, it is generally milder than the climate found in Galicia. In Czernowitz, the mean annual temperature is 46.9° F.

## Land Use and Agriculture
The name of the country is derived from the abundance of beech trees, and woodland occupies 43.17% of the total area. The remaining land is distributed as follows:
* Arable land: 27.59%
* Meadows: 12.68%
* Pastures: 10.09%
* Gardens: 0.78%

The soil is fertile, leading to significant progress in agriculture. The primary agricultural products include barley, oats, rye, wheat, maize, hemp, flax, and potatoes. Additionally, cattle-rearing is an important source of revenue.

## Industry and Commerce
The chief industries in Bukovina are milling, distilling, and brewing. The government operates a salt mine at Kaczyka, as salt is the principal mineral of the region. Commerce is primarily conducted by Armenians and Jews and is mostly limited to raw products, including wood, cattle, wool, and agricultural produce.

## Population and Demographics
In 1900, Bukovina had a population of 729,921, which represents a population density of 181 inhabitants per sq. m. The population is divided by nationality as follows:
* Ruthenians: Over 40%
* Rumanians: 35%
* Jews: 13%
* Others: Germans, Russians, Poles, Armenians, and Hungarians.

The official language used for instruction in the university, the law-courts, and the administration is German.

## Religion
The majority of the population (nearly 70%) belongs to the Greek Orthodox Church and falls under the ecclesiastical jurisdiction of the metropolitan or archbishop of Czernowitz. Other religious affiliations include the Roman Catholic Church (11%), the Greek United Church (3.25%), and Protestants (2.5%).
