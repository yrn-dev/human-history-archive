# Kirby Wiske

## Overview and Location
Kirby Wiske is a village situated in the North Riding of Yorkshire, England. It is located near the town of Northallerton.

## Notable Residents
The village is the birthplace of Roger Ascham, an English writer and scholar who was born there around the year 1515. Ascham was the third son of John Ascham, who served as the steward to Lord Scrope of Bolton. While the family name of Roger Ascham's mother, Margaret, is unknown, it is reported that she was well connected. 

Much of the available information regarding Ascham's early life is attributed to Edward Grant, the headmaster of Westminster. Grant edited Ascham's letters and delivered a panegyrical oration concerning his life in 1576. It has been noted that the name Ascham would more accurately be spelled Askham, as it is likely derived from Askham in the West Riding.

The North Riding, along with the East Riding, is a site for the extensive breeding of the Yorkshire Coach-horse. This breed is characterized by a fine head, sloping shoulders, strong loins, lengthy quarters, sound feet, and flat bone. They typically stand between 16 and 16 hands 2 inches in height and are usually bay in color with brown or black points.
