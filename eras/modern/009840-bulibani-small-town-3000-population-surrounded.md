# Bulibani

## Overview and Location
Bulibani is a small town located within Bondu, a French protectorate in West Africa that is dependent on the colony of Senegal. The broader region of Bondu is situated between 13 and 15 degrees North and 12 and 13 degrees West, positioned between the upper course of the Gambia and the Faleme river.

## Geography and Environment
The territory surrounding Bulibani consists of an elevated plateau. The central and southern portions of this plateau are characterized by hills that are generally unproductive and covered with stunted wood. In contrast, the lower regions of the country are fertile and feature various valuable fruit-trees, as well as the tamarind and the baobab. The area is traversed by torrents known as *marigots*, which flow rapidly during the rainy season but remain empty during the dry season.

## Town Characteristics and Administration
Bulibani serves as the royal residence of the reigning prince of Bondu. The residence was moved to Bulibani from its previous location at Fatteconda prior to 1818, as noted during the visit of Major W. Gray, a British officer who attempted to solve the Niger problem. The town has a population of approximately 3,000 inhabitants and is enclosed by a strong clay wall.

## Regional Demographics and Culture
The inhabitants of the region are predominantly Fula, although the Mandingos largely control the trade. The laws and religion of the country are Mahommedan, though the precepts of the faith are not observed with great rigor.

## Political History
The region has seen various European contacts and political shifts:
*   **1795:** Mungo Park, the first European traveller to visit the country, passed through Bondu and was subjected to many exactions by the reigning prince.
*   **1845:** In August of this year, the king of Bondu signed a treaty that recognized French sovereignty over the country.
*   **1858:** Although the natives initially disregarded the 1845 treaty, Bondu came definitely under French control in 1858.

Following the establishment of definitive French control, the country experienced considerable prosperity.
