# BALLARAT and BALLARAT EAST

Ballarat (also spelled Ballaarat) and Ballarat East are a city and a town, respectively, located in Grenville county, Victoria, Australia. Situated 74 miles west-north-west of Melbourne by rail, the two municipalities are distinct entities separated only by the Yarrowee Creek. As of 1901, the population of Ballarat was 25,448, while Ballarat East had a population of 18,262.

## History and Development
The existence of these settlements is the result of heavy immigration that followed the discovery of gold-fields in 1851. Ballarat has since become the chief gold-mining centre of the state and is the second largest city in Victoria. Initially, the area featured alluvial gold-fields, which were the richest ever opened up. Following the exhaustion of these deposits, mining shifted to quartz reefs at deep levels, with several mines currently operating at depths exceeding 2,000 feet.

The early history of the town was marked by civil unrest. In 1854, miners engaged in armed conflict with authorities in resistance to an arbitrary tax. This led to the appointment of a commission to investigate the grievances of the miners, and a charter was subsequently granted to the town in 1855. Ballarat was later raised to the rank of a city in 1870.

## Economy and Industry
While the district is primarily devoted to mining, the land is well adapted for sheep-farming, producing some of the finest wool in the world. The city also serves as an important railway centre. Its diverse industrial base includes:
* Distilling
* Brewing
* Flour-milling
* Iron-founding
* Woollen-milling

## Education and Public Institutions
Ballarat is the seat of Roman Catholic and Anglican bishops. The city is home to a number of admirable public buildings and the school of mines, which is affiliated with the university of Melbourne and is considered the most important school of mines in Australia.

The city features several recreation grounds and parks, most notably a botanical garden covering 750 acres. Within this garden is Lake Wendouree, where pisciculture is carried out with great success.

## Geography and Climate
Ballarat is situated at an elevation of 1,438 feet. Due to this height, the city possesses a healthy and exceptionally cool climate.
