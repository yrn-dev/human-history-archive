# University of Manchester

The University of Manchester is a British educational institution that provides instruction across a diverse range of academic disciplines, including accounting, law, engineering, and languages.

## Study of Book-keeping and Accounting
In Great Britain, the Victoria University of Manchester is noted as one of the few universities to have treated the subject of book-keeping seriously and upon adequate lines, alongside the university of Birmingham and the London School of Economics (university of London). While various other bodies in England—such as Owens College, Manchester, the London Chamber of Commerce, and the Society of Arts—grant diplomas and hold examinations in book-keeping, and many technical schools and polytechnics provide instruction, the latter typically view the subject merely as a "craft." In contrast, the scientific study of book-keeping is primarily the domain of purely educational institutions, with universities in the United States being the first to include accounting in their curricula.

## Legal Education
The University of Manchester is one of the institutions where law is taught, although it is noted that this is not done on a very large scale. This contrasts with the universities of Oxford and Cambridge, which have attempted to promote the study of law since 1850 through a curriculum covering International, Constitutional, Roman, and Jurisprudence, as well as Criminal law, Procedure, Evidence, Tort, and the Law of Contract and Real and Personal Property. Other institutions providing legal education include the university of Liverpool and London University, the latter of which encourages the study through law degree examinations and teaching at King's College and University College, London.

## Engineering and Technical Instruction
The university's academic reach extends to technical and engineering fields. William Charles Popplewell, M.Sc., A.M.I.C.E., serves as a Lecturer in Engineering at the Manchester School of Technology, which is part of the University of Manchester. Popplewell is the author of works including *Heat Engines* and *Compressed Air*.

## Language and Humanities
The University of Manchester provides specialized instruction in foreign languages. Edgar Prestage serves as a Special Lecturer in Portuguese Literature at the university and also acts as an examiner in Portuguese for the University of Manchester and the University of London. Prestage is the editor of *Azurara's Chronicle of Guinea* and *Letters of a Portuguese Nun*.
