## Regional Economic and Industrial Data
### Bellaire, Ohio
Settled around 1795 and chartered as a city in 1874, Bellaire is a shipping center for the Belmont county coalfield. In 1907, this coalfield produced 19.3% of the total coal output for the state. The city's population grew from 9,934 in 1890 to 12,946 by 1910. Between 1900 and 1905, the value of factory products in the city increased by 21.2%, rising from $8,837,646 to $10,712,438.
