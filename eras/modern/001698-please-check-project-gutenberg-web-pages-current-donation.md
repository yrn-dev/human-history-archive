# Project Gutenberg

## Overview and Origins
Project Gutenberg is a library of electronic works designed to be shared freely with any individual. The concept for this library was originated by Professor Michael S. Hart. For a period of forty years, Professor Hart was responsible for the production and distribution of Project Gutenberg eBooks, operating with the assistance of a loose network of volunteers.

## eBook Production and Copyright
The eBooks provided by Project Gutenberg are frequently developed using several different printed editions. The project ensures that these editions are confirmed to be free of copyright protection in the United States, provided that no copyright notice is included. Because of this process, the electronic books are not necessarily kept in compliance with any one specific paper edition.

## Access and Search
The primary point of entry for most users is the Project Gutenberg website. This site hosts the main search facility for the library and can be accessed at www.gutenberg.org.

## Donation Policies and Methods
Project Gutenberg accepts donations to support its operations. These contributions can be made through various methods, including credit card donations, online payments, and checks. Individuals wishing to donate are directed to visit www.gutenberg.org/donate.

The organization maintains specific policies regarding the solicitation and acceptance of funds:
* **Solicitation:** Project Gutenberg does not solicit contributions from states where it has not met the necessary solicitation requirements. However, there is no known prohibition against accepting unsolicited donations from donors in those states who offer them.
* **International Contributions:** Donations from international sources are gratefully accepted.
* **Tax Treatment:** The organization cannot provide statements regarding the tax treatment of donations received from outside the United States, noting that its small staff is already overwhelmed by the requirements of U.S. laws.

For current addresses and specific donation methods, users are advised to check the Project Gutenberg web pages.
