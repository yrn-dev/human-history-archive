# Meshed i Sar

## Overview and Location
Meshed i Sar is a small town in Persia that serves as the port and roadstead for Barfurush. It is situated on the southern shore of the Caspian Sea, approximately 15 miles from Barfurush.

## Connection to Barfurush
Barfurush, located in the province of Mazandaran at 36deg 32' N. and 52deg 42' E., is the commercial capital of the province. The town is positioned on the left bank of the river Bawul [Babul], which is crossed at this location by a bridge consisting of eight arches. Barfurush is situated 26 miles from Sari and 90 miles from Teheran.

Despite being built in a swampy, low-lying area with deep roads that are nearly impassable, Barfurush has achieved significant size and importance through its commercial prosperity. The town is populated entirely by tradesmen and merchants. Its physical appearance is unique, as buildings are concealed and separated by a forest of tall trees, giving the town little appearance of being populous outside of the bazars.

## Commerce and Trade
The commercial activity of the region is centered on the bazars of Barfurush, which extend for a full mile and consist of roofed ranges of shops made of tiles and wood. The town's trade is primarily based on cotton and rice, though hemp (_Kanab_), flax (_Kat[=u]n_), and sugar cane (_nai shakar_) are also grown in the area. To support this commerce, the town contains about ten commodious caravanserais.

## Maritime Activity at Meshed i Sar
As the roadstead for Barfurush, Meshed i Sar facilitates a brisk shipping trade with other ports located on the Caspian Sea. The steamers of the Mercury and Caucasus Company call at the port on a weekly basis.
