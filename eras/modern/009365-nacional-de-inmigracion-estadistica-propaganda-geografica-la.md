# Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion, Estadistica y Propaganda Geografica

The Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion, Estadistica y Propaganda Geografica is an institution based in La Paz, Bolivia. It serves as a center for the collection of geographical data and the publication of official information regarding the Republic of Bolivia.

## Publications and Records
The office is responsible for the production of the *Boletin de la Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion, Estadistica y Propaganda Geografica*, published in La Paz. Additionally, the archive of this office contains the *Relaciones Geograficas de Bolivia*, which were documented in 1898.
