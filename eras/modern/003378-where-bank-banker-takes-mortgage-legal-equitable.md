# Banking Mortgages and Securities

In the context of banking, a mortgage—whether legal or equitable—or a guarantee may be taken by a bank or banker as cover for overdrafts or advances. In such instances, the position of the bank is not necessarily different from that of any other guaranteed party or mortgagee.

## Equitable Mortgages and Title-Deeds
In the law of mortgage, it is common practice to use the deposit of title-deeds as security for the repayment of money that has been advanced. Such a deposit of deeds operates as an equitable mortgage.

## Negotiable Securities and Pledges
Banks have been involved in leading legal decisions regarding the taking of securities. In the case of *London Joint Stock Bank v. Simmons* ([1892], A.C. 201), the House of Lords determined that commercial or other negotiable securities may be safely taken in pledge for advances. This applies even if the person providing the securities is likely, based on their known position, to be holding them merely as an agent for others, provided the securities are taken honestly and there is nothing tangible outside the person's position to arouse suspicion.

Furthermore, in *Lloyd's Bank v. Cooke* [1907], the principle of the common law of estoppel was vindicated regarding cheques, notes, and bills, except where the Bills of Exchange Act has distinctly abrogated or annulled it. Consequently, a person who places inchoate negotiable instruments into the hands of an agent to raise money is responsible to any party taking them for value and bona fide, even if the agent fraudulently abused or exceeded their authority, provided the case does not fall under the provisions of the Bills of Exchange Act.

## Impact of Mortgages on Banker-Customer Relations
The relationship between a banker and a customer allows for certain practices, such as the charging of interest on overdrafts based on universal custom. In some cases, compound interest or interest with periodical rests has been supported if the system was brought to the customer's attention via a pass-book and not objected to. However, this specific system of charging interest only continues as long as the relation of banker and customer remains in force. The act of taking a mortgage for an existing debt puts an end to this relationship.

## International Mortgage Practices
Mortgage loans are utilized by various state and private institutions globally:
* **Bulgaria:** The National Bank, a state institution with a capital of £400,000 and a central establishment in Sofia, issues loans on mortgage. Additionally, the Bulgarian Agricultural Bank (formerly agricultural chests founded in 1863 and reorganized in 1894) provides loans to peasants on mortgage or via the security of two solvent landowners at a rate of 8%.
