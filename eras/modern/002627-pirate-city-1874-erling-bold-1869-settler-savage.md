# Pirate City (1874), Erling the Bold (1869), and The Settler and the Savage

The titles *The Pirate City* (1874), *Erling the Bold* (1869), and *The Settler and the Savage* (1877) are works of fiction authored by Robert Michael Ballantyne.

## Author Biography
Robert Michael Ballantyne was a Scottish writer of fiction born in Edinburgh on April 24, 1825. He belonged to the same family as famous publishers and printers. At the age of sixteen, Ballantyne traveled to Canada, where he spent six years in the service of the Hudson's Bay Company. After returning to Scotland in 1847, he published his first book, *Hudson's Bay: or, Life in the Wilds of North America*, in 1848.

Ballantyne was employed by the publishers Messrs Constable for a period, but he transitioned fully to the profession of literature in 1856. From that point, he began writing a series of adventure stories for young readers. His writing philosophy was to write as far as possible based on personal knowledge of the scenes he described. His work is characterized by a thoroughly healthy tone and considerable graphic force.

In addition to his writing, Ballantyne was an artist who exhibited water-colours at the Royal Scottish Academy. In his later years, he resided at Harrow. He died in Rome on February 8, 1894, having traveled there to try to recover from the effects of overwork. In 1893, he published a volume titled *Personal Reminiscences of Book-making*.
