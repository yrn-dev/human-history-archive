## Taxation in Queensland
In Queensland, income tax is categorized into two primary types: income derived from personal exertion and income derived from property, such as royalties, interest, rents, and income from companies.

For property-derived income, amounts not exceeding £100 are exempt. Incomes between £100 and £120 are taxed at £1. For those earning over £120 but under £300, £100 is exempt, and a tax of 1s. is paid on every pound over £100. Incomes exceeding £300 are taxed at 1s. per pound. Additionally, a charge of 1s. per pound is applied to the incomes of all absentees and all companies.

Income from personal exertion follows a different scale:
* £100 to £125: 10s.
* £126 to £150: £1
* £151 to £300: £100 is exempt, with 6d. paid on every pound over £100.
* £301 to £500: 6d. per pound.
* £501 to £1000: 6d. per pound for the first £500, and 7d. per pound for the amount over £500.
* £1001 to £1500: 7d. per pound for the first £1000, and 8d. per pound for the amount over £1000.
* Over £1500: 8d. per pound.
