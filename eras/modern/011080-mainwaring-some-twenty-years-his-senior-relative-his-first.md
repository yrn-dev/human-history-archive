# Lady Mainwaring and the Ashmole Family

## Marriage to Elias Ashmole
In 1649, Lady Mainwaring married Elias Ashmole, an English antiquarian and founder of the Ashmolean Museum at Oxford. Lady Mainwaring was approximately twenty years older than Ashmole and was a relative of his first wife, who had passed away eight years prior to this marriage.

## Impact on Ashmole's Career
The union with Lady Mainwaring provided Elias Ashmole with a position of affluence. This financial stability allowed him to dedicate his entire time to his preferred studies. During the years following his marriage, Ashmole's interests shifted; while he had previously been interested in astrology—an interest sparked by William Lilly and Captain George Wharton—he began to favor antiquarian research and heraldry.

## Domestic Crisis and Later Years
The marriage experienced a period of instability in 1657 when Lady Mainwaring petitioned for a separation. However, she failed to win her case and subsequently returned to live with Ashmole. Following this domestic crisis and leading up to her death in 1668, Ashmole enjoyed significant favor at court, serving in various roles including comptroller and accountant-general of excise, Windsor herald, and commissioner for Surinam.
