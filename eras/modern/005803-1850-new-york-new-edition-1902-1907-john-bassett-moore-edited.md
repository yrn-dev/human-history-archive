# Publications and Literary Works of 1850

The year 1850 saw the release of various significant literary, philosophical, and historical works across different disciplines, including the publication of edited collections, original treatises, and the commencement of pioneering historical series.

## Philosophical and Theological Works
In the field of philosophy, 1850 marked the publication of *OEuvres choisies* by Jules Simon, a collection of the works of Descartes. Additionally, J. Veitch began producing English translations of Descartes' *Method, Meditations and Selections from the Principles*, a project that spanned from 1850 to 1853.

In theological and moral philosophy, the work *Method of Divine Government, Physical and Moral* was published in Edinburgh in 1850. This text was later republished frequently in New York, with a fifth edition appearing in 1856.

## Historical and Biographical Publications
The year 1850 was the starting point for the first issue of Benson John Lossing's *Pictorial Field-Book of the Revolution*. Issued in 30 parts between 1850 and 1852 before being compiled into two volumes, this work is noted as a pioneer in American historical literature. To prepare the text, Lossing traveled approximately 9,000 miles over nearly two years, creating more than a thousand sketches of battlefields and extant buildings.

In the realm of biography and literary editing, Rufus Wilmot Griswold served as a literary executor for Edgar Allan Poe, editing the works of Poe in 1850.

## Political and Social Writings
The year 1850 also saw the publication of *Hints Toward Reforms*, written by Horace Greeley. 

## Periodicals and Editorial Work
During this period, Rufus Wilmot Griswold expanded his editorial career in New York City. From 1850 to 1852, he edited the *International Magazine*, which subsequently merged into *Harper's Magazine* in 1852.

## Related Historical Contexts
While not a publication of 1850, the "Compromise of 1850" serves as a chronological marker for later historical analysis. James Ford Rhodes utilized this event as a starting point for the first two volumes of his *History of the United States since the Compromise of 1850*, a work released in a new edition between 1902 and 1907.
