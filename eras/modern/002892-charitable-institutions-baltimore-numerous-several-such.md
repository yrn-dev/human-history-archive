# Charitable Institutions of Baltimore

Baltimore possesses a numerous array of charitable institutions. These include several organizations that are supported either wholly or in part by the state of Maryland, as well as scores of other independent entities.

## Organizational and Social Welfare Institutions
Among the city's charitable organizations is the Charity Organization Society, which serves the primary purpose of organizing the work performed by other institutions. Another key entity is the Baltimore Association for the Improvement of the Condition of the Poor, an organization that seeks to discourage the practice of indiscriminate alms-giving. 

For those in need of basic necessities or specialized support, the city provides several services:
* **The Thomas Wilson Fuel-Saving Society**: This institution furnishes coal at low rates.
* **The Woman's Industrial Exchange**: This organization assists women in need to support themselves.
* **The Free Summer Excursion Society**: This society provides a change of air for the indigent sick.
* **Bay View Asylum**: This facility serves as the city poorhouse.

## Medical and Specialized Healthcare
Baltimore's medical charitable landscape includes a wide variety of hospitals and specialized clinics. Johns Hopkins hospital is specifically noted for the excellence of its heating and ventilating equipment. Other general and specialized medical facilities include:
* **General Hospitals**: Saint Joseph's general hospital and the hospital for the women of Maryland of Baltimore city.
* **Pediatric Care**: The nursery and child's hospital, and the Thomas Wilson sanatorium for children, which is intended for diseased children under three years of age during the warm summer months.
* **Specialized Care**: The Baltimore eye, ear and throat charity hospital.

The city also maintains several institutions for those with chronic or mental health conditions, including a home for the incurables and the Maryland hospital for the insane. Mental health treatment is further supported by the Sheppard asylum, which is intended especially for the cure of the insane, and the Sheppard and Enoch Pratt hospital.

## Care for Children, the Aged, and the Disabled
Baltimore provides extensive support for vulnerable populations through various asylums and homes. Care for children is provided by the Baltimore orphan asylum, Saint Vincent's infant asylum, and various homes for friendless children. For the elderly, the city maintains homes for the aged. Additionally, there are dedicated institutions for the blind and institutions for the deaf and dumb.

## Educational Institutions for the Poor
Beyond direct charity, Baltimore offers educational opportunities for the underprivileged. The McDonogh farm school, located approximately 12 miles northwest of the city, was established through a bequest from John McDonogh. Spanning 835 acres, the school features a printing-office as well as machine and carpenter shops. Its mission is to prepare poor boys to enter any college in the country.
