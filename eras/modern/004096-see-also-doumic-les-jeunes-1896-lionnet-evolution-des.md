# Rene Doumic

## Biography
Rene Doumic (1860– ) was a French man of letters and critic born in Paris. He began his professional career with a distinguished tenure at the Ecole Normale, after which he served as a teacher of rhetoric at the College Stanislas. 

## Literary Career and Criticism
Doumic established himself as a prominent literary critic, most notably through his work with the *Revue des Deux Mondes*, where he was recognized as an independent and uncompromising critic. In addition to this role, he contributed to several other publications, including the *Journal des Debats*, the *Revue bleue*, and the *Moniteur*.

His critical work extended to various formats and subjects. He authored "La Comedie de moeurs," which appeared in L. Petit de Julleville's *Histoire de la langue et de la litterature francaise* (viii. pp. 82 et seq.). He also provided commentary on Jules Barbey d'Aurevilly in the September 1902 issue of the *Revue des deux mondes*.

## Published Works
Doumic produced a wide range of scholarly and critical texts between 1888 and 1905. His bibliography includes:

*   **Literary History and Portraits:** *Elements d'histoire litteraire* (1888), *Portraits d'ecrivains* (1892), and *Ecrivains d'aujour-d'hui* (1894).
*   **Theatrical and Contemporary Studies:** *De Scribe a Ibsen* (1893), *Les Jeunes* (1896), and *Essais sur le theatre contemporain* (1897).
*   **Historical and Cultural Analysis:** *Etudes sur la litterature francaise*, a five-volume work published between 1896 and 1905, and *Les Hommes et les idees du XIX^e siecle* (1903).
*   **Editorial Work:** An edition of the *Lettres d'Elvire a Lamartine* (1905).
