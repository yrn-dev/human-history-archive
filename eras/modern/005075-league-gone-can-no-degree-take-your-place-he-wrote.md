While Cobden and Bright were the leaders of the movement, Bright publicly discouraged the tendency of the public to view them as the primary movers. ## Sacred Teachings on Covenants and Replacement

The Qur'an provides specific guidance on the replacement of persons and the nature of covenants. Furthermore, the text forbids marrying women whom one's fathers had previously married, describing such a custom as odious, indecent, and an abominable way.

Additionally, the text notes that all individuals have degrees or ranks according to their deeds, as the Lord is not heedless of what they do.
