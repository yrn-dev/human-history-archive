## Regional History and Documentation
The history of the West Indies, with a particular focus on Barbados, has been documented in several historical texts. Notable works include:
* *History of Barbados* by Ligon (1657)
* *British Empire in America* by Oldmixon (1741)
* *A Short History of Barbados* (1768) and subsequent *Remarks upon the Short History* (1768)
* *History of Barbados* by Poyer (1808)
* *Chron. Hist. of W. Indies* by Capt. Thom. Southey (1827)
* *History of Barbados* by Schomburgk (1848)
* *The Cavaliers and Roundheads of Barbados* by N. D. Davis (1887)
* *History and Guide to Barbados* by J. H. Stark (1893)

Additionally, the region's health and infrastructure were noted in J. H. S. Moxby's 1886 *Account of a West Indian Sanatorium*. Other regional studies include R. T. Hill's 1897 work, *Cuba and Porto Rico*.

## Geology and Physical Development
Scientific research into the West Indies has focused heavily on the geological composition of Barbados and Trinidad. Key studies published in the *Quart. Journ. Geol. Soc. London* include:
* Research by A. J. Jukes-Browne and J. B. Harrison on the geology of Barbados (1891–1892).
* Contributions by J. W. Gregory regarding the physical geology and palaeontology of the West Indies (1895).
* Analysis of basal reef-rocks and Globigerina-marls in Barbados by G. F. Franks and J. B. Harrison (1898).
* A study by J. W. Spencer (1902) concerning the physical and geological development of Barbados, which also included notes on Trinidad.
