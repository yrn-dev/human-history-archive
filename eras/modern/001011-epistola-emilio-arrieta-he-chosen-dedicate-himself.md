## Collaboration of Ayala and Arrieta
Adelardo López de Ayala y Herrera (1828–1879) was a Spanish politician and writer who began his career writing plays for amateurs in his native town of Guadalcanal. After moving to Madrid in 1850 to pursue a professional career as a dramatist, he achieved significant success with works such as *Un Hombre de Estado*, *Castigo y Perdon*, and *Los dos Guzmanes*.

In 1855, Ayala collaborated with Emilio Arrieta on a *zarzuela* titled *Guerta a muerte*, for which Arrieta composed the music. The two also collaborated on another work titled *El Agente de Matrimonios*.
