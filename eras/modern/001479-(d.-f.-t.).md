## Spherical Trigonometry and Geodesy
The symbol $D$ is used to represent distance or angles in spherical triangles. When two places share the same latitude, the relationship is expressed as $\sin \frac{1}{2} D = \cos l \sin \frac{1}{2} t$. For differing latitudes, the formula $\cos D = \sin l \cos(l' - p) / \sin p$ is used, where $p$ is a subsidiary angle such that $\cot p = \cot l \cos t$. These calculations assume the earth is a sphere, though reductions are made for the spheroidal shape and elevation above sea level.
