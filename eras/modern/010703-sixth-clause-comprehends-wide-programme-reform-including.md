# The Reform Programme of the Samaj

The Samaj is guided by a set of foundational principles centered on the belief that God is the source of all true knowledge. According to the archive's records, God is described as the cause of the universe, all-pervading, omniscient, eternal, holy, and without a beginning. He is further characterized as being all truth, all knowledge, all bliss, boundless, almighty, just, and merciful. Because God is the incomparable support and Lord of all, the Samaj teaches that worship is due to him alone.

## Principles of Knowledge and Conduct
The Samaj identifies the *Vedas* as the medium through which true knowledge is obtained. The organization maintains that truth must be accepted and utilized as a guiding principle. In terms of practical behavior, the Samaj holds that justice and love serve as the correct guides for conduct. 

The organization emphasizes the necessity of spreading knowledge and seeking the good of others. This commitment to the collective welfare requires members to subordinate their general interests to the good of others, although they are encouraged to maintain their independence regarding their personal interests.

## The Sixth Clause and Social Reform
The object of the Samaj is to improve the physical, social, intellectual, and moral conditions of the world to provide benefit to humanity. This objective is specifically detailed in the sixth clause of its principles, which encompasses a wide-ranging programme of reform.

The reforms outlined in the sixth clause include:
*   **Health and Personal Habits:** The promotion of physical exercise, physical cleanliness, and abstinence from animal food and spirituous liquors.
*   **Social Structure:** The abolition of idolatry and the abolition of caste.
*   **Education and Family:** The promotion of education for females and the implementation of marriage reform.
