# Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation

## Mission and Objectives
Project Gutenberg™ is an initiative dedicated to increasing the availability of licensed works and those in the public domain. Its primary mission is to ensure these works can be freely distributed in a machine-readable format. A key goal of this effort is to maintain accessibility across the widest possible array of equipment, which specifically includes outdated equipment.

## Funding and Public Support
The survival and continued operation of Project Gutenberg™ are dependent upon widespread donations and public support. Financial contributions are essential for the project to carry out its mission. In particular, small donations ranging from $1 to $5,000 are noted as being especially important for the purpose of maintaining the organization's tax-exempt status with the IRS.
