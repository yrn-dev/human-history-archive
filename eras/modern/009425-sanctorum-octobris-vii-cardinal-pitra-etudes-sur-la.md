# Acta Sanctorum

The *Acta Sanctorum* is a collection of works that has been released in three distinct editions. The original edition, produced in Antwerp, Tongerloo, and Brussels, consists of 63 volumes published between 1643 and 1902. A Venice edition was also produced, though it stopped at volume v. of September, with publication dates spanning from 1734 to 1770. A third edition was published in Paris, which stopped at volume xiii. of October; this edition comprises 61 volumes published between 1863 and 1883.

In addition to these primary editions, the archive notes the existence of a volume of tables edited by the abbe Rigollot.

## Related Literature and Scholarship

Several works provide historical and critical analysis of the *Acta Sanctorum* and the Bollandists. These include:
* *Acta Sanctorum apologelicis libris ... vindicata*, published in Antwerp in 1755.
* *Memoire historique sur les Bollandistes*, written by L.P. Gachard and published in Brussels in 1835.
* "De ratione operis Bollandiani," authored by van Hecke and found in *Acta Sanctorum Octobris*, vii.
* *Etudes sur la collection des Actes des Saints*, written by Cardinal J.B. Pitra and published in Paris in 1880.
