# Just Lucas-Championniere

## Contributions to Fracture Treatment
Just Lucas-Championniere was a French surgeon who significantly influenced the medical approach to treating fractures. He is credited with doing more than any other individual to demonstrate the specific advantages of utilizing exercises, massage, and discreet movements during the recovery process for fractures.

## Evolution of Fracture Care
The treatment of fractures has seen considerable improvement in recent years, moving away from the practice of keeping simple fractures at rest in splints for extended periods. Instead, limbs are now constantly "taken down" to allow for movement and massage. When performed with the utmost gentleness, this method ensures that pain, swelling, and other signs of serious injury disappear more quickly, leading to a more complete and rapid recovery. This approach has also resulted in a decrease in the frequency of stiff feet and hands following a fracture.

## Surgical Advancements and X-rays
The use of X-rays has provided surgeons with the ability to easily verify that fractured surfaces are in close apposition and have been well adjusted. In cases where ordinary methods cannot achieve a satisfactory position for the broken ends, surgeons now employ a more direct intervention. This involves cutting down upon the broken ends to fix them together using a strong wire suture that remains permanently within the tissues.

In the case of compound fractures—where the fracture is associated with an open wound—immediate wiring of the fragments is now considered a necessary component of primary treatment if the broken ends cannot be easily adjusted.
