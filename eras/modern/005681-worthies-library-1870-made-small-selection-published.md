# Fuller Worthies Library

The *Fuller Worthies Library* is a publication series that, in 1870, featured the complete works of Fulke Greville, edited by Dr. A.B. Grosart.

## Contents of the Greville Volumes
The volumes edited by Dr. Grosart for the *Fuller Worthies Library* comprise a wide array of Greville's literary and intellectual output. These include:
*   **Treatises and Inquisitions:** *A Treatise of Religion*, *A Treatie of Humane Learning*, *An Inquisition upon Fame and Honour*, and *A Treatie of Warres*.
*   **Poetry:** *Poems of Monarchy* and *Caelica in CX Sonnets*, the latter of which is a collection of lyrics written in various forms.
*   **Correspondence and Speeches:** A letter addressed to an "Honourable Lady," a letter to Grevill Varney in France, and a short speech delivered on behalf of Francis Bacon.
*   **Additional Material:** The collection also contains minor poems and an introduction that includes some of the author's letters.

## Related Publications and Reprints
Following the 1870 publication in the *Fuller Worthies Library*, Dr. A.B. Grosart curated a small selection of these works for publication in the *Elizabethan Library* in 1894. Other works by Greville have appeared in various other collections and editions:
*   **Caelica:** This work was reprinted in 1898 within M.F. Crow's *Elizabethan Sonnet Cycles*.
*   **Life of Sidney:** This text was reprinted in 1816 by Sir S. Egerton Brydges, and later in 1907 in the "Tudor and Stuart Library" with an introduction provided by N. Smith.

Additionally, an essay regarding Greville can be found in Mrs. C.C. Stopes's 1907 work, *Shakespeare's Warwickshire Contemporaries*.
