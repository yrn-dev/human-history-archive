# Indemnity and Legal Provisions of Project Gutenberg

## Overview of Indemnity Obligations
Under the terms of the agreement regarding Project Gutenberg™ electronic works, users agree to indemnify and hold several parties harmless from all expenses, costs, and liability. This protection extends to the Foundation, the owner of the trademark, and any employee or agent of the Foundation. Additionally, the indemnity covers any volunteers involved in the distribution, promotion, and production of Project Gutenberg™ electronic works, as well as any individual providing copies of these works in accordance with the agreement.

## Scope of Liability
The requirement to hold the aforementioned parties harmless includes the coverage of legal fees. This obligation applies to any costs or liabilities that arise, whether directly or indirectly, from specific actions taken by the user. The agreement identifies three primary triggers for this indemnity:
* The distribution of any Project Gutenberg work or the specific work in question.
* Any deletions, additions, modifications, or alterations made to any Project Gutenberg work.
* Any Defect caused by the user.

## Warranty Disclaimers and State Law
The agreement acknowledges that certain states do not permit the exclusion or limitation of specific types of damages, nor do they allow disclaimers of certain implied warranties. In instances where a limitation or disclaimer within the agreement violates the law of the applicable state, the agreement is to be interpreted to ensure the maximum limitation or disclaimer permitted under that state's law.

## Enforceability of Provisions
The agreement contains a provision ensuring that the remaining terms remain in effect even if a specific part of the agreement is found to be unenforceable or invalid. The invalidity of one provision does not void the other provisions of the agreement.
