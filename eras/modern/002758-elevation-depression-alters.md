# D. 5' Elevation or Depression Alters

In the context of ballistics and artillery, the measurement of 5' elevation or depression is a factor that alters the point of impact. This alteration can occur either laterally or vertically.

## Tangent and Quadrant Elevation
The tangent elevation (T.E.) is represented by the value of [phi]. The quadrant elevation (Q.E.) is calculated as [phi] - S, where S represents the angular depression of the line of sight. If a point O is situated h ft. vertically above point B, the angle S at a range of R yards is determined by the formula sin S = h/3R. For small angles expressed in minutes (taking the radian as 3438'), the formula is S = 1146h/R.

When firing up or down a slope, the alteration of the tangent elevation is almost insensible. However, the quadrant elevation requires the addition or subtraction of the angle of sight.
