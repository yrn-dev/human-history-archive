# Project Gutenberg

## Overview and Mission
Project Gutenberg is characterized as being synonymous with the free distribution of electronic works. A primary goal of the project is to ensure that its collection remains freely available for future generations. To achieve this, the project provides electronic works in formats that are compatible with the widest possible variety of computers, specifically including those categorized as new, middle-aged, old, and obsolete.

## Operational Support
The existence of Project Gutenberg is made possible through the contributions of people from all walks of life. This support manifests in two primary ways:
* **Volunteers:** The project relies on the efforts of hundreds of volunteers.
* **Financial Support:** Donations are used to provide these volunteers with the necessary assistance to reach the project's goals.

## The Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation
In 2001, the Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation was established. The purpose of this foundation is to ensure a permanent and secure future for both Project Gutenberg and subsequent generations.

The Foundation is organized as a non-profit 501(c)(3) educational corporation. It operates under the laws of the state of Mississippi and has been granted tax-exempt status by the Internal Revenue Service. For identification purposes, the Foundation's federal tax identification number (EIN) is 64-6221541.

## Contributions and Legal Status
Because of its status as a 501(c)(3) corporation, contributions made to the Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation are tax deductible. These deductions are permitted to the full extent allowed by the laws of the United States federal government and applicable state laws. Further information regarding the Foundation and how to provide help through donations and efforts is available at www.gutenberg.org.
