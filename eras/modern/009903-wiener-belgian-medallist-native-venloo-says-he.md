# Jacques Wiener and the Bonheur Family

## Jacques Wiener
Jacques Wiener was a Belgian medallist who was a native of Venloo. He provided personal testimony regarding the religious background of the artist Rosa Bonheur, noting that he and her father, Raymond Bonheur, used to attend synagogue in the town of Venloo.

## Rosa Bonheur
Rosa Bonheur (1822–1899) was a French painter born in Bordeaux on March 22, 1822. She was of Jewish origin. Some authorities claim that she was known in common parlance as Rosa Mazeltov, utilizing the Hebrew term for "good luck," which translates to Bonheur in French.

The eldest of four children, Rosa was taught to draw at an early age by her father, who died in 1849. Recognizing her remarkable talent, he allowed her to abandon the business of dressmaking—a profession she had been put into against her will—to devote herself entirely to art.

## Artistic Career and Style
Rosa Bonheur exhibited at the salon from 1840 to 1845, receiving a prize five times, and was awarded a medal in 1848. Her fame grew significantly following the exhibition of 1855, after which her works became highly sought after by public galleries and collectors in England.

Her work is characterized by a "manly and firm" treatment and a lack of feminine handling. Like her contemporary Jacques Raymond Brascassat (1804–1867), Bonheur represented animals as she saw them in the country, as they really are. While her anatomy of animals is described as faultlessly true, her style is noted for a certain dryness and a frequent failure to provide a perfect sense of atmosphere.

Notable works by Bonheur include:
* "Hay Harvest in Auvergne" (1835)
* "Ploughing in the Nivernais" (1848), located in the Luxembourg gallery
* "The Horse Fair" (1853), with the original in the United States and one of two replicas in the National Gallery, London.

For her achievements, she was decorated with the Legion of Honour by Empress Eugenie and later promoted to the rank of officer within the order.

## The Bonheur Siblings
Rosa was part of a family of four children, all of whom were artists. Her siblings included:
* Auguste (1824–1884), who painted landscapes and animals.
* Isidore (born 1827), a sculptor of animals.
* Juliette (1830–1891), who received an "honourable mention" at the exhibition of 1855.

## Geography of Venloo
Venloo is a town associated with the native origins of Jacques Wiener. In the context of the delimitation of the boundaries of the Belgian kingdom, the town of Venloo, along with Maestricht, Dutch Flanders (the north of the ancient county), and a strip of Limburg surrounding them, were stripped from the Belgians and given to Holland according to the second article of the London protocols. This annexation occurred despite the fact that the inhabitants of these regions had participated in the Belgian revolt and did not wish to be annexed by Holland.
