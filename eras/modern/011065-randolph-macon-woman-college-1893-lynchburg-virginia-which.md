# Randolph-Macon Woman's College

## Overview and Establishment
Randolph-Macon Woman's College is an educational institution located in Lynchburg, Virginia. Opened in 1893, the college is conducted under the auspices of the Methodist Episcopal Church, South. It is managed by a self-perpetuating board.

## Location and Campus
The college buildings are situated on an elevation in Lynchburg, positioned between the James river and Rivermont Avenue. The city of Lynchburg, located in Campbell county, Virginia, is approximately 125 miles west by south of Richmond. The college's location provides views of river, valley, and mountain scenery, including the lesser spurs of the Blue Ridge and the Peaks of Otter, which are about 20 miles distant.

## The Randolph-Macon System
Randolph-Macon Woman's College is a member of the "Randolph-Macon System of Colleges and Academies." This system is governed by a single board of trustees to ensure that the work of the preparatory schools is correlated with that of the colleges. The system includes the following five institutions:

*   **Randolph-Macon College:** Located in Ashland, Virginia, this is one of the oldest Methodist Episcopal colleges in the United States. It opened in 1832 near Boydton in Mecklenburg county before moving to Ashland in 1868.
*   **Randolph-Macon Woman's College:** Located in Lynchburg, Virginia.
*   **Randolph-Macon Academy (Bedford City):** A school for boys established in 1890.
*   **Randolph-Macon Academy (Front Royal):** A school for boys established in 1892.
*   **Randolph-Macon Institute:** A school for girls in Danville, Virginia, which was admitted into the system in 1897.

## Enrollment
During the 1907-1908 academic year, Randolph-Macon Woman's College had an enrolment of 390 students.
