# Limited Right of Replacement or Refund and Warranty Terms

## Overview of Refund and Replacement Rights
Under the provisions outlined in paragraph 1.F.3, users who discover a defect in an electronic work are granted a limited right to a refund or replacement. To qualify for a refund of any money paid for the work, the recipient must send a written explanation to the person from whom the work was received. This request must be made within 90 days of receiving the work.

## Procedures for Physical and Electronic Media
The requirements for obtaining a refund or replacement vary depending on the medium used to deliver the work:

*   **Physical Medium:** If the work was received on a physical medium, the recipient is required to return that medium along with their written explanation.
*   **Electronic Delivery:** If the work was received electronically, the providing person or entity may choose to offer a second opportunity to receive the work electronically instead of issuing a refund.

## Resolution of Defective Works
The entity or person that provided the defective work has the option to provide a replacement copy in lieu of a refund. In cases where the work was delivered electronically and a second copy is provided, the recipient may demand a refund in writing if that second copy is also defective. In such an instance, the refund may be demanded without further opportunities for the provider to fix the problem.

## Warranty Limitations
According to paragraph 1.F.4, the work is provided on an "AS-IS" basis. Aside from the specific limited rights of replacement or refund detailed in paragraph 1.F.3, there are no other warranties of any kind, whether express or implied. This exclusion of warranties specifically includes, but is not limited to, warranties of fitness for any purpose or merchantability.
