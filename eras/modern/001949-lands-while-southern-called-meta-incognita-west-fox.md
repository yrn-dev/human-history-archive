# Baffin Land

Baffin Land is a barren insular tract located within the Franklin district of Canada. It covers an approximate area of 236,000 square miles and is situated between 62deg and 74deg N. latitude and 61deg and 90deg W. longitude.

## Geography and Coastlines
The northern and eastern coasts of Baffin Land are mountainous and rocky. These coastlines are deeply indented by large bays, including Home Bay and Frobisher Bay, as well as Admiralty Inlet and Cumberland Sound. 

The land is separated from surrounding regions by several bodies of water:
* **Greenland:** Separated by Davis Strait and Baffin Bay.
* **Ungava:** Separated by Hudson Strait.
* **Keewatin and Melville Peninsula:** Separated by Fury-and-Hecla Strait and Fox Channel.
* **North Devon:** Separated by Lancaster Sound.
* **North Somerset and Boothia Peninsula:** Separated by Prince Regent Inlet and the Gulf of Boothia.

A submarine ridge across Davis Strait unites the Cumberland Peninsula of Baffin Land with Greenland.

## Regional Designations and Interior
Various names are used to describe different parts of Baffin Land. The western region is known as Fox Land, while the southern part is called Meta Incognita. The north-western portion is designated as Cockburn Land, and North Galloway lies further to the east. On the extreme eastern peninsula, the land is referred to as Penny Lands and Cumberland Lands.

The interior of the southern part of Baffin Land contains two large lakes: Nettiling (also known as Kennedy) and Amadjuak, the latter of which is situated at an altitude of 289 feet.
