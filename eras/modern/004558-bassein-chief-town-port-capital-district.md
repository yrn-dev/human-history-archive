# BASSEIN

Bassein is a town and district located in the Irrawaddy division of Lower Burma, situated within the delta of the Irrawaddy. The town serves as the capital of both the district and the division.

## Geography and Environment
The town of Bassein is positioned on the eastern bank of the Bassein river. This river is one of the primary arteries used by the waters of the Irrawaddy to discharge into the sea. The broader district is characterized by a delta intersected with salt water creeks; other important arms of the sea in this region include the Pyinzalu, Pyamalaw, Kyunton, and Ngawun Shagegyi (also known as the Bassein river). 

The district's landscape includes a mountain range called the Anauk-pet Taungmyin, which runs from north to south along the coast. Additionally, the district contains two lakes: Inyegyi and the Irrawaddy. In terms of size, the district has been reduced to 4,127 square miles, down from 8,954 square miles in 1871, following the 1896 formation of the Myaungmya district, to which a large tract of land was given.

## Economy and Trade
Bassein is an important center for the rice trade and possesses several steam rice mills. Because it commands the great outlet of the Irrawaddy, the town is considered to have significant capabilities from both a military and mercantile perspective. 

Agriculture is a primary driver of the local economy. Wherever the land in the Irrawaddy delta is cultivable, it forms a vast sheet of rice. Subsidiary crops grown in the region include tobacco, sesamum, and cotton.

## Infrastructure and Transport
Transportation to and from Bassein is facilitated by water and rail. The Irrawaddy Flotilla Company operates vessels that ply between Bassein and Rangoon via inland waters. In 1903, a railway was opened that runs northeastward through the center of the district, connecting Bassein to Letpadan and Henzada.

## History and Population
During the second Burmese war, Bassein fell to British arms in May 1852. By 1901, the population of the town was recorded at 31,864, while the total population of the district in the same year was 391,427.
