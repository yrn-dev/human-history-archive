# Some Answered Questions Collected

## Overview
*Some Answered Questions Collected* is a work authored by Laura Clifford Burney. It is categorized within the literature composed in English specifically for American converts of the Baha'i faith.

## Context and Related Literature
The work exists within a broader body of Baha'i literature, which is described as being very voluminous, though much of it remains in manuscript form. Significant public collections of these manuscripts are located in Paris (Bibliotheque Nationale), London (British Museum), and St Petersburg. In Paris, the collection includes rare manuscripts gathered by Gobineau, such as a history written by Hajji Mirza Jani of Kashan, a contemporary of the Bab.

Other English-language works produced for American converts include:
* *Baha'u'llah* (The Glory of God), written by Ibrahim Khayru'llah with the assistance of Howard MacNutt (1900).
* *Facts for Bahaists* (1901) and *The Three Questions* (no date), both by Ibrahim Khayru'llah.
* *The Revelations of Baha'u'llah, in a Sequence of Four Lessons* (1902) by Isabella Brittingham.
* *Life and Teachings of 'Abbas Efendi* (1903) by Myron H. Phelps, which features a preface by E. G. Browne.

## Associated Scholars and Bibliographies
The study of this sect's literature has involved several scholars. E. G. Browne edited, translated, and annotated a Persian work by 'Abbas Efendi (the son of Baha) titled *Traveller's Narrative, written to illustrate the Episode of the Bab* (1891). Browne also authored *The New History of the Bab* (1893) and provided a "Catalogue and Description of the 27 Babi Manuscripts" in the *Journal of R. Asiat. Soc.* (1892).

Other notable contributions to the bibliography of the sect include:
* *Die Babi's in Persien* (1896) by Andreas.
* Scientific collections by Baron Victor Rosen in the *Collections scientifiques de l'Institut des Langues orientales* (1877–1891).
* Articles by Captain A. G. Toumansky in the *Zapiski vostochnava otdyeleniya Imperatorskava Russkava Archeologicheskava Obshchestva* (1890–1900). Toumansky also produced an edition of the *Kitab-i-Aqdas* (1899), which is identified as the most important of Baha's works, including a Russian translation and introduction.
* *Za'imu'd-Duwla*, an Arabic history of the Babis written by the Persian Mirza Muhammad Mahdi Khan and printed in Cairo (1903–1904), which is described as well-informed though unsympathetic.
