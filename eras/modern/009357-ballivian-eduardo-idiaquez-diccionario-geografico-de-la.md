# Diccionario Geografico de la Republica de Bolivia

The *Diccionario Geografico de la Republica de Bolivia* is a geographical reference work published in La Paz in 1900. It was co-authored by M.V. Ballivian and Eduardo Idiaquez.

## Context and Authorship
The work is part of a broader body of geographical and industrial documentation produced by M.V. Ballivian. Ballivian authored several other significant texts published in La Paz between 1896 and 1900, including:
* *Apuntes sobre la industria de goma elastica, &c.* (1896)
* *Breves Indicaciones para el Inmigrante y el Viajero a Bolivia* (1898)
* *Relaciones Geograficas de Bolivia existentes en el Archivo de la Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion, &c.* (1898)
* *Monografias de la Industria Minera en Bolivia*, which was released in three parts between 1899 and 1900.
* *Noticia Politica, Geografica, Industrial, y Estadistica de Bolivia* (1900)

## Role in Bolivian Bibliography
The *Diccionario Geografico de la Republica de Bolivia* serves as an authority for the study of the region, appearing alongside other key geographical and statistical sources. These include the *Sinopsis estadistica y geografica de la Republica de Bolivia* (published in three volumes between 1902 and 1904) and the *Boletin de la Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion, Estadistica y Propaganda Geografica*.

The archive also notes other contributors to the geographical and historical understanding of Bolivia, such as J.L. Moreno, who wrote *Nociones de geografia de Bolivia* (Sucre, 1889), and M. Dalence, who produced the *Bosquejo estadistico de Bolivia* (Chuquisaca, 1878).
