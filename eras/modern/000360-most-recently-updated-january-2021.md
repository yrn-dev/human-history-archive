## Technical Production and Formatting
The eBooks were created using scans of public domain material from the Robinson Curriculum. To maintain the integrity of the original printed texts, the transcribers implemented specific notation systems for characters that cannot be easily replicated in plain text:
* **Subscripts**: Numbers following letters without a space (e.g., C2) represent original subscripts. When letters are subscripted, they are preceded by an underscore (e.g., C_n).
* **Superscripts**: Characters following a carat (^) indicate original superscript text.
* **Special Characters**: In certain volumes, letters topped by a Macron are represented as [=x].
* **Layout Adjustments**: Side-notes from the original print have been relocated to serve as titles for their respective paragraphs.
