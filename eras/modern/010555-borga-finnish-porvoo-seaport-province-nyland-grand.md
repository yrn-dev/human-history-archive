# BORGA

Borga, known in Finnish as Porvoo, is a seaport located in the province of Nyland within the grand duchy of Finland. The town is situated at the entrance of the river Borga, which flows into the Gulf of Finland. Geographically, it is positioned approximately 33 miles northwest of Helsingfors, accessible by rail.

## Demographics and Urban Development
The population of Borga has shown steady growth over the 19th and early 20th centuries. In 1810, the population was recorded at 1,693; by 1870, it had increased to 3,478, and by 1904, it reached 5,255. While the town was once characterized by great importance and dignity, its prominence has been somewhat eclipsed by the rapid growth of Helsingfors.

## Governance and Education
Borga serves as a significant administrative and religious center. It is the seat of a court of appeal and the seat of a Lutheran bishopric. This bishopric's jurisdiction extends over the provinces of St Michel and Viborg, as well as portions of Nyland and Tavastehus. 

The town is home to a beautiful cathedral and a high school. The high school is notable for having been the place where the well-known Finnish poet Runeberg lectured for many years.

## Economy and Trade
The local economy is supported by both manufacturing and maritime trade. The principal industries in Borga are the manufacture of tobacco and the weaving of sail-cloth. As a seaport, its chief articles of trade consist of furs, butter, and wood.

## Political History
Borga played a pivotal role in the political history of Finland in 1809. During this year, the estates of Finland were summoned to a special diet to determine the future of the country, and Borga was selected as the meeting place. It was within the town's cathedral that Emperor Alexander I, acting as the grand duke of Finland, pledged himself to maintain the liberties and the constitution of the grand duchy.
