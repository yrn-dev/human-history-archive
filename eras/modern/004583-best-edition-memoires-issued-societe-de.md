# The Memoires of the Societe de l'Histoire de France

## Edition and Publication
The best edition of the *Memoires* is the one issued by the Societe de l'Histoire de France, published in Paris in 1877.

## Related Historical Works
In addition to the *Memoires*, other historical texts of note include the *Historiettes de la princesse de Conti, et du marechal de Bassompierre*, published in Paris between 1854 and 1860 by G. Tallemant des Reaux.

### Scientific and Regional Memoirs
Eugène Eudes-Deslongchamps (1830-1889), a French palaeontologist and professor of zoology and geology at Caen, contributed several memoirs to the Société Linnéenne de Normandie. These works covered topics such as Jurassic brachiopods, the *Terrain callovien* (1859), and *Nouvelle-Calédonie* (1864). He also wrote memoirs on the geology of Calvados (1856-1863) and the department of La Manche (1856), as well as *Études sur les étages jurassiques inférieurs de la Normandie* (1864). Following the death of his father in 1867, Eudes-Deslongchamps completed a memoir on the Teleosaurs, which was embodied in the *Prodrome des Téléosauriens du Calvados*.

### Historical and Biographical Memoirs
Other historical records include the *Mémoires* of the Société de l'histoire de Paris et de l'Île de France, which in 1903 (vol. xxx) published a work by P. Frémaux titled *La Famille d'Étienne Marcel*. Additionally, the *Memoires de la Societe de Born* contains a tract by M. Muret.

In the realm of personal memoirs, one account extending to the year 1672 was published at Vevey in 1698-1699. This work, described as realistic and lifelike despite being strongly partisan, was issued in a new 1894 edition by C. H. Firth, which included an introductory memoir, notes, and illustrative material.
