# CONFEDERATION OF BAR

The Confederation of Bar was a prominent alliance of the Polish gentry and nobles established in 1768. The group formed at the small fortress of Bar, located in Podolia, with the primary objective of defending Poland's external and internal independence. Specifically, the confederation sought to resist the aggressions of the Russian government, which was represented in Warsaw by Prince Nicholas Repnin.

## Origins and Early Conflict
The confederation was originated by Michael Krasinski, Osip Pulawski, and Adam Krasinski, the bishop of Kamenets. While King Stanislaus initially attempted to mediate between the Russian government and the confederates, he eventually found such mediation impossible. Consequently, the king dispatched a force to oppose the alliance under two generals and the grand hetman Ksawery Branicki, who successfully captured the town of Bar.

Despite the fall of the fortress, the movement expanded throughout Lithuania and the eastern provinces of Poland. This growth was aided by a simultaneous outbreak of a *jacquerie* in Little-Russia.

## Military Operations and Leadership
The confederates operated in bands led by Prince Charles Radziwill, Michael Pac, and Ignaty Malchewsky. These forces ravaged the land in various directions and achieved several victories over Russian forces. Eventually, the confederates began to ignore King Stanislaus entirely, sending their own envoys to the principal powers of Europe.

The army of the confederation was later thoroughly reorganized by Dumouriez, a commander-in-chief sent by the court of Versailles. Although the army maintained a gallant struggle for several years, Dumouriez is described as an adroit adventurer who did not particularly distinguish himself as a politician or a soldier.

## Diplomatic Efforts and Decline
In 1770, the Council of the Confederation moved its seat from Silesia to Hungary. From this new location, the council engaged in diplomatic negotiations with Turkey, Austria, and France in an attempt to establish a league against Russia. The confederates' appeals for foreign assistance contributed to the onset of a war between Turkey and Russia. The situation became so severe that Frederick II advised Catherine to reach terms with the confederates.

The alliance's standing in Europe was compromised by the actions of Dumouriez. At a time when King Stanislaus was preparing to accede to the Confederation, Dumouriez pronounced the king to be a traitor and a tyrant. This caused the king to return to the Russian faction, resulting in the Confederation losing the confidence of European powers.

## Geography of Bar
The town of Bar, where the confederation began, is located in the government of Podolia on an affluent of the Bug, approximately 50 miles northeast of Kamenets. Formerly known as Rov, the town was rebuilt by Bona Sforza, the consort of Sigismund I of Poland, who renamed it in memory of Bari in Italy. The town had previously been destroyed by the Tatars in 1452 and was held by the Turks from 1672 to 1699. Following the 1768 confederation, the town was taken by storm and remained separate from Russia until the partition of 1793.
