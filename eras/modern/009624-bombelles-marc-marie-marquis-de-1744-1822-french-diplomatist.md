# BOMBELLES, MARC MARIE, MARQUIS DE (1744-1822)

Marc Marie, Marquis de Bombelles, was a French ecclesiastic and diplomatist. He was born in Bitsch, located in Lorraine, and was the son of the comte de Bombelles, who served as the guardian and tutor to the duke of Orleans.

## Military and Diplomatic Career
Bombelles began his professional life in the army, serving throughout the Seven Years' War. He transitioned into the diplomatic service in 1765. Following several diplomatic missions, he was appointed as the ambassador of France to Portugal in 1786. His primary objective in this role was to win the country over to the Family Compact; however, he was unsuccessful due to the death of the king and the madness of the queen.

In early 1789, Bombelles was transferred to Vienna. His diplomatic career was cut short by the French Revolution, and he was deprived of his post in September 1790. Despite this, he remained attached to Louis XVI and was tasked with secret missions to various sovereigns to secure aid for the king.

## Emigration and Religious Life
In 1792, Bombelles emigrated from France. Following the events at Valmy, he spent a period of retirement in Switzerland. After the death of his wife in 1804, he withdrew to the monastery of Brunn in Austria. He subsequently entered the clergy, becoming the bishop of Oberglogau in Prussia.

## Final Years
Bombelles returned to France in 1815. In 1819, he was appointed bishop of Amiens. He died in Paris in 1822.

## Family
Bombelles had a son, Louis Philippe, comte de Bombelles (1780-1843), who was born at Regensburg. Unlike his father's service to France, Louis Philippe spent his life in the diplomatic service of Austria. He served as the Austrian ambassador to Denmark in 1814 and held a similar diplomatic position at Dresden in 1816.
