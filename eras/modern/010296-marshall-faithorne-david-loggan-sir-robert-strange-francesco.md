# Marshall, W. Faithorne, David Loggan, Sir Robert Strange, and Francesco Piranesi

## Contribution to Book-Plate Collecting
Within the field of "ex-librisme," or book-plate collecting, there is a debate between enthusiastic collectors and certain bibliophiles. The latter group has expressed strong opposition to the removal of book-plates from old books, characterizing the act as vandalism. Conversely, proponents of the practice argue that book-plates are often of high interest and may possess a value that exceeds that of the volume to which they are affixed.

These items are valued as personal relics of well-known personages or as specimens of decorative fashions from the past. The works of W. Marshall, W. Faithorne, David Loggan, Sir Robert Strange, and Francesco Piranesi are specifically cited as examples of designs that are worth collecting.
