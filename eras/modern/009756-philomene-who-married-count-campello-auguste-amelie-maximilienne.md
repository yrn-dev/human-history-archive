# Philomene and Auguste Amelie Maximilienne

Marie Desiree Eugenie Josephine Philomene and Auguste Amelie Maximilienne Jacqueline were daughters of Charles Lucien Jules Laurent, the prince of Canino.

## Family Background
Their father, Charles Lucien Jules Laurent (1803–1857), was the son of Lucien Bonaparte. A scientist by inclination rather than a politician, he established a scientific reputation through the publication of *American Ornithology* (1825–1833) and several other works concerning European zoology, including catalogs of European birds, fish, and mammals. He was a member of the academies of Berlin and Upsala, and a correspondent of the Institute of France.

In 1822, Charles Lucien Jules Laurent married his cousin, Zenaide Bonaparte, who was the daughter of Joseph. Together they had children, including Philomene and Auguste Amelie Maximilienne.

## Philomene and Auguste Amelie Maximilienne
Marie Desiree Eugenie Josephine Philomene and Auguste Amelie Maximilienne Jacqueline were among the eight children who survived their father. 

*   **Marie Desiree Eugenie Josephine Philomene** married the count Campello.
*   **Auguste Amelie Maximilienne Jacqueline** married Count Gabrielli.

They had several siblings, including Joseph Lucien Charles Napoleon (the prince of Canino), who died without heirs; Lucien Louis Joseph Napoleon, who became a cardinal in 1868; Julie Charlotte Zenaide Pauline Laetitia Desiree Bartholomee, who married the marquis of Roccagiovine; Charlotte Honorine Josephine, who married Count Primoli; and Napoleon Charles Gregoire Jacques Philippe, who married the princess Ruspoli and had two daughters.
