# Global Population and Demographics

## Bangkok and Regional Administration
The population of Bangkok is estimated at 450,000 and is characterized as mixed. The majority of the population consists of Siamese and Chinese, though persons of almost every race found between Japan and Bombay may be seen there. Additionally, Europeans of various nationalities number over 1,000. 

The city's government is entrusted to a cabinet member serving as the minister of the capital. This minister oversees the revenue offices, the harbour master, and the sanitary and police departments. The police force consists of 3,000 men led by a European commissioner. The sanitary department, which includes an engineer's office, a bacteriological laboratory, and a board of health, is managed with expert European assistance. Under an act of 1905, the harbour master controls the city water-ways and the port. While the ministry of justice controls ordinary law courts, foreign powers in Siam enjoy extra-territorial rights; consequently, each consulate has an attached court with jurisdiction over cases where a subject of that power is the defendant. 

Public health in Bangkok has been challenged by a high death rate, particularly among children, due to fevers, smallpox, and cholera during dry weather. However, sanitation is improving, aided by the boring of numerous artesian wells that provide good water.

## Populations of the British Empire
The total population of the British Empire is estimated at approximately 400,000,000, which represents more than one-fourth of the world's total population (calculated at over 1,500,000,000, including a liberal estimate of 650,000,000 for peoples not captured by census). 

The empire's population is divided into white and native races. The white population, totaling over 53,000,000 in 1901, is mainly of Anglo-Saxon race but includes Spanish, Dutch, and French peoples. This group is distributed as follows:
* United Kingdom and home dependencies: 41,608,791
* British North America: 5,500,000
* Australasia: 4,662,000
* Africa (British and Dutch): 1,000,000
* West Indies and Bermuda: 100,000
* India: 169,677

Within the white races, particularly in the United Kingdom, there is a considerable sprinkling of Jewish blood. The native population includes the principal yellow, brown, and black races. In India, the native population was returned at 294,191,379 in the 1901 census. This population is divided into 118 linguistic groups, which can be collected into principal groups: Semitic, Hamitic, Indo-European (Indo-Aryan sub-family), Dravido-Mu[n.][d.][=a] (Dravidian and Mu[n.][d.][=a]/Kolarian), Indo-Chinese (Tibeto-Burman, Mon-Khmer, and Siamese-Chinese), Malayo-Polynesian, and unclassed groups such as Gipsy.

## Regional Demographics of the Empire
### Africa and the West Indies
In Africa, the population is chiefly black. Estimates include 5,211,329 in the South and 2,000,000 in the Central region. In South Africa, the aboriginal Bushmen and Hottentots are diminishing and are expected to become extinct in British South Africa during the twentieth century. The majority of the native population consists of Bantu stock (including Damara, Bechuana, Zulu, and Kaffirs). A "Mixed Races" population, varying in color from dark brown to yellow, has emerged from the intermarriage of natives with Indians and Malays introduced from Mozambique, Madagascar, Ceylon, Java, and British India. Central African tribes may show an admixture of Arab blood.

In the West Indies, the population is largely made up of imported African negroes; in Jamaica, these constitute four-fifths of the population. Other groups include Chinese, imported East Indian coolies, and aboriginal races such as Caribs and American Indians of the mainland. Intermixture with Portuguese and Spanish blood has created many mixed types. The total coloured population of the West Indies, including Honduras and British Guiana, is about 1,912,655.

### Asia and Other Dependencies
In Ceylon, the population is primarily composed of 2,000,000 Sinhalese and 954,000 Tamil. Other inhabitants include Malays, Arabs, Eurasians, Chinese, and Dyaks. The total population of Ceylon and the Eastern Colonies is 5,144,954. This includes the Straits Settlements (570,000), Hong-Kong (306,130), and North Borneo and Sarawak (700,000).

In Canada, the only coloured native races are the Red Indians, numbering 100,000.

## Other Global Populations
### Korea
Korea has an estimated area of 82,000 sq. m. A census in 1897 returned a population of 17,000,000 (with females in the majority), though it was later estimated at a maximum of 12,000,000. The native population is absolutely homogeneous, with a large majority engaged in agriculture. There is a foreign population of about 65,000, of whom 60,000 are Japanese. While emigration is rare, some Koreans have moved to Mexico, Hawaii, and Russian or Chinese territory.

### Greenland and South America
The Danish colony in Greenland had a population of 11,893 in 1901, including about 300 Europeans. The Eskimo population has fluctuated; Hans Egede estimated it at 30,000 in the mid-18th century, though this is considered an over-estimate. Population decreases were linked to infectious diseases like smallpox. In the north, settlements in the Franz Josef and Scoresby Fjords have died out or moved south, while the "Arctic Highlanders" (Smith Sound Eskimo) number about 240.

In Colombia, the department of Bolivar (until 1908) had a population estimated at 323,097 in 1899. This population is largely composed of mixed races, who in some areas constitute four-fifths of the inhabitants.

## Urban Centers: Calcutta
The population of Calcutta grew from an estimated 12,000 in 1710 to 949,144 in 1901. Between 1801 and 1901, the city's population increased sixfold. As of 1901, the population of the town and suburbs included 615,000 Hindus, 286,000 Mahommedans, and 38,000 Christians.
