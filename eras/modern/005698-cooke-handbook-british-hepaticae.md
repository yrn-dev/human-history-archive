# Handbook of British Hepaticae

The *Handbook of British Hepaticae* is a botanical work by Cooke that serves as a source for the study of liverworts. Its contents are utilized in botanical illustrations to document the physical characteristics and reproductive stages of specific species of hepaticae.

## Pellia epiphylla
The *Handbook of British Hepaticae* provides detailed data and visual references for *Pellia epiphylla*. The text and associated illustrations describe several stages of the plant's development:

*   **Mature Sporogonia**: The work documents groups of plants that bear mature sporogonia.
*   **Fertilization**: The archive contains longitudinal sections of the thallus during the process of fertilization. This stage identifies the presence of antheridia, archegonia, and the involucre.
*   **Sporogonium Development**: The sources describe the longitudinal section of an almost mature sporogonium as it attaches to the thallus. The anatomical components identified at this stage include the capsule, seta, foot, calyptra, and the involucre.
