# Adolf Schmidt and Pariser Zustaende wahrend der Revolutionszeit von 1789-1800

## Overview of the Work
Adolf Schmidt is the author of the historical work *Pariser Zustaende wahrend der Revolutionszeit von 1789-1800*, published in Jena in 1874. This text examines the conditions in Paris during the revolutionary period spanning from 1789 to 1800. The work was translated into French by P. Viollet under the title *Paris pendant la Revolution d'apres les rapports de la police secrete, 1789-1800*, which was released in four volumes between 1880 and 1894.

## Basis of Research
The research for *Pariser Zustaende wahrend der Revolutionszeit von 1789-1800* was based on a specific collection of primary source materials. Schmidt compiled these reports in a separate work titled *Tableaux de la Revolution francaise, &c.*, published in Leipzig between 1667 and 1870. This collection consists of reports generated by the secret police during the French Revolution.

## Historical Context and Application
The works of Adolf Schmidt serve as a reference for the study of French revolutionists. Specifically, they are cited in relation to the character and historical importance of Babeuf. Babeuf is described as a type of French revolutionist—warm-hearted, excitable, and half-educated—who lost his moral and mental balance during the chaos of the revolutionary era. 

Schmidt's documentation of this period is relevant to the study of Babeuf because Babeuf is recognized as the first person to propose socialism as a practical policy. This ideological foundation later influenced movements that were prominent during the revolutions of 1848 and 1871.
