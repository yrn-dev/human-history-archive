# Jugemens des savans

The *Jugemens des savans sur les principaux ouvrages des auteurs* is a scholarly work authored by the French critic and scholar Adrien Baillet. It is regarded as the most useful and celebrated of Baillet's numerous publications.

## Authorship and Content
Adrien Baillet (1649–1706) was born in the village of Neuville near Beauvais, Picardy. After receiving an education at a theological seminary through the kindness of the bishop of Beauvais, he served as a teacher in the college of Beauvais and was ordained as a priest in 1676. Baillet was known for his intense devotion to study, allowing himself only five hours of rest per day.

The *Jugemens des savans* was published in nine volumes between 1685 and 1686. The work provides critical evaluations of the primary works of various authors. Due to the praise Baillet bestowed upon the Jansenists within this text, he incurred the hatred of the Jesuits.

## Editions and Related Works
A notable edition of the *Jugemens des savans* was produced by M. de la Monnoye, published in Amsterdam in 1725 across four volumes. This specific edition is significant for containing the *Abrege de la vie de Mr Baillet* as well as the *Anti-Baillet* written by Gilles Menage.

## Scholarly Application
The *Jugemens des savans* has served as a reference for other historians and scholars. For instance, in the study of the poet Claudian, the work is cited (specifically volume IV, page 227) regarding the preference given by the commentator Barthius to the two books Claudian composed against Eutropius over his other productions.
