# Bucherzeichen and Les Ex-libris et les marques de possession du livre

## Overview of Book-plate Literature
The study of book-plates and marks of possession is documented through various specialized authorities and series. For those interested in the composition of book-plates, the *Ex-libris Series* published in London serves as a primary resource. Additionally, several scholarly works provide regional and chronological perspectives on the subject.

## Notable European Works on Book-plates
Literature regarding book-plates spans several languages and countries, focusing on the identification and study of these marks:

*   **French Works:** A. Poulet-Malassis authored *Les Ex-libris francais* in 1875. Henri Bouchot published *Les Ex-libris et les marques de possession du livre* in 1891. Walter Hamilton also contributed to the field with *French Book-plates* in 1892.
*   **German Works:** Friedrich Warnecke published *Die deutschen Bucherzeichen* in 1890. Furthermore, Count K.E. zu Leiningen-Westerburg wrote *German Book-plates*, which was translated by G.R. Denis in 1901.
*   **English Works:** The Hon. J. Leicester Warren (Lord de Tabley) produced *A Guide to the Study of Book-plates* in 1880. Other significant English texts include *English Book-plates* by Egerton Castle (1892) and *Notes on Book-plates, 1574-1800*, a private publication by Sir A.W. Franks in 1887.

## Specialized Studies and Engravers
Beyond general guides, specific texts focus on the artists behind the works and the dating of the plates. H.W. Fincham's *Artists and Engravers of British and American Book-plates* (1897) examines the creators of these marks, while Walter Hamilton's *Dated Book-plates* (1895) provides a chronological approach to the study.

## Related Typographic Marks
The study of book-plates is closely related to the study of printers' marks. Authorities on these facsimiles include:
*   Berjeau's *Early Dutch, German and English Printers' Marks* (1866).
*   W. Roberts's *Printers' Marks* (1893).
*   Silvestre's *Marques typographiques* (1853-1867).
*   *Marques typographiques des imprimeurs et libraires qui ont exerce dans les Pays-Bas* (1894).
*   *Die Buchermarken oder Buchdrucker und Verlegerzeichen* (1892-1898), which contains devices used in Cologne, Mainz, Frankfort, Basel, Italy, and Alsace.
