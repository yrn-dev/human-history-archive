# Nyasa-Zambezi Waterparting

The Nyasa-Zambezi waterparting is a geographical region located within the territory of British Central Africa. This area is characterized by its mineral deposits and its position within the broader hydrological basins of the region.

## Mineral Resources
The hills along the Nyasa-Zambezi waterparting are noted for several specific mineral findings. Gold has been discovered in this area, as well as in the Shiré Highlands and the mountainous region situated to the west of Lake Nyasa. Additionally, the hills of the Nyasa-Zambezi waterparting contain silver, found as galena or silver-lead. Lead is also present in this same district.

Other potential resources associated with the waterparting include coal, which occurs in the north and west Nyasa districts—particularly within the Karroo sandstones of the Rukuru valley—and may also be found along the Zambezi-Nyasa waterparting. There is further indication that petroleum may exist in certain places along the Nyasa-Zambezi waterparting.

## Regional Geology and Geography
The broader geological formation of the region is Primary and Archean, consisting primarily of schists, gneiss, quartz, felspar, granite, amphibolite, and other Archean rocks. In the regions within the northern watershed of the Zambezi river, as well as in the river Shiré and the basin of Lake Nyasa (which is a great rift depression), there are Primary limestones and sandstones.

The territory of British Central Africa, which encompasses these features, covers approximately 250,000 square miles. It includes the majority of Lake Nyasa and the courses of the rivers Luangwa and Shiré. More than two-thirds of this country is situated within the Zambezi basin, specifically within the subordinate basins of the rivers Luengwe-Kafukwe, Luangwa, and Lake Nyasa.

## Related Mineral Deposits in the Nyasa Region
Beyond the waterparting, the surrounding districts contain various other minerals:
* **Graphite:** Found in the western basin of Lake Nyasa.
* **Copper:** Located in the west Nyasa region and the hills of North Eastern and North Western Rhodesia (occurring as pure ore and pyrites).
* **Limestone:** Found in the Shiré basin.
* **Malachite:** Located in North Western Rhodesia and south-west Angoniland.
* **Iron and Mica:** These are found almost universally throughout the region.

## Administrative Context
The region is part of what was generally termed British Central Africa, a name applied to British protectorates north of the Zambezi river. This area specifically lay between 8° 25' S. on Lake Tanganyika and 17° 6' S. on the river Shiré. In 1907, the official title of the protectorate was changed to the Nyasaland Protectorate. Other nearby divisions include North Western Rhodesia (Barotseland) and North Eastern Rhodesia.
