# Biographisches Lexikon des Kaisertums Oesterreich

The *Biographisches Lexikon des Kaisertums Oesterreich* is a comprehensive biographical reference work authored by C. von Wurzbach. Published in Vienna between 1856 and 1891, the work consists of 60 volumes.

## Scope and Content
The *Biographisches Lexikon des Kaisertums Oesterreich* serves as a primary biographical authority for the history of the Austrian Empire.
