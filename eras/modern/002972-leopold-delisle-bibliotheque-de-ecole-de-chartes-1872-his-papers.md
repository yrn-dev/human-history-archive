# Leopold Delisle

Leopold Delisle was a French scholar and librarian who held several prominent positions within the academic and archival institutions of Paris. He served as the Secretary of the École des Chartes and as an Honorary Librarian at the Bibliothèque Nationale in Paris. Additionally, he is identified as the Principal Librarian of the Bibliothèque Nationale.

## Published Works and Research
Delisle authored several scholarly works, including *Le Royaume de Provence sous les Carolingiens* and the *Recueil des chartes de Saint-Germain*. His research also extended to the history of significant libraries; he wrote the *Cabinet des Manuscrits* (vol. i.), which includes a history of the Colbertine library.

In his capacity as a researcher and editor, Delisle published the will of the French scholar Etienne Baluze in 1872, appearing in the *Bibliotheque de l'Ecole de Chartes*.

## Contributions to Other Scholars
Delisle's expertise and position as Principal Librarian of the Bibliothèque Nationale allowed him to assist other researchers. He provided the opportunity for the study of the inventory created following the death of Doge Marino Faliero, facilitating the production of editions of the travels of Marco Polo.
