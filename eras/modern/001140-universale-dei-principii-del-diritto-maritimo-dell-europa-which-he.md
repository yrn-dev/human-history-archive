# Sistema Universale dei Principii del Diritto Maritimo dell' Europa

The *Sistema Universale dei Principii del Diritto Maritimo dell' Europa* is a systematic work regarding the maritime law of Europe. It was authored by the Italian jurist Domenico Alberto Azuni.

## Authorship and Development
Domenico Alberto Azuni (1749–1827) was born in Sassar, Sardinia. He pursued legal studies in Turin and Sassari. Before the publication of his systematic work on European maritime law, Azuni served as a judge of the consulate at Nice starting in 1782. Between 1786 and 1788, he published a different work titled *Dizionario Universale Ragionato della Giurisprudenza Mercantile*.

The *Sistema Universale dei Principii del Diritto Maritimo dell' Europa* appeared in 1795. Following its initial publication, Azuni recast the work and translated it into the French language.

## Professional Context of the Author
Azuni's expertise in maritime law extended beyond this specific volume; he authored various minor works and pamphlets on the subject, including a significant treatise published in Paris in 1810 concerning the progress and origin of maritime law.

His legal and administrative career included several high-profile appointments:
* In 1806, he was named as a member of a French commission tasked with the creation of a general code of commercial law.
* In 1807, he served as the president of the court of appeal in Genoa.
* Following the fall of Napoleon in 1814, he spent a period in retirement in Genoa.
* He later returned to Sardinia at the invitation of Victor Emmanuel I, where he held the positions of director of the university library and judge of the consulate at Cagliari.

Azuni died in Cagliari in 1827. In addition to his legal texts, he wrote a political, geographical, and historical account of Sardinia, first published in 1799 and later enlarged in 1802.
