# Marienkirche (1892)

The Marienkirche (1892) is a church located in the town of Bonn, Germany. Bonn is situated in the Prussian Rhine province on the left bank of the Rhine, approximately 15 miles south by east from Cologne.

## Religious Institutions in Bonn
The town of Bonn contains several religious buildings, including six Roman Catholic and two Protestant churches. Among these is the Marienkirche, which dates to 1892. Other notable churches in the town include:

*   **The Munster (minster):** An imposing grey stone edifice built in the Transition and Romanesque styles between the 11th and 13th centuries. It features five towers, with a central tower reaching 315 feet. The building was restored in 1875 and subsequent years, and received paintings between 1890 and 1894.
*   **The Stiftskirche (monasterial church):** This structure was rebuilt between 1879 and 1884.
*   **The Jesuitenkirche:** Dating from 1693.
*   **The Minoritenkirche:** Built between 1278 and 1318.
*   **The Herz Jesu-kirche:** Dating from 1862.

In addition to these churches, the town possesses a synagogue. Furthermore, the university chapel in Bonn serves as an English church.

## The University of Bonn
The university is described as the finest of the town's buildings and occupies a large portion of the southern frontage. The current establishment was founded in 1818 by King Frederick William III of Prussia. This followed an earlier academy founded approximately nine years before 1786, which Archbishop Maximilian Frederick of Cologne raised to university rank in 1786; that institution functioned until it was dissolved by the last elector in 1794.

The university is housed in a building that was originally the electoral palace, constructed around 1717 using materials from old fortifications and later remodelled after the town became a Prussian possession.

### Academic Structure and Facilities
The university consists of five faculties:
*   Medical
*   Legal
*   Philosophic
*   Roman Catholic theology
*   Protestant theology

The university's library contains more than 230,000 volumes, and its antiquarian museum holds a valuable collection of Roman relics found in the surrounding area. The university is also connected to a laboratory, five clinical departments, and physiological, pathological, and chemical institutes.

Additionally, an academy of agriculture is established at the palace of Clemensruhe in Poppelsdorf, which is accessible via a mile-long avenue lined with double rows of chestnut trees. This academy includes a botanic garden and a natural history museum. A splendid observatory is also located on the south side of the road.

## Other Town Features
Bonn is located on the main railway line to Mainz and serves as a junction for lines to the Eifel and the right bank of the Rhine (via ferry). A bridge 1,417 feet in length was constructed between 1896 and 1898, flanked by a two-mile embankment. Parallel to this is the Coblenzer-strasse, characterized by pretty gardens and beautiful villas.

The town's central area consists of narrow streets, while the outskirts feature numerous fine buildings. Other civic structures include a theatre, several high-grade schools, a block of law-court buildings, and a town hall on the market square dating from 1737.
