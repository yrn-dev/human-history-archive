### Chronological Studies
A separate individual, Mr. J.K. Fotheringham, contributed a scholarly article titled "The Date of the Crucifixion," published in the *Journal of Philology* (xxix. 100-118) in 1904. In this work, Fotheringham provides astronomical and Talmudical information to analyze the timing of the Crucifixion. He argues that the evidence from Christian sources is too uncertain to be the primary basis for inquiry, suggesting instead that the statements of the Mishnah should serve as the starting point. 

By utilizing the phasis of the new moon as the true beginning of Nisan, J.K. Fotheringham concluded that Friday could not have coincided with Nisan 14 between A.D. 28 and 35, except in the year A.D. 33 (specifically April 3rd). However, the archive notes that an empirical test of these calculations revealed the new moon appeared one day earlier than his established rules allowed, leading some to suggest it would be premature to disregard other historical evidence against the A.D. 33 date.
