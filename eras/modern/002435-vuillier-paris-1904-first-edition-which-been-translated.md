# Les Iles oubliees

## Overview
*Les Iles oubliees* is a book written by G. Vuillier and published in Paris in 1904. The work is categorized as one of the most valuable books providing a general account of the islands known as the Baleares.

## Translations and Editions
The first edition of the work was translated into English. This translated version was published in London in 1896 under the title *The Forgotten Isles*.

## Context within Baleares Bibliography
Vuillier's work is situated among several key texts used for the study of the Baleares. Other significant general accounts of the islands include:
* *Die Balearen geschildert in Wort und Bild* by the archduke Ludwig Salvator of Austria (Leipzig, 1896).
* *Islas Baleares*, an illustrated volume consisting of 1,423 pages by P. Pifferrer, published in Barcelona in 1888 as part of the "Espana" series.

Additionally, the archive notes a relevant article regarding the islands written by George Sand in the *Revue des deux mondes* in 1841.

## Related Specialized Works
While *Les Iles oubliees* provides a general account, other monographs in the archive address specific aspects of the region:
* **History:** Sir C. R. Markham wrote *The Story of Majorca and Minorca* (London, 1908). For scientific history, the archive cites *La Historia general del regno balearico* by J. Dameto and V. Mut (Majorca, 1632-1650).
* **Moorish Rule:** A. Campaner y Fuertes authored *Bosquejo historico de la dominacion islamita en las islas Baleares* (Palma, 1888).
* **French Relations:** A. Lecoy de la Marche wrote an elaborate treatise titled *Les Relations de la France avec le royaume de Majorque* (Paris, 1892).
* **Flora and Monuments:** M. Willkomm produced *Illustrationes florae insularum Balearium* (Stuttgart, 1881-1892), and E. Cartailhac wrote *Monuments primitifs des iles baleares* (Toulouse, 1892) as part of the *Mission scientifique du ministere de l'instruction publique*.
