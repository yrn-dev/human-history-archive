# Napoleon I. and the Bonaparte Family

The history and genealogy of the Bonaparte family, including the life of Napoleon I., have been the subject of extensive documentation and biographical study. These records trace the family from its origins to the extinction of the branch that existed in the city of S. Miniato.

## Family Relations and Siblings
Napoleon I. was part of a larger family structure. His parents were Carlo and Letizia. Among his siblings, Joseph Bonaparte is listed as the first when taken in order of age. Other members of the family, such as Jerome Bonaparte, have also been the focus of detailed historical accounts. Jerome's life and correspondence are documented in several works, including the seven-volume *Memoires et correspondance du roi Jerome et de la reine Catherine* and *Les Rois freres de Napoleon*.

## Biographical and Genealogical Sources
A wide array of literature exists regarding the Bonaparte family and the early life of Napoleon I. These include:
*   **Family Origins:** Works such as *Storia genealogica della famiglia Bonaparte* (1846) and *Le antichita dei Bonaparte* (1857) examine the family's roots.
*   **Imperial History:** *La Famille imperiale* (1860) and *Histoire de la famille Bonaparte de l'an 1050 a l'an 1848* (continued to 1866) provide broader historical contexts.
*   **Early Life and Parents:** The youth of Napoleon I. is detailed in *La Jeunesse de Napoleon* and *Memoires sur l'enfance et la jeunesse de Napoleon jusqu'a la age vingt-trois ans*, the latter of which includes a historical notice regarding his father. Specific studies on his parents include *Die Eltern und Geschwister Napoleons I.* (1876) and *Madame Mere* (1892), as well as *Napoleons Mutter*.
*   **Marriages and Family Ties:** The marriages within the family are explored in *The Marriages of the Bonapartes* (1881) and *The Bonaparte-Patterson Marriage*.

## Later Descendants and Legacy
The fortunes of the Bonaparte family continued through the descendants of various members, most notably the descendants of Lucien and Jerome. The family's influence and history are further analyzed in multi-volume sets such as *Napoleon et sa famille* (1897-1900). Additionally, Napoleon's personal relations and the events in which he was involved are described in the four-volume *Memoires* edited by Duvergier de Hauranne in 1875-1876.
