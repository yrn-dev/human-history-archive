# ASHLAND, VIRGINIA

Ashland is a village located in Hanover county, Virginia, U.S.A., situated 17 miles northwest of Richmond. Settled in 1845, the village was incorporated in 1856. Its population was recorded as 1,147 in 1900 and grew to 1,324 by 1910. The village is served by the Richmond, Fredericksburg & Potomac railway and is considered a favourite resort for those visiting from Richmond.

## Educational Institutions
Ashland is the site of Randolph-Macon College, a Methodist Episcopal, South institution and one of the oldest Methodist Episcopal colleges in the United States. The college received its charter in 1830 and opened in 1832 near Boydton in Mecklenburg county, Virginia, before being moved to Ashland in 1868. During the 1907-1908 academic year, the college had a faculty of 16 and a student body of 150. The institution publishes an endowed historical series known as *The John P. Branch Historical Papers of Randolph-Macon College*.

Randolph-Macon College is part of the "Randolph-Macon System of Colleges and Academies," which is managed by a single board of trustees to ensure the work of preparatory schools is correlated with that of the colleges. This system includes the following institutions:
* **Randolph-Macon Academy (Bedford City, Virginia):** A school for boys established in 1890.
* **Randolph-Macon Academy (Front Royal, Virginia):** A school for boys established in 1892.
* **Randolph-Macon Woman's College (Lynchburg, Virginia):** Opened in 1893, this college had an enrolment of 390 students in 1907-1908.
* **Randolph-Macon Institute (Danville, Virginia):** A school for girls that was admitted into the system in 1897.

## Local Geography and Landmarks
The area surrounding Ashland is noted for the birthplaces of two prominent figures. The birthplace of Henry Clay is located approximately 7 miles from Ashland, and the birthplace of Patrick Henry is situated about 15 miles away.
