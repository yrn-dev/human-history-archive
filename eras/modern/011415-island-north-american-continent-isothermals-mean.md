# Melville Island and Mean Annual Temperature

## Temperature Extremes
In the study of global temperatures, certain regions are noted for reaching the lowest and highest extremes. While some of the lowest temperatures on the globe are found in northern Asia, the lowest mean temperature observed anywhere is approximately 4 deg. Fahr. This measurement was recorded at Melville Island, which is located north of the American continent. In contrast, some of the highest temperatures on the globe are found in southern Asia, where the highest mean temperature attained is not much above 82 deg. Fahr.

## Isothermals of Mean Annual Temperature
Isothermals of mean annual temperature over northern Asia are characterized by curves that are tolerably regular in their outline. These curves exhibit western branches that are situated in a somewhat higher latitude than their eastern branches. 

In these regions, there is a general correlation between latitude and temperature: a reduction of 1 deg. of latitude corresponds approximately to a rise of 1/2 deg. Fahr. This relationship holds as far as 30 deg. N, where the mean temperature is approximately 75 deg. Fahr. As one moves farther south, the rate of temperature increase becomes slower.

## Regional Comparisons
For comparison, other regions exhibit different thermal patterns. On the north coast of eastern Siberia, the mean temperature is only a few degrees above zero Fahrenheit. In the Caspian basin, temperatures vary widely both seasonally and geographically. For instance, the January isotherm of 15 deg. F. skirts the northern shore of the basin, while the 40 deg. isotherm crosses its southern border. The July isotherm of 75 deg. crosses the middle section of the Caspian, nearly coinciding with the January isotherm of 25 deg.
