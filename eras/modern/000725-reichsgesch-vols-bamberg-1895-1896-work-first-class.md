# Oesterreichische Reichsgesch. (A. Luschin von Ebengreuth)

## Overview
*Oesterreichische Reichsgesch.* is a historical work authored by A. Luschin von Ebengreuth. Published in Bamberg, the work consists of two volumes released in 1895 and 1896. Within the context of constitutional history, it is characterized as a work of first-class importance.

## Context and Related Literature
The text is situated among several other significant works concerning the constitutional and imperial history of Austria. Other contemporary scholarly contributions in this field include:

*   **E. Werunsky**: *Oesterreichische Reichs- und Rechtsgeschichte* (Vienna, 1894).
*   **A. Bechmann**: *Lehrbuch der oesterreichischen Reichsgesch.* (Prague, 1895-1896).
*   **A. Huber**: *Oesterreichische Reichsgesch.* (Leipzig and Vienna, 1895), which saw a second edition by A. Dopsch in 1901.
*   **G. Kolmer**: *Parlament und Verfassung in Oesterreich*, a three-volume set covering the period from 1848 to 1885 (Vienna, 1902-1905).

Additionally, A. Luschin von Ebengreuth authored a related work titled *Grundriss der oesterreichischen Reichsgesch.*, published in Bamberg in 1899.
