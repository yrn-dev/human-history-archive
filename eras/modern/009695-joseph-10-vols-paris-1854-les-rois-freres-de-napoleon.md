# Baron Pierre Emmanuel Albert du Casse and His Works on the Bonaparte Family

## Biography of Baron du Casse
Baron Pierre Emmanuel Albert du Casse was a French historian born on November 16, 1813, in Bourges. His professional life was closely linked to the Bonaparte family. In 1849, he served as the aide-de-camp to Prince Jerome Bonaparte, the former king of Westphalia and then governor of the Invalides. Under Prince Jerome's commission, du Casse authored *Memoires pour servir a l'histoire de la campagne de 1812 en Russie* in 1852.

Du Casse later served as an attaché to Prince Napoleon, the son of Jerome, during the Crimean War. This experience led to the publication of a *Precis historique des operations militaires en Orient, de mars 1854 a octobre 1855* (1857). He expanded upon this work years later with *La Crimee et Sebastopol de 1853 a 1856, documents intimes et inedits*, and he produced a comprehensive list of French officers who were wounded or killed during that conflict in 1892. Baron du Casse died in 1893.

## Memoires et correspondance politique et militaire du roi Joseph
One of the significant historical contributions by du Casse is the work dealing with Joseph Bonaparte, titled *Memoires et correspondance politique et militaire du roi Joseph*. Published in Paris in 1854, this work consists of 10 volumes. In some records, it is referred to as *Memoires du roi Joseph* (1853-1855). 

As a sequel to this work, du Casse published *Histoire des negociations diplomatiques relatives aux traites de Morfontaine, de Luneville et d'Amiens* (also cited as *Histoire des ... traites de Mortfontaine, de Luneville et d'Amiens*), published between 1855 and 1857. This sequel included the unpublished correspondence between Emperor Napoleon I and Cardinal Fesch.

## Les Rois freres de Napoleon
Published in 1883, *Les Rois freres de Napoleon* is a collection of documents that du Casse compiled from papers held by the imperial family. The work is cited as a chief source for the lives and reigns of several of Napoleon's brothers:
* **Joseph Bonaparte**: It is listed among the works dealing with his life.
* **Louis Bonaparte**: It is considered one of the chief works regarding his life and reign.
* **Jerome Bonaparte**: It is cited as a primary work relating to him.

While the publication of *Les Rois freres de Napoleon* is noted for throwing significant light on Napoleon I and his entourage, it has been criticized for being edited with little care and is described as not being entirely trustworthy.

## Other Historical and Literary Works
Baron du Casse utilized papers from the imperial family to compile several other historical texts, including the *Memoires du prince Eugene* (1858-1860) and the *Refutation des memoires du duc de Raguse* (1857). The latter was partially inserted into volume IX of the *Memoires*. He also edited the *Journal de la reine Catherine de Westphalie* (1893) and worked on the *Correspondance* of Napoleon I, later publishing omitted letters in the *Revue historique*.

Beyond his formal histories, du Casse wrote *Les Dessous du coup d'etat* (1891) and *Souvenirs d'un officier du 2^e Zouaves*, both of which contain piquant anecdotes but are sometimes characterized as degenerating into "tittle-tattle." He also authored several slight novels; it is noted that the levity found in his fictional writing occasionally appeared in his serious historical publications.
