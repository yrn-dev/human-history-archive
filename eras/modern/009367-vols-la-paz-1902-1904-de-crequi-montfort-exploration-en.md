# Exploration and Documentation of Bolivia

The study of Bolivia's geography, industry, and natural resources has been documented through a variety of specialized reports, dictionaries, and exploration narratives produced between the late 19th and early 20th centuries. These works cover diverse aspects of the region, ranging from industrial monographs to the accounts of international travelers.

## Industrial and Statistical Records
Significant data regarding the economic and physical state of Bolivia has been preserved in official and statistical publications. M.V. Ballivian authored several key works, including *Apuntes sobre la industria de goma elastica, &c.* (1896) and *Noticia Politica, Geografica, Industrial, y Estadistica de Bolivia* (1900). Ballivian also collaborated with Eduardo Idiaquez to produce the *Diccionario Geografico de la Republica de Bolivia* in 1900.

Other essential statistical and administrative records include:
* *Breves Indicaciones para el Inmigrante y el Viajero a Bolivia* (1898).
* *Relaciones Geograficas de Bolivia*, found in the archives of the Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion (1898).
* *Monografias de la Industria Minera en Bolivia*, published in three parts between 1899 and 1900.
* *Bosquejo estadistico de Bolivia* (1878) by M. Dalence.
* *Sinopsis estadistica y geografica de la Republica de Bolivia*, a three-volume set published in La Paz between 1902 and 1904.
* The *Boletin de la Oficina Nacional de Inmigracion, Estadistica y Propaganda Geografica*.

## Geographical and Exploratory Works
The physical landscape of Bolivia has been explored and described by numerous authors. J.L. Moreno provided *Nociones de geografia de Bolivia* in 1889, while Carlos Matzenauer wrote *Bolivia in historischer, geographischer und cultureller Hinsicht* (1897). The International Bureau of the American Republics also released a *Geographical Sketch* of Bolivia's natural resources in 1904.

Explorations of the region's waterways and mountains are detailed in several accounts:
* G.E. Church documented the route to Bolivia via the River Amazon (1877) and the Rio de la Plata route (1902).
* Edward D. Mathews wrote of his travels through Bolivia and Peru, moving up the Madeira and Amazon rivers (1879).
* Sir W.M. Conway focused on the highlands in *Climbing and Exploration in the Bolivian Andes* (1903).
* Enrique Bolland conducted explorations in the Laguna Gaiba and the Alto Paraguay (1901).
* G. de Crequi-Montfort contributed a piece titled "Exploration en Bolivie" in *La Geographie*.

## International Perspectives and Narratives
Various foreign travelers and scholars have contributed to the archive of Bolivian history and geography. Andre Bresson detailed seven years of stays and voyages in South America (1886), and the Comte C. d'Ursel recorded his travels in Brazil and Bolivia (1879). Charles Wiener published *Perou et Bolivie* in 1880.

Additional regional contexts are provided by C.B. Cisneros and R.E. Garcia in their *Geografia Comercial de la America del Sur* (1898). General travel narratives that include Bolivia are found in the works of C.M. Pepper (*Panama to Patagonia*, 1906) and A. Petrocokino (*Along the Andes, in Bolivia, Peru, and Ecuador*, 1903).

## Military History
The archive also contains records of conflict involving the region, specifically the *Narracion de Guerra de Chile contra Peru y Bolivia* (1884) written by M.F. Soldan.
