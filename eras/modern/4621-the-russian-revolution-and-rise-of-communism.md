# The Russian Revolution and the Rise of Communism

## Overview
The Russian Revolution of 1917 was not a single event, but a series of upheavals that dismantled the Tsarist autocracy and led to the creation of the Soviet Union, the world's first socialist state. The revolution was precipitated by decades of social inequality, the incompetence of Tsar Nicholas II, and the catastrophic toll of World War I, which exhausted Russia's economy and decimated its peasantry and military.

The process unfolded in two primary stages. The **February Revolution** saw spontaneous protests in Petrograd lead to the abdication of the Tsar and the establishment of a weak Provisional Government. However, this government failed to exit the war or implement land reform, creating a power vacuum. The **October Revolution**, led by Vladimir Lenin and the Bolsheviks, successfully seized power under the slogan "Peace, Land, and Bread."

Following the seizure of power, Russia descended into a brutal Civil War (1917–1923) between the "Reds" (Bolsheviks) and the "Whites" (a loose coalition of monarchists, capitalists, and social democrats). The Bolshevik victory solidified the Communist Party's control, transitioning the state from a revolutionary movement into a centralized totalitarian regime.

## Key Facts
*   **Ideological Shift:** The revolution applied Marxism to a predominantly agrarian society. Lenin adapted Marx’s theories (Marxism-Leninism), arguing that a "vanguard party" was necessary to lead the proletariat.
*   **The Red Terror:** To consolidate power, the Bolsheviks established the Cheka (secret police) to eliminate "class enemies" and political dissidents.
*   **Historical Debate:** Historians continue to debate whether the October Revolution was a genuine popular uprising or a disciplined *coup d'état* carried out by a small minority. While some emphasize the support of the soviets (workers' councils), others point to the strategic seizure of infrastructure by the Bolsheviks.

## Did You Know?
1. **The Calendar Gap:** The "October Revolution" actually took place in November according to the Gregorian calendar; Russia was using the Julian calendar at the time.
2. **The Romanov Fate:** Tsar Nicholas II and his entire immediate family were executed by Bolsheviks in a basement in Yekaterinburg in July 1918 to prevent them from becoming a rallying point for the White Army.
3. **War Communism:** During the Civil War, the state implemented "War Communism," which involved the forced requisitioning of grain from peasants to feed the cities and the army.
