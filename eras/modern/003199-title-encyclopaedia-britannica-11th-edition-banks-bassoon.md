## Early Bassoons and Musical Instruments
The archive contains detailed references regarding the history and documentation of early bassoons and related musical instruments. Documentation of these instruments can be found in various catalogs and manuscripts, including:

*   **British Museum Collections:** Harl. MS. 2034, fol. 207_b_ provides references communicated by Augustus Hughes-Hughes. These are found in the appendix to part iii (Instrumental Music and Works on Music) of a *Catalogue of MS. Music in the British Museum* published in London between 1908 and 1909. This appendix lists typical musical instruments found in illuminated manuscripts or described in other manuscripts within the British Museum, providing brief descriptions and full references.
*   **Comparative Instruments:** The archive notes comparisons between Randle Holme's "double curtail" and the dolcian in C (pl. vi. H. of Capt. C. R. Day's catalogue). Additionally, a dolcian or "single curtail" by J. C. Denner is referenced in Paul de Wit's *Katalog des Musikhistorischen Museums von Paul de Wit* (Leipzig, 1903), specifically on page 127, No. 380, with an illustration on page 121. This collection was later transferred to Cologne.
*   **Historical Descriptions:** Both Michael Praetorius, in his *Syntagma Musicum* (Wolfenbuettel, 1618), and Mersenne provide descriptions and figures of these early forms of bassoons.
