# Towns Prominent for Industry or Commerce

Across various global regions, certain smaller towns have attained prominence not through administrative status as provincial or national capitals, but through their specialized industrial output or strategic commercial positions. These centers often develop around specific natural resources, transportation junctions, or historical trade routes.

## Industrial and Commercial Centers in Bolivia

In Bolivia, several smaller towns in the department of Potosi are noted for their economic roles. Pulacayo serves as a mining center for the Huanchaca silver mining company, employing 3,200 men in its surface works and mines. Uyuni acts as a converging point for important highways, projected railways, and the junction of the Antofagasta and Oruro railway with the Pulacayo branch. Near the Argentine frontier, Tupiza serves as a mining and commercial center and the terminus for the Argentine railway extension.

Other notable towns include Viacha, a railway station on the line from Guaqui to Alto de La Paz and the starting point for a projected railway to Oruro. In the department of Cochabamba, Totora and Tarata are identified as important trading centers, while Ascension in the department of Santa Cruz is a large mission station.

Bolivia's broader industrial profit is heavily tied to forest products. Rubber-collecting, which began between 1880 and 1890, was the most profitable industry, reaching an annual output of nearly 3,500 metric tons before the best forests were transferred to Brazil in 1903. The collecting of cinchona bark is another old industry; the Bolivian product is highly valued for its high percentage of quinine sulphate.

## European Industrial Towns

### Germany
Several German towns are defined by their industrial specializations:
*   **Dortmund:** The chief commercial center of the Prussian province of Westphalia. Located in the Westphalian coal basin with nearby iron ore, it competes with cities like Essen and Duisburg in the iron industry, producing sewing machines, safes, machinery, wire ropes, mining plant, and steel railway rails. It also maintains extensive breweries and trades in wood and corn.
*   **Lüneburg:** This town's importance is derived from a productive salt-spring used since the 10th century and the lime and gypsum quarries of the Kalkberg, which support its cement works.
*   **Grimma:** Located in the kingdom of Saxony, its industries include machine building, ironfounding, and dyeworks, as well as the manufacture of gloves and paper. Agriculture and gardening are also important.
*   **Kreuznach:** A town in the Prussian Rhine province known for the manufacture of glass, tobacco, leather, and marble-polishing. It also has a trade in corn and wine.
*   **Calbe (Calbe-an-der-Saale):** A railway junction in Prussian Saxony where industries include the manufacture of bricks, sugar, stoves, paper, and cloth, as well as wool-weaving. Soft coal is mined in the vicinity.

### Belgium and Sweden
In Belgium, during the thirteenth century, trade and industry were central to the people's lives. Ghent became a center for commercial relations between Germany and Flanders, while Antwerp grew into a commercial center via its connection to the Cologne-Bruges road through the Scheldt.

In Sweden, the town of Boras, founded in 1632 by King Gustavus Adolphus, ranks among the first twelve towns in the country for population and manufacturing value. Its industries are primarily textile, featuring a technical weaving school and numerous weaving and cotton spinning mills.

## Other International Commercial Centers

### Russia
Dvinsk (known as Dunaburg until 1893) is a fortress town and important commercial center in western Russia. It is particularly prominent in the trade of timber, tallow, hemp, and flax. Its industrial establishments include limekilns, brick and tile works, flour-mills, saw-mills, and breweries and tanneries.

### Philippines
In the province of Sámar, the town of Calbáyog is noted for an important export trade in hemp shipped to Manila. The town also produces copra in considerable quantities and has fine timber in its vicinity.
