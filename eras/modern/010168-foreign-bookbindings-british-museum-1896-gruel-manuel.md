# Foreign Bookbindings in the British Museum (1896) and L. Gruel, Manuel

## Overview of Bookbinding Authorities
The study of bookbinding is supported by a variety of specialized texts and authorities. Among the works documenting the history and art of the craft are those focusing on specific collections and regional styles. Notable among these is *Foreign Bookbindings in the British Museum*, published in 1896 by W.Y. Fletcher. This work complements Fletcher's earlier 1895 publication, *English Bookbindings in the British Museum*.

Other recognized authorities in the field include H.B. Wheatley, who wrote *Remarkable Bindings in the British Museum* (1889), and J.W. Zaehnsdorf, author of *The Art of Bookbinding* (1880). Technical and historical perspectives are further provided by S.T. Prideaux in *Historical Sketch of Bookbinding* (1893) and H.P. Horne in *The Binding of Books* (1894).

## Regional and Specialized Binding Studies
The archive identifies several works dedicated to specific national or thematic traditions of bookbinding. French binding is covered in E. Thoinan's *Les Relieurs francais* (1893) and O. Uzanne's *La Relieure moderne* (1887). Additionally, C.J. Davenport has contributed several works to the field, including *Royal English Bookbindings* (1896), *Cantor Lectures on Bookbinding* (1898), and *English Embroidered Bookbindings* (1899).

## L. Gruel and the Manuel de l'amateur de relieures
Included in the authorities for bookbinding is L. Gruel, who authored the *Manuel de l'amateur de relieures*, published in 1887. This text serves as a resource for those interested in the collection and study of bindings.

## Related Bibliographical Resources
The broader study of books and their physical properties is supported by various bibliographies. Alfred William Pollard, the Assistant Keeper of Printed Books at the British Museum and Hon. Secretary of the Bibliographical Society, is a key figure in this area. He has served as the editor of *Bibliographica* and *Books about Books*, and as joint-editor of the *Library*.

General bibliographical guides also include the *Manuel de bibliographie generale* by Henri Stein (1898), the *Manuel de bibliographie historique* by Ch. V. Langlois (1901), and *A Register of National Bibliography* by W.P. Courtney (1905). For those utilizing the British Museum's resources, the *List of Books of Reference in the Reading-Room of the British Museum* (first published in 1889) and the *Subject-index of the Modern Works added to the Library of the British Museum in the years 1881-1900*, edited by G.K. Fortescue, provide extensive entries on bibliographical works.
