# Ad. Jurien de la Graviere

Ad. Jurien de la Graviere was a writer and historian who produced several readable treatises focused on naval history, specifically concerning the epoch of the regular wars and the beylerbeys. His works are cited as authorities on the history of the Barbary states and the maritime activities of the Mediterranean.

## Major Works and Contributions

Jurien de la Graviere authored several publications, all of which were published in Paris. Among these are:

*   ***Les Corsaires barbaresques et la marine de Soliman le Grand*** (1887): In this work, the author describes the campaigns of the Barbarossas. The text is noted for presenting these events in a readable manner, benefiting from the author's technical knowledge.
*   ***Doria et Barberousse*** (1886): This treatise also covers the campaigns of the Barbarossas.
*   ***Les Chevaliers de Malte*** (1887): A work dealing with the epoch of the beylerbeys and regular wars.
*   ***La Guerre de Chypre*** (1888): Another treatise focusing on the period of the beylerbeys and regular wars.

## Historical Context and Subject Matter

The writings of Jurien de la Graviere primarily concern the "corsairs" of the Barbary coast. In the Mediterranean context, the term "corsair" (derived from the French and Medieval Latin *cursarius*) referred to privateers from the Barbary coast who plundered the shipping of Christian nations. While the term became synonymous with "pirate" in English, these individuals were not strictly pirates because they operated under commissions from their respective governments.

Jurien de la Graviere's work on the Barbary corsairs and the navy of Soliman the Grand provides a technical and readable account of these maritime forces. His research contributes to the broader historical record of the Barbary states, alongside other authorities such as Luis del Marmol Carvajal, Diego de Haedo, and Pere Pierre Dan.
