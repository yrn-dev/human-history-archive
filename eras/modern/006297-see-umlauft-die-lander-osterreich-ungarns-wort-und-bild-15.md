# Die Länder Österreich-Ungarns in Wort und Bild

## Overview
*Die Länder Österreich-Ungarns in Wort und Bild* is a multi-volume collection published in Vienna between 1881 and 1889. The work consists of 15 volumes and serves as a descriptive record of the lands within the Austro-Hungarian empire.

## Regional Coverage and Content
The collection provides detailed accounts of various territories and administrative regions. Specific volumes are dedicated to different areas of the empire:

*   **The Archduchy of Austria:** The collection includes *Das Erzherzogtum Oesterreich unter der Enns* (Volume I), which covers the original archduchy. This region, including Upper Austria, is identified as the nucleus of the Austrian empire and represents the oldest possession of the House of Habsburg within its current dominions.
*   **Bohemia:** Volume VII of the collection focuses on Bohemia. This province is characterized by a high level of educational advancement, maintaining the lowest proportion of illiterates among the Austrian provinces. The educational system is led by two universities in Prague—one Czech and one German.
