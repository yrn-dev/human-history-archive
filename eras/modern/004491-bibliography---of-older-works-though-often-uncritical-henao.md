## Regional History and Legislation of the Basque Provinces
Documentation regarding the Basque Provinces includes several key historical and legislative works. R. P. Henao's *Averiguaciones de las Antiguedades de Cantabria* (Salamanca, 1688; new edition 1894) is noted as valuable, despite being uncritical. For information on the customs and manners of the people, R. P. M. de Larramendi, S.J., wrote *Corografia de Guipuzcoa* around 1750, which was first printed in Barcelona in 1882. Additionally, D. Guillermo Bowles' *Introduccion a la Historia Natural, y a la Geografia Fisica de Espana* (Madrid, 1775) contains excellent chapters on the Basque Provinces.

Details regarding the rights, duties, and life of a Basque citizen as of 1780 are found in the dictionary *El Guipuzcoano instruido* (San Sebastian, 1780). Local information is also available in the *Diccionario Geografico-Historico de Espana* (Madrid, 1802), specifically tome i and ii, though this work exhibits a strong bias toward the central government.

Legislative records and *fueros* are covered in *Historia de la Legislacion ... civil de Espana* by Cayetano Manrique and A. Marichalar, Marques de Montesa, as well as *Fueros de Navarra Vizcaya, Guipuzcoa y Alava* (2nd ed., 1868). D. Pablo de Gorosabel's *Noticia de las cosas memorables de Guipuzcoa* (Tolosa, 1899-1901) includes legislative acts up to May 1900 in its final volume by C. de Echegaray. Other notable works include D. Carmelo de Echegaray's *Las Provincias Vascongadas a Fines de la Edad Media* (1895), Dr E. de Labayru's *Historia de Bizcaya*, and a *Compendio* of that history by Fermin Herran (1903).

Furthermore, the municipal archives of all towns were examined, indexed, and catalogued by D. Carmelo de Echegaray (Cronista de las Provincias Vascongadas) and colleagues including F. Soraluce and D. Serapio Mugica, leading to the discovery of missing manuscripts and documents. The Basque mining industry's development from 1870 to 1899 is detailed in *Las Minas de hierro de la provincia de Vizcaya*.

## Biographical and Individual Studies
The archive contains bibliographical references for several prominent figures:

**René Descartes:** Biographical works include A. Baillet's *La Vie de M. Des Cartes* (1691), which is exhaustive but uncritical, and A. Hoffmann's *René Descartes* (1905). Elizabeth S. Haldane's *Descartes, his Life and Times* (1905) provides a full bibliography. Other sources include Richard Lowndes (1878), A. Barbier (1901), and J. P. Mahaffy (1902), the latter of whom included an appendix on mathematical work by Frederick Purser. Correspondence with the Princess Palatine is covered by C. J. Jeannel (1869) and Victor de Swarte (1904). Original letters were edited by Claude Clerselier in 1657.

**Cardinal Bellarmine:** The primary source for his life is his Latin *Autobiography* (1675; 1753), later reprinted by Dollinger and Reusch in *Die Selbst-biographie des Cardinals Bellarmin* (1887). Early works by J. Fuligatti (1624, 1650), D. Bartoli (1678), and M. Cervin (1622) are characterized as panegyrics of small historical value. Modern studies include J.B. Couderc's *Le Venerable Cardinal Bellarmin* (1893) and X. le Bachelet's article in A. Vacant's *Dict. de theol, cat*. A complete bibliography of Bellarmine's works and controversial writings against him is found in C. Sommervogel's *Bibliotheque de la Compagnie de Jesus* (1890 et seq.).

**Johann Sebastian Bach:** P. Spitta's biography (1873 and 1880) is described as having superseded all previous works. Other biographies include J. N. Forkel (translated 1820), C. H. Bitter (1865), Ernest David (1882), E. Heinrich (1885), and A. Pirro (1907). A. Schweitzer also wrote *J. S. Bach: Le Musicien poete*.

## Technical and General Reference Works
**Billiards:** Older scientific works on the game include treatises by E. White (1807), *Le Musee des jeux* (1820), Monsieur Mingaud (1834), Kentfield (1839), Edward Russell Mardon (1849), Turner (1849), Captain Crawley (1866-1875), Roberts (1868), Fred. Hardy (1867), and Joseph Bennett (1873). These have been largely superseded by modern authorities such as J. Roberts (1898), W. Cook, J.P. Buchanan, Broadfoot, Locock, M. Vignaux (1889), A. Howard Cady, and Thatcher (1898). Hemming's *Billiards Mathematically Treated* focuses on the mathematical aspects.

**General Bibliography:** "Bibliographies of bibliographies" include Dr Julius Petzholdt's *Bibliotheca bibliographica* (1866), Henri Stein's *Manuel de bibliographie generale* (1898), Ch. V. Langlois' *Manuel de bibliographie historique* (1901), and W.P. Courtney's *A Register of National Bibliography* (1905). Reference lists from the British Museum (1889 and 1881-1900) and Professor Ferguson's *Some Aspects of Bibliography* (1900) are also noted.
