### General Information and Definitions
The work provides detailed definitions for various objects. A demijohn is defined as a glass bottle or jar with a narrow neck and a large round body, encased in wicker-work and provided with handles; it can also refer to an earthenware jar with similar wicker covering. The capacity of a demijohn typically ranges from two to twelve gallons, with five gallons being the common size. According to the *New English Dictionary*, the term is an adaptation of the French *Dame Jeanne* (or Dame Jane), reflecting a practice of applying personal names to objects, similar to the use of "Jack" or "Toby" for a jug.
