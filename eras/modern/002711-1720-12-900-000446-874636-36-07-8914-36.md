## Colonial Settlements in North America
Around 1720, the French established several permanent settlements in the region known as the Illinois country. These settlements grew from the bartering posts of French traders and the missions of the Jesuits. Key establishments from this period include:

* **Cahokia**: Founded approximately at the same time as Kaskaskia, located slightly below the mouth of the Missouri river.
* **Fort Chartres**: Founded in 1720 on the Mississippi river between Cahokia and Kaskaskia. This fort was intended to serve as a link in a chain of fortifications extending from the St Lawrence.

### Charles Bonnet
Charles Bonnet, a Swiss philosophical writer and naturalist, was born in Geneva on March 13, 1720. Born into a French family that had been driven into Switzerland due to 16th-century religious persecution, Bonnet eventually pursued the study of natural science despite making law his profession. His later scientific contributions included experiments on parthenogenesis in tree-lice and the discovery of *stigmata* (respiration pores) in butterflies and caterpillars.

### Giovanni Battista Bononcini
Giovanni Battista Bononcini was an Italian musical composer who spent the years between 1700 and 1720 dividing his time between Italy and Vienna. In 1720, he was summoned to London by the Royal Academy of Music. While in London, he produced several operas and received the protection of the Marlborough family.
