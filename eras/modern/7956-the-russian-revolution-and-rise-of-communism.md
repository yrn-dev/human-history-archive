# The Russian Revolution and the Rise of Communism

## Overview
The Russian Revolution of 1917 was not a single event, but a series of upheavals that dismantled the Tsarist autocracy and led to the creation of the Soviet Union, the world's first socialist state. The collapse of the Romanov dynasty was precipitated by systemic social inequality, an archaic political structure, and the catastrophic toll of World War I.

The revolution occurred in two primary stages. The **February Revolution** saw the abdication of Tsar Nicholas II and the establishment of a Provisional Government. However, this government failed to address the populace's demands for "Peace, Land, and Bread." This power vacuum allowed Vladimir Lenin and the Bolsheviks to seize control during the **October Revolution**, implementing a Marxist-Leninist ideology that sought to abolish private property and establish a "dictatorship of the proletariat."

Historians continue to debate the nature of the October Revolution. Some, following the traditionalist view, see it as a popular uprising of the masses. Others, such as Richard Pipes, argue it was a disciplined *coup d'état* carried out by a small minority of Bolsheviks who manipulated the chaos of the era to seize power.

## Key Facts
*   **The Role of WWI:** The war acted as a catalyst, draining Russia's economy and causing millions of casualties, which eroded the legitimacy of the Tsar.
*   **The Soviets:** These were grassroots councils of workers and soldiers. While initially democratic, they were eventually subsumed by the Bolshevik Party's central authority.
*   **War Communism to NEP:** To survive the Civil War, Lenin implemented "War Communism" (state seizure of grain). After its failure, he introduced the **New Economic Policy (NEP)** in 1921, which allowed limited capitalism to stabilize the economy.
*   **The Red Terror:** To consolidate power, the Bolsheviks established the Cheka (secret police) to eliminate "class enemies" and political dissidents.

## Did You Know?
1. **The Calendar Gap:** The "October Revolution" actually took place in November according to the Gregorian calendar; Russia was still using the Julian calendar at the time.
2. **The Romanov Fate:** Tsar Nicholas II and his entire immediate family were executed by Bolsheviks in a basement in Yekaterinburg in July 1918 to prevent them from becoming a rallying point for the White Army.
3. **Lenin's Influence:** While Marx provided the theory, Lenin adapted it into "Marxism-Leninism," arguing that a professional vanguard party was necessary to lead the workers toward revolution.
