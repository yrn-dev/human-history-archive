# Borga

Borga, known in Finnish as Porvoo, is a seaport located in the province of Nyland within the grand duchy of Finland. The city is situated at the entrance of the river Borga into the Gulf of Finland and is positioned approximately 33 miles northwest of Helsingfors, accessible by rail.

## Demographics and Urban Development
The population of Borga has seen steady growth over the 19th and early 20th centuries. In 1810, the population was 1,693; by 1870, it had increased to 3,478; and by 1904, it reached 5,255. While Borga was once a city of great importance and dignity, its prominence has been somewhat eclipsed by the rapid growth of Helsingfors.

## Religious and Educational Institutions
Borga serves as the seat of a Lutheran bishopric. The jurisdiction of this bishopric extends over the provinces of St Michel and Viborg, as well as portions of Nyland and Tavastehus. The city is home to a beautiful cathedral and a high school. The high school is notable for having been the place where the well-known Finnish poet Runeberg lectured for many years.

## Governance and Economy
The city is the seat of a court of appeal. Its economy is driven by specific industrial manufactures and trade. The principal industries in Borga are the manufacture of tobacco and the weaving of sail-cloth. The chief articles of trade for the seaport include furs, butter, and wood.

## Political History
Borga played a significant role in the political history of Finland. In 1809, the estates of Finland were summoned to a special diet to determine the future of the country, and Borga served as the meeting place. It was within the city's cathedral that Emperor Alexander I, acting as the grand duke of Finland, pledged himself to maintain the liberties and the constitution of the grand duchy.
