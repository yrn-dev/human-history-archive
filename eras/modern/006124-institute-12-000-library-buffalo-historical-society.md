# Catholic Institute and the Library of the Buffalo Historical Society

## The Catholic Institute
The Catholic Institute is one of the notable libraries located in Buffalo. According to archival records, the institution maintains a collection of approximately 12,000 books.

## The Buffalo Historical Society Library
Founded in 1862, the library of the Buffalo Historical Society possesses a collection of 26,600 volumes. The library is housed in a handsome building located in Delaware Park. This structure was originally erected as the New York state building for the Pan-American Exposition held in 1901.

## Context of Buffalo's Libraries and Education
The libraries of the Catholic Institute and the Buffalo Historical Society are part of a broader network of educational and reference resources in the city. Other significant libraries in the area include:
* **The Buffalo Public Library:** Founded in 1837 and housed in a building erected in 1887 with a valuation of $1,000,000, containing roughly 300,000 pamphlets and books.
* **The Grosvenor:** A reference library founded in 1859, containing 7,000 pamphlets and 75,000 volumes.
* **The John C. Lord:** A collection of 10,620 volumes located within the building of the Historical Society.
* **The Law Library (8th judicial district):** Containing 17,000 volumes.

The city's academic landscape further includes the University of Buffalo, organized in 1845, which features schools of pharmacy (1886), law (1887), dentistry (1892), and medicine (1845). Additionally, Canisius College, a Jesuit institution for men established in 1870 and chartered in 1883, maintains its own library of about 26,000 volumes.

## Related Institutions and Facilities
The Buffalo Historical Society building in Delaware Park is situated near other principal structures, such as the Albright art gallery. Within the city's public library building, the Buffalo Society of Natural Sciences also maintains a museum.
