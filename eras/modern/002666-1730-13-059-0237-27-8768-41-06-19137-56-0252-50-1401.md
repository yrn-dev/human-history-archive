## Political Transitions in Denmark
In 1730, the reign of Frederick IV came to an end. Frederick IV had ruled from 1699 to 1730, with the final ten years of his reign focused on the development and nursing of national resources following the effects of the Great Northern War, which had impacted the country nearly as severely as it had Sweden. During his tenure, the court was noted for being seriously pious and contributing significantly to education. Frederick IV also demonstrated a deep interest in the welfare of the peasantry and implemented a wise economy to bring the national debt within manageable limits. A notable reform occurred in 1722, when serfdom was abolished for all peasants on royal estates who were born after his accession. Following the end of his reign in 1730, Christian VI ascended to the throne, ruling from 1730 to 1746.
