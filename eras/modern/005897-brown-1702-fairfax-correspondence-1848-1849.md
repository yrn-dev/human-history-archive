# Brown (1702) and the Fairfax Correspondence (1848-1849)

## Overview of Miscellania Aulica
The work titled *Miscellania Aulica* was written by T. Brown and published in 1702. Within the historical record, this text is cited as one of the sources used to trace the life of Buckingham.

## The Fairfax Correspondence
The *Fairfax Correspondence*, published between 1848 and 1849, serves as another authority for the study of the life of Buckingham. It is listed alongside various other biographical and historical materials, including works by Brian Fairfax and Lady Burghclere, as well as entries in *Biographia Britannica* and Wood's *Athenae Oxon*.
