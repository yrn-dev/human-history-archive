## Administrative Divisions
The republic is organized into one territory and eight departments. These are further subdivided into 232 vice-cantons, 415 cantons, 54 provinces, 18 missions, and one colony.

## National and Provincial Capitals
Four of the provincial capitals have served as the national capital: Oruro, Cochabamba, La Paz, and Sucre (also known as Chuquisaca). While Sucre was originally chosen as the capital, the capital was located at La Paz following the revolution of 1898. La Paz is described as the commercial metropolis and is more accessible than Sucre.

## Prominent Towns and Industry
Several smaller towns are noted for their commercial or industrial significance, particularly within the department of Potosi:
*   **Pulacayo:** A mining centre for the Huanchaca silver mining company, employing 3,200 men in surface works and mines, with a population of 6,512.
*   **Uyuni:** A junction for several projected railways and important highways, as well as the junction of the Antofagasta and Oruro railway with the Pulacayo branch; population 1,587.
*   **Tupiza:** A mining and commercial centre located near the Argentine frontier and serving as the terminus for the Argentine railway extension into Bolivia; population 1,644.

Other notable locations include Viacha, a small station on the railway from Guaqui to Alto de La Paz (14 m. away) and the starting point for a projected railway to Oruro, with a population of 1,670. In the department of Cochabamba, Totora (3,501) and Tarata (4,681) serve as important trading centres. In the department of Santa Cruz, Ascension (population 4,784) is a large mission station located in the Chiquitos hills.
