# Bulawayo

## Geography and Location
Bulawayo serves as the capital of Matabeleland, which is the western province of southern Rhodesia, South Africa. The town is situated on a tableland positioned between the Zambezi and Limpopo rivers, at an elevation of 4,469 feet above sea level. In terms of regional connectivity, it is located 1,362 meters north-east of Cape Town when traveling by rail. The nearest port is Beira, which is 398 miles away in a direct line, though the distance by railway is 675 miles.

## Urban Layout and Infrastructure
The town is designed around a large market square located in the center, with roads extending from this point in regular lines to the north, south, east, and west. The roads running north and south are designated as streets and are given names, while those running east and west are called avenues and are numbered. Rhodes Street runs through the center of the Market Square.

The town's infrastructure includes water and electric supply works and the railway station, both of which are situated in the south-west quarter. One railway line runs north-west from Bulawayo as part of the Cape to Cairo connection, crossing the Zambezi river just below the Victoria Falls.

## Public Buildings and Landmarks
Bulawayo features numerous private and handsome public buildings. A monument dedicated to the 257 settlers who were killed during the Matabele rebellion of 1896 stands in front of the stock exchange. Additionally, a colossal bronze statue of Cecil Rhodes is located at the junction of two principal streets.

A notable feature of the town is a tree-lined avenue, nearly 1½ miles long and 130 feet broad, which leads from the town to Government House. Government House was constructed on the site of the royal kraal of the chieftain Lobengula; the tree under which Lobengula sat while giving judgment has been preserved.

## Surrounding Areas and Natural Features
To the east of the town lies a residential suburb, which is preceded by a large park and botanical gardens. The surrounding district is intersected by a number of gold reefs, some of which are used for gold mining. To the south-south-east of the town are the Matoppo Hills. Cecil Rhodes is buried in a grave located in one of these hills, 33 miles from Bulawayo.
