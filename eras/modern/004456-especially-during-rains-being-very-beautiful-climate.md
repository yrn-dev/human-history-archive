# Regional Climates and Environmental Conditions

The archive's records provide detailed observations of climatic conditions across various global regions, ranging from the extreme frosts of Scythia to the tropical humidity of the Konkan and the temperate heights of Madeira.

## South Asian Climates
In the Berar region of India, specifically in the town of Basim, the climate is noted as being superior to other districts in the area. During the summer months, hot winds that blow during the day are succeeded by cool breezes at night. The geography of the Basim taluk includes a fertile tableland approximately 1000 ft. above sea level, while the Pasud taluk features low hills and wide stretches of woodland. In Pasud, some peaks reach 2000 ft., and the scenery is described as very beautiful, particularly during the rains.

Across the broader presidency, climatic variety is significant. Upper Sind is characterized by extreme heat and dryness, resembling the sultry deserts of Africa due to its sandy soil. In Upper Sind, temperatures in the shade have been recorded at 130 deg., and in Lower Sind, the mean maximum temperature during the six hottest months is 98 deg. F. in the shade, with the waters of the Indus reaching blood heat. In contrast, the Konkan is hot and moist, with monsoon rains sometimes approaching 300 in. The Deccan table-land above the Ghats and the southern Mahratta country generally possess an agreeable climate, except during the hot months. In detached heights such as Singarh and Mahabaleshwar, Europeans can go out at all hours with impunity. Bombay Island is typically cooled by sea breezes but remains oppressively hot in October and May. The south-west monsoon usually begins in the first week of June; from June to October, travel is unpleasant and difficult, though the monsoon has little influence in Sind.

In Calcutta, the climate is hot and damp. The period from November to March is a pleasant cold season, while April, May, and June are hot. The monsoon months, spanning June to October, are marked by malaria and damp heat. The mean annual temperature is 79° F., ranging from 85° in the hot season and 83° in the rains to 72° in the cool season.

## African and Middle Eastern Climates
In Cairo, the climate is generally healthy with a mean temperature of approximately 68° F. Rain is rare, though districts near the Nile become damp during September, October, and November as floodwaters subside. Early morning fogs occur in winter, while the rise of the water and the prevalent north wind help keep the air cool during the summer.

Along certain coast regions, four distinct seasons are observed: the "little rains" (September 15 to December 1), the harmattan or long dry season (December 1 to March 15), the season of the great rains (March 15 to July 15), and the short dry season (July 15 to September 15). Near the sea, the average temperature is about 80 deg. F. During the harmattan, the thermometer drops about 10 deg., the atmosphere loses all moisture, woodwork may warp and crack, and vegetation droops. A sea-breeze tempers the climate for nine months of the year, reaching as far inland as Abomey (60 m.). In the interior, there are only two seasons: the rainy season (June to October) and the dry season (November to May), with rains diminishing in the north.

In other provinces, the summer is very hot with a mean temperature of 97 deg. F. No rain falls from April to November; rains begin in November, and winter temperatures drop to 46 deg. F.

## European and Atlantic Regions
The island of Madeira is described as having a very temperate climate, where the thermometer seldom drops below 60° and occasionally rises to 90° F. Rain falls in all seasons, and heavy dews are found on mountainous parts. This variety of elevation allows the island to produce both temperate and tropical products.

In other coastal regions, such as the north coast of the Black Sea, winters are cold with heavy snow and torrents of rain driven by winds from the Russian steppes. East of Samsun, the climate is more moderate, though summer heat is enervating and damp, with vegetation becoming almost subtropical near Trebizond. The south coast experiences mild winters with occasional frosts and heavy rain, and very great summer heat. The west coast is moderate, though cold north winds reach as far south as Smyrna. A daily north wind called the *inbat* blows from noon until near sunset during the summer.

## Other Global Regions
In Scythia, the climate is exceedingly severe. For eight months of the year, the frost is so intense that poured water cannot make mud unless a fire is kindled, and the sea and Kimmerian Bosphorus freeze over. This allows Scythians to drive wagons into the country of the Sindians. Unlike other regions, there is almost no rain during the usual season; instead, it rains continually in the summer, which is also when thunder is most frequent.

Bolivia, located in the torrid zone, experiences temperatures dictated by prevailing winds, mountain barriers, and elevation. This ranges from tropical lowlands to Arctic cold on snow-capped peaks. In the north and east of the Cordillera Oriental, rain falls throughout the year, though November to March is the rainy season. On the west side of the Cordillera, rainfall is limited in the north and diminishes toward the south until the land is absolutely barren. The northern plateau occasionally experiences brief, furious rain-storms and frequent electrical wind storms.
