## Political Developments in the Balkans
The growth of Balkan nations was often hindered by international jealousies related to the "Eastern Question." Various politicians sought a combination of young states to resist foreign interference. The Servian statesman Ristich was an early advocate of this idea; later, King Charles of Rumania and Prince Alexander of Bulgaria viewed the movement favorably. However, the project was sidelined following the revolt of Eastern Rumelia, the Servo-Bulgarian War, and the coercion of Greece.

In 1891, Tricoupis proposed an offensive alliance of Balkan states to partition the Sultan's European possessions, a scheme that found favor in Servia but was frustrated by Stamboloff. In 1897, Greece rejected a Bulgarian proposal for joint pacific action regarding reforms in Macedonia.

## Religious Movements
The modern Baptist movement spread across Europe during the 19th century. In Protestant countries and Russia, the movement began without intervention from England or America. Baptist churches were established in Switzerland in 1849 and in Rumania in 1869. Bulgaria followed in 1884.
