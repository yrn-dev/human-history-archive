# Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis quae feruntur Magna Moralia et Ethica Eudemia

*Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis quae feruntur Magna Moralia et Ethica Eudemia* is a scholarly work published in 1844 by the German scholar Hermann Bonitz. The text serves as a critical commentary on the works attributed to Aristotle, specifically the *Magna Moralia* and the *Ethica Eudemia*.

## Author Biography
Hermann Bonitz (1814–1888) was a prominent German scholar born on July 29, 1814, in Langensalza, Saxony. His academic foundation was established through studies at Berlin under Lachmann and Bockh, as well as at Leipzig under G. Hermann. 

Bonitz held a variety of educational and academic positions throughout his career. He served as a teacher at the Blochmann institute in Dresden starting in 1836, and later as an Oberlehrer in Berlin at the Friedrich-Wilhelms gymnasium (1838) and the Graues Kloster (1840). He expanded his professional reach as a professor at the gymnasium at Stettin in 1842 and later as a professor at the university of Vienna in 1849. His administrative and honorary contributions included membership in the imperial academy (1854), membership in the council of education (1864), and the directorship of the Graues Kloster gymnasium in 1867.

Bonitz retired in 1888 and died in Berlin on July 25 of that same year. While he was influential in the Prussian high school system of teaching and examination after 1882, he gained international recognition primarily as a commentator on the works of Aristotle and Plato.

## Scholarly Context and Related Works
The *Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis quae feruntur Magna Moralia et Ethica Eudemia* is part of a broader body of work by Bonitz dedicated to classical philology and the analysis of ancient texts. His contributions to the study of Aristotle include several other significant publications:
* *Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis Libros Metaphysicos* (1842)
* *Aristotelis Metaphysica* (1848–1849)
* *Uber die Kategorien des A.* (1853)
* *Aristotelische Studien* (1862–1867)
* *Index Aristotelicus* (1870)

Additionally, Bonitz produced work on Alexander Aphrodisianus (*Alexandri Aphrodisiensis Commentarius in Libras Metaphysicos Aristotelis*, 1847) and Plato, including *Disputationes Platonicae Duae* (1837) and *Platonische Studien* (3rd ed., 1886). His scholarly interests extended beyond these philosophers to include works on Thukydides, Sophokles, and the origins of Homeric poems.
