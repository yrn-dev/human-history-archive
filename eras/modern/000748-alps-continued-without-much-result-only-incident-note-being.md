# The Alps

The Alps are a major mountainous region, with specific ranges and peaks located within the duchy and crown-land of Austria known as Carniola.

## Geography of the Alps in Carniola
Carniola is a mountainous region where the Alps occupy the northern portion. This area is traversed by several specific ranges belonging to the southern zone of the Eastern Alps, including the Steiner Alps, the Karawankas, and the Julian Alps.

The Julian Alps feature the Triglav (also known as Terglou), which reaches a height of 9,394 feet. The Triglav is characterized by three sugar-loaf peaks and contains the only glacier in the province on its northern declivity. This mass serves as a barrier between the Italian, Slavonic, and German races and acts as the dividing range between the Alps and the Karst Mountains. Other notable high peaks in the Julian Alps include the Jaluz (8,708 feet) and the Mangart (8,784 feet).

The Karawankas form the boundary between Carniola and Carinthia. Their highest peak is the Stuhlberg (also called Stou), reaching 7,344 feet, and the range is traversed by the Loibl Pass at 4,492 feet. The Steiner Alps (or Santhaler Alps) continue from the Karawankas, with the highest peak being the Grintovc (or Grintouz) at 8,393 feet. The Grintovc is situated on the threefold boundary of Styria, Carniola, and Carinthia.

## Military History in the Alps
The Alps were the site of conflict during a Spanish-Piedmontese war. While the broader conflict in the region continued without significant results, a notable incident occurred at Casteldelfino, where a combat was won by the king of Sardinia in person.
