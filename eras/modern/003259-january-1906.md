# January 1906

The month of January 1906 was marked by significant political shifts in Britain and France, as well as various administrative and demographic recordings across Europe and the Americas.

## Political Events in Britain and France

In Great Britain, the polls held in January 1906 resulted in a "Liberal landslide." This event was described as being comparable to nothing in British parliamentary history since the election following the great Reform Bill. The Conservatives, who had held power for nearly twenty years, returned to the House of Commons with barely 150 members. In contrast, the Liberals achieved a majority of more than one hundred over all other combined parties. A notable example of this shift occurred in Manchester, where Mr. Balfour and eight Conservative colleagues were replaced by nine individuals from the Labour or Liberal parties. The political atmosphere was characterized by deep grievances, specifically the "wrong of the Khaki Election" and the "unfair Chinese Labour cry."

In France, Paul Doumer, a Radical politician and former governor of Indo-China, was re-elected as president of the chamber in January 1906. On January 17, 1906, Doumer stood as a candidate in the presidential election in opposition to M. Fallieres. Doumer received 371 votes, while M. Fallieres received 449. Following this, the new chamber chose Henri Brisson as its new president, passing over Doumer.

## Financial and Economic Data

The Bank of France operated with a note circulation averaging L186,300,000 in 1906. By that year, the bank had expanded its reach to 447 offices, including towns "connected with the branches." In 1906, the bank discounted more than 20,000,000 bills totaling L559,234,996, and provided advances on securities amounting to L106,280,124. Approximately 232,000 bills below 8s. (including those as low as 4s. 2d.) were discounted. Additionally, the rate of discount in Paris remained uniform at 3% from May 1900 until January 1906. The limit of the Bank of France's circulation reached 5,800 million francs (L232,000,000) in 1906.

In the United Kingdom, trade records for January 1906 show that 61,940 quantities of tomatoes were imported. The value of fresh flowers imported into the United Kingdom during January 1906 was L31,035.

## Demographics and Regional Reports

A census conducted on January 12, 1906, provided population data for Bulgaria. The population of northern Bulgaria was recorded as 2,853,704, and Eastern Rumelia had 1,174,535. The total population of united Bulgaria was 4,028,239, which equated to 88 people per square mile.

In the United States, state reports from 1906 indicated that most neat cattle in Idaho were located on ranges in Owyhee, Cassia, Washington, and Lemhi counties. The largest numbers of sheep were found in Blaine, Canyon, and Owyhee counties, while Idaho and Nez Perce counties served as the principal regions for raising swine. Furthermore, the pasture lands of Idaho were greatly decreased by the increase of forest reserves, specifically due to large reservations made during 1906-1907.
