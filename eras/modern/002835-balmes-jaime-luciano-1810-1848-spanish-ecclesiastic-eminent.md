# BALMES, JAIME LUCIANO (1810-1848)

Jaime Luciano Balmes was a Spanish ecclesiastic who achieved eminence as a philosopher and a political writer. He was born on August 28, 1810, in Vich, Catalonia, and passed away in the same location on July 9, 1848.

## Political Activity and Journalism
Balmes engaged in political conflict during his life, specifically attacking the regent Espartero. This action led to his exile. Upon his return, he became the editor and founder of *El Pensamiento de la Nacion*, which was a weekly publication characterized as Conservative and Catholic.

## Major Works and Religious Defense
The primary source of Balmes' fame is his work titled *El Protestantismo comparado con el Catolicismo en sus relaciones con la Civilisacion Europea*. Published in three volumes between 1842 and 1844 (with a sixth edition appearing in 1879 and an English translation released in London in 1849), the text serves as an able defense of Catholicism. Balmes argued that Catholicism represents the spirit of order or obedience, contrasting this with Protestantism, which he characterized as the spirit of anarchy or revolt. From a historical perspective, however, the work is considered to be of little value.

## Philosophical Contributions
Balmes also produced significant philosophical writings that provided clear expositions of the scholastic system of thought. His most notable works in this field include:

*   ***Filosofia Fondamental***: A four-volume work published in 1846. It was later translated into English in two volumes by H. F. Brownson and published in New York in 1856.
*   ***Curso de Filosofia Elemental***: A four-volume work published in 1847. Balmes translated this specific text into Latin so that it could be utilized in seminaries.
