# Basnage

Basnage was a prolific writer and a skilled preacher. His literary contributions spanned several genres, including dogmatic and polemical treatises, though his most significant works are categorized as historical.

## Historical Works
Basnage authored several historical texts, some of which were written from the perspective of Protestant polemics. These include the *Histoire de la religion des eglises reformees*, published in Rotterdam in 1690, and the *Histoire de l'eglise depuis Jesus-Christ jusqu'a present*, published in 1699. 

Other works of his are noted for having greater scientific value. These include the *Histoire des Juifs*, published in Rotterdam in 1706 and translated into English in 1708, as well as the *Antiquites judaiques ou remarques critiques sur la republique des Hebreux*, published in 1713.

## Other Contributions
Beyond his major historical and polemical treatises, Basnage contributed to the arts. He wrote notes and short explanatory introductions for a collection of copper-plate engravings titled *Histoires du Vieux et du Nouveau Testament, representees par des figures gravees en taille-douce par R. de Hooge*. This work, published in Amsterdam in 1704, is highly valued by connoisseurs.

## Influence and Legacy
Basnage is recognized as one of the modern guides to the knowledge of the Platonic system, alongside other writers such as Cudworth, Le Clerc, and Brucker. Observers seeking instruction on this system may find value in the disputes between these writers or certainty where their views agree.
