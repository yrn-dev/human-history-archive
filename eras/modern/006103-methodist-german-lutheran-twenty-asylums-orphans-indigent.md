# Religious and Charitable Institutions in Urban Centers

## Protestant and Catholic Presence in Buenos Aires
In the city of Buenos Aires, the religious landscape is characterized by a variety of denominations. The city contains approximately twenty-five Roman Catholic churches, with the Merced on Calle Reconquista being noted as one of the most popular and richest. The cathedral, dating from 1752, is among the largest in South America; it features a classical portico with twelve Corinthian columns and a sculptured pediment, resembling the Madeleine of Paris. Adjoining the cathedral is the archbishop's palace, following the city's establishment as an archiepiscopal see in 1866.

Alongside the Catholic presence, there are four Protestant churches in the city: English, Scottish Presbyterian, American Methodist, and German Lutheran.

## Public and Private Welfare in Buenos Aires
Social welfare in Buenos Aires is managed through a combination of public expense and private religious associations. These entities maintain twenty asylums for indigent persons and orphans, as well as one asylum for lunatics. Medical needs are addressed by fifteen well-appointed hospitals that provide a total of 2,600 beds and treat 17,000 patients annually. Five of these hospitals belong to foreign nationalities. Additionally, the city maintains six cemeteries spanning 230 acres.
