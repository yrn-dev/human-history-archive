# Brialmont and the Evolution of Fortification

## Career and Engineering Philosophy
Brialmont was a prolific engineer whose career evolved through several distinct stages of fortification theory. In the initial phase of his professional life, his plans were based on the ideas of Vauban with only slight modifications. This approach was evident in his original scheme for the fortification of Antwerp, which utilized a bastioned trace for both the forts and the enceinte.

By 1859, during the implementation of the great entrenched camp at Antwerp, Brialmont transitioned to the school of polygonal fortification and adopted the ideas of Montalembert. Approximately twenty years later, his own specific plans and types became distinct. This development occurred during a period of general confusion regarding fortification, which was caused by the events of 1870-71 and the introduction of long-range guns.

## Final Principles and Belgian Defences
As the inspector-general, Brialmont was responsible for drafting and executing the entire scheme for the defences of Belgium. His final principles of fortification were applied to the fortifications on the Meuse at Namur and Liége, as well as the extreme detached forts of the Antwerp region. These principles were characterized by three primary elements:
* The multiplication of intermediate batteries.
* The suppression of all artillery positions that were open to overhead fire.
* The lavish use of armour to protect the artillery located inside the forts.

## Literary Contributions and Publications
Brialmont was an indefatigable writer who contributed extensively to journals through reviews, essays, and other papers. In 1850, he originated the *Journal de l'armée Belge*. Throughout his career, he produced forty-nine pamphlets and twenty-three important works.

His most significant publications include:
* *La Fortification du temps présent* (Brussels, 1885)
* *Influence du tir plongeant et des obus-torpilles sur la fortification* (Brussels, 1888)
* *Les Régions fortifiées* (Brussels, 1890)
* *La Défense des états et la fortification à la fin du XIX^e siècle* (Brussels, 1895)
* *Progrès de la défense des états et de la fortification permanente depuis Vauban* (Brussels, 1898)
