# Atlantic Ocean

The Atlantic Ocean is a major global body of water that serves as a boundary for various landmasses and a corridor for biological and military activity. It interacts with other seas, influences regional climates, and supports diverse marine life.

## Geography and Regional Boundaries
The Atlantic Ocean borders several regions across different continents. In Europe, it bounds the north and northwest of Cornwall, the south-westernmost county of England. In France, it borders Brittany (also known as Armorica), where the region's greatest length between the Atlantic Ocean and the English Channel is 250 kilometres (approximately 155 English miles). 

In Africa, the continent's great elevated inland plateau—which sits between 4,000 and 6,000 feet above sea level—slopes toward the Atlantic Ocean in the far distance. In some areas, such as Central Africa, this slope is very sudden, with mountain ranges forming a western buttress for the plateau. On the African coast, Boma and Lobito Bay are noted as points of Atlantic coastal access. In Central America, the Atlantic coast of Costa Rica is characterized by trade-winds that can bring rain during any month of the year.

## Marine Life and Biology
The distribution of shore-fishes allows for the Atlantic to be categorized as one of four great world divisions, alongside the Arctic, Indo-Pacific, and Antarctic regions. The Atlantic region is further subdivided into Northern, Tropical, and Southern zones. Some classifications further divide the area into East Atlantic and West Atlantic areas. While the Atlantic supports a variety of fish, its fauna is less rich in terms of species and genera than that of the Indo-Pacific.

Other marine organisms are also found within the ocean. The *Balanoglossus* species are distributed among principal marine provinces, and a specific species, *Glandiceps abyssicola* (of the *Spengelidae* family), was dredged by the "Challenger" expedition off the coast of Africa at a depth of 2,500 fathoms.

## Hydrography and Climate
The Atlantic Ocean maintains a relationship with the Mediterranean Sea. The Mediterranean is described as a "sea of evaporation" where the rivers flowing into it cannot compensate for surface evaporation. Consequently, there is a constant current of water pouring from the Atlantic into the Mediterranean.

In Costa Rica, the proximity of the Atlantic and Pacific oceans contributes to varied climatic extremes. On the Atlantic coast, the heat is less oppressive than on the Pacific coast because damp is comparatively rare and cool breezes prevail. However, the Atlantic seaboard is associated with the prevalence of rheumatism.

## Historical and Military Context
In antiquity, the reign of the Roman Emperor Augustus extended over sea and land from the Nile and Euphrates to the Atlantic Ocean.

In more recent history, the Atlantic was a theater for naval and submarine operations. During the submarine warfare proclaimed by Germany, American submarines crossed the Atlantic to go out on patrol.
