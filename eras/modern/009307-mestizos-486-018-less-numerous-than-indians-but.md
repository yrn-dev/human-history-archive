# Mestizos

The term *mestizos* (also referred to as *ladinos* or *cholos*) describes populations of mixed racial descent, primarily resulting from the intermarriage of European settlers—most notably Spanish colonists—and native Indians. This demographic group is found across various regions of the Americas, including Bolivia, Honduras, and Costa Rica.

## Mestizos in Bolivia

In Bolivia, the population is composed of Indians, Caucasians of European origin, and a mixture of the two races known as *mestizos*. According to a roughly-taken census from 1900, *mestizos* constituted 26.8% of the total population of 1,816,271. In numerical terms, there were 486,018 *mestizos*, a figure that outnumbers the white population (231,088) by more than two to one, although they remain less numerous than the Indian population.

The white population in Bolivia is largely descended from early Spanish adventurers seeking mineral wealth, supplemented by some Portuguese Americans from Brazil and Spanish Americans from neighboring republics. Because there has been no direct immigration from Europe, the white population comprises only one-eighth of the total and is described as wanting in energy. Consequently, the future of Bolivia is said to depend mainly on the *mestizos*.

Characterizations of the *mestizo* population in Bolivia vary. Some accounts suggest they inherit the vices of both races and the virtues of neither, describing them as generally apathetic, unprogressive, ignorant, cruel, intemperate, and intensely superstitious, though some individual strong characters have emerged. While it is suggested that education and experience might develop them into a progressive nationality, the first century of self-government is noted as providing little promise of this result. Furthermore, *mestizos* (referred to as *cholos*) are often more familiar with native idioms than with Spanish.

## Mestizos in Central America

In other Central American republics, the *mestizo* or *ladino* population is also prominent:

*   **Honduras:** The Spanish-speaking half-castes (*Ladinos* or *Mestizos*) are the most numerous section of the population. They share social, political, and economic preponderance with the small European element.
*   **Costa Rica:** There is a large population of half-castes (*ladinos* or *mestizos*) resulting from intermarriage with native Indians. In this region, the percentage of Spanish blood is higher than in other Central American republics.

## Other Mixed Populations

Beyond the *mestizo* category, other hybrid groups exist in the region. In British Honduras, the majority of the population belongs to a hybrid race descended from white settlers, aboriginal Indians, and negro slaves. Specific mixed groups identified in British Honduras include a Spanish-Indian population in the south (from Guatemala and Honduras) and another Spanish-Indian group in the north that arrived from Yucatan in 1848. Additionally, Honduras contains a disappearing mixed race of Indians and negroes known as Sambos.
