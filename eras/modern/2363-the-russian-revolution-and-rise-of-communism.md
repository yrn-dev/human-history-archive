# The Russian Revolution and the Rise of Communism

## Overview
The Russian Revolution of 1917 was not a single event, but a series of upheavals that dismantled the Tsarist autocracy and led to the creation of the Soviet Union, the world's first socialist state. The revolution was precipitated by decades of social unrest, the incompetence of Tsar Nicholas II, and the catastrophic toll of World War I, which left the Russian economy in ruins and the peasantry starving.

The process occurred in two primary stages. The **February Revolution** saw a spontaneous uprising in Petrograd that forced the Tsar to abdicate, replacing the monarchy with a fragile Provisional Government. However, this government failed to exit the war or implement land reform, creating a power vacuum. This allowed the **Bolsheviks**, a radical faction of the Social Democratic Labour Party led by Vladimir Lenin, to seize power in the **October Revolution**.

Following the coup, a brutal Civil War (1917–1923) erupted between the "Reds" (Bolsheviks) and the "Whites" (a loose coalition of monarchists, capitalists, and other socialists). The Bolshevik victory solidified the Communist Party's control, leading to the establishment of the USSR in 1922. Historians continue to debate whether the October Revolution was a genuine popular uprising of the masses or a disciplined military coup d'état orchestrated by a small elite.

## Key Facts
*   **Ideological Foundation:** The revolution was based on a modified version of Marxism known as **Marxism-Leninism**, which argued that a "vanguard party" was necessary to lead the proletariat toward revolution.
*   **War Communism:** To survive the Civil War, the Bolsheviks implemented "War Communism," involving the forced requisitioning of grain from peasants, which led to widespread famine.
*   **The Red Terror:** To consolidate power, the Bolsheviks established the **Cheka** (secret police) to eliminate "class enemies" through mass arrests and executions.

## Did You Know?
1. **The Calendar Gap:** The "October Revolution" actually took place in November according to the Gregorian calendar; Russia was still using the Julian calendar at the time.
2. **The Romanov Fate:** Tsar Nicholas II and his entire immediate family were executed by Bolsheviks in a basement in Yekaterinburg in July 1918 to prevent them from becoming a rallying point for the White Army.
3. **The NEP Shift:** After the failures of War Communism, Lenin introduced the **New Economic Policy (NEP)** in 1921, which temporarily reintroduced elements of capitalism to stabilize the economy.
