# Poute k Eldoradu and Sonety Samotare

*Poute k Eldoradu* (Pilgrimages to Eldorado) and *Sonety Samotare* (Sonnets of a Recluse) are lyric poetry collections authored by the Bohemian writer Jaroslav Vrchlicky.

## Author and Literary Context
Jaroslav Vrchlicky, who was born in 1853 and wrote under the pseudonym Emil Frida, is identified as one of the best later Bohemian poets. His work emerged during a period of significant development for Bohemian literature, particularly following the establishment of the national university in 1882. 

While other Bohemian poets of his era, such as Svatopluk Cech, focused their poetry on national enthusiasm—as seen in works like *Basne otroka* (the songs of a slave) and *Vaclav z Michalovic, Lesetinsky Kovar* (the smith of Lesetin)—Vrchlicky is noted for being more catholic in his choice of subjects. In addition to his poetry and prose, Vrchlicky is recognized as a very brilliant dramatist.

## The Lyric Collections
Among Vrchlicky's numerous collections of lyric poems, *Poute k Eldoradu* and *Sonety Samotare* are singled out for their particular value, alongside the collection *Rok na jihu* (a year in the south). 

The use of the sonnet form in *Sonety Samotare* aligns with a broader literary tradition of the form as a vehicle for subjective confession or a "cry from the heart," a characteristic attributed to poets such as Petrarch, Wordsworth, and Shakespeare.

## Related Bohemian Literature
Vrchlicky's contributions exist alongside a diverse body of Bohemian literary work from the 19th century. This includes the philosophical epic *Kosmicke basne* (cosmic poems) by John Neruda (1834-1891) and the epic poem *Vysehrad* by Julius Zeyer (1841-1901), which celebrates the ancient glory of the acropolis of Prague. Other notable figures in Bohemian letters include:
* **Bozena Nemceva**: Author of *Babicka*, which has been translated into multiple languages.
* **Benes Trebizky**: A writer of many historical novels.
* **Alois Jirasek**: A novelist and distinguished dramatic author.
* **Jacob Arbes**: Author of the meritorious *Romanetta*.
* **Vaclav Hladik**: A copious dramatic author whose novel *Evzen Voldan* represents the life of modern Prague.
