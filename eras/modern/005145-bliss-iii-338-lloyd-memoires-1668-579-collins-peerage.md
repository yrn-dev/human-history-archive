# Bristol

The historical record of Bristol is preserved across a variety of biographical and diplomatic sources. Much of the detailed account of his life and career is found in the *History of England* and the *History of the Civil War* authored by S. R. Gardiner. Gardiner also provided a short sketch of Bristol's career in the *Dictionary of National Biography*, in which he highly eulogized Bristol's diplomacy and character.

## Biographical Sources
Several biographical works provide accounts of Bristol's life. These include:
* *Biographia Britannica* by Kippis (v. 199)
* *Ath. Oxon.* by Wood (Bliss), iii. 338
* *Memoires* by D. Lloyd (1668), 579
* *Peerage* by Collins (Brydges, 1812), v. 362
* *Worthies* by Fuller (Nichols, 1811), ii. 412
* *Royal and Noble Authors* by H. Walpole (Park, 1806), iii. 49

## Historical and State Records
Bristol is featured extensively in official state and parliamentary documentation. Key records include the *Clarendon State Papers* and the *Calendar of Clarendon State Papers*, as well as the *Old Parliamentary History*. His activities are further documented in the *Hardwicke State Papers* (i. 494) and the *Somers Tracts* (1809), ii. 501. 

Additionally, the *Thomason Tracts* held in the British Museum and the *Miscellany* of the Camden Society (vol. vi., 1871) contain relevant material. S. R. Gardiner also edited a work titled *Defence of his Spanish Negotiations*.

## Archival Manuscripts
A significant portion of the primary source material for Bristol is located in the manuscripts at Sherborne Castle. A selection of these manuscripts was transcribed and deposited in the Public Record Office, with calendars produced by the Historical Manuscripts Commission in *Report* viii. app. i. p. 213 and the 10th *Report* app. i. p. 520. 

Further references to Bristol are found in various collections calendared in the same publication and in the *Calendar of State Papers, Domestic Series*. Other notable manuscript sources include the *Harleian MSS.* (Brit. Mus. 1580, art. 31-48) and various *Additional MSS.* indexes and calendars.
