# Volunteers and Financial Support for Project Gutenberg

## Overview of Project Gutenberg
Project Gutenberg is characterized by the free distribution of electronic works. These works are provided in formats designed to be readable by the widest possible variety of computers, encompassing those categorized as new, middle-aged, old, and obsolete.

## Role of Volunteers and Donations
The existence of Project Gutenberg is made possible through the contributions of people from all walks of life. Specifically, the project relies on the efforts of hundreds of volunteers and the provision of donations. Financial support is considered critical to the project's success, as it provides volunteers with the necessary assistance they need to reach the goals of Project Gutenberg. This combination of volunteer labor and financial backing is essential to ensuring that the collection remains freely available for future generations.

## The Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation
In 2001, the Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation was established. The purpose of this foundation is to ensure a permanent and secure future for Project Gutenberg and for generations to come. 

The Foundation is organized as a non-profit 501(c)(3) educational corporation under the laws of the state of Mississippi. It has been granted tax-exempt status by the Internal Revenue Service, and its federal tax identification number (EIN) is 64-6221541.
