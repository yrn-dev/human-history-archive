# Microparasites in Disease: Selected Essays

## Overview
*Microparasites in Disease: Selected Essays* is a publication released in 1886 by the New Sydenham Society. The work consists of a collection of essays that were translated from German and French. These essays provide accounts of classical researches within the field of bacteriology.

## Context within Bacteriological Literature
The publication emerged during a period when bacteriological literature was becoming so extensive that providing comprehensive references to original articles became nearly impossible. *Microparasites in Disease: Selected Essays* served as a resource for classical research alongside other significant works of the era. 

Other important texts in the field published around the turn of the century include:
* **General Bacteriology and Technique:** Works such as Abbott's *Principles of Bacteriology* (1905), Eyre's *Bacteriological Technique* (1902), and Hewlett's *Manual of Bacteriology* (1902).
* **Pathogenic Micro-organisms:** Key texts include the *Handbuch der pathogenen Mikroorganismen* (1904) by Kolle and Wassermann, which is noted as the most important work on the subject, as well as McFarland's *Text-book upon the Pathogenic Bacteria* (1906) and Park's *Pathogenic Micro-organisms* (1906).
* **Specialized Studies:** Research on immunity was notably advanced by Ehrlich in *Studies in Immunity* (1906) and Metchnikoff in *Immunity in Infective Diseases*.
