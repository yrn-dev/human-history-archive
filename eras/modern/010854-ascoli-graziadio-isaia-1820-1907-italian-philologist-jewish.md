# ASCOLI, GRAZIADIO ISAIA (1820-1907)

Graziadio Isaia Ascoli was a prominent Italian philologist born in 1820. Born into a Jewish family in Gorz, Ascoli demonstrated a significant talent for linguistics from an early age. He spent his career contributing to the study of various languages and became a leading figure in the field of Italian linguistics.

## Academic Career and Publications
Ascoli's professional contributions began with the publication of his *Studii orientali e linguistici* in 1854. His academic standing grew over the following years, leading to his appointment as a professor of philology at Milan in 1860. 

Throughout his career, Ascoli produced learned contributions across several linguistic domains. His research extended to the study of Semitic and Indo-European languages, as well as the gipsy language. However, his primary area of expertise and specialization was the study of Italian dialects.

## Contributions to Italian Linguistics
In 1873, Ascoli founded the *Archivio glottologico italiano*. This publication served as a major organ for original scholarship on Italian dialects and linguistic studies. Within this archive, Ascoli published his *Saggi Ladini*.

Ascoli achieved universal recognition as the preeminent authority on Italian linguistics. His expertise was further cemented by his contribution to the *Encyclopaedia Britannica* (specifically the 9th edition, which was revised for the 11th edition), where his article became the classic exposition of the subject in the English language.

## Death
Graziadio Isaia Ascoli died in 1907.
