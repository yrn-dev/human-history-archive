# Russian Exploration of Chinese Turkestan and Tibet

## Scientific Expeditions of 1889-1894
During the late 19th century, Russian explorers conducted several expeditions into Central Asia based on scientific principles. Following the work of Prjevalsky, the explorers Pevtsov and Roborovski undertook missions in 1889-1890, and again in 1894. These specific expeditions were of the highest value, as they greatly expanded the existing knowledge regarding the northern borders of Tibet and the topography of western Chinese Turkestan.
