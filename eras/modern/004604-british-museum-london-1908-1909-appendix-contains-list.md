# British Museum

The British Museum is a major institution in London that houses extensive collections across various disciplines, including music, zoology, antiquities, and a vast library. Its staff and catalogs provide detailed records of the items held within the museum and the research conducted by its associates.

## Music and Manuscripts
The museum maintains a significant collection of musical manuscripts. A *Catalogue of MS. Music in the British Museum*, published in London between 1908 and 1909, includes a valuable appendix provided by Augustus Hughes-Hughes. This appendix contains a list of typical musical instruments that are either described in other manuscripts or represented in illuminated manuscripts held by the museum. Each entry in this list is accompanied by full references and a brief description.

## Natural History and Zoology
The British Museum manages diverse biological and paleontological collections. George A. Boulenger, F.R.S., D.Sc., Ph.D., is in charge of the Department of Zoology's collections of fishes and reptiles. Additionally, the museum's holdings include fossil mammals, reptiles, and birds, which have been the subject of specific catalogs authored by staff members of the Geological Survey of India.

## Antiquities and Archaeology
The museum's department of Egyptian and Assyrian Antiquities is supported by specialists such as Leonard William King, M.A., F.S.A., who serves as Assistant to the Keeper. King has conducted excavations at Kuyunjik (Nineveh) on behalf of the British Museum. Other notable holdings include the Franks Bequest, which features "The Treasures of the Oxus," as detailed in a 1905 catalogue by Ormonde M. Dalton.

## Library and Bibliographical Resources
The British Museum Library provides extensive resources for researchers. Robert Nisbet Bain served as an Assistant Librarian from 1883 until 1909. The library's accessibility is enhanced by several key publications:
* The *List of Books of Reference in the Reading-Room of the British Museum*, first published in 1889.
* The *Subject-index of the Modern Works added to the Library of the British Museum in the years 1881-1900*, edited by G.K. Fortescue, which includes supplements published every five years.

These resources are noted for including entries for a vast number of bibliographical works.
