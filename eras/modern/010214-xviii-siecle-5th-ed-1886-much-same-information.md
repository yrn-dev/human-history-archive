# XVIII^e Siècle

The eighteenth century (XVIII^e siècle) is a period of significant study across various disciplines, including the arts, philosophy, astronomy, and regional administration. Documentation of this era is preserved through specialized bibliographies, historical accounts, and scientific records.

## Illustrated Books and Art
The study of illustrated books from the eighteenth century is well-documented. H. Cohen's *Guide de l'amateur de livre a gravures du XVIII^e siecle* (5th ed., 1886) serves as a chronicle of French books from this period. Similar information, with expanded details regarding English books, is provided in Lewine's *Bibliography of Eighteenth Century Art and Illustrated Books* (1898). 

Further research into the artistic production of the century includes the work of Roger Portallis, who wrote *Les Dessinateurs d'illustrations au XVIII^e siècle*, and D. H. Beraldi, author of *Les Graveurs d'illustrations au XVIII^e siècle*. Additionally, G. Desnoireterres' *Le Chevalier Dorat et les poetes legers au XVIII^e siecle* (1887) examines specific literary and poetic figures of the era, while the bibliographical value of such works is detailed in the editions of Henry Cohen's *Guide de l'amateur de livres a figures et a vignettes du XVIII^e siecle* published by R. Portalis (1887) and Ch. Mehl (1876).

## Philosophy and Intellectual History
The intellectual landscape of eighteenth-century France is addressed in several historical surveys. Damiron authored *Memoires a servir pour l'histoire de la philosophie en France au XVIII. siecle*, published between 1858 and 1864. Other academic contributions to the understanding of the period's intellectual and institutional connections include Rene Kerviler's *La Bretagne et l'Academie francaise du XVIII^e siecle* (1889).

## Astronomy and Cartography
Scientific progress in the eighteenth century is highlighted in the field of astronomy. J. Delambre's *Hist. de l'astronomie au XVIII^e siecle* provides detailed accounts of the era, including specific information on the life and work of J.D. Cassini. 

In the realm of cartography, the eighteenth century marks a significant point in the mapping of the world. Gabriel Marcel's *Reproductions de cartes et de globes relatives à la découverte de l'Amérique du XVI^e au XVIII^e siècle* (1893) documents the progression of American discovery and mapping through the end of the century.

## Regional Administration and History
In France, the eighteenth century is a focal point for studies on regional governance and social structures. A. Dupuy's *Études sur l'administration municipale en Bretagne au XVIII^e siècle* (1891) examines municipal administration in Brittany. Furthermore, Henri Sée's *Les Classes rurales en Bretagne du XVI^e siècle à la Revolution* (1906) tracks the evolution of rural classes in that region leading up to the Revolution.
