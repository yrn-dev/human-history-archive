# Azoxy Compounds

Azoxy compounds, represented by the general formula R.[=N.O.N].R', are organic substances that typically manifest as red or yellow crystalline solids.

## Preparation and Synthesis
Azoxy compounds can be synthesized through several chemical processes:
*   **Reduction of Nitro or Nitroso Compounds:** These compounds result from heating nitro or nitroso compounds with alcoholic potash, with methyl alcohol being the preferred alcohol for this process.
*   **Oxidation of Azo Compounds:** They may be obtained via the oxidation of azo compounds.
*   **Electro-reduction:** Azoxybenzene specifically can be found among the products of the electro-reduction of nitrobenzene when the process is conducted in an alcoholic-alkaline solution.

## Chemical Properties and Reactions
Azoxy compounds undergo various transformations depending on the reagents used:
*   **Reduction:** When reduced in an acid solution, azoxy compounds yield amines. Distillation with reduced iron produces azo compounds, while warming them with ammonium sulphide results in hydrazo compounds.
*   **Acid Interaction:** The use of concentrated sulphuric acid converts azoxybenzene into oxyazobenzene.

## Azoxybenzene
Azoxybenzene, with the chemical formula (C$_6$H$_5$N)$_2$O, is a specific example of this class of compounds. It crystallizes from alcohol in the form of yellow needles and has a melting point of 36°C. Upon distillation, azoxybenzene yields azobenzene and aniline.
