# Imperial Agricultural Department of the West Indies

## Overview and Headquarters
The Imperial Agricultural Department of the West Indies is an organization tasked with agricultural development in the region. Its headquarters are located in Barbados.

## Impact on Barbados
Under the leadership of Sir Daniel Morris, the Imperial Agricultural Department of the West Indies is credited with the development of cotton growing on the island of Barbados.

## Administration of Barbados
Barbados is administered by a governor who is supported by an executive council, a house of assembly consisting of 24 members elected via a limited franchise, and a legislative council composed of 9 nominated members. While the colony possesses representative institutions, it does not have responsible government. Public officials, with the exception of the treasurer, are appointed by the home government, and the crown maintains a veto over legislation.

## Population and Demographics
Barbados is recognized as one of the most densely populated areas in the world. Population records indicate that in 1901, the population was 195,588 (approximately 1178 people per square mile), increasing to 196,287 by 1906. 

The majority of the population consists of negroes, who outnumber whites by a ratio of 9 to 1. This population is described as being passionately attached to the island, possessing their own distinct dialect and physiognomy, and being more intelligent than other West Indian negroes.

## Education and Religion
The Church of England is the largest denomination on the island, exceeding the combined total of all other denominations. The government provides payment for the clergy of all creeds as well as for the bishop, as the island serves as a see.

Educational institutions on the island include:
* **Codrington College**: Founded by Colonel Christopher Codrington, who bequeathed two estates to the Society for the Propagation of the Gospel in 1710. The college is affiliated with the university of Durham and trains young men for holy orders.
* **Secondary Schools**: Harrison College and The Lodge serve boys, while Queen's College serves girls.
* **Other Schools**: The island also maintains several second grade schools and a large number of primary schools.

## Geography and Land Use
There are no forests or crown lands located on the island of Barbados.
