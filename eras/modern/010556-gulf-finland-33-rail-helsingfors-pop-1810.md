# Gulf of Finland

## Geography and Physical Characteristics
The Gulf of Finland is an arm of the Baltic Sea that runs eastwards for approximately 250 miles. It serves as a geographical boundary, separating Finland from Esthonia. The Gotland deep extends directly into this gulf. 

In terms of geological history, the region has undergone various stages of subsidence and elevation. During glacial times, the Baltic area experienced significant changes, including a period where it was transformed into a vast freshwater lake known as the Ancylus lake, which covered a large part of Finland and Lake Ladoga. Following subsequent periods of subsidence and elevation that gave the Baltic its current form, Dr. R. Sieger identified isobasic lines (lines of equal rate of elevation). These findings indicate that the rate of elevation is currently almost nil along the axial lines of the Gulf of Finland, though the amplitude of movement increases northwards toward the Gulf of Bothnia.

## Borga
Borga (known in Finnish as *Porvoo*) is a seaport located in the province of Nyland within the grand duchy of Finland. It is situated at the entrance of the river Borga into the Gulf of Finland and is located approximately 33 miles by rail northwest of Helsingfors. 

The population of Borga has grown over the 19th and early 20th centuries, recorded at 1,693 in 1810, 3,478 in 1870, and 5,255 in 1904. The city serves as the seat of a court of appeal and a Lutheran bishopric, the latter of which covers the provinces of St Michel and Viborg, as well as portions of Nyland and Tavastehus. Notable landmarks in the city include a high school—where the Finnish poet Runeberg lectured for many years—and a beautiful cathedral.

The primary industries in Borga are the manufacture of tobacco and the weaving of sail-cloth. Its chief articles of trade include furs, butter, and wood. While Borga was once a city of great importance and dignity, it has been somewhat eclipsed by the rapid growth of Helsingfors.

## Historical Significance
Borga played a critical role in the political history of Finland. In 1809, the estates of Finland were summoned to a special diet to determine the future of the country, and Borga was chosen as the meeting place. It was within the cathedral of Borga that Emperor Alexander I, acting as the grand duke of Finland, pledged to maintain the liberties and the constitution of the grand duchy.
