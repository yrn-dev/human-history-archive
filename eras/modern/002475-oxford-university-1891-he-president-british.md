# Mr. Balfour

## Academic Honors and University Appointments
Mr. Balfour received numerous honorary degrees from several universities. In 1881, he was made LL.D. of Edinburgh University, followed by the same honor from St Andrews University in 1885 and Cambridge University in 1888. In 1891, he was awarded LL.D. degrees from both Glasgow and Dublin Universities, and he was also named D.C.L. of Oxford University in that same year.

His leadership roles within these institutions were extensive. He served as the lord rector of St Andrews University in 1886 and the lord rector of Glasgow University in 1890. In 1891, he became the chancellor of Edinburgh University. Additionally, he was a member of the senate of London University starting in 1888.

## Professional Affiliations and Leadership
Beyond his university appointments, Mr. Balfour held significant positions in scientific and professional organizations. He became a fellow of the Royal Society in 1888 and later served as the president of the British Association in 1904.

## Publications and Personal Interests
Mr. Balfour authored several works, including *The Foundations of Belief, being Notes introductory to the Study of Theology* (1895) and *Essays and Addresses* (1893).

In his private life, he was recognized as a cultured musician from an early age. He also developed an enthusiasm for golf, which led to his appointment as captain of the Royal and Antient Golf Club of St Andrews from 1894 to 1895.
