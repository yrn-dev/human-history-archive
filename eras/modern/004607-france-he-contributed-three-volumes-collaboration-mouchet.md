# Louis George Oudard Feudrix de Bréquigny

Louis George Oudard Feudrix de Bréquigny was a scholar and historian who contributed extensively to the documentation and study of French history. His work focused on the collection of historical documents, the study of French communes, and the preservation of records.

## Contributions to French History
Bréquigny is noted for his collaboration with Mouchet on the *Table chronologique des diplômes, chartes, lettres, et actes imprimés concernant l'histoire de France*, to which he contributed three volumes between 1769 and 1783. He also produced important research into French communes, which can be found in the preface to volume xi of a series spanning volumes x through xiv.

In addition to these works, Bréquigny was tasked with the supervision of a large collection of documents regarding French history. This collection was considered analogous to Rymer's *Foedera*. From this effort, he published the first volume, titled *Diplomatat. Chartae*, &c., in 1791.

## Other Scholarly Pursuits
Beyond his focus on French national history, Bréquigny engaged in the study of Chinese culture and history. In 1776, at the request of the minister Bertin, he began collecting *Mémoires concernant l'histoire, les sciences, lettres, et les arts des Chinois*. This project resulted in the publication of fifteen volumes before it was interrupted by the Revolution.
