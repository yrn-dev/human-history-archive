# Azorian Commerce and Documentation

## Overview of Azorian Commerce
The progress of commerce in the Azores is most effectively documented and demonstrated through the reports provided by American and British consuls. These consular reports serve as primary sources for understanding the commercial development of the archipelago.

## Related Bibliographic Sources
For those seeking a broader understanding of the islands beyond commercial reports, several general accounts exist. These include *The Azores* by W. F. Walker, published in London in 1886, and *Madeira and the Canary Islands, with the Azores* by A. S. Brown, published in London in 1901.

## Natural History of the Islands
The archive contains references to the flora and fauna of the Azores, primarily through the works of H. Drouet. His contributions to the study of the islands include:
* *Elements de la faune acoreenne* (1861)
* *Mollusques marins des iles Acores* (1858)
* *Lettres acoreennes* (1862)
* *Catalogue de la flore des iles Acores, precede de l'itineraire d'une voyage dans cet archipel* (1866)

## Historical Records
The history of the Azores is documented in specialized texts. C. Fernandez Duro's *La Conquista de las Azores en 1583* (Madrid, 1886) provides an account of the conquest. Additionally, J. Mees published *Histoire de la decouverte des iles Azores et de l'origine de leur denomination d'iles flamandes* in Ghent in 1901, which examines the discovery of the islands and the origins of their designation as the Flemish Islands.
