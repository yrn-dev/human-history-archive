# Campaner y Fuertes (Palma, 1888)

## Overview
A. Campaner y Fuertes is the author of the work *Bosquejo historico de la dominacion islamita en las islas Baleares*, which was published in Palma in 1888. This text serves as a historical resource specifically focusing on the period of Moorish rule within the Balearic Islands.
