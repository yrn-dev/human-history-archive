# British Walcheren Expedition (1809)

The British Walcheren expedition was a military operation conducted in 1809. ## Naval Participation
The expedition included the service of various British naval officers. Among them was Frederick Marryat, an English sailor and novelist who had entered the navy at the age of fourteen. Marryat served as a midshipman during the expedition, gaining experience that contributed to his later career as a commander and his eventual nomination as a C.B. in 1826.

## Political Consequences and French Annexation
The aftermath of the Walcheren expedition served as a catalyst for Napoleon's restructuring of the territories surrounding the French Empire. At the time, Napoleon had destined his brother, Louis, for the throne of Holland, proclaiming him king on June 6, 1806. However, the relationship between the two brothers was strained by Napoleon's reproaches regarding Louis's perceived leniency toward his subjects and his desire for popularity. This tension was further exacerbated by the rigors of the continental system and personal disputes involving Louis's wife.

By the latter part of 1809, Napoleon intended to annex Holland to halt secret Dutch trade with England. In January 1810, Napoleon formally annexed the island of Walcheren. The justification provided for this annexation was the allegation that Louis had failed to perform his share in defending French interests during the British Walcheren expedition of 1809.

## French Military Occupations
In conjunction with the annexation of Walcheren, French troops occupied other strategic locations, specifically Bergen-op-Zoom and Breda. Although Louis initially acquiesced to these developments and the points of dispute, the crisis continued. Following the collapse of negotiations with Great Britain in the spring of 1810, Napoleon increased pressure on Louis and dispatched French troops against the Dutch capital.

Unable to offer resistance, Louis fled his kingdom and eventually settled in Bohemia at Toplitz. On July 9, 1810, Napoleon officially annexed Holland to the French Empire.
