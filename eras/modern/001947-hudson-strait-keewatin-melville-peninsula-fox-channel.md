# Hudson Strait

## Geographic Location and Boundaries
Hudson Strait is a body of water located in the Arctic region of North America. It serves as a geographic boundary that separates Baffin Land from Ungava. 

Baffin Land, a barren insular tract included in Canada's Franklin district, is situated between 61° and 90° W. and 62° and 74° N. It encompasses an approximate area of 236,000 square miles. The land is characterized by rocky and mountainous northern and eastern coasts, which are deeply indented by Admiralty Inlet, Cumberland Sound, Home Bay, and Frobisher Bay.

## Regional Connections
Hudson Strait is part of a complex network of waterways and landmasses in the Franklin district. Baffin Land is separated from other regions by several distinct channels and straits:
* **To the West:** Baffin Land is separated from the Melville Peninsula and Keewatin by the Fury-and-Hecla Strait and Fox Channel.
* **To the North:** It is separated from North Devon by Lancaster Sound, and from North Somerset and the Boothia Peninsula by Prince Regent Inlet and the Gulf of Boothia.
* **To the East:** Baffin Land is separated from Greenland by Davis Strait and Baffin Bay.

The Boothia Peninsula, which has an area of 13,100 square miles, forms the western side of the Boothia Gulf. This gulf separates the Melville Peninsula and the north-western portion of Baffin Land from the Boothia Peninsula. The Boothia Gulf is connected to Fox Channel via the Fury and Hecla Strait.

## Associated Landmasses
The regions surrounding these waterways include various named territories. Within Baffin Land, the north-western area is known as Cockburn Land, while North Galloway lies further east. The southern portion of Baffin Land is called Meta Incognita, and the western area is referred to as Fox Land. The interior of the southern region contains two large lakes: Nettiling (also known as Kennedy) and Amadjuak, the latter of which sits at an altitude of 289 feet.

To the east, Greenland is a large continental island of arctic character with an estimated area of 827,275 square miles. It is separated from Baffin Land by Baffin Bay and Davis Strait. A submarine ridge connects Greenland to the Cumberland Peninsula of Baffin Land across the Davis Strait.

## Regional Fauna
The territories surrounding the Hudson Bay and Baffin Land areas are habitats for various species of foxes. The Blue Fox, characterized by a slaty or drab tone and thick, long underwool, is found in Greenland, Archangel, Alaska, and the Hudson Bay territory. Additionally, the Cross Fox—which features a pale yellowish or orange tone with a darkish cross marking on the shoulders—is found in Hudson Bay and Labrador, where the darkest and best quality specimens originate.
