# ASCENSION

## Geography and Location
Ascension is an island situated in the Atlantic Ocean. Its geographical coordinates place it between 7 deg. 53' and 8 deg. S. latitude, and between 14 deg. 18' and 14 deg. 26' W. longitude. The island is located approximately 800 m. northwest of St Helena. In terms of size, Ascension is about 7-1/2 m. in length and 6 m. in breadth, encompassing an area of 38 sq. m. and possessing a circumference of roughly 22 m.

## Geology and Topography
The island is a volcanic mass that has been erected on a submarine platform, and products of volcanic action are found throughout the island. The landscape is characterized by numerous cones. The principal elevation is Green Mountain, which is a huge elliptical crater reaching 2820 ft. above the sea. Surrounding this peak are table-lands or plains that vary in height from 1200 to 2000 ft. 

The topography differs significantly between the north and south; while the plains sweep gradually down toward the shore on the north side, they terminate in lofty and bold precipices on the south. The plains are further intersected by rugged and steep ravines that open into coves or small bays along the shore, which are fenced with cellular and compact masses of lava.

## Climate and Vegetation
Ascension is located within the immediate influence of the south-east trade-wind. The island's climate is described as remarkably healthy despite high temperatures. The average day temperature on the shore is 85 deg. F., while on Green Mountain it is 75 deg. F. The average rainfall is approximately 20 in., with the rainy months occurring in March and April. The air is light and clear.

Originally, the island was destitute of vegetation, with the exception of the summit of Green Mountain. The verdure of the summit is attributed to the mists that frequently enshroud it. However, shrubs and grasses have since been planted on the lower hills.

## Flora and Fauna
The island does not possess any indigenous vertebrate land fauna, a characteristic it shares with St Helena. Despite this, various animals have been introduced or are present; numbers of sheep are bred on the island, and there are goats, wild cats, as well as a few deer and cattle. Feathered game is abundant, and "wideawake" birds frequent the island in large numbers; their eggs are collected for consumption. Beetles and land-shells are also found.

The shores of Ascension are noted for the presence of turtle eggs and turtles, with the season lasting from December to May or June. These turtles are caught and kept in large ponds. The surrounding coasts are rich in various high-quality fish, the most important of which include the "soldier," the conger-eel, the cavalli, and the rock-cod.

## Maritime Conditions
The lee side of the island is subject to the visitation of "rollers," which break upon the shore with very great violence.
