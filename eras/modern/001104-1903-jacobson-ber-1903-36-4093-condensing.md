## Synthesis of Meta-oxyazobenzene
The production of meta-oxyazobenzene involves a multi-step chemical sequence. The process begins with the condensing of diazo benzene with ortho-anisidine. Following this initial condensation, the resulting compound undergoes diazotization. This is then reduced by alcohol to produce benzene-azo-meta-anisole. The final stage of the process is the hydrolysis of benzene-azo-meta-anisole using aluminium chloride, which yields meta-oxyazobenzene.
