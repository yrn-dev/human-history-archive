# Walter Bagehot and His Literary Works

Walter Bagehot was a writer whose studies were deeply influenced by the atmosphere of his active life in business and politics. His literary output grew out of these activities, and his writing is characterized by a remarkable gift for a conversational, epigrammatic style that provides a peculiar zest. A defining quality of his books is the use of scientific, dispassionate description applied to subjects that had not previously been considered as candidates for scientific study.

## Political and Social Connections

Bagehot's integration into the political life of his era was facilitated partly by his marriage to the daughter of the first editor of the *Economist*, the Right Hon. James Wilson. Wilson served as the secretary of the treasury and later as the secretary of finance in India. Through these connections, Bagehot became an intimate friend of Sir George Cornewall Lewis and maintained constant communication with permanent heads of great state departments and political chiefs, including Grant Duff, Robert Lowe, and Gladstone.

His influence extended into the financial sector of the city, where he was intimate with the directors and governor of the Bank of England, as well as leading commercial and banking magnates. Additionally, his involvement with the Political Economy Club provided further contact with both the political and city spheres.

## Major Works

Two of Bagehot's books are noted for bringing him the most reputation: *The English Constitution* (1867) and *Lombard Street* (1873). Both are categorized as books of observation and description.

### The English Constitution
In *The English Constitution*, Bagehot avoids the approach of a lawyer and does not rely on law books for his description. Instead, he describes the constitution based on its actual working. This perspective was derived from his direct contact with the heads of government departments, ministers, and the society in which politicians moved. This approach allowed him to describe the methods and true springs of action with a vivid freshness.

### Lombard Street
Along with *The English Constitution*, *Lombard Street* is recognized as one of his most reputable works, applying his method of scientific description to its subject matter.
