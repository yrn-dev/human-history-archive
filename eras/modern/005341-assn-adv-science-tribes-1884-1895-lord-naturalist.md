# Assn. Adv. Science on N.W. Tribes (1884-1895) and Lord, Naturalist in Vancouver Island

## Overview of Reports on N.W. Tribes
The archive contains references to the "Reports of Committee of Brit. Assn. Adv. Science on N.W. Tribes," which were produced between the years 1884 and 1895. These reports serve as authorities for historical and scientific data regarding the Northwest tribes.

## Lord and the Natural History of Vancouver Island
In addition to the committee reports, the archive lists the work of Lord, titled *Naturalist in Vancouver Island*, published in London in 1866. This text is cited as an authority alongside other regional records and surveys.

## Related Regional Authorities
The reports on the Northwest tribes and the work of Lord are part of a broader collection of sources used to document the history and geography of British Columbia and Vancouver Island. Other supporting authorities include:
*   **Voyage Records:** Cook's *Voyage to the Pacific Ocean* (1784) and Vancouver's *Voyage of Discovery to the Pacific Ocean* (1798).
*   **Regional Histories:** Works by H.H. Bancroft, specifically *History of British Columbia* (1887), and Begg's *History of British Columbia* (1894).
*   **Government and Official Reports:** The *Annual Reports of Minister of Mines*, other departmental reports from the Dominion and Provincial governments, the *Catalogue of Provincial Museum* in Victoria, and the *Annual Reports British Columbia Board of Trade*.
*   **Surveys:** Reports from the Geological Survey of Canada (from 1871 onwards) and the *Reports of Canadian Pacific (Government) Surveys* covering the period from 1872 to 1880.
*   **Other Documentation:** The *Year Book* by Gosnell (1897 and 1903), the *Report of Bering Sea Commission* (1892), and a reprint of letters to the *Times* regarding the *Bering Sea Arbitration* (1893). A French work, *La Colombie Britannique* by A. Métin (1908), is also noted.

## Historical Context of the Region
The areas discussed in these authorities, specifically British Columbia and Vancouver Island, saw settlement under the Hudson's Bay Co. in 1821. These regions subsequently entered the Canadian confederation in 1871.
