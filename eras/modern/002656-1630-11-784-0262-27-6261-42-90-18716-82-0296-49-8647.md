## Architectural Developments in India
In the year A.D. 1630, two significant structures were established in India: the Taj Mahal and the Gol Gumbaz. The Gol Gumbaz, located at Bijapur, serves as the tomb of Mahommed Adil Shah. Its architectural design features domes supported by pendentives, which are composed of arches that cross one another and project inwards; this structure allows the weight of the pendentives to counteract any thrust produced by the dome.

Similarly, the Taj Mahal utilizes a bulbous dome. This specific form was employed because the increased thickness of the dome below the haunches provides weight that serves as a counterpoise to any thrust exerted by the upper portion of the dome.
