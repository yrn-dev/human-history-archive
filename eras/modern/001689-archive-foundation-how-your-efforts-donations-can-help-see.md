## The Literary Archive Foundation
In 2001, the Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation was established. The primary purpose of creating the Foundation was to ensure a permanent and secure future for Project Gutenberg and for future generations. 

Legally, the Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation is organized as a non-profit 501(c)(3) educational corporation. It operates under the laws of the state of Mississippi and has been granted tax-exempt status by the Internal Revenue Service. The federal tax identification number (EIN) for the Foundation is 64-6221541.
