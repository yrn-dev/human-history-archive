# The Bolivian Judiciary

The judiciary of Bolivia is organized into a hierarchical structure comprising several levels of courts and specialized officials tasked with the investigation and preparation of legal cases.

## Court Structure and Composition
The national judiciary is headed by a national supreme court. This court is composed of seven justices. The selection process for these justices involves the Senate, which sends lists of three names for each available seat to the Chamber of Deputies, who then elect the justices. Once appointed, a justice can only be removed from office through impeachment proceedings conducted before the Senate.

Below the national supreme court, the judicial system includes:
* Eight superior district courts.
* Lower district courts.
* *Juzgados de instruccion*, which are specifically responsible for the preparation and investigation of cases.

## Local Justice and Administration
In rural districts and cantons, the functions of a justice of the peace are exercised by *alcaldes* and *corregidores*. These roles are part of a broader administrative hierarchy where the national executive maintains significant influence. 

The supreme administration of each department is managed by a prefect. The prefect is appointed by the president and is responsible solely to the president. Because the prefect holds the power to appoint subordinate department officials—including the *alcaldes*—the authority of the national executive extends to every hamlet within the republic. This structure is noted for its potential to become autocratic.

## Departmental and Provincial Governance
Government at the departmental level rests with the congress and the national executive, as there are no legislative assemblies within the departments. The administrative hierarchy subordinate to the prefects consists of:
* Sub-prefects, who operate in the provinces.
* *Corregidores*, who operate in the cantons.
* *Alcaldes*, who operate in the rural districts.

All of these positions are appointed officials. In the capitals of the departments, municipal councils are established. These councils have jurisdiction over specific local affairs and are responsible for the maintenance and construction of certain highways.

## National Territory and Border Governance
The areas of national territory adjacent to Peru and Brazil are governed by *delegados nacionales*. These officials are appointees of the president.
