# Provinces and Towns

The administrative organization of various protectorates and regions across Africa, Asia, and the Americas involves a complex division of provinces, districts, and urban centers, often established or reorganized under colonial administration.

## East Africa Protectorate
The East Africa protectorate is divided into several provinces, each further subdivided into districts and sub-districts. These include:
*   **Seyyidie:** The south coast province with its capital at Mombasa.
*   **Ukamba:** Occupying the center of the protectorate with its capital at Nairobi.
*   **Kenya:** The district of Mt. Kenya, with Fort Hall as its capital.
*   **Tanaland:** Located north of Seyyidie and Ukamba, with its capital at Lamu.
*   **Jubaland:** The northern region, with Kismayu as its capital.
*   **Naivasha and Kisumu:** Both have capitals of the same name and adjoin the Victoria Nyanza. These were transferred to the East Africa protectorate from the eastern province of Uganda on April 1, 1902.

Mombasa serves as the protectorate's chief port, housing approximately 30,000 people. Its harbor, Kilindini, is the terminus for the Uganda railway. Nearby on the mainland is Freretown (known natively as Kisaoni), a settlement for freed slaves and the headquarters of the Church Missionary Society in East Africa. Other notable coastal towns include Malindi—where Vasco da Gama erected a pillar in 1498—Patta, Kipini, and Kismayu. Kismayu, 320 miles northeast of Mombasa, is the protectorate's northernmost port.

Nairobi, located 327 miles from Mombasa, was designated the administrative capital of the protectorate in 1907. Naivasha is situated 64 miles north-north-west of Nairobi in the rift-valley at an elevation of 6,230 feet.

## Nigeria
The protectorate of Nigeria was divided into North and South Nigeria in 1900.

### Northern Nigeria
Several provinces exist within Northern Nigeria, including:
*   **Bassa:** Located where the Benue river meets the Niger, this heavily forested province covers 7,000 square miles with an estimated population of 1.5 million. It is bounded by the Benue to the north, the Niger to the west, the province of Muri to the east, and the frontier of Southern Nigeria to the south.
*   **Kontagora:** A province of 14,500 square miles with approximately 80,000 inhabitants. It is bounded by the Niger to the west, the province of Nupe to the south, Zaria to the east, and Sokoto to the north. Formerly a Fula emirate, it was brought under British domination in 1901.
*   **Nupe:** The capital of this province is Bida, a walled town founded in 1859. Bida is a center for glass manufacturing, indigo preparation, and embossed brass and copper work.

### Southern Nigeria
Calabar (or Old Calabar) is a seaport on the left bank of the Calabar river and serves as the capital of the eastern province of the Southern Nigeria protectorate. It has a population of about 15,000 and is a center for the import of spirits and cotton goods and the export of palm kernels and palm oil.

### West Africa (British)
Illorin is a province in the protectorate of Nigeria with an area of 6,300 miles and a population of about 250,000. It is divided into three divisions: Patiji (northern), Illorin (central), and Offa (southern). The province is rich in sylvan products (rubber, palm-oil, and shea) and agricultural products (cotton, tobacco, kolas, rice, ground-nuts, and peppers). The capital, Illorin, is a major trading center connected by railway to Lagos and Jebba.

## Other Global Regions
### Asia and the Americas
*   **Philippine Islands:** In the province of Iloilo on Panay, the town of Barotac Nuevo is located near the Jalaur river. As of 1903, it had a population of 9,904, after which the town of Dumangas (population 12,428) was annexed to it.
*   **India:** The province of Behar is divided by the Ganges river. To the north lie the districts of Purnea, Tirhoot, Champaran, Saran, and parts of Bhagalpur and Monghyr. To the south are the Santal parganas, Gaya, Patna, Shahabad, and the remainder of Monghyr and Bhagalpur. The town of Patna serves as the headquarters for the Behar Opium Agency.
*   **Japan:** In mythological tradition, the province of Izumo served as the seat of power for the Great-Name Possessor.
*   **Canada:** Manitoba was created as a province of Canada in 1870. British Columbia and Vancouver Island entered the Canadian confederation in 1871.
