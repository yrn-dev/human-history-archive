## Agricultural and Industrial Practices
Agricultural fencing practices vary between the United States and Argentina. In the western United States, barbed wire fencing is used extensively by farmers, though new types of meshed wire field fencing have become common since 1899. While Argentina also uses barbed wire on a comparatively large scale, it utilizes a larger quantity of plain wire fence. Furthermore, Argentina has an important consumption of high-carbon oval fence wire of great strength, which is the only type of fence that satisfactorily meets the conditions there.

## Political History and the Portuguese Empire
The Portuguese Empire's relationship with Brazil evolved significantly during the early 19th century. In 1807, following the occupation of Portugal by Napoleon's French armies, the Portuguese monarchy fled to Brazil. This shift resulted in a period where Portugal became more of a dependency of Brazil than vice versa. In 1822, Brazil declared itself a separate Empire under Pedro I, a son of the Portuguese King. This monarchy lasted until 1889, when the Emperor was shipped back to Europe and the United States of Brazil became a republic, aligning with other republican states in America.

Portugal also maintained overseas territories including the Cape Verde Islands (featuring the port of Porto Grande) and the Azores. The Azores include the islands of St Michael's, Graciosa, and St George. The port of Ponta Delgado in the Azores is noted as one of the most frequented coaling stations and a significant center for the transatlantic cable system.

## Religious and Cultural Influence
The congregational system of the Benedictine order, which emerged from reforming councils in the later middle ages, was imported into various Latin lands. This system was established in Spain and Portugal, and from there, it was extended into Brazil.

## Global Conflict and Diplomacy
During the First World War, Portugal and the United States were both part of the Allied powers. Portugal joined the Allies toward the end of the European Powers' alignment, and the United States of America joined in April 1917. The entry of the United States was considered vital for the Allied victory, as it provided a necessary counterbalance to the collapse of Russia following the revolution in March 1917.
