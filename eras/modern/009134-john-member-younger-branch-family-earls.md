# Henry St John, Viscount Bolingbroke

## Early Life and Education
Henry St John, Viscount Bolingbroke (1678–1751), was an English writer and statesman. He was the son of Lady Mary Rich, who was the daughter of the 2nd earl of Warwick, and Sir Henry St John, Bart. Sir Henry St John later became the 1st Viscount St John and was a member of a younger branch of the family of the barons St John of Bletso and the earls of Bolingbroke.

Henry St John was baptized on October 10, 1678. He received his education at Eton and subsequently traveled abroad during the years 1698 and 1699, where he gained an exceptional knowledge of the French language.

## Personal Conduct and Reputation
The youth of Henry St John was characterized by extravagance and dissipation that surprised his contemporaries and exceeded all limits. He was known to engage in riotous orgies lasting for weeks and was said to outdrink even the most experienced drunkards. An informant of Goldsmith reported one instance where St John, in a state of intoxication, ran naked through the park.

According to his intimate friend, Swift, St John desired to be viewed as the Petronius or Alcibiades of his era, seeking to combine the highest political responsibilities with licentious orgies.

## Marriage and Later Life
In 1700, Henry St John married Frances, the daughter of Sir Henry Winchcombe, Bart., of Bucklebury, Berkshire. While this matrimony served to improve his fortune, it did not redeem his morals.
