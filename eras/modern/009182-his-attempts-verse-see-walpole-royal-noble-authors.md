# Royal and Noble Authors

*Royal and Noble Authors* is a work by Horace Walpole (also referred to as H. Walpole) that serves as a source for the literary attempts and biographical details of various historical figures. Published in 1806 (with some references citing a version edited by Park), the text is utilized as a bibliographic authority for the study of several noblemen and their contributions to verse and wit.

## Literary Analysis of Noble Figures

Walpole uses *Royal and Noble Authors* to evaluate the intellectual and poetic capabilities of his subjects. In the case of Dorset, Walpole notes in volume IV that he possessed a level of wit comparable to his contemporaries, such as Rochester and the duke of Buckingham, as well as his first master. Walpole further distinguishes Dorset by suggesting he lacked the "royal want of feeling" seen in others, the "want of principles" attributed to the duke, or the "want of thought" associated with the earl.

The work is also cited as a reference for the verse attempts of Bolingbroke, specifically in volume IV, page 209 and following.

## Use as a Biographical Authority

The text is frequently cited in historical and biographical archives to trace the lives of various English figures:

*   **The Duke of Buckingham:** The work is referenced in the bibliography of George Villiers, 2nd Duke of Buckingham, specifically in volume III, page 304.
*   **Bristol:** *Royal and Noble Authors* (1806) is listed as a source for the life of Bristol, with specific references found on page 49 of volume III.
*   **Other Authorities:** The text is cited in the authorities for other figures in volume III, page 191.

## The Author and His Context

Horace Walpole is characterized in the archive as a writer of European knowledge and sentiment, as well as one of aristocratic experience. He is grouped with other prose writers of the middle period of the 18th century, such as Lord Chesterfield and Oliver Goldsmith. Walpole's writing style was influenced by the "sovereign lucidity" of the best French style of that era.

In addition to *Royal and Noble Authors*, Walpole authored other works including *Memoirs of the Reign of George II* (1845), *Memoirs on Turkey*, and a *Catalogue of Pictures of George Duke of Buckingham* (1758).
