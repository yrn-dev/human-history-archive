# BULDUR

Buldur, also known as Burdur, is a town in Asia Minor and serves as the chief town of a sanjak within the Konia vilayet. Among the Christian population, the town is referred to as *Polydorion*.

## Geography and Environment
The town is situated at an altitude of 3,150 feet and is located in the midst of gardens. It lies approximately 2 miles from the Buldur Geul, a brackish salt lake that was known in antiquity as *Ascania Limne*. The Buldur Geul is situated 2,900 feet above sea level.

## Economy and Industry
The principal industries in Buldur are leather-tanning and linen-weaving. The surrounding Buldur plain is a source of grain, which is transported via a good carriage road to Dineir.

## Infrastructure and Transport
Buldur is connected to Egirdir and Dineir by a railway.

## Administrative Context
Buldur is one of five sanjaks that comprise the Konia vilayet; the others are Adalia, Hamid-abad, Konia, and Nigdeh. The Konia vilayet was established in 1864 through the addition of part of south-eastern Anadoli and the western half of Adana to the former eyalet of Karamania. This vilayet encompasses parts or the entirety of several regions, including Lycaonia, Phrygia, Pisidia, Pamphylia, Cappadocia, and Cilicia.

The broader vilayet has a population consisting of 990,000 Moslems and 80,000 Christians, most of whom are engaged in pastoral and agricultural work. While Buldur focuses on leather and linen, the wider vilayet's industries include the manufacture of silk and cotton stuffs, as well as carpet-weaving. The region is also known for its mineral wealth, including rock salt, cinnabar, mercury, chrome, and argentiferous lead. The Anatolian railway traverses the vilayet and contains the Ottoman line's railhead from Smyrna.

## Demographics
The population of Buldur is 12,000.
