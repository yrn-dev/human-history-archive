# Nikolayev and the Southern Bug

## Geography and Hydrology
Nikolayev is a port city located in south Russia, situated at a point where the Southern Bug river picks up the Ingul from the left. From this location, the river enters a *liman*, or lagoon, which also serves as the discharge point for the Dnieper river. 

The Southern Bug is a stream of European Russia that rises in the south of the government of Volhynia. It flows generally in a south-easterly direction through the governments of Podolia and Kherson. The river has a total length of 470 meters. Its physical characteristics vary by section: the upper part is characterized by rapids, while the lower part is of little value for navigation due to the presence of numerous blocks of rock and sandbanks that choke its bed.

The landscape of the Kherson government, particularly in the south, is primarily open steppe and is almost entirely devoid of forest. As a port, Nikolayev plays a key role in the commercial infrastructure of south Russia. It is part of a network of important ports in the region that includes Odessa, Ochakov, and Kherson. The city is also connected via railway to other regional centers, such as Kremenchug, a town in the government of Poltava located on the left bank of the Dnieper.

The broader Kherson government is a center for developed agriculture, utilizing extensive machinery. Major agricultural products include maize, hemp, flax, mustard, and tobacco, as well as ordinary cereals. The region is also known for the wide growth of vines, producing 1,220,000 gallons of wine annually, and the cultivation of fruit trees such as the apricot, peach, plum, cherry, and mulberry. Large-scale animal husbandry is also practiced, including horse-breeding, cattle-breeding, and sheep-farming, with some merino sheep farmers owning 30,000 to 40,000 head.

Industrial activities in the region include chemical works, sugar factories, steam flour-mills, iron-works, and the manufacture of leather, hemp, and wool. There is a particular emphasis on the production of agricultural machinery.

## Naval History
Nikolayev's importance as a naval center was solidified in 1794. During this year, the naval establishments were removed from the town of Kherson and relocated to Nikolayev. This transition occurred after Kherson had been founded in 1778 by the courtier Potemkin to serve as a seaport and naval station.
