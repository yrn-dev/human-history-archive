# Tieftrunk, Dr Vaclav Flajshans and Mr Jaroslav Vlaek

The historical and literary scholarship of Bohemia has been shaped by various researchers and authors who have documented the region's national language, its early history, and the lives of its influential figures. Among these contributors are Dr Karel Tieftrunk, Dr Vaclav Flajshans, and Mr Jaroslav Vlaek.

## Contributions to Bohemian Literature
Dr Karel Tieftrunk, Dr Vaclav Flajshans, and Mr Jaroslav Vlaek are recognized as authors of modern histories of Bohemian literature. These works were written in the national language.

## The Scholarship of Dr Vaclav Flajshans
In addition to his work on the history of Bohemian literature, Dr Vaclav Flajshans has produced several studies described as excellent regarding the writings and life of John Huss.

## The Editorial Work of Professor Tieftrunk
Professor Tieftrunk has contributed to the preservation of Bohemian historical records through editorial work. Specifically, he edited and published the most valuable portion of the *Histoire cirkevni* (history of the church), a vast work consisting of ten enormous manuscript volumes written by Paul Skala ze Zhore. Paul Skala ze Zhore (1582-c. 1640) was an official for Frederick of the Palatinate, known as the "winter king," and wrote his historical accounts after his political activity had ceased. The section edited by Professor Tieftrunk focuses on the events occurring between 1602 and 1623, a period during which Skala wrote as a contemporary and frequently as an eye-witness.
