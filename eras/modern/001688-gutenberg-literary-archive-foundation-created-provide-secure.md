# Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation

## Overview of Project Gutenberg
Project Gutenberg is characterized by the free distribution of electronic works. These works are provided in formats designed to be readable by the widest possible variety of computers, spanning across different eras including new, middle-aged, old, and obsolete systems. The existence of the project is made possible through the contributions of donations from people from all walks of life and the efforts of hundreds of volunteers.

## Goals and Sustainability
The primary objective of Project Gutenberg is to ensure that its collection remains freely available for future generations. To achieve these goals, the project relies heavily on volunteers and the financial support necessary to provide those volunteers with required assistance.

## The Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation
In 2001, the Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation was established. The purpose of creating the Foundation was to provide a permanent and secure future for both Project Gutenberg and future generations.
