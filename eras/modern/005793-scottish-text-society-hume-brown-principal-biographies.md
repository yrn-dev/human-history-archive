# Scottish Text Society and the Biographies of P. Hume Brown

The Scottish Text Society is an organization involved in the editing and publication of historical and literary texts. Its work includes the reproduction of scholarly editions, such as the revised version of W. W. Skeat's edition of *The Brus* (published 1893-1895) and W. M. Metcalfe's three-volume *Legends of the Saints in the Scottish Dialect of the Fourteenth Century* (1896).

## Editorial Work of P. Hume Brown
P. Hume Brown contributed to the Scottish Text Society by editing the *Vernacular Writings* of George Buchanan. This collection includes two letters, a tract concerning the Reformation of St Andrews University, the *Chamaeleon*, and *Ane Admonitioun to the Trew Lordis*.

## Biographies of George Buchanan
P. Hume Brown authored two principal biographies of George Buchanan: *George Buchanan, Humanist and Reformer*, published in Edinburgh in 1890, and *George Buchanan and his Times*, published in Edinburgh in 1906. 

Other significant biographies of Buchanan include:
* David Irving's *Memoirs of the Life and Writings of George Buchanan* (Edinburgh, 1807 and 1817).
* Rev. D. Macmillan's *George Buchanan, a Biography* (Edinburgh, 1906).

Additionally, the quatercentenary of Buchanan in 1906 led to the publication of *George Buchanan: Glasgow Quatercentenary Studies* (Glasgow, 1906) and *George Buchanan, a Memoir*, edited by D.A. Millar (St Andrews, 1907).

## Biography of John Knox
P. Hume Brown also wrote a biography of John Knox, published in Edinburgh in 1895. This work is cited as one of two express biographies of Knox that should be especially consulted, alongside the work of Thomas McCrie (Edinburgh, 1811; revised 1813). 

In the study of Knox's life, Professor Hume Brown held a differing view from John Hill Burton regarding certain dialogues; while Burton believed the earlier dialogues must have been spoken in French—the language in which Knox had preached for a year—Hume Brown disagreed.
