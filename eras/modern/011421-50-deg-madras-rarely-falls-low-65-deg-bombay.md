# Temperature Variations in Southern Asia and Other Regions

## Temperature Ranges in Southern Asia
In southern Asia, particularly in areas near the sea, the variation between the hottest and coldest monthly means is significantly lower than in regions like Siberia. Under the equator, this variation is reduced to approximately 5 degrees. In northern India, the greatest difference between monthly means does not exceed 40 degrees. This range decreases further in coastal cities: it falls to about 15 degrees at Calcutta and to approximately 10 or 12 degrees at Bombay and Madras. Similar temperature ranges are found at the head of the Persian Gulf (approximating northern India) and at Aden (approximating Madras). In Batavia in Java, Galle in Ceylon, and Singapore, the range is about 5 degrees or less.

## Extreme Temperatures of Southern Asia
Maximum temperatures in southern Asia hardly ever exceed 110 or 112 degrees Fahr. Minimum temperatures vary by location:
* **Northern India (lat. 30 deg.):** The absolute minimum hardly drops below 32 degrees.
* **Calcutta:** The absolute minimum is about 40 degrees, though the thermometer seldom falls to 50 degrees.
* **Madras:** Temperatures rarely fall as low as 65 degrees.
* **Bombay:** Temperatures rarely fall below 60 degrees.
* **Singapore and Batavia:** The thermometer very rarely rises above 90 degrees or falls below 70 degrees.
* **Aden:** The maximum does not much exceed 90 degrees, while the minimum is a few degrees below 70 degrees.

## Regional Climatic Characteristics in India
The Bombay Presidency exhibits great climatic variety. Upper Sind is characterized by extreme heat and dryness, resembling African sultry deserts; the thermometer there has been known to register 130 degrees in the shade. In Lower Sind, the mean maximum temperature at Hyderabad during the six hottest months is 98 degrees in the shade. The Konkan region is moist and hot, with monsoon rains sometimes approaching 300 inches. In contrast, the southern Mahratta country and the table-land of the Deccan above the Ghats possess an agreeable climate, except during hot months. Bombay Island is generally cooled by sea breezes but remains oppressively hot during October and May.

On the Coromandel Coast—the eastern seaboard of India between the mouths of the Kistna river and Cape Calimere—hot southerly winds blow by day from April to October, while sea-breezes temper the heat at night.

## Comparative Global Temperatures
The temperature variations in southern Asia contrast sharply with those in Siberia, where the difference between the mean of the hottest and coldest months is hardly anywhere less than 60 degrees Fahr., reaching more than 100 degrees near the coast. In Siberia, maxima lie between 80 and 90 degrees Fahr., while minima range between -40 and -70 degrees Fahr. Near the Aral and Caspian Seas, heat rises to nearly 100 degrees Fahr. and cold falls to -20 degrees Fahr. or lower. Specifically, the difference between the means of the hottest and coldest months is 80 degrees Fahr. on the Sea of Aral and more than 50 degrees at Astrakhan.

Other regional variations include:
* **China:** In Peking, the difference is 55 degrees, reducing to 30 degrees at Canton and 20 degrees at Manila.
* **Tiflis:** The difference is 45 degrees.
* **Costa Rica:** The tropical zone (coast and foothills) has a mean annual temperature between 72 and 82 degrees F. In the San Jose plateau, the average is 68 degrees with a seasonal variation of only 5 degrees.
* **Castile:** New Castile has a mean annual temperature of about 59 degrees Fahr.
