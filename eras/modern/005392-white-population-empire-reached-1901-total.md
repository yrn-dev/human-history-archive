# Population of the Empire

## White Population and Distribution
In 1901, the white population of the empire totaled over 53,000,000 people. This figure represents slightly more than one-eighth of the empire's entire population. While this group includes Spanish, Dutch, and French peoples, it is primarily composed of the Anglo-Saxon race. Additionally, there is a considerable sprinkling of Jewish blood among the white races throughout the empire, particularly within the United Kingdom.

The distribution of the white population is recorded as follows:
*   **United Kingdom and home dependencies:** 41,608,791
*   **British North America:** 5,500,000
*   **Australasia:** 4,662,000
*   **Africa (British and Dutch):** 1,000,000
*   **India:** 169,677
*   **West Indies and Bermuda:** 100,000

The total calculated white population for these regions is 53,040,468.

## Native Populations
The native population of the empire is estimated at approximately 400,000,000, consisting of the principal brown, yellow, and black races. This classification includes high-type races of the East, which are described as almost white.

In India, the native population—which is mainly brown and high type—was recorded in the 1901 census as 294,191,379. This population is categorized into 118 groups based on language, which are further collected into principal groups:

## Total Imperial and Global Context
When combining the white population (53,040,468) and the summarized native populations (335,672,317), the total is 388,712,785. This total does not account for the population of lesser crown colonies or potential increases from later censuses.

On a global scale, the total population of the world is estimated at over 1,500,000,000, a figure that includes a liberal estimate of 650,000,000 people who have not been brought under any census. Consequently, the population of the empire is calculated to be more than one-fourth of the total world population.

## Regional Observations
In specific territories, racial and ethnic compositions vary. In Barbados, the population in 1901 was 195,588, with negroes outnumbering whites by a ratio of 9 to 1. In British Columbia, the 1901 census recorded a population of 190,000, which included 14,201 Chinese individuals and 25,488 Indians. The Indian population there is divided into many tribes, including the Tinneh or Athapackan in the northern interior, the Salish and Kootenay in the southern interior, and coastal groups such as the Tsimshian, Haida, Nootka, and Kwakiatl.
