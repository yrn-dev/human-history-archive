# The Russian Revolution and the Rise of Communism

## Overview
The Russian Revolution of 1917 was not a single event, but a series of upheavals that dismantled the Tsarist autocracy and led to the creation of the Soviet Union, the world's first socialist state. Driven by systemic inequality, the failures of the Romanov dynasty, and the catastrophic toll of World War I, the revolution transitioned Russia from a feudal empire to a centralized communist state under the leadership of Vladimir Lenin and the Bolsheviks.

The process occurred in two primary stages. The **February Revolution** saw the abdication of Tsar Nicholas II and the establishment of a Provisional Government. However, this government's insistence on continuing the war effort alienated the masses. This vacuum allowed the **October Revolution** to occur, where the Bolsheviks seized power under the slogan "Peace, Land, and Bread."

Historians continue to debate the nature of the October Revolution. Some, following the traditionalist view, see it as a popular uprising of the proletariat. Others, such as Richard Pipes, argue it was a disciplined *coup d'état* carried out by a small minority of radicals who manipulated the chaos of the era.

## Key Facts
*   **The Role of the Soviets:** "Soviets" were workers' councils that acted as grassroots democratic bodies. The Bolsheviks gained power by infiltrating these councils to claim they represented the "will of the people."
*   **War Communism:** To survive the Civil War, the Bolsheviks implemented "War Communism," involving the forced requisitioning of grain from peasants to feed the Red Army and urban centers.
*   **The Red Terror:** To consolidate power and eliminate "class enemies," the Cheka (secret police) conducted a campaign of mass arrests and executions known as the Red Terror.
*   **The NEP:** Recognizing the economic collapse caused by War Communism, Lenin introduced the New Economic Policy (NEP) in 1921, which allowed for a limited return to private trade and capitalism.

## Did You Know?
1. **The Calendar Gap:** The "October Revolution" actually took place in November according to the Gregorian calendar; Russia was still using the Julian calendar at the time.
2. **The Romanov Fate:** Tsar Nicholas II and his entire immediate family were executed in a basement in Yekaterinburg in July 1918 to prevent them from becoming a rallying point for White Army forces.
3. **Lenin's Influence:** While Marx provided the theoretical framework, Lenin adapted communism for a rural society (Marxism-Leninism), arguing that a "vanguard party" was necessary to lead the workers.
