# Fernandez Duro and the History of the Azores

## C. Fernandez Duro
C. Fernandez Duro is a historian whose work focuses on Spanish military and territorial history. In 1886, he published a work in Madrid titled *La Conquista de las Azores en 1583*, which serves as a historical resource regarding the conquest of the Azores.

Additionally, Captain Cesareo Fernandez Duro authored *Armada Espanola*, published in Madrid between 1895 and 1901. This work is cited as an authority regarding the Spanish share of naval operations during the Dutch Wars.

## Histoire de la decouverte des iles Azores
The discovery and naming of the Azores are detailed in the work *Histoire de la decouverte des iles Azores et de l'origine de leur denomination d'iles flamandes*. This text was written by J. Mees and published in Ghent in 1901.

## Related Azorean Studies
Beyond the historical accounts of conquest and discovery, other sources provide comprehensive data on the Azores. General accounts of the islands include *The Azores* by W. F. Walker (London, 1886) and *Madeira and the Canary Islands, with the Azores* by A. S. Brown (London, 1901).

The natural history of the archipelago is documented in several works by H. Drouet, including:
* *Mollusques marins des iles Acores* (1858)
* *Elements de la faune acoreenne* (Paris, 1861)
* *Lettres acoreennes* (1862)
* *Catalogue de la flore des iles Acores, precede de l'itineraire d'une voyage dans cet archipel* (1866)

Furthermore, the commercial progress of the Azores is documented within American and British consular reports.
