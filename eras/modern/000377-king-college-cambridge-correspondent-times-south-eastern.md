## Notable Alumni and Literary Figures

The college has educated individuals who achieved prominence in literature and archaeology. Arthur Christopher Benson (born 1862) was educated at King's College, Cambridge, and Eton. He later became a master at Eton College from 1885 to 1903 and a fellow of Magdalene College, Cambridge. Benson's literary output includes *Memoirs of Arthur Hamilton* (1886), published under the pseudonym "Christopher Carr," as well as *Poems* (1893), *Lyrics* (1895), and *Lord Vyet and other Poems* (1897). His other works include *Fasti Etonenses* (1899), *The Schoolmaster* (1902), and *From a College Window* (1906). He also collaborated with Lord Esher to edit the *Correspondence of Queen Victoria* (1907).

Edward Frederick Benson (born 1867), the third son of the same family, was also educated at King's College, Cambridge, and Marlborough College. He pursued archaeological work for the Hellenic Society in Egypt and for the British Archaeological Society in Athens between 1892 and 1895. He is known for his fiction, specifically the society novel *Dodo* (1893), followed by *The Vintage* (1898) and *The Capsina* (1899).
