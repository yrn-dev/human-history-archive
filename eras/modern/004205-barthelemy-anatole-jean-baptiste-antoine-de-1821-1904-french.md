# BARTHELEMY, ANATOLE JEAN-BAPTISTE ANTOINE DE (1821-1904)

Anatole Jean-Baptiste Antoine de Barthelemy was a French numismatist and archaeologist. Born in Reims on July 1, 1821, he spent his professional life contributing to the study of history and coinage before his death at Ville d'Avray on June 27, 1904.

## Academic and Regional History
Barthelemy produced significant research regarding the history and monuments of Brittany. In 1858, he collaborated with J. Geslin de Bourgogne to publish *Etudes sur la revolution en Bretagne*. Between 1855 and 1879, the two authors produced an exhaustive six-volume work titled *Anciens eveches de Bretagne; histoire et monuments*. Later, in 1880, Barthelemy published *Choix de documents inedits sur l'histoire de la ligue en Bretagne* as a sole author.

## Numismatic Contributions
While his regional historical work was extensive, Barthelemy's reputation was primarily established through his work in numismatics. He authored several popular publications, including the *Nouveau manuel complet de numismatique ancienne*, first published in 1851 with a revised second edition appearing in 1890. He also wrote the *Nouveau manuel complet de numismatique du moyen age et moderne* in 1853, which was later revised in a new edition by Adrien Planchet.

Beyond these manuals, Barthelemy contributed numerous articles to technical reviews and wrote several monographs. Notable among these are:
* *Numismatique merovingienne* (1865)
* *Essai sur la monnaie parisis* (1874)
* *Note sur l'origine de la monnaie tournoise* (1896)

Additionally, he edited the volume on *La Numismatique de la France* (1891) for the series of instructions issued by the *Comite des travaux historiques et scientifiques*.

## Honors and Recognition
In recognition of his scholarly contributions, Barthelemy was elected as a member of the Academie des Inscriptions et Belles-lettres in 1897.
