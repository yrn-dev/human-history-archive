# The Earls of Ashburnham

The earldom of Ashburnham is an English title with a lineage tracing back to the 17th century. The family's ascent through the peerage began with William, the eldest son of a man who had eight children. William's son, John (1656–1710), was created Baron Ashburnham in 1689.

## Creation of the Earldom
The title of earl was established in 1730. John (1687–1737), the second son of the first Baron Ashburnham, succeeded his brother in 1710 to become the 3rd Baron Ashburnham. In 1730, he was further elevated in rank, being created both Viscount St Asaph and earl of Ashburnham. The lineage continued through his direct descendants, including the 5th earl, who was born in 1840.

## The 4th Earl and the Ashburnham Library
Bertram (1797–1878), the 4th earl, is noted for his role as a collector. He assembled the famous Ashburnham library, a collection that was later dispersed in 1883 and 1884.
