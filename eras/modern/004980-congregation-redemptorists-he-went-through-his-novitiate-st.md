# Congregation of the Redemptorists

The Congregation of the Redemptorists, also referred to as the Congregation of the Most Holy Redeemer, is a Roman Catholic religious organization that maintains a presence in both Europe and the United States.

## Educational Institutions and Training
The Congregation operates theological seminaries to train its members. In the United States, the Redemptorist Fathers are in charge of a theological seminary located at Mount St Clement's College in De Soto, Missouri. 

The process of entering the order involves a period of training known as a novitiate. For example, Thomas Edward Bridgett, who joined the Congregation after becoming a Roman Catholic in 1850, completed his novitiate at St Trond in Belgium. Following this initial stage, members may undergo extended theological study; Bridgett completed a five-year course of study at Wittem in Holland before being ordained as a priest.

## Missionary Work and Ministry
Members of the Congregation, such as Father Thomas Edward Bridgett, have engaged in extensive missionary activities. After returning to England in 1856, Bridgett spent over forty years as a missioner in Ireland and England. His work included preaching in more than 140 retreats for nuns and clergy, as well as over 80 missions. During his time in Limerick, he established a religious confraternity for laymen that grew to include 5,000 members.

## Literary Contributions
Some members of the Congregation have produced scholarly works focusing on religious and ecclesiastical history. Thomas Edward Bridgett authored several volumes concerning the history of the Reformation in England, including:
* *The Life of Blessed John Fisher, Bishop of Rochester* (1888)
* *The Life and Writings of Sir Thomas More* (1890)
* *History of the Eucharist in Great Britain* (published in two volumes in 1881)
* *Our Lady's Dowry* (1875, with a third edition in 1890)
