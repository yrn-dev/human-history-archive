# Geographical Magazine and N. Ekholm

## The Geographical Magazine
The *Geographical Magazine* was a publication conducted by Sir Clements Markham from 1872 until 1878. Following this period, the magazine was merged into the *Proceedings of the Royal Geographical Society*. 

During its operation, the publication featured various geographical studies and reports. For example, in 1874, the magazine published "Telegraph and Travel" by Sir Frederic Goldsmid in April and October. Additionally, in May 1874, it published "Geographical Notes on the Basins of the Oxus and the Zarafshán," a work by the late Alexis Fedchenko.
