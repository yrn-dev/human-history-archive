# Social Clubs and Institutions of Buffalo

## Social Clubs of Buffalo
Within the city of Buffalo, several social clubs are specifically noted for their prominence. These include the University club, the Park club, the Saturn club, and the Country clubs, as well as the Buffalo club.

## Principal Buildings and Architecture
Buffalo is recognized for the beauty of its residential areas, where houses are generally detached, surrounded by attractive lawns, and set well back from the street. The city features several significant structures:

*   **Government and Civic Buildings:** The Federal building was constructed at a cost of $2,000,000. The city and county hall cost $1,500,000 and features a clock tower reaching 245 feet in height. Other notable civic structures include the city convention hall, the chamber of commerce, and the builders' exchange.
*   **Commercial and Professional Buildings:** The city's skyline includes the Masonic temple, the Prudential, Fidelity Trust, White, and Mutual Life buildings. The Ellicott Square building is distinguished as being one of the largest office structures in the world.
*   **Cultural and Public Sites:** Delaware Park houses the Albright art gallery and the Buffalo Historical Society building. The latter served as the New York state building for the Pan-American Exposition held in 1901.
*   **Entertainment:** The city's theatres include the Teck, Star, and Shea's Park.

## Religious Institutions
Buffalo contains many handsome churches. These include the St Paul's (Protestant Episcopal) and St Joseph's (Roman Catholic) cathedrals. Other prominent churches are Trinity (Protestant Episcopal), the First Presbyterian, the Westminster Presbyterian, and the Delaware Avenue Baptist.

## Hospitality
Among the hotels in Buffalo that may be especially mentioned are the Niagara, Lafayette, Iroquois, and Genesee.
