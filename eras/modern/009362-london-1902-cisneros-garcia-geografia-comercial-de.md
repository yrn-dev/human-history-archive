## Theological and Spiritual Texts
The archive contains references to the works of Garcias de Cisneros, a former abbot who died in 1510. His Latin work, *Exercitatorio de la vida spiritual* (1500), is described as being divided into three ways or periods focused on enlightenment, purity of soul, and union, with a fourth part dedicated to contemplation. This text followed the monastic tradition of the soul's direct intercourse with God.

The archive notes that this work served as the root idea for the more famous *Spiritual Exercises* by Ignatius. Ignatius encountered a popular translation of Cisneros's work while visiting the Benedictine abbey of Montserrato around March 1522. While Ignatius adopted the title and the division by periods from Cisneros, he introduced a different method involving a director to guide the exercitant. A separate work, *A Book of Spiritual Exercises, written by Garcias de Cisneros*, was published in London in 1876.

## Commercial Geography of South America
The archive lists *Geografia Comercial de la America del Sur* as an authority on the region. This work was authored by C.B. Cisneros and R.E. Garcia and was published in Lima in 1898.
