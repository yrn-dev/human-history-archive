# Lake Shore and "The Front"

## Geography of Buffalo
The city of Buffalo is situated on a broad, undulating tract of land that rises gradually from the lake to elevations ranging between 50 and 80 feet. The city's average altitude is somewhat less than 600 feet above sea level. Due to its high land, temperate climate, and effective water-supply and drainage systems, Buffalo is considered one of the most healthy cities in the United States. In 1900, the city recorded a death rate of 14.8 per thousand, which rose to 15.58 by 1907.

## Municipal Parks and "The Front"
Buffalo features a municipal park system of unusual beauty, consisting of a chain of parks totaling approximately 1,030 acres. These parks encircle the city and are linked by driveways and boulevards. Among these are Humboldt Park, Cazenovia Park, and South Park on the Lake Shore. 

One notable area is "The Front," which is situated on a bluff that overlooks the source of the Niagara river. This location houses Fort Porter, named in honor of Peter B. Porter, where the United States government maintains a garrison. 

Other significant green spaces include Delaware Park, the largest in the system at about 365 acres. This park, located in the north part of the city, includes a lake covering 46½ acres. The northern section of Delaware Park was used for the grounds of the Pan-American Exposition of 1901. Adjoining this park is the Forest Lawn cemetery, which contains monuments to President Millard Fillmore and the Seneca chief Red Jacket (1751-1830). Red Jacket, regarded by many as the greatest orator of his race, was a friend of the whites who warned Americans of danger after being approached by the Prophet and Tecumseh.

## City Planning and Layout
The original plan for Buffalo was platted by Joseph Ellicott and bore some resemblance to the plan of Washington, although the layout was later significantly altered and the original plan was not strictly followed. The modern city is characterized by many small squares and parks, as well as broad, spacious streets that are mostly lined with trees.
