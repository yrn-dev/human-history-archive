# Johns Hopkins Hospital

## Overview and Facilities
Johns Hopkins Hospital is one of the numerous charitable institutions located in Baltimore. It is recognized for the excellence of its equipment, with particular note made of its systems for ventilating and heating. The buildings of the hospital are cited as being among the notable structures within the city.

## Context within Baltimore
The hospital exists within a broad network of charitable and medical institutions in Baltimore. Other medical facilities in the city include Saint Joseph's general hospital, the hospital for the women of Maryland of Baltimore city, the nursery and child's hospital, and the Baltimore eye, ear and throat charity hospital. The city also hosts specialized care centers such as the Maryland hospital for the insane, the Sheppard asylum (focused on the cure of the insane), the Sheppard and Enoch Pratt hospital, and the Thomas Wilson sanatorium for children under three years of age suffering from disease during summer months.

Additionally, the city provides various other social services, including the Charity Organization Society, the Baltimore Association for the Improvement of the Condition of the Poor, the Bay View asylum (city poorhouse), the Children's Aid Society, the Woman's Industrial Exchange, and the Thomas Wilson Fuel-Saving Society.
