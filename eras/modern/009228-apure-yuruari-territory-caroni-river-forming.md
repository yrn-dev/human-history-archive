# Bolivar

## Geography and Boundaries
Bolivar is an inland state located in Venezuela. Its geographical position is south of the Orinoco and Apure. The state's boundaries are defined by the Caroni river and the Yuruari territory to the east, while the Amazonas territory and Brazil form its southern border. 

The state is characterized by a landscape of great forests, numerous rivers, and extensive plains known as *llanos*. These plains are covered with nutritious grass during the rainy season, though this vegetation disappears completely during the dry season. Bolivar comprises large areas of uninhabited territory.

## Administration and Population
The capital of the state is Ciudad Bolivar, which was formerly known as Angostura. The city is situated on the right bank of the Orinoco, approximately 240 meters above the river's mouth, and had a population of 11,686. 

The total population of the state was recorded as 135,232 in 1894. However, the size and outlines of the state have undergone various modifications due to frequent political changes in Venezuela, and its overall area has been largely reduced since 1894.

## Economy and Trade
Trade in Bolivar is facilitated by the Orinoco river, as vessels of light draught are able to ascend the river easily to reach the capital. A considerable amount of trade is conducted in the region. The primary exports from the state include:
* Cocoa
* Sugar
* Cotton
* Hides
* Jerked beef
* Various forest products
