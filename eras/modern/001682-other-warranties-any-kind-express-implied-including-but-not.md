# OTHER WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED

## Overview of Warranty Status
According to the provided archival records, electronic works are provided on an "AS-IS" basis. This designation means that there are no other warranties of any kind, whether they are express or implied. This exclusion of warranties specifically includes, but is not limited to, warranties of fitness for any purpose or warranties of merchantability.

## Limited Right of Replacement or Refund
While general warranties are excluded, there is a limited right regarding refunds or replacements if a defect is discovered in an electronic work. This right is applicable if the defect is found within 90 days of receiving the work. To initiate this process, the recipient must send a written explanation to the person from whom the work was received.

## Procedures for Physical and Electronic Media
The process for seeking a remedy depends on the medium through which the work was delivered:

*   **Physical Medium:** If the work was received on a physical medium, the recipient is required to return that medium along with their written explanation.
*   **Electronic Delivery:** If the work was received electronically, the entity or person providing the work may choose to offer a second opportunity to receive the work electronically instead of providing a refund.

## Resolution and Finality
The person or entity that provided the defective work has the option to provide a replacement copy in lieu of a refund. In cases of electronic delivery, if a second copy is provided and that copy is also found to be defective, the recipient may demand a refund in writing. At that stage, the refund may be demanded without further opportunities for the provider to fix the problem.
