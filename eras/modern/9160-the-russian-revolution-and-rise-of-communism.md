# The Russian Revolution and the Rise of Communism

## Overview
The Russian Revolution of 1917 was not a single event, but a series of upheavals that dismantled the Tsarist autocracy and led to the creation of the Soviet Union, the world's first socialist state. The revolution was precipitated by decades of social inequality, the incompetence of Tsar Nicholas II, and the catastrophic toll of World War I, which pushed the Russian peasantry and urban proletariat to a breaking point.

The process occurred in two primary stages. The **February Revolution** saw the collapse of the monarchy and the establishment of a Provisional Government. However, this government failed to exit the war or implement land reform, creating a power vacuum. The **October Revolution**, led by Vladimir Lenin and the Bolsheviks, seized power under the slogan "Peace, Land, and Bread." This transition triggered a brutal Civil War (1917–1923) between the "Reds" (Bolsheviks) and the "Whites" (a loose coalition of monarchists, capitalists, and social democrats).

Historians continue to debate the nature of the October Revolution. Some, following the traditional Soviet narrative, view it as a popular mass uprising of the working class. Others, such as Richard Pipes, argue it was a disciplined *coup d'état* carried out by a small minority who manipulated the masses.

## Key Facts
*   **The Vanguard Party:** Lenin adapted Marxism into "Marxism-Leninism," arguing that a professional revolutionary party (the vanguard) was necessary to lead the proletariat, as workers alone would only develop "trade union consciousness."
*   **The Red Terror:** To consolidate power during the Civil War, the Bolsheviks established the Cheka (secret police) to eliminate "class enemies" and political dissidents.
*   **The NEP:** Following the economic collapse of War Communism, Lenin introduced the New Economic Policy (NEP) in 1921, which allowed for a limited return to private enterprise to stabilize the economy.

## Did You Know?
1. **The Calendar Gap:** The "October Revolution" actually took place in November according to the Gregorian calendar; Russia was still using the Julian calendar at the time.
2. **The Romanov Fate:** Tsar Nicholas II and his entire immediate family were executed in a basement in Yekaterinburg in July 1918 to prevent them from being rescued by the White Army.
3. **Global Influence:** The revolution led to the creation of the Comintern (Communist International) in 1919, an organization dedicated to exporting the Bolshevik model of revolution worldwide.
