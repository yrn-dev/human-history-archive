# Histoire des oeuvres

The term *Histoire des oeuvres* appears in the archive's records primarily in relation to the bibliographic documentation of the author Balzac, as well as in various biographical and historical titles concerning other figures and subjects.

## The Bibliography of Balzac

In the context of Balzac's literary output, the *Histoire des oeuvres* (published in 1875 and later) serves as a comprehensive guide to his extraordinarily complicated bibliography. This work was attached by M. Spoelberch de Lovenjoul to the *Edition definitive* of Balzac's works. Lovenjoul further supplemented this bibliographic record through several smaller publications, including *Une Page perdue de Balzac* and *Autour de Balzac*.

The *Edition definitive*, which began publication from 1869 onwards, included the *Histoire des oeuvres* and provided the miscellaneous works of the author in four volumes, along with an invaluable volume of correspondence. This edition differed from earlier versions; for instance, the earliest *Comedie humaine* was issued between 1842 and 1846 in sixteen volumes. Additionally, there was a widely known edition consisting of fifty-five small, closely printed 24mos published by Michel (Calmann) Levy. While the Levy edition included the *Oeuvres de jeunesse*, it did not follow the author's last disposition and omitted the miscellaneous works.

In 1893, the bibliographic record was expanded with the addition of another volume titled *Repertoire des oeuvres de Balzac*. Authored by MM. Christophe and Cerfbeer, this volume tracked the history of the various personages of the *Comedie* throughout the works, organizing them under separate articles.

## Other Works of History and Biography

The archive contains several other titles utilizing the phrase "histoire de" or "oeuvres" to document the lives and legacies of various historical, religious, and artistic figures:

### Religious and Clerical Figures
The life and works of Mgr. Darboy are documented in J. A. Foulon's *Histoire de la vie et des oeuvres de Mgr. Darboy* (1889). Similarly, the life and works of the Abbé Gregoire are detailed in L. Maggiolo's *La Vie et les oeuvres de l'abbe Gregoire* (1884). Gregoire himself authored several historical texts, including *Histoire des sectes religieuses, depuis le commencement du siecle dernier jusqu'a l'epoque actuelle* (1810), *Histoire des confesseurs des empereurs, des rois, et d'autres princes* (1824), and *Histoire du mariage des pretres en France* (1826).

### Art and Archaeology
In the field of art, the works of Corot are cataloged in Alfred Robaut's *L'OEuvre de Corot, catalogue raisonne et illustre*, which is preceded by the *histoire de Corot et de ses oeuvres* written by Etienne Morceau-Nelaton (1905). In archaeology, the diverse works of Auguste Mariette are referenced in a biographical notice by Maspero within *Auguste Mariette. Oeuvres diverses* (tome 1, 1904).

### General History and Textual Studies
The archive also lists *Histoire de la Vulgate* (1893) by S. Berger, which provides the history and bibliography of the Vulgate text. Other historical works include P.A.N.B. Daru's *Histoire de la republique de Venise* (1853) and A. Baschet's *Histoire de la chancellerie secrete a Venise* (1870).
