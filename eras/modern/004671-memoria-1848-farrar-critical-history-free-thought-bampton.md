## Critical History of Free Thought
A.G. Farrar authored the *Critical History of Free Thought*, which was presented as part of the Bampton Lectures in 1862. This work is cited in the archive's records concerning K.G. Bretschneider.

The Bampton Lectures are also associated with other scholars and legacies. T.K. Cheyne published his Bampton Lectures, titled *Origin of the Psalter*, after 1890; this work is noted for its vast learning and its lack of restraint regarding the freedom of judgment. Additionally, the late missionary Duff used his personal property to establish a lectureship on foreign missions that was modeled after the Bampton Lectures.
