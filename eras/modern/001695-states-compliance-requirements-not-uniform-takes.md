# Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation

## Mission and Objectives
Project Gutenberg™ is an organization dedicated to increasing the availability of licensed works and those in the public domain. Its primary mission is to ensure these works can be freely distributed in a machine-readable format. A key goal of this initiative is to maintain accessibility across the widest possible array of equipment, which specifically includes outdated equipment.

## Organizational Support and Funding
The survival and operation of Project Gutenberg™ are dependent upon widespread donations and public support. The organization notes that small donations, ranging from $1 to $5,000, are particularly significant for the purpose of maintaining its tax-exempt status with the IRS.

## Legal Compliance and State Regulations
The Foundation maintains a commitment to comply with the laws governing charitable donations and charities across all 50 states of the United States. However, the Foundation notes that compliance requirements are not uniform across these states. Meeting and maintaining these requirements necessitates a considerable effort, involving many fees and a significant amount of paperwork.

Due to these varied regulations, the Foundation does not solicit donations in any location where it has not first received written confirmation of compliance.
