# Bangalore

## Geography and Elevation
Bangalore is a city in India and serves as the capital of the native state of Mysore. The city is situated at an elevation of 3,113 feet above sea level. In terms of regional connectivity, it is located 219 miles west of Madras by rail.

## The District of Bangalore
The district of Bangalore borders the Madras district of Salem. Its total area is 3,079 square miles, with the main portion of the district consisting of the valley of the Arkavati river, which joins the Cauvery on the southern frontier. The district is traversed by several railway lines. In 1901, the population of the district was 789,664, which represented a 15% increase over the previous decade. Outside of the city itself, the district hosts a woollen mill that produces woollen stuffs, cloth for greatcoats, and blankets.

## Military Significance
Bangalore is the largest British cantonment in southern India. The city features a fort, the foundation of which was laid in probably the 16th century by a descendant of Kempe-Goude, a husbandman and warrior who had settled north of Bangalore to avoid the tyranny of the local *wadeyar*. 

The fort has a varied history of ownership and strategic use:
* It was initially wrested from Kempe-Goude's family by Bijapur.
* It later became one of the *jagirs* of Shahji, the father of Sivaji.
* In 1687, Venkaji, a son of Shahji, attempted to sell the city to Chikka Deva, the raja of Mysore, for three lakhs of rupees. However, Kasim Khan, the commander of Aurangzeb's forces, entered the city almost without resistance. Kasim Khan subsequently delivered the city to Chikka Deva upon payment of the stipulated three lakhs.
* In 1758, the powerful minister Nanjiraj granted Bangalore as a *jagir* (fief) to Hyder Ali. Hyder Ali strengthened and enlarged the fort, using it as a refuge in 1760 following his expulsion from Seringapatam.
* The fort is also noted as the site of the first captivity of Sir David Baird after the defeat at Perambakam in 1780.

## City Demographics and Surroundings
As of 1901, the population of Bangalore city was 69,447. The city is located 43 miles west of the town and district of Kolar, which is also in the state of Mysore.
