# Documents Relating to the Colonial History of New York

## Overview and Origins
*Documents relating to the Colonial History of New York* is a 15-volume collection of historical transcriptions published by the state between 1853 and 1883. The project originated from the research of John Romeyn Brodhead, an American historical scholar born in Philadelphia in 1814. After being admitted to the bar in New York City in 1835, Brodhead focused his efforts on the study of American colonial history after 1837.

To gain access to records concerning early Dutch settlements in America, Brodhead obtained an appointment in 1839 as an attaché of the American legation at the Hague. His investigations revealed that Dutch archives contained extensive material regarding the early history of New York. This discovery prompted the state legislature to allocate funds for the systematic gathering of document transcripts from various European archives.

## Collection and Compilation
In 1841, Governor William H. Seward appointed Brodhead to lead the effort of gathering these records. Over several years, Brodhead collected approximately eighty manuscript volumes of transcriptions from Holland, France, and England. A significant portion of these documents had not been previously utilized by historians.

The resulting 15-volume set was edited in two stages:
* Volumes I through XI were edited by Edward O'Callaghan.
* Volumes XII through XV were edited by Berthold Fernow.

## Related Scholarship
The gathering of these colonial documents complemented other scholarly work on the region. John Romeyn Brodhead also authored a two-volume *History of the State of New York*, published between 1853 and 1871. This work covers the period from 1609 to 1690 and is generally regarded as the best history for that specific timeframe.

## Biography of John Romeyn Brodhead
John Romeyn Brodhead was the son of Jacob Brodhead (1782-1855), a prominent clergyman within the Dutch Reformed Church. He graduated from Rutgers College in 1831. In addition to his historical research and the compilation of the state documents, Brodhead held several other positions:
* From 1846 to 1849, he served as the secretary of legation under George Bancroft, who was then the minister to Great Britain.
* From 1853 to 1857, he served as the naval officer of the port of New York.

Brodhead died in New York City on May 6, 1873.
