# Freretown

## Overview and Location
Freretown is a settlement located on the mainland of the East Africa protectorate. It is situated nearly opposite the town of Mombasa. The settlement was established for freed slaves and was named in honor of Sir Bartle Frere. Among the native population, the settlement is known as Kisaoni.

## Religious Significance
Freretown serves as the headquarters in East Africa for the Church Missionary Society. The settlement is the residence of the bishop of the diocese of Mombasa. Its infrastructure includes a mission house and a fine church.

## Regional Context
Freretown is located near Mombasa, which is the chief port of the protectorate and has a population of approximately 30,000. The harbor on the south-west side of Mombasa island, known as Kilindini, serves as the terminus for the Uganda railway. 

The broader protectorate is organized into several provinces and towns, including:
* **Seyyidie:** The south coast province with Mombasa as its capital.
* **Ukamba:** Occupying the center of the protectorate with Nairobi as its capital.
* **Kenya:** The district of Mt. Kenya with Fort Hall as its capital.
* **Tanaland:** Located north of Seyyidie and Ukamba, with Lamu as its capital.
* **Jubaland:** The northern region with Kismayu as its capital.
* **Naivasha and Kisumu:** Both capitals of their respective regions, these areas adjoin the Victoria Nyanza and were transferred to the East Africa protectorate on April 1, 1902.
