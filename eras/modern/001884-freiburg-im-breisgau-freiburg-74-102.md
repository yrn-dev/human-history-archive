# Freiburg-im-Breisgau

Freiburg-im-Breisgau is a city located in the region of Baden. As of 1905, it served as the chief town of its namesake district, with a recorded population of 74,102.

## Education and Religion
Freiburg-im-Breisgau is a center for higher education and religious administration. The city is home to a Roman Catholic university, which stands as one of the two universities in Baden (the other being the Protestant university at Heidelberg). Additionally, the city serves as the seat of the Roman Catholic archbishop of Freiburg, who holds the position of metropolitan of the Upper Rhine.

## Notable Residents
The city is the birthplace and early place of education for Adolf Furtwängler (1853–1907). Furtwängler was a prominent German archaeologist who later studied at Munich and Leipzig. He is recognized for his work in archaeological criticism and his excavations at Olympia in 1878 and at Aegina. His major publications include works on Greek vases, ancient gems, and a volume on the bronzes found at Olympia, as well as the work *Masterpieces of Greek Sculpture*.
