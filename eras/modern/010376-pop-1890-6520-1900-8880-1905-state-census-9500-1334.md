# Boone, Iowa

## Overview and Location
Boone is a city in the United States, located in the state of Iowa. It serves as the county-seat of Boone county and is situated near the center of the state, a short distance from the Des Moines river.

## Population
The population of Boone has shown steady growth over the late 19th and early 20th centuries. In 1890, the population was 6,520, increasing to 8,880 by 1900. A state census conducted in 1905 recorded a population of 9,500, which included 1,334 foreign-born residents. By 1910, the population had reached 10,347.

## Transportation
The city is served by several railway lines:
*   The Chicago & North-Western, which maintains repair and construction shops within the city.
*   The Chicago, Milwaukee & St Paul railways.
*   The Fort Dodge, Des Moines & Southern railway, an inter-urban line that provides connections to Ames and Des Moines.

## Economy and Industry
Boone is recognized as an important center for coal. The city's industrial base includes the manufacture of tiles and bricks, produced from clay sourced nearby. Additionally, the city operates a packing plant for the production of pork and beef products. 

The city is surrounded by a rich farming section that produces live-stock and large quantities of grain, some of which is milled within the city.

## History
Boone was laid out in 1865. It was incorporated as a town in 1866 and subsequently chartered as a city in 1868.
