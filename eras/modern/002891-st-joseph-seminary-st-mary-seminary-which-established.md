# St Joseph's Seminary and St Mary's Seminary

## Overview and Establishment
St Joseph's Seminary and St Mary's Seminary are professional educational institutions located in Baltimore. They were established in 1791 by the Society of St Sulpice. These institutions are recognized as being, according to some accounts, the oldest Catholic theological seminaries in the United States.

## Institutional Context
The seminaries are part of a broader landscape of professional and higher education in their city. They exist alongside other specialized institutions, including the University of Maryland and Baltimore University—both of which provide courses in dentistry, medicine, and law. Other medical and professional schools in the vicinity include the Woman's Medical College, the Baltimore Medical College, the College of Physicians and Surgeons, the Baltimore College of Dental Surgery, and the Baltimore Law School. Additionally, the Maryland College of Pharmacy became part of the University of Maryland in 1904.

The city's educational infrastructure also includes a Polytechnic Institute and separate high schools for coloured and white pupils.

## Regional Libraries
The academic and professional environment surrounding the seminaries is supported by several principal libraries. These include the Maryland Historical Society, the Peabody Institute, the Bar Association, and Johns Hopkins University. Other notable libraries in the area are the New Mercantile, the Enoch Pratt, and the Maryland Diocesan library, the latter of which is Protestant Episcopal.
