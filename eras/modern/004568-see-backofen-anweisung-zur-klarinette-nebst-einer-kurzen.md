# J. G. H. Backofen and the Clarinet

## Overview of Anweisung zur Klarinette
J. G. H. Backofen authored the work *Anweisung zur Klarinette, nebst einer kurzen Abhandlung ueber das Basset-Horn*. Published in Leipzig by Breitkopf & Haertel in 1803, this text includes illustrations and provides instruction on the clarinet as well as a short treatise concerning the basset-horn.

## Related Clarinet Literature
Backofen's work is part of a broader body of technical and historical literature regarding the clarinet and its related instruments. Other significant instructional texts include:

*   **Iwan Mueller**: Published *Anweisung zu der neuen Clarinette und der Clarinette-alto, nebst einigen Bemerkungen fuer Instrumentenmacher* in Leipzig (Freidrich Hofmeister, 1826), which also featured illustrations.
*   **Gottfried Weber**: Contributed a piece titled "Ueber Clarinette und Bassethorn" in *Cacilia*, Band xi, pp. 35-37, published in Mainz in 1834.
*   **Wilhelm Altenburg**: Wrote *Die Clarinette, ihre Entstehung und Entwickelung bis zur Jetztzeit in akustischer, technischer u. musikalischer Beziehung* (Heilbronn, 1904).

## The Basset-Horn and Instrument Collections
The basset-horn is discussed in the works of Backofen and Weber. Visual records of early basset-horns are available in the *Descriptive Catalogue of the Musical Instruments at the Royal Military Exhibition, London* (1890), compiled by Capt. C. R. Day and published in 1891. 

Additionally, the collection of the Rev. F. W. Galpin of Hatfield, Broad Oak, contains an instrument of this type that bears the stamp "H. Grenser, S. Wiesner, Dresden."

## Evolution of Woodwind Instruments
Historical accounts of woodwind development suggest a process of transformation. J. J. Quantz, who served as the flute-master for Frederick the Great, credited France with the transformation of the schalmey into the oboe and the bombard (pommer) into the bassoon, as detailed in his 1752 work, *Versuch einer Anweisung die Flote traversiere zu spielen*.
