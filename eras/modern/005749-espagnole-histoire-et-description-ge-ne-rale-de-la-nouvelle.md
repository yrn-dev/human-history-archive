# Histoire de l'isle Espagnole & Histoire et description générale de la Nouvelle France

The *Histoire de l'isle Espagnole, &c.* and the *Histoire et description générale de la Nouvelle France* are historical works authored by Père Charlevoix. These texts contribute to the broader body of literature concerning the exploration and colonization of the Americas.

## History and General Description of New France
The work titled *Histoire et description générale de la Nouvelle France* (also referred to as *History and General Description of New France*) is described as a famous old work. A six-volume edition of this text was edited by J. G. Shea and published in New York in 1866. Despite its historical fame, the archive notes that the work is now considered negligible.
