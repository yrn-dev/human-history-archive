### Villeroi
Villeroi served as a general for Louis. In the spring of 1706, following a period where French generals were instructed to act on the defensive and entrench themselves behind earthworks, Villeroi attempted to meet allied forces in an open fight. This decision resulted in the total rout of the French at the battle of Ramillies on May 23, 1706, due to the superior tactics of Marlborough. This defeat led to the transfer of Flanders and nearly all of Brabant to the allies.

### Vendôme
Vendôme is described as one of the ablest French generals. His military judgment was evidenced during the conflict involving Marlborough's advance toward Oudenarde. Vendôme provided suggestions and counsels regarding the fight on the high ground above Oudenarde; however, these suggestions were disregarded by the duke of Burgundy, the grandson of Louis. Consequently, the battle ended in a French defeat on July 11, 1708.
