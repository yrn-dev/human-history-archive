# Barondo-Bakundu Group

The Barondo-Bakundu group is a linguistic classification of Bantu dialects located in the region of Cameroon. This group begins in the north at the Rio del Rey, which is situated on the extremity of the Bantu field near the estuary of the Cross river.

## Geographic Distribution and Dialects
The group encompasses several dialects, some of which are described as much-debased. These include:
* Barombi
* Bas[=a]
* Bonken
* Abo
* Nkosi

These dialects are spoken across a specific geographic range that includes the Cameroon river (Magombe), the eastern slopes of the Cameroon mountains, and extends from there to the Nyong and Sanaga rivers.

## Neighboring Linguistic Groups
The Barondo-Bakundu group is situated among various other Bantu and semi-Bantu linguistic classifications:

**Semi-Bantu Languages**
To the north-east and east of the Barondo-Bakundu group are languages described as "semi-Bantu." These include Mbudikum, Bafut, Bayon, Nki, Bati, and Mbe. These forms of speech show affinities with the Fan (a group occupying the interior of French Gabun and German Cameroon) as well as with speech forms found in the basin of the Central Benue river.

**Other Regional Groups**
* **The Duala Group:** This group is characterized as being of a "much purer Bantu type" and consists of languages spoken on the delta and estuary of the Cameroon river.
* **The Isubu-Bakwiri Group:** Located on the west slopes of the Cameroon Mountains and the coast-lands north of the Cameroon delta (Ambas Bay).
* **The Fan or Pangwe Group:** These forms of speech are described as so corrupt that they are only just recognizable as Bantu. They occupy the interior of French Gabun and German Cameroon, extending as far north and east as the Mbam, Sanaga, and Sanga rivers, as well as the immediate hinterland of the "Duala" Cameroon.
* **Fernando Po Dialects:** The Bantu dialects of Fernando Po, including Bani, Bateti, and Ediya, are noted as being distantly allied to other groups.
