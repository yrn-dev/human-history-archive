## Biblical Recensions and the Septuagint
In the study of the Septuagint, the number 118 is associated with the recension of Lucian, a priest of Antioch who was martyred in A.D. 311 or 312. Lucian's revision was an attempt to align the "common text" of the Septuagint with the critical principles accepted at Antioch. 

The identification of this recension was advanced by the scholar Ceriani, who discovered that codices 19, 82, 93, and 108 represented Lucian's work. This conclusion was independently reached by Lagarde, who combined codex 118 with the aforementioned four codices. The Lucian recension is noted for using synonyms in place of original Septuagint words and employing double renderings.
