# THE KINGDOM OF CASCAR

## Geography and Administration
Cascar is a region situated between the east and north-east. The territory of the kingdom extends for a distance of five days' journey. While it was constituted as a kingdom in former days, the archive records that it became subject to the Great Kaan. Some sources suggest that this subjection may be understood in the sense that all territories under Mongol rule owed homage to the Kaan.

The region is part of a larger area referred to as Great Turkey, which includes other provinces such as Pein and those extending as far as the city of Lop.

## Urban Centers and Economy
The kingdom contains a significant number of villages and towns, the most prominent and finest of which is the city of Cascar itself. The inhabitants of the region sustain themselves through handicrafts and trade. Many merchants from this country travel throughout the world on trading journeys.

The land is characterized by fine estates, as well as beautiful vineyards and gardens. Agriculture in the region is notable for the production of a great deal of cotton.

## Population and Culture
The people of Cascar possess a peculiar language. The dominant religion is the worship of Mahommet, although the country is also home to many Nestorian Christians who maintain their own churches.

Observations regarding the native population describe them as a wretched and niggardly set of people who eat and drink in a miserable fashion.
