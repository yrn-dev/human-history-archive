# West Africa

West Africa is a region of the African continent characterized by a diverse population and a variety of colonial administrative divisions. It is noted as an area that, along with East and Central Africa, is an exception to the temperate zones of the British Empire, making it less suitable for white settlement.

## Population and Demographics
The estimated population of West Africa within the British sphere is 18,863,000. This total is comprised of several regions:
*   **Nigeria (including Lagos):** 15,000,000 (black and brown)
*   **Gold Coast and hinterland:** 2,700,000 (chiefly black)
*   **Sierra Leone:** 1,000,000 (chiefly black)
*   **Gambia:** 163,000 (chiefly black)

The aboriginal nations of the region are mostly of the black negroid type. In Nigeria, a large proportion of the population is Arab and relatively light-skinned. In some areas of the west coast, lower negroid types are noted to still practice fetish rites and cannibalism.

## French West Africa
France maintains several colonies and territories in the region. Cartographic efforts for these areas have been managed by the *service géographique des colonies* and the *service géographique de l'Afrique occidental*. Official maps compiled by A. Meunier between 1902 and 1909 include French West Africa, French Guinea, and the Ivory Coast and Dahomey. Additionally, a map of Senegal has been in progress since 1905.

### The Ivory Coast and Kong
The Ivory Coast colony includes the "circle of Kong," an administrative division covering 46,000 square miles with a population of approximately 400,000. The inhabitants are negroes, primarily Mandingo and Bambara. Regarding religious beliefs, about one-fourth of the population profess Mahommedanism, while the remainder are spirit worshippers.

The geography of the region features the Kong range of hills in the northwest, which separates the coast regions from the inner plains of West Africa. The highest point in this system is the Pic des Kommono, reaching 4,757 feet. Historically, the town of Kong was visited by René Caillié in 1827. In 1888, Captain L. G. Binger led native chiefs to accept French protection, and the protectorate was attached to the Ivory Coast colony in 1893. The district was briefly overrun by the armies of Samory, but following the capture of that chief in 1898, France developed the district peacefully.

## British Possessions
In addition to the colonies of Nigeria, Gambia, and Sierra Leone, the British possess Ashanti. Ashanti is an irregular oblong territory with an area of 23,000 square miles and an estimated population of 500,000 as of 1907. It is bounded to the west by the French Ivory Coast colony and to the east by the river Volta, which serves as the border with the German colony of Togoland. Its northern boundary is the British Protectorate of the Northern Territories of the Gold Coast, and its southern frontier is the northern frontier of the Gold Coast colony. The region also includes a triangular projection southward known as the country of the Adansi.

## Regional Geopolitics
The territories of West Africa, including Senegambia, Sierra Leone, the Gold Coast, Dahomey, and the well-populated Nigeria with the port of Lagos, have been identified as areas of significant strategic and economic value. These regions are described as being rich in men for labor and war, as well as rich in trade possibilities and natural treasures.
