# Urmia Lake and the Western Districts of Azerbaijan

Urmia Lake is located in the north-western province of Persia known as Azerbaijan (also referred to as Azerb[=a]ijan, the *Azerb[=a]deg[=a]n* of medieval writers, or the *Athropatakan* and *Atropatene* of the ancients). The lake serves as a drainage point for several rivers, including the Aji, Murdi, Tatava, and Jaghatu.

## Geography and Administration of Azerbaijan
Azerbaijan is described as the most important province in the north-west of Persia, with an estimated area of 32,000 sq. m. and a population ranging between 1.5 and 2 million people. This population is composed of various races, including Armenians, Syrians, Kurds, Turks, and Persians proper. 

The province is bordered by the river Aras (Araxes) to the north, which separates it from Russian territory. To the east lie the Caspian Sea, Khamseh (Zenj[=a]n), and Gilan; to the south is Kurdistan; and to the west is Asiatic Turkey. The terrain consists of undulating eminences and extensive plains. Notable geographical features include the Talish Mountains (9,000 ft.) running parallel to the Caspian, the Sahand mountains in the center (12,000 ft.), and Mount Savelan in the east, which is the province's highest peak at 15,792 ft.

## The Western Districts
The region to the west of Urmia Lake, which includes the districts of Urmia and Selmas, is noted as the most prosperous part of the province of Azerbaijan. 

Further toward the extreme north-west frontier, situated between the river Aras and Urmia Lake, is the district and town of Khoi. This district is characterized by an elevated plateau measuring 60 m. by 10 to 15 m. The area is highly cultivated through a skillful system of irrigation and drainage, resulting in fertile fields and meadows. Primary agricultural products of this district include barley, wheat, rice, cotton, and various fruits. In the northern section of this district, bordering Maku, lies the plain of Chaldaran (Kalderan). This site was the location of a great victory for the Turks under Sultan Selim I over the Persians under Shah Ismail in August 1514.

## Economy and Natural Resources
Azerbaijan is one of the most productive provinces of Persia and is superior in fertility to most others. The province is well-known for its gardens and orchards that produce a wide variety of delicious fruits; these are exported in large quantities, primarily to Russia. While provisions are abundant and cheap, the region lacks timber trees and forests.

The province possesses several mineral resources, including:
*   **Metals and Minerals:** Copper, lead, sulphur, and orpiment.
*   **Fuel:** Lignite.
*   **Stone:** A translucent, variegated, and beautiful marble known as Tabriz or Maragha marble. This stone can be polished to a high shine and is utilized in the construction of baths, tanks, and palatial buildings.

## Regional Demographics
The area around Urmia Lake is also a point of reference for the distribution of the Kurd population. Kurds are scattered from Lake Urmia in the east to the river Sakaria in the west, and from Jebel Sinjar in the south to Kars in the north. The total Kurd population is estimated to exceed 2.5 million, divided among Persian (800,000), Turkish (1,650,000), and Russian (50,000) regions, though these statistics are noted as not being entirely trustworthy.
