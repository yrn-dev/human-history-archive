# Manyema Group and Related Dialects

The Manyema (also known as Baenya) group consists of a collection of Bantu dialects located in Central Africa. This group primarily encompasses the corrupt Bantu dialects situated between the main stream of the Luapula-Congo and the western watershed of Tanganyika.

## Geographic Distribution and Boundaries
The Manyema genus extends northward and includes the languages spoken within the Aruwimi basin. The group is geographically bounded on the west by the languages of the Lomami valley, which are categorized under groups 15 and 16. To the east, the Manyema genus merges into the Bantu dialects of groups 1 and 12, which are described as being much purer.

## Included Languages and Dialects
The Manyema group comprises several languages, including:
* Lokele
* Kusu
* Tu-rumbu
* Soko
* Yalulema

Additionally, the Lihuku-Kuamba section (No. 2) has been shown to be connected with the Manyema group. The Kibira dialects, found in the Ituri district of the north-eastern Congo Forest, may also be placed within this section.

## Neighboring Linguistic Groups
The Manyema group is situated among several other distinct linguistic assemblages in the region:

**Tanganyika Languages:** To the east and north-east are the Tanganyika languages (such as Kabwari, Kiguha, and Ki-rega). These are spoken from the Lukuga outlet of Tanganyika in the south to the vicinity of Lake Albert Edward in the north. On their western boundary, these languages are bordered by the Congo Forest and the Manyema genus.

**Rua-Luba-Lunda-Marungu Group:** This group, which includes Ki-tabwa, Lulua, and Kanyoka, occupies a significant portion of the south central Congo basin. It is located between the main streams of the Kwango and Kasai on the west and the south-west coast-line of Tanganyika on the east.

**Bakuba Assemblage:** This group of Central Congo dialects includes Babuma, Shilange, and Songe. It likely encompasses all Bantu languages between the Kwa-Kasai and Upper Kwilu on the west and the Lomami river on the east.

**Balolo Group:** This group consists of the languages of the Northern Congo bend. It may also include corrupt dialects of the Kwango, Kwilu, and Northern Kasai, such as Bakutu, Bambala, Bahuana, and Babuma, provided they are not more closely allied to the Bakuba or Teke groups.
