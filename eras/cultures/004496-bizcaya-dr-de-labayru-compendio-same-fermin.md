# Historia de Bizcaya and Compendio

## Overview of the Works
The *Historia de Bizcaya* is a historical work authored by Dr. E. de Labayru. A related work, the *Compendio* of the *Historia de Bizcaya*, was produced by Fermin Herran and published in Bilbao in 1903.

## Context within Basque Historiography
These texts exist among a broader body of literature concerning the Basque Provinces. Other significant historical and descriptive works of the region include:
*   **Legislative and Legal Records:** Research into the *fueros* (local laws) has been conducted by Cayetano Manrique and A. Marichalar, Marques de Montesa, in *Historia de la Legislacion ... civil de Espana*, as well as in *Fueros de Navarra Vizcaya, Guipuzcoa y Alava* (second edition, 1868). Additionally, D. Pablo de Gorosabel authored *Noticia de las cosas memorables de Guipuzcoa* (1899-1901), with the final volume by C. de Echegaray providing legislative acts up to May 1900.
*   **Regional History and Geography:** D. Carmelo de Echegaray wrote *Las Provincias Vascongadas a Fines de la Edad Media* (1895). Earlier works include D. Guillermo Bowles's *Introduccion a la Historia Natural, y a la Geografia Fisica de Espana* (1775), which contains chapters on the Basque Provinces, and the *Diccionario Geografico-Historico de Espana* (1802), which covers the Senorio de Vizcaya, the Reyno de Navarra, and the provinces of Alava and Guipuzcoa.
*   **Customs and Citizenship:** The *Corografia de Guipuzcoa* by R. P. M. de Larramendi, S.J., written around 1750, is noted as an indispensable source for the manners and customs of the people. Furthermore, *El Guipuzcoano instruido* (1780) utilizes a dictionary format to detail the obligations, duties, rights, and life of a Basque citizen of that era.

## Archival Research and Industrial Records
The writing of true history for the region has been supported by the efforts of D. Carmelo de Echegaray, the Cronista de las Provincias Vascongadas, and his colleagues F. Soraluce and D. Serapio Mugica. This group of historians examined, indexed, and catalogued the municipal archives of all towns, a process that led to the discovery of important manuscripts and missing documents.

In addition to general history, specific industrial development in the region is documented in *Las Minas de hierro de la provincia de Vizcaya, progressos realizados en esta region derde 1870 hasta 1899*, which describes the growth of the Basque mining industry.
