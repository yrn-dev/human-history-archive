## Marine Currents and the Baltic Stream
The circulation of water in the channels that connect the North Sea with the Baltic proper is described as being of a complex character. To understand this system, a distinction must be made between inflowing and outflowing waters, which is achieved by measuring salt content: outflowing water contains less than 30 parts *pro mille* of salt, while inflowing water contains more than 32 *pro mille*.

Because the Baltic receives more water through river discharge and rainfall than it loses via evaporation, a surplus is created. This results in an outflowing current known as the "Baltic Stream." This stream is governed by three general laws:
1. It is a surface current because it originates from redundant fresh water.
2. It remains close to the coast of the Scandinavian peninsula due to the rotation of the earth.
3. It is periodic, as river discharge varies by season. While the water is abundant enough to inundate the Skagerrak and Kattegat during summer and spring, the sources are largely dried up in winter due to the freezing of land water.

## Mathematical Complex Numbers
In mathematics, a complex number is represented as $\zeta = \xi + i\eta$. For such a number to have a limit, it is necessary and sufficient that both $\xi$ and $\eta$ have limits. This principle extends to infinite series of complex numbers; a series is convergent if the real series formed by the real parts and the real series formed by the imaginary parts are both convergent. If the real series formed by the moduli of the terms is convergent, the series is termed "absolutely convergent," meaning its sum remains unaltered regardless of the order of the terms.

Furthermore, the function $\exp(z)$ allows for the definition of generalized sine and cosine functions when $z$ is complex. These functions follow ordinary relations used when $z$ is real, with the exception that their moduli are not inferior to unity.

## Linguistic and Social Complexity in China
The Chinese system of sign-writing is characterized as a peculiar and complex system. It requires the learner to habituate the mind to a very great number of characters. This complexity is contrasted with the simpler and swifter alphabets of western civilizations.

This linguistic system led to the creation of the mandarins, a special reading-class that served as the official and ruling class. Because this class concentrated on classical forms and words rather than realities and ideas, it is suggested that the social and economic development of China was greatly hampered. The archive notes that this complexity of writing and speech may be the primary cause of China becoming a "vast pool of backward people" rather than a foremost global power.

## Organizational and Professional Complexity
Complexity also manifests in administrative and professional frameworks:

*   **Marine Forces:** The marine system is designed to be less complex in its pay and accounts than the army. Its administrative staff is simpler and less expensive than a numerical army equivalent. Marines are enlisted for 12 years, with the option to re-engage for a total of 21 years to earn a pension.
*   **Legal Representation:** Under FRPC 4-1.1, competent legal representation requires knowledge and skill based on the "relative complexity and specialized nature of the matter." Major litigation and complex transactions generally require more extensive treatment and preparation than matters of lesser consequence.
*   **Naval Intelligence:** During the conflict involving the German Navy, the British Admiralty undertook the study of German wireless messages. This work was of "great complexity" because the cypher was only one element used to preserve secrecy. Despite the Germans repeatedly changing keys and codes to baffle interceptors, the British were occasionally able to translate routine messages.

## Theoretical Astronomy
In theoretical astronomy, the determination of orbits is a primary problem used to predict the motions of stars, satellites, or planets. While some computations are simple, others are extremely complex. For instance, while a new comet's orbit can be computed in a single day from three observations, obtaining definitive results requires a complicated process of computing perturbations produced by major planets.
