# Bael Fruit

It belongs to the genus *Aegle*, which is part of the botanical natural order Rutaceae. This genus consists of three species in total: two located in tropical Asia and one in west tropical Africa.

## Botanical Characteristics
The bael- or bel-fruit tree is characterized by the presence of strong spines. Its leaves are compound and alternate, with each leaf consisting of three leaflets. The tree produces panicles of white flowers that are described as sweet-scented. The wood of the tree is hard, though it is not durable, and is yellowish-white in color.

The fruit of the tree is oblong to pyriform in shape, measuring between 2 and 5 inches in diameter. It possesses a rind that is either yellow or grey, protecting a thick, orange-coloured pulp that is sweet.

The naming of the plant is derived from two different linguistic sources. The genus name *Aegle* refers to one of the Hesperides, a reference to the golden nature of the fruit. The species name *marmelos* is the Portuguese word for quince.
