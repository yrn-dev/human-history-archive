# Chhandogya-upanishad (St Petersburg, 1889)

The *Chhandogya-upanishad* (St Petersburg, 1889) is a scholarly publication consisting of a translation and a critical examination of the ancient text. This specific edition was produced by the German Sanskrit scholar Otto von Bohtlingk.

## Authorship and Scholarship
The work was authored by Otto von Bohtlingk (1815–1904), a distinguished 19th-century scholar whose contributions are considered of pre-eminent value to the fields of comparative philology and Indian studies. Bohtlingk's academic foundation in Oriental languages, including Sanskrit, Arabic, and Persian, began at the university of St Petersburg between 1833 and 1835, followed by further studies in Berlin and Bonn.

Upon returning to St Petersburg in 1842, Bohtlingk became attached to the Royal Academy of Sciences, eventually being elected as an ordinary member of the society in 1855. His career was marked by high official standing, having been named a "Russian state councillor" in 1860 and later a "privy councillor" with a title of nobility.

## Context within Bohtlingk's Works
The 1889 critical examination and translation of the *Chhandogya-upanishad* was published in St Petersburg alongside Bohtlingk's translation of the *Brihadaranyaka-upanishad*. These works were part of a broader body of philological research that included:

*   **Grammatical Studies:** An edition of Panini's *Acht Bucher grammatischer Regeln* (1839-1840), later republished as *Paninis Grammatik mit Ubersetzung* in 1887, and *Vopadevas Grammatik* (1847).
*   **Linguistic Research:** Works on the language of the Jakutes (*Uber die Sprache der Jakuten*, 1851) and a treatise on Sanskrit accents (*Uber den Accent im Sanskrit*, 1843).
*   **Collections:** *Indische Spruche*, published in three parts between 1870 and 1873.

Bohtlingk's most significant achievement, or *magnum opus*, was the *Sanskrit-Worterbuch*. This seven-volume dictionary was produced in St Petersburg between 1853 and 1875, with a new edition appearing between 1879 and 1889. The dictionary was a collaborative effort completed over twenty-three years with the assistance of Albrecht Weber and Rudolf Roth.

## Publication History
The *Chhandogya-upanishad* translation and critical examination were released in 1889 in St Petersburg. This publication occurred during the later period of Bohtlingk's career, after he had settled in Jena in 1868 and subsequently moved to Leipzig in 1885, where he remained until his death on April 1, 1904.
