# Santa Anna

## Location and Geography
Santa Anna is a location in South America situated in the province of Corrientes. It served as the final residence of the French traveller and botanist Aime Jacques Alexandre Bonpland.

## Association with Aime Jacques Alexandre Bonpland
Aime Jacques Alexandre Bonpland, born in La Rochelle on August 22, 1773, under the real name Goujand, was a surgeon in the French army and a student of J.N. Corvisart in Paris. Bonpland is noted for his five years of travel with A. von Humboldt through Colombia, Mexico, and the districts bordering the Amazon and Orinoco. During these travels, he classified and collected approximately 6,000 plants, the majority of which were previously unknown in Europe. These findings were later detailed in his work *Plantes equinoxiales* (1808-1816).

Following his explorations, Bonpland held the superintendence of the gardens at Malmaison and published the *Description des plantes rares de Navarre* (1813) and *Monographie des Melastomees* (1806). In 1816, he traveled to Buenos Aires with various European plants and was elected professor of natural history, though he eventually left this post to explore central South America.

## Later Years and Death
While traveling toward Bolivia, Bonpland was arrested in 1821 by order of the dictator of Paraguay, Dr. Francia. He remained in detention until 1831. After his release, he lived in San Borga within the province of Corrientes. He was eventually moved to Santa Anna, where he resided until his death on May 4, 1858.
