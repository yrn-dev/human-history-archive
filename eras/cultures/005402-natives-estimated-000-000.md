# Native Populations of the British Empire and Other Regions

## Overview of the British Empire
The native population of the British Empire includes various types of the principal brown, yellow, and black races. This includes high-type races of the East, which are described as almost white. As of 1901, the native population of India, primarily high-type and brown, was recorded at 294,191,379. The total native population of the empire is estimated at approximately 400,000,000, while the white population—mainly of Anglo-Saxon race but including some Spanish, Dutch, and French peoples—reached over 53,000,000 in 1901.

## African Native Populations
The aboriginal nations across Africa are mostly of the black negroid type. In South Africa, the aboriginal races consist of the Hottentots and Bushmen, both of which are diminishing rapidly and are expected to become extinct in British South Africa during the twentieth century. The majority of the native population in South Africa is composed of Bantu stock, known in tribal groups as Damara, Bechuana, Zulu, and Kaffirs. 

Additionally, a hybrid population known as the Mixed Races exists in South Africa, resulting from intermarriage between natives and individuals introduced over the last two hundred years from British India, Ceylon, Madagascar, Mozambique, and Java. These individuals vary in color from dark brown to yellow.

Regional population estimates for Africa include:
*   **West Africa:** 18,863,000 (including 15,000,000 in Nigeria, including Lagos; 2,700,000 in the Gold Coast and hinterland; 1,000,000 in Sierra Leone; and 163,000 in Gambia).
*   **East Africa:** 7,425,000 (including 4,000,000 natives in the Protectorate, 3,200,000 in Uganda, and 200,000 in Zanzibar).
*   **South Africa:** 5,211,329.
*   **British Central Africa:** 2,000,000.

Racial characteristics vary by region; while some lower negroid types on the west coast still practice fetish rites and cannibalism, higher types on the east coast approach the Christian races of Abyssinia. There is a wide admixture of Arab blood from east to west, producing a light-brown type, particularly in Nigeria and Uganda.

## Australasia and the Pacific
The native populations of Australasia and the Islands total 824,000. These are divided into dark and light Polynesian types. The dark Polynesian type is black and of a very low order, with cannibal habits remaining in some islands. The aboriginal tribes of Australia are a low-class black race, though generally inoffensive and peaceful, numbering 200,000. In contrast, the white Polynesian races are of a superior type, as seen in the Maoris of New Zealand.

Population breakdowns include:
*   **Papua (New Guinea):** 400,000 (approximate, as no census was taken; described as being in a very low state of civilization).
*   **Fiji:** 121,000 (Polynesian, black and brown).
*   **New Zealand:** 53,000 (Maoris, brown, and Chinese/half castes).
*   **Australia:** 200,000 (black) and 50,000 (Chinese and half castes).

## Other Global Native Populations
In Canada, the only coloured native races are the Red Indians, who are brown and vary in tribe but are few in number, totaling 100,000. In Danish Greenland, the Eskimo population on the west coast has decreased since the mid-18th century due to infectious diseases, specifically a severe smallpox epidemic. While Hans Egede estimated the population at 30,000 in the mid-18th century, this is considered a likely over-estimate. Current groups include the "Smith Sound Eskimo" or "Arctic Highlanders" near Cape York, numbering about 240.

In Korea, the native population is described as absolutely homogeneous. The population was estimated at a maximum of 12,000,000 following an 1897 census that initially returned 17,000,000. The population is concentrated in the warm provinces of the west and south, while the north is thinly peopled due to the severe climate.
