# Indians--Brown

Within the records of the Human History Archive, the designation "brown" is used to categorize various native populations across the British Empire, particularly in Asia, Africa, and the Americas. These populations are distinguished from the "white," "black," and "yellow" races, though some are described as "high-type" races of the East that may be nearly called white.

## Populations of India and the Eastern Colonies
The native population of India is described as being mainly "high type, brown." According to the census of 1901, this population totaled 294,191,379. The population of India is further categorized into 118 groups based on language, which are collected into the following principal groups:
*   Indo-European (specifically the Indo-Aryan sub-family)
*   Dravido-Mu[n.][d.][=a] (including Dravidian and Mu[n.][d.][=a] or Kolarian)
*   Indo-Chinese (including Siamese-Chinese, Tibeto-Burman, and Mon-Khmer)
*   Malayo-Polynesian
*   Semitic
*   Hamitic
*   Unclassed (such as Gipsy)

In the Eastern Colonies, brown and mixed populations are also prevalent. In Ceylon, the population is described as high type, brown, and mixed, totaling 3,568,824; this includes 2,000,000 Sinhalese and 954,000 Tamil. Other Eastern dependencies include Hong-Kong (Chinese and brown, 306,130), the Straits Settlements (brown, mixed, and Chinese, 570,000), and North Borneo and Sarawak (mixed brown, 700,000).

## Brown Populations in Africa and the West Indies
In South Africa, a "hybrid population" has emerged over the last two hundred years through the intermarriage of natives with individuals introduced from British India, Ceylon, Java, Madagascar, and Mozambique. These individuals are generally classified as "Mixed Races" and exhibit colors varying from yellow to dark brown.

In East Africa, the combined "black and brown" populations are estimated at 7,425,000. This total includes 4,000,000 natives in the Protectorate, 200,000 in Zanzibar, and 3,200,000 in Uganda. Additionally, there are approximately 25,000 Asiatics in the Protectorate. In West Africa, the "black and brown" population of Nigeria (including Lagos) is estimated at 15,000,000.

In the West Indies, which includes seventeen islands or groups of islands and the continental colonies of Honduras and British Guiana, the total coloured population is approximately 1,912,655. Specific populations identified as having brown elements include Trinidad and Tobago (black and brown, 250,000), British Guiana (black and brown, 286,000), and Jamaica (chiefly black, with some yellow and brown, 790,000).

## North American Populations
In Canada, the archive notes that the only coloured native races are the Red Indians, who are many in tribal variety but few in number. The population of Indians--Brown in Canada is listed as 100,000.

## Global and Imperial Context
The total native population of the empire, which includes the brown races, is estimated at approximately 400,000,000. When combined with the white population of over 53,000,000 (mainly of Anglo-Saxon race), the total population of the empire is estimated at 388,712,785, excluding lesser crown colonies and later census increases. This figure represents more than one-fourth of the total world population, which is calculated to be over 1,500,000,000.
