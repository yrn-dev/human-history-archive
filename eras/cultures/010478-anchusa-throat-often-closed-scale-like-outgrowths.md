# Anchusa

*Anchusa*, commonly known as alkanet, is a genus of plants belonging to the Boraginaceae order. This order is part of the sympetalous section of dicotyledons and is a member of the Tubiflorae series.

A defining feature of this group is the dorsiventral, or one-sided, inflorescence. ## Floral Structure of Anchusa
The flowers of the Boraginaceae are generally regular, though the form of the corolla varies by genus. In *Anchusa*, the corolla is salver-shaped. A specific characteristic of the floral structure is the presence of scale-like outgrowths from the corolla that often close the throat. These outgrowths form what is known as the corona.

These scales around the throat of the corolla serve functional purposes, protecting honey and pollen from undesirable visitors or wet conditions. Additionally, because these scales may differ in color from the corolla-lobes—such as the yellow eye found in the forget-me-not—they may serve to indicate the position of the honey to insects.
