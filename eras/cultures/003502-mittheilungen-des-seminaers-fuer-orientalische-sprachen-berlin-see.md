# Mittheilungen des Seminaers fuer Orientalische Sprachen

The *Mittheilungen des Seminaers fuer Orientalische Sprachen* is a publication based in Berlin. It serves as a venue for the dissemination of academic research, specifically within the fields of linguistics and the study of African languages.

## Contributions to Bantu Linguistics
The publication has been used to feature the work of Carl Meinhof, who contributed numerous essays to the *Mittheilungen des Seminaers fuer Orientalische Sprachen*. Meinhof's research in this journal focused on the existing languages of South-East and East Africa, as well as the original structure of the Bantu mother-speech. 

Through these essays, Meinhof collected a vast amount of information considered valuable to the field. Some of these works were also issued separately through Brockhaus in Leipzig in 1899. While the information gathered is extensive, the archive notes that Meinhof's deductions based on this data are not always in agreement with those of other authorities.

## Context within Linguistic Study
The research published in the *Mittheilungen* exists alongside other significant linguistic efforts of the late 19th and early 20th centuries. These include comparative grammars of South African languages by Dr. W. I. Bleek and Father J. Torrend, as well as the classification of Congo groups of Bantu speech found in the work *George Grenfell and the Congo* (1908). Meinhof's contributions to the Berlin seminar's publications complement his other major work, *Grundzuege einer vergleichenden Grammatik der Bantusprachen*, published in Berlin in 1906.
