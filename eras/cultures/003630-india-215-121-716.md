## Architecture and Monasteries
Historical accounts from Hiuen Tsang describe the Indian monasteries at Nalanda in Bahár as featuring elaborate and fantastic ornamentation. These structures were built during a period when timber served as a primary construction material. The pavilions of these monasteries were characterized by:
* Pillars adorned with dragons.
* Posts that glowed with the colors of the rainbow.
* Columns set with jade, which were richly lackered and chiselled.
* Balustrades of vermilion and carved open work.
* Roofs covered in shining tiles that created multiplied splendours through mutual reflection.

Such architectural styles are suggested to have been a direct origin for similar fantasies found in Chinese and Burmese architecture.

## Geography and Climate
Ancient geographical texts, specifically the *Liber Junioris Philosophi*, mention a people in India who reside in the Terrestrial Paradise and lead a life described as the Golden Age. Due to the extreme heat of the sun, these people are described as remaining in the river all day.

## Political Representation
In the context of the Treaty of Peace following the World War, India was represented by two delegates: Mr. Montagu, who served as the Secretary of State for India, and Maharajah Ganga Singh.
