# Ba-Luba

The Ba-Luba are a Bantu negroid race consisting of several subdivisions and are recognized as one of the most cultivated and important peoples of Central Africa. They are distributed across eight degrees of longitude, with their territory extending from the Kasai in the west to Lakes Bangweulu, Mweru, and Tanganyika in the east.

## Regional Distribution and Political History
The Ba-Luba are found in their greatest racial purity in the east, where they established the states of Uguha, Urua, and Katanga. In the western regions, they have intermixed to some extent with the Ba-Kete aborigines. Through this process, the Ba-Luba partially dispossessed the Ba-Kete, dividing them into northern and southern portions. The western Ba-Luba are specifically referred to by the name Ba-Shilange.

The founders of the great Lunda empire, which is currently divided between Angola and the Belgian Congo, are connected with the Ba-Luba. This empire was ruled by a monarch known as the Muata Yanvo (or Jamvo). The movement of the Ba-Luba toward the west occurred in relatively recent history, specifically at the beginning of the 19th century or the end of the 18th century. Shortly after this movement, a chief named Kalamba Mukenge founded a large state.

## Social and Cultural Practices
The Ba-Luba are an agricultural people, though the labor in the fields is performed by slaves and women. The men are renowned as admirable craftsmen, particularly in the areas of iron-work, cloth-weaving, and wood-carving. 

Physical and aesthetic customs vary by region:
* **Body Modification:** Circumcision is practiced by the Ba-Luba, and scar-tattooing is common. Tooth-filing is very frequent in the east, whereas it is comparatively rare in the west.
* **Appearance:** Hairdressing fashions are highly varied and often extremely fantastic.
* **Housing:** Houses are rectangular and built by the women; however, square pile-houses are found on the Lulua.
* **Weaponry:** Spears are the principal weapons used in the east, while bows and arrows are the chief weapons in the west.

Physically, the Ba-Luba are described as a fine race and generally appear prosperous, although there has been considerable cultural, moral, and physical deterioration in the extreme west.

## Religion and Ideology
In the east, the old form of religion persists, characterized by the common use of carved anthropomorphic figures and various charms. This region remained untouched by a later communistic movement.

In 1870, a remarkable politico-religious revolution occurred, leading to the establishment of a secret society called the *Bena Riamba*. This society was connected to a cult of hemp-smoking. Members of the *Bena Riamba* abandoned their previous fetish worship in favor of a form of communism centered on the idea of blood-brotherhood among all members. The practice of hemp-smoking becomes less common as one moves toward the east.

## Linguistic Classification
The Ba-Luba are part of the *Rua-Luba-Lunda-Marungu* group, which also includes the *Lulua*, *Kanyoka*, and *Ki-tabwa*. This group occupies a significant portion of the south central basin of the Congo, situated between the Zambezi watershed to the south, the Bakuba country to the north, the main streams of the Kwango and Kasai to the west, and the south-west coastline of Tanganyika to the east. 

Additionally, the *Bakuba* assemblage of Central Congo dialects—which includes *Shilange*, *Songe*, and *Babuma*—likely encompasses all Bantu languages between the Upper Kwilu and Kwa-Kasai in the west and the Lomami river in the east, with the Sankuru river potentially forming the northern boundary.
