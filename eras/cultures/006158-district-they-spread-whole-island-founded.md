# The Bugis People

## Origins and Expansion
The Bugis (also referred to as the Bughis) are a people of Malayan stock. Originally, they occupied only the kingdom of Boni, located on the south-western peninsula of the island of Celebes. From this specific district, the Bugis spread across the entirety of the island and established settlements throughout the Malay Archipelago.

## Physical and Cultural Characteristics
The Bugis are described as being of robust build and middle size, possessing an enterprising and very active nature. Their complexion is slightly lighter than that of the average Malay. In terms of disposition, they are characterized as fierce, haughty, and brave; they are noted as being more predisposed to "running amuck" than any other Malayan people.

The Bugis language is allied to that of the Macassars and utilizes similar characters for writing. This language was studied and its letters reproduced in type by Dr. B.F. Mathes of the Netherlands Bible Society.

## Economy and Industry
Industrious and ingenious, the Bugis practice agriculture more extensively than the tribes neighboring them. They manufacture cotton-cloth, which is produced both for their own internal use and for export. 

Their love of the sea has allowed them to maintain an almost total monopoly on trade around Celebes. They engage in a significant trade involving vegetable and mineral products from Boni, including pearls, gold-dust, camphor, nut-megs, and tortoise-shell. Additionally, the Bugis maintain their own schools and possess well-built towns.

## Governance and Political Structure
The confederation of Boni is composed of eight petty states. The king is generally elected for life from among their own number by the chiefs of these eight states. The king is restricted in his authority and cannot decide on any public measure without the consent of these chiefs.

The nature of leadership within the individual states varies:
* In some states, the position of chief is hereditary.
* In other states, any member of the privileged classes may aspire to the dignity of chief.
* It is not infrequent for a woman to govern the state.

## Religion
Since the 17th century, the Bugis have been Mahommedans. Prior to this, their original form of nature-worship was heavily influenced by Hindu elements. Even following their conversion, they continue to retain rites associated with the worship of Siva.
