# BAMBUTE

The Bambute, a people sometimes incorrectly referred to as the Batwa, are a race of pygmies inhabiting the Semliki Forest. Their territory is located on the western borders of the Uganda Protectorate, situated between Albert Edward Nyanza and Albert Nyanza. It is probable that they represent a branch of the pygmy race of Equatorial Africa, which is also represented further to the west by the Batwa.

## Physical Characteristics
The Bambute possess an average height of 4 feet 9 inches. Their complexion ranges from brownish-black to reddish-yellow. Their physical appearance is further characterized by:
*   **Hair:** Head-hair is often russet-brown. Body-hair is black and bristly on the chest, pubes, axillae, chin, and upper lip, while it is fleecy and yellowish on the limbs, back, and cheeks.
*   **Facial Features:** They are noted for having a long upper lip and a bridgeless nose with enormous alae (the cartilage above the nostrils).
*   **Dental Modification:** The canines and upper incisors are sharpened to a point.
*   **Scent:** Sir H. H. Johnston has noted that their skins emit a rancid odour, even when forced to keep clean, which he describes as being between the smell of a negro and a monkey.

## Lifestyle and Culture
The Bambute are nomad hunters who live within the forest. They do not utilize permanent architecture, building only huts made of leaves and sticks. For hunting, they use a tiny bow to shoot poisoned arrows, which they employ to hunt the largest game.

They maintain a minimal approach to personal adornment; they do not use scars or tattoos and possess no love for ornaments, eschewing the use of anklets, necklets, or ear-rings. When in the forests, they go entirely naked. 

Sir H. H. Johnston states that the Bambute possess a good ability for drawing, using sharpened sticks to sketch birds and beasts known to them in mud or sand.

## Language and Music
The Bambute speak a corrupted version of the dialects spoken by their negro neighbours. Their manner of speaking is described as a peculiar way of singing their words. Their voices are musical and low, characterized by a singularly staccato pronunciation where every syllable is uttered separately. They are described as being very musical.

## Social Organization and Beliefs
The Bambute lack a formal tribal government. Instead, they accept an adept hunter as a temporary lawgiver. Their social structure includes:
*   **Marriage:** Marriage is conducted by purchase, and polygamy appears to exist, though domestic affections are strong.
*   **Spirituality:** They show no evidence of ancestor or spirit worship. However, they hold the belief that rain, lightning, and thunder are manifestations of an Evil Power. They also believe that the dead are reincarnated as the red bush-pig.
*   **Funerary Customs:** The dead are buried in graves that are dug. It is common to place weapons, tobacco, and food with the corpse.
