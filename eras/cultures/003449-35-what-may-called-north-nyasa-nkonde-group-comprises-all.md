# North Nyasa (Nkonde) Group

The North Nyasa group, also referred to as the Nkonde group, is a classification of Bantu languages spoken in the regions surrounding Lake Nyasa and the adjacent plateaus.

## Geographic Distribution and Dialects
The North Nyasa or Nkonde group comprises all the dialects found along the north coasts and north-west coasts of Lake Nyasa. Specific examples of these dialects include *Iki-nyikiusa* and *Ici-wandia*. The group's reach extends to the Nyasa-Tanganyika plateau, where *Ishi-nyi[chi]a* is spoken.

The geographic extent of this group potentially reaches as far north west as the shores of Lake Rukwa (*Ici-wungu*) in the vicinity of the Nyamwezi domain, as well as the Fipa country (*Iki-fipa*). However, *Iki-fipa* is noted to have some affinities with the western Victoria-Nyanza and Tanganyika languages.

## Linguistic Characteristics
The Nkonde dialects, along with the Kese dialects of the north and north-east coasts of Lake Nyasa, are identified as being among the most archaic dialects of the Bantu family spoken at the present day.

## Neighboring Language Groups
The North Nyasa group is situated among several other distinct linguistic sections:
* **To the South:** The western part of Nyasaland is occupied by the Tumbuka section, which includes the languages of the A-tonga, Henga, and Tumbuka peoples. This section occupies the area between the Upper Luangwa river and the western shores of Lake Nyasa.
* **To the East:** The Kinga speech is located in the lofty Livingstone mountains. This speech is classified as a separate group because it is sufficiently distinct from its neighbors.
* **Further East:** Beyond the Livingstone mountains and west of the Ruaha river lies the Sango-Bena-Hehe-Sutu group.
* **North-East Shores:** The Yao genus of languages extends to the north-east shores of Lake Nyasa (*Ki-kese*).

## Regional Geography
The region associated with these languages includes the Konde Mountains to the north-west of Lake Nyasa, where peaks probably exceed 8,000 feet in height. The Nyasa-Tanganyika plateau, where *Ishi-nyi[chi]a* is spoken, is located between the vicinity of Tanganyika, the vicinity of Lake Mweru, and the basin of the river Luangwa, with its highest points reaching 7,000 to 8,000 feet.

In the north-west coast of Lake Nyasa, the town of Karonga is located. Additionally, the settlement of Fife is situated on the Nyasa-Tanganyika plateau.
