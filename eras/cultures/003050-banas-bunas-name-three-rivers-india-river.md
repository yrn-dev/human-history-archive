# BANAS

The name Banas, also referred to as Bunas, is associated with several rivers located in India.

## Rivers of India
There are three distinct rivers in India known as the Banas or Bunas:

*   **Rajputana River:** This river originates in the Aravalli range in Udaipur. It serves as the drainage for the Udaipur valley and flows for 300 miles before emptying into the Chambal.
*   **Bengal (Shahabad District) River:** Located in the Shahabad district of Bengal, this river functions as the drainage channel situated between the Sone canals system and the Arrah canal. It eventually flows into the Gangi nadi.
*   **Bengal (Chota Nagpur) River:** This river rises within the state of Chang Bhakar in Chota Nagpur, Bengal, and flows into the Sone near Rampur.

Additionally, a river named the Banas, along with the Saraswati, originates in the Aravalli hills. These streams flow eastward through the native state of Palanpur and empty into the Runn of Cutch.

## Other Geographical References
The term "Bana" appears in other geographical contexts outside of the Indian river systems:
*   **Bana Bank:** A bank extending for 9 miles that obstructs the entrance to the southern part of the Corisco bay on the Guinea Coast of West Africa, making navigation in that area dangerous.
*   **Bana Island:** One of the four principal islands enclosed by the Corisco bay on the Guinea Coast of West Africa.

## Unrelated Terms
The term "bana" also appears in the Japanese art of *ike-bana*. Literally meaning "living flower," *ike-bana* is the science of arranging flowers to suggest natural life. Unlike Occidental methods that focus on color, the Japanese approach emphasizes the manner of growth and a "symmetry by suggestion" or a balance of inequalities.
