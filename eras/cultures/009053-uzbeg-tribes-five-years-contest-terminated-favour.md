# Uzbeg Tribes

The Uzbegs are a dominant race located in Central Asia, specifically within the regions of Turkestan proper, Bokhara, and Khiva. They maintain a social structure based on their old divisions into tribes or clans.

## Regional Dominance and Distribution
In Turkestan proper, the Uzbeg Turks are the dominant group. In the oasis regions, they serve as the dominating race among the settled inhabitants, and the area's officials are recruited from among them. In the region of Bokhara, the Uzbegs are the dominant race; the total population of this area is estimated at 1,250,000. In Khiva, the Uzbeg population is estimated to be between 150,000 and 200,000. Additionally, archive records note the presence of Uzbegs in Kunduz.

## Characteristics and Culture
The Uzbegs of Bokhara are described as fanatical Moslem Sunnites. They are characterized as scorning work and despising their Iranian subjects. 

## Geography and Environment
The Uzbegs inhabit a territory between the Caspian and Aral Seas and the northern border of the Persian highlands. This area is primarily a low-lying, nearly desert plain that extends to the foot of the north-western extremity of the Tibeto-Himalayan mountains. The region is prolonged eastward through the valleys of the Jaxartes (Syr-Darya) and the Oxus (Amu Darya). 

The environment is characterized by insufficient rainfall for agriculture, making farming possible only through irrigation. Consequently, the fixed population is relatively small and restricted to towns and districts near the rivers. In the lowlands of Bokhara, the climate is extreme, featuring very hot summers and short, cold winters where frosts can reach -20 deg. Fahr. and the Oxus may freeze for a fortnight. The lowlands are also subject to terrible sand-storms from the west that can kill cotton trees and exhaust people. Malaria is widely prevalent in this region, sometimes becoming malignant after a wet spring.

## Related Groups and Populations
The Uzbegs coexist with several other ethnic and social groups in their territories:
* **Sarts and Tajiks:** In Khiva, Sarts and Tajiks are tradespeople and agriculturists of mixed race. In Bokhara, Sarts (a mixture of Turks and Iranians) constitute the bulk of the town populations, while Iranian Tajiks make up the great bulk of the general population.
* **Nomadic Tribes:** The region is largely inhabited by nomadic tribes, including the nomad Turkomans and nomad Kirghiz, both of whom are of Turkish origin. In the area of Turkestan proper, these nomads are described as marauders. In Khiva, Turkomans (approximately 170,000) live in the steppes to the south and west of the oasis and previously conducted raids to plunder settled inhabitants.
* **Other Groups:** The population of Bokhara also includes Afghans, Persians, Arabs, Armenians, and the sons of Israel. Trade in the region is significantly handled by a colony of Hindus from Shikarpur. In Khiva, there is also a Turki tribe known as the Kara-kalpaks (or Black Bonnets), numbering some 50,000, who live south of Lake Aral.
