# "I do not know it, oh child!"

The phrase "I do not know it, oh child!" is a specific linguistic form found in the Basque language. Within Basque grammar, genders are distinguished primarily through verbal forms, where a special suffix is used to indicate the sex of the person being addressed. While the basic form *eztakit* translates to "I do not know it," the language employs different suffixes depending on the recipient of the statement. For instance, when addressing a woman, the form *eztakinat* ("I do not know it, oh woman!") is used, and when addressing a man, *eztakiat* (or *eztakikat*) is used. Certain dialectic varieties further refine these forms to include a respectful version, *eztakizut* ("I do not know it, you respectable one"), from which the childish form *eztakichut* ("I do not know it, oh child!") is derived.
