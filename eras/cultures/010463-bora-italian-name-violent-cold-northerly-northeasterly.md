# BORA

The Bora is an Italian name used to describe a violent, cold wind that blows from the north and northeast. It is a common meteorological phenomenon in the Adriatic region, appearing with particular frequency along the coasts of Dalmatia and Istria.

## Meteorological Characteristics and Causes
The Bora is characterized by its cold temperature and violent nature. Its occurrence is linked to the general tendency of winds on the northern shores of the Mediterranean during winter, where cold air from the mountains slides down toward the sea due to lower pressure in those areas. When a cyclone forms over the Mediterranean, the currents in its north-western sector draw air from cold northern regions, leading to the prevalence of the Bora during the cyclone's passage.

In terms of its character, the Bora is precisely similar to the mistral wind found along the French Mediterranean littoral and in Provence. While it is most associated with the Adriatic, the Bora also occurs at Novorossiysk on the Black Sea. In certain regions, such as Dalmatia, the Bora (N.N.E.) is described as "terrible" and is capable of blowing during any season of the year, contrasting with the sirocco, which is the prevailing south-easterly wind of that area.

## Regional Impact and Climate
The Bora significantly influences the climate of the regions where it occurs. In certain areas, the southern portions of the territory are specifically exposed to this cold north-eastern wind, contributing to a rather severe climate. For example, in a province where the Bora is present, the mean annual temperature at Laibach is recorded at 48.4 deg. F., with annual rainfall reaching 72 inches. In such environments, the land use is divided among forests (44.4%), meadows (17.2%), pastures (15.7%), and vineyards (1.17%), with only 14.8% of the total area under cultivation.

In Dalmatia, where the Bora is a known factor, the general climate is otherwise described as warm and healthy. Mean temperatures in this region are recorded at 57 deg. F. in Zara, 62 deg. in Lesina, and 63 deg. in Ragusa, with an average annual rainfall of approximately 28 inches.
