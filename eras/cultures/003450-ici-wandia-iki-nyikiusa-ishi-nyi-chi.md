# Ici-wandia, Iki-nyikiusa, and Ishi-nyi[chi]a

The terms *Ici-wandia*, *Iki-nyikiusa*, and *Ishi-nyi[chi]a* refer to specific dialects and linguistic domains located within the African interior, specifically associated with the North Nyasa or Nkonde group.

## The North Nyasa Group
The North Nyasa group, also referred to as the Nkonde group, encompasses all the dialects found along the north-west and north coasts of Lake Nyasa. This group includes the dialects of *Ici-wandia* and *Iki-nyikiusa*. Additionally, the group includes *Ishi-nyi[chi]a*, which is situated on the Nyasa-Tanganyika plateau.

The geographical extent of this linguistic group potentially reaches as far north west as the shores of Lake Rukwa (*Ici-wungu*) in the vicinity of the Nyamwezi domain, and as far as the Fipa country (*Iki-fipa*). However, it is noted that *Iki-fipa* possesses some affinities with the western Victoria-Nyanza languages and the Tanganyika languages.

## Regional Linguistic Context
The North Nyasa group is situated among several other distinct linguistic and ethnic sections in the region:

*   **To the South:** The western part of Nyasaland, south of the North Nyasa group, is occupied by the Tumbuka section. This section includes the languages of the A-tonga, Henga, and Tumbuka peoples, spanning the area between the Upper Luangwa river and the western shores of Lake Nyasa.
*   **To the East:** The Kinga speech is located east of the North Nyasa group in the lofty Livingstone mountains. This speech is classified as a separate group due to being sufficiently distinct from its neighbors.
*   **Further East:** East of the Livingstone mountains and west of the Ruaha river, south of the Unyamwezi domain, lies the Sango-Bena-Hehe-Sutu group.
*   **Allied Dialects:** Closely allied to these are the Emba or Bemba dialects. This genus occupies the ground between Lake Mweru, the Upper Chambezi river, and the south and south-west coasts of Tanganyika. The domain of Ki-bemba also includes the Ki-mambwe and Ki-lungu dialects of South-East and South Tanganyika, which are locally modified.
