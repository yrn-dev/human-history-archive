# West Africa

The study of West Africa, particularly the Gold Coast and the Benin region, has been documented through a variety of ethnological, historical, and geographical accounts produced during the 19th and early 20th centuries. These records range from early missionary and diplomatic missions to official colonial reports and native histories.

## The Gold Coast and Ashanti
Historical documentation of the Gold Coast and the Ashanti region includes early valuable works such as T.E. Bowdich's *A Mission from Cape Coast Castle to Ashantee* (1819) and J. Dupuis's *Journal of a Residence in Ashantee* (1824). For a broader historical perspective, Col. A.B. Ellis authored *A History of the Gold Coast of West Africa* (1893). Additionally, C.C. Reindorf, a native pastor of the Basel mission, wrote the *History of the Gold Coast and Asante... from about 1500 to 1860*, published in 1895.

Ethnological studies of the region include Col. A.B. Ellis's 1887 work, *The Tshi-speaking Peoples of the Gold Coast*. General surveys of the country have been provided by R.A. Freeman in *Travels in Ashanti and Jaman* (1898) and C.P. Lucas in the *Historical Geography of the British Colonies* (1900). From 1906 onward, the Colonial Office in London issued *Annual Reports, Ashanti* to provide ongoing data.

## The Benin Region
The Benin region has been the subject of comprehensive study regarding its art, customs, and history. H.L. Roth's *Great Benin, its Customs, Art and Horrors* (1903) is noted as a profusely illustrated and comprehensive work. Other contributions to the study of Benin's antiquities include works by Pitt Rivers (1900) and C.H. Read and O.M. Dalton (1899), the latter focusing on antiquities housed in the British Museum.

The region's more violent history is documented in A. Boisragon's *The Benin Massacre* (1897) and R.H. Bacon's *Benin, the City of Blood* (1898), written by a member of the punitive expedition of 1897. Further accounts include Sir R. Burton's *Wanderings in West Africa* (1863) and H.L. Gallwey's "Journeys in the Benin Country," published in the *Geographical Journal* in 1893. Official data on the area is maintained in the *Reports on Southern Nigeria* issued by the Colonial Office in London.
