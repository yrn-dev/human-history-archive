# Aboriginal Tribes of Australia

The aboriginal tribes of Australia are described as a low-class black race. In terms of their general behavior and social habits, they are characterized as being generally inoffensive and peaceful.

## Cultural Expressions and Music
The musical traditions of the Australian aborigines include song-dances. While some theories have suggested that ballads were composed communally by a whole people, evidence indicates that song is not entirely communal among the aborigines. New forms of song and dance are devised by known men who are said to be inspired by the All Father or through dreams. These creations are subsequently carried across the country. Additionally, examples of individual lyric exist within their culture.

## Weaponry and the Boomerang
A primary missile weapon used by the Australian aborigines is the boomerang. The term is derived from a native name used by a single tribe in New South Wales; it was referred to as the "Port Jackson term" by Captain King in 1827. Contrary to some beliefs, the weapon is not onomatopoeic, as it whistles in the air rather than making a "boom" sound. It has also been erroneously linked to the *womera*, or spear-thrower.

There are two main types of boomerangs:
*   **The Return Boomerang:** In Australia, this type is always curved at an angle of 90 degrees or more. It typically measures 2 to 3 feet in length and weighs approximately 8 ounces.
*   **The Non-Return (War) Boomerang:** This version possesses a skew in the opposite direction of the return boomerang but is otherwise similar in form.

Both types are sickle-shaped and constructed from wood. Their general proportions are modeled such that the thickness is about 1/6th of the breadth, and the breadth is 1/12th of the length. Total length varies from 6 inches to 4 feet. The return boomerang features a cross-section that is asymmetrical, with a convex upper side and a flat or nearly flat lower side, and must be thrown using the right hand.

## Geography and Population
The aboriginal populations are found across the continent, including regions such as the Northern Territory of South Australia and the Cape York Peninsula, which bound the Gulf of Carpentaria. The Gulf of Carpentaria itself is an extensive arm of the sea on the north coast of Australia, stretching between 10 deg. 40' and 17 deg. 40' S. and 135 deg. 30' and 142 deg. E.

In terms of population statistics, the black aboriginal population of Australia is estimated at 200,000. This is distinct from other groups in the region, such as the 50,000 Chinese and half castes.

## Prehistoric Origins
It is suggested that approximately three thousand years ago, stranded patches of Palaeolithic Australoids inhabited the islands of the East Indies. These groups had wandered there during immemorial ages when a nearly complete land bridge existed between the East Indies and Australia.
