## Political Transitions in Denmark
In 1730, the reign of Frederick IV ended. His rule, which lasted from 1699 to 1730, concluded with a final decade focused on developing the country's resources following the effects of the Great Northern War, from which Denmark had suffered nearly as severely as Sweden. During this period, the court was noted for being seriously pious and contributing to education. Frederick IV also demonstrated a deep interest in the welfare of the peasantry, implementing a wise economy to bring the national debt within manageable limits and abolishing serfdom in 1722 for all peasants on royal estates born after his accession. He was succeeded in 1730 by Christian VI, who reigned until 1746.
