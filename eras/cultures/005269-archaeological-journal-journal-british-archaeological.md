## Military Illustrations
The *Archaeological Journal* has provided visual documentation regarding the history of military units. Specifically, it contains illustrations related to grenadiers—soldiers originally tasked with the duty of throwing hand-grenades. The journal provides these illustrations in volume xxiii, page 222, and volume xlvii, pages 321-324. These records coincide with the historical development of grenadier companies, which were first organized in the French *Regiment du Roi* between 1667 and 1670 before the practice was adopted in England.
