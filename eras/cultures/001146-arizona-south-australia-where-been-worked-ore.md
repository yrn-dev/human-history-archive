# Azurite and Regional Mineralogy

## Azurite and Copper Ore
Azurite is a mineral that occurs alongside malachite in the upper sections of copper ore deposits. Its origin is attributed to the alteration of native copper or sulphide by water that contains oxygen and carbon dioxide. Because of this process, azurite is a common mineral found in all copper mines. 

In certain regions, such as South Australia and Arizona, azurite occurs in large masses. In these locations, it has been worked as an ore of copper, an element of which azurite consists of 55%. Azurite is less hydrated than malachite, making it susceptible to alteration into that mineral; consequently, pseudomorphs of malachite after azurite are not uncommon. While the massive material is occasionally polished and cut for decorative use, this application is less extensive than the decorative use of malachite.

## Regional Geography and Culture of Arizona
The Hopis (also known as Moquis) of Arizona utilize a non-return form of the boomerang. The boomerang is a sickle-shaped missile weapon made of wood, though in India it is made of steel or ivory. The general dimensions of these weapons are modeled such that the breadth is 12 times the thickness, and the length is 12 times the breadth, with lengths ranging from 6 inches to 4 feet. While the return boomerang is characterized by a specific skew and curvature (always 90 degrees or more in Australia), the non-return form used by the Hopis has a skew in the opposite direction.

## South Australia: Governance and Economy
South Australia maintains specific legal and financial systems. Regarding taxation, an income tax has been in place since 1884. This tax is levied on all incomes derived from or accruing in South Australia, with exceptions for friendly societies, district councils, municipal corporations, and societies that do not carry on business for gain. 

The tax rates are divided by the source of the income:
*   **Personal Exertion:** The rate is 4½d. in the pound for income up to £800, and 7d. in the pound for amounts exceeding £800.
*   **Property:** The rate is 9d. in the pound for income up to £800, and 1s. 1½d. in the pound for amounts exceeding £800.
An exemption of £150 is provided for incomes up to £400, but no exemption exists for incomes above that limit.

In terms of legal frameworks, South Australia's bankruptcy laws are modeled after the English system, similar to the systems in New Zealand and Western Australia.

## South Australia: Natural History and Society
South Australia contains strata from the Eocene age, which are also found in the Great Australian Bight. In terms of biological distribution, the equatorial zone extends south in Australia, with its boundary likely following the southern coast of the continent. Tropical fishes in the south-western region follow the Murray river toward the south and likely to its mouth.

Socially, the Baptist movement has a presence in the region, and South Australia is one of the colonies with a union of Baptist churches.
