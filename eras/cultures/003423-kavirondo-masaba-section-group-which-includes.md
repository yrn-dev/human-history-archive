# The Kavirondo-Masaba Section

The Kavirondo-Masaba section is a distinct division of Bantu tongues located in East Africa. This group is primarily situated along the northern Kavirondo and Mount Elgon territories, as well as the eastern and north-east shores of the Victoria Nyanza.

## Linguistic Classification and Characteristics
Within the broader classification of Bantu speech, the Kavirondo-Masaba section is more closely related to the Luganda section than to any other group of Bantu tongues. Despite this relationship, it remains a very distinct division. A notable linguistic feature of this section is that its prefixes are the most archaic among the groups.

## Geographic Distribution and Languages
The languages of the Kavirondo-Masaba section are spoken across several specific regions:
* **Mount Elgon:** The group includes languages spoken along the western flanks of Mount Elgon.
* **Victoria Nyanza:** The section encompasses the eastern coast-lands of the Victoria Nyanza, where the Igizii language is spoken, as well as the north-east and eastern shores.
* **Regional Dialects:** The group includes the Lu-nyara, Luwanga, Lukonde, and Igizii languages. It also encompasses the languages of the Bantu Kavirondo.

## The Kavirondo People
The Kavirondo dwell by the north-east shores of Victoria Nyanza. Within the protectorate, they are described as a race that is remarkable for their nudity.
