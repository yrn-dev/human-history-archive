# Christian

## Origins and Early History
The Christian Era began following the birth of Jesus of Nazareth, the true date of which is recorded as 4 B.C. According to historical outlines, Jesus of Nazareth was later crucified in A.D. 30. 

## Presence in the Roman Empire
During the period of the Roman Empire, the presence of Christians was noted in various contexts. Records indicate the existence of Christian churches, which were distinguished from the temples dedicated to all the gods. Historical accounts from witnesses such as Sozomen—a native of Gaza who conversed with Zeno, the bishop of Maiuma—and Philostorgius describe the experiences of early Christians. These accounts include reports of Christians who were literally sacrificed at the altars of the gods.
