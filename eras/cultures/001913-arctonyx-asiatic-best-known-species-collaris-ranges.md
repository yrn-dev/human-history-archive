# Arctonyx

The genus *Arctonyx* consists of members of the badger group. These animals are characterized by a specific dental formula: incisors 3/3, canines 1/1, premolars 4/4, and molars 1/2, totaling 38 teeth. The incisor line is curved, with the outer teeth positioned posteriorly to the others and the lower incisors inclined forwards. In these animals, the first premolars are often absent or rudimentary. The upper molar is longer in the antero-posterior direction than it is broad and is significantly larger than the sectorial; the lower sectorial features a very large, low, tuberculated heel.

## Physical Characteristics
The skull of *Arctonyx* is depressed and elongated, featuring a long, narrow face that is concave on top. The palatal bones are dilated, and the bony palate extends back to the level of the glenoid fossa. The suborbital foramina are very large. The skeletal structure consists of 7 cervical, 16 dorsal, 4 lumbar, 4 sacral, and 20 caudal vertebrae.

The animal possesses a long, mobile, and truncated snout with large terminal nostrils, which closely resemble those of a pig. Its eyes and rounded ears are very small. The body is compressed rather than depressed, and the limbs are of moderate length, with a walking style that is partially digitigrade. The tail is moderate in size and tapering. The animal's coat consists of a full, soft under-fur interspersed with longer, bristly hairs.

## Arctonyx collaris
The longest-known species within the genus is *A. collaris*. Known to the natives of the mountains of Borneo, Burma, and north-eastern India as the *bali-soor* (sand-pig) or *bhalu-soor* (bear-pig), it is also referred to as the sand-badger. 

*A. collaris* is larger than the typical badger and stands higher on its legs. It has a general aspect that is very pig-like, with a light grey color and feet and snout that are flesh-colored. In terms of behavior, the species is omnivorous and nocturnal. It has been described as a small animal that resembles a bear but possesses the tail, eyes, and snout of a hog.

## Distribution and Varieties
The genus is found in Asia. *A. collaris* ranges through Borneo, Burma, and north-eastern India, including the zoology of Assam. Beyond this species, other local varieties or species of *Arctonyx* have been described from Burma and north China.
