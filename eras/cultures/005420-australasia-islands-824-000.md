# Australasia and Islands

## Geography and Territorial Extent
Australasia and South Africa together cover 5,308,506 square miles in the southern hemisphere. Within the broader context of the British Empire, Australasia is situated in the eastern hemisphere, contributing to a total eastern hemisphere area of 6,925,975 square miles (which also includes India, South Africa, and the United Kingdom). 

The eastern portions of Australasia are located so close to the border of the western hemisphere that they facilitate a near-complete distribution of day and night across the empire. While much of the empire's land is in temperate zones suitable for white settlement, the northern portion of Australia and New Guinea are noted as exceptions to this suitability.

## Population and Demographics
The total native population of Australasia and the Islands is estimated at 824,000. This figure is composed of the following groups:
*   **Australia:** 200,000 black individuals, described as a very low type, and 50,000 yellow individuals, consisting of Chinese and half castes.
*   **Papua (New Guinea):** 400,000 black and brown Polynesian individuals.
*   **Fiji:** 121,000 black and brown Polynesian individuals.
*   **New Zealand:** 53,000 brown Maoris, as well as Chinese and half castes.

In addition to the native populations, Australasia has a white population of 4,662,000.

## Racial Classifications
The native races of the Polynesian islands and Australia are categorized into two primary types: the dark and light Polynesian. 

The dark Polynesian type is black and described as being of a very low order; in certain islands, this group still retains cannibal habits. Similarly, the aboriginal tribes of Australia are classified as a low-class black race, though they are generally characterized as inoffensive and peaceful in their habits. The natives of Papua (New Guinea) are described as being in a very low state of civilization, and because no census has been conducted, their population estimate is approximate.

Conversely, the white Polynesian races are described as a very superior type. The Maoris of New Zealand are cited as an example of this group, exhibiting characteristics of a high order.

## Trade and Industry
Australasia participates in the export of cheese to the United Kingdom. The cheese produced in Australasia, similar to that from North America, is mostly of the substantial Cheddar type. This contrasts with the "fancy" or soft cheeses primarily shipped from France.
