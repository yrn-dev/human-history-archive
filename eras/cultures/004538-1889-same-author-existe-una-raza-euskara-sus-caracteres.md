# Basque Studies and Literature

The study of the Basque people, their language, and their culture is supported by a specialized body of literature encompassing linguistics, anthropology, and folklore.

## Linguistic Works and Bibliographies
The literature regarding the Basque language is described as restricted, with the *Essai d'une bibliographie de la langue basque* by Julien Vinson (published in Paris in 1891, with additions and corrections in 1898) serving as a practically exhaustive resource and a mine of information on principal works. 

Key linguistic texts include:
*   **Early and Grammar Works:** A. Oihenart's *Notitia utriusque Vasconiae* (Paris, 1638 and 1656), Fl. Lecluse's *Manuel de la langue basque* (Toulouse, 1826), and L'Abbe Ithurry's *Grammaire basque* (Bayonne, 1895-1906).
*   **Comparative and Analytical Studies:** W. J. Van Eys authored the *Grammaire comparee des dialectes basques* (Paris, London, Amsterdam, 1879) and a *Dictionnaire basque-francais* (Paris, 1873). Prince L. L. Bonaparte produced *Le Verbe basque en tableaux* (London, 1864-1869). Dr. H. Schuchardt wrote *Die Entstehung der Bezugsformen des Baskischen* (Wien, 1893).
*   **Translations and Dictionaries:** C. Ribary's *Essai sur la langue basque* (1866) was translated from Hungarian by Julien Vinson in 1877. R. M. de Azkue published the *Diccionario vascongado espanol-francais* (Tours, 1906).
*   **Other Sources:** The *Monumenta Linguae Ibericae*, edited by Aemilius Hubner (Berlin, 1893), provides texts and an introduction, though its interpretation and analysis are noted as faulty. The first Basque vocabulary is found in *Le Codex de Saint-Jacques de Compostella*, lib. iv. (Paris, 1882), by J. Vinson and R. P. F. Fita.

## Anthropology and Folklore
Research into the Basque people extends to their physical and cultural characteristics. T. de Aranzadi authored *El Pueblo Euskalduna. Estudio de Antropologia* (San Sebastian, 1889) and *Existe una raza Euskara? Sus caracteres antropologicos* (1905).

Cultural and folkloric studies include:
*   **General Surveys:** Julien Vinson wrote *Les Basques et le pays basque* (Paris, 1882), which is considered a sufficient survey for general readers.
*   **Folklore:** Vinson's *Le Folk-Lore du pays basque* (Paris, 1883) covers the whole of the region's folklore and specifically treats the Pastorales.
*   **Legends and Traditions:** Wentworth Webster published *Basque Legends* (London, 1877 and 1879). *La Tradition au pays basque* (Paris, 1899) is a collection of papers written by local authorities.

## Regional and Specialized Studies
Additional research touches upon specific regional aspects and geographical distributions. Puyol y Camps' "La Epigraphia Numismatica Iberica," found in tomo xvi of the *Boletin de la Real Academia de la Historia* (Madrid, 1890), provides information on the geographical distribution of alphabets. Furthermore, *Les Coutumes generales gardees et observees au pais & baillage de Labourt* examines the customs of the Labourt region.
