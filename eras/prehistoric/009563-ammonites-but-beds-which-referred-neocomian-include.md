# Ammonites

The term "ammonites" refers to both a group of fossilized marine organisms found in various geological strata worldwide and a historical people involved in conflicts with the Israelites.

## Geological Occurrences and Fossil Record

Ammonites are abundant in several geological formations across different continents, serving as key indicators for dating rock layers.

### Asia and Africa
In the region of Bombay, specifically within Cutch and Kathiawar, Jurassic beds are marine and contain numerous ammonites. In East Africa, shales and limestones of the Jurassic age found near Changamwe contain gigantic ammonites. According to Dr. Waagen, these specimens show a striking analogy to forms found in the Acanthicus zone of East India. Additionally, in British Burma, the identification of Cretaceous beds rests on the discovery of a single Cenomanian ammonite.

### British Isles
In England, ammonites are found in various formations:
*   **Lower Lias:** In Somersetshire, a dark brown limestone known as "ammonite marble" is crowded with ammonites, primarily the species *A. planicostata*.
*   **Corallian Rocks:** These Jurassic rocks are divided into an upper series, characterized by the ammonite *Perisphinctes plicatilis*, and a lower series where *Aspidoceras perarmatus* serves as the zonal fossil.
*   **Kimeridgian Formation:** This formation contains a large number of peculiar ammonites, including *Reineckia eudoxus*, *R. Thurmanni*, and *Aspidoceras longispinus*. The lower division of the Kimeridgian is specifically characterized by the zonal ammonite *Cardioceras alternans*.

## Historical Ammonites

The Ammonites were a people who engaged in a deadly conflict with the Israelites. This war was provoked by a gross insult directed toward friendly ambassadors of Israel. The conflict expanded in scale when the Ammonites sought and procured assistance from their Aramean neighbors.

The war concluded with the fall of Rabbah, which marked the end of the war-like exploits of David. Following the victory, David took the jewelled crown of the Ammonite god, Milcom. The Ammonite people were subsequently subjected to severe menial labour, though they were not subjected to torture.
