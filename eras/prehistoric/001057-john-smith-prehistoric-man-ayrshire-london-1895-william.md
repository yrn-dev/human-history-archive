# John Smith, Prehistoric Man in Ayrshire

## Overview of Prehistoric Man in Ayrshire
The study of prehistoric inhabitants in the region of Ayrshire is documented in the work *Prehistoric Man in Ayrshire*, published in London in 1895 by John Smith. This text is cited alongside other regional historical and agricultural studies, including William Robertson's *History of Ayrshire* (1894), James Paterson's *History of the County of Ayr* (published in the *Transactions of Ayrshire and Galloway Archaeological Associations* between 1879 and 1900), D. Landsborough's *Contributions to Local History* (1878), and Archibald Sturrock's "On the Agriculture of Ayrshire" in the *Transactions of Highland and Agricultural Society*.
