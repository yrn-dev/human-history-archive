# Trinucleus seticornis Beds

The *Trinucleus seticornis* beds are a geological formation located in South Wales. They are composed of the Slade beds, the Redhill shales, and the Sholeshook limestone.

## Geological Classification
The *Trinucleus seticornis* beds are part of the Bala Series. In South Wales, the Bala Series is organized into a specific descending order of beds:
*   The *Trinucleus seticornis* beds (comprising the Sholeshook limestone, Redhill shales, and Slade beds)
*   The Robeston Wathen beds
*   The *Dicranograptus* shales

The Bala Series itself consists of dark sandstones and slates with limestone beds. It is currently positioned at the top of the Ordovician System, situated above the Llandeilo beds.

## Regional Equivalents and Distribution
The Bala Series is recognizable across most of North Wales, where the Bala limestone ranges from 20 to 40 feet in thickness. In the Lake District, the Bala limestone is regarded as the equivalent of the Coniston limestone. 

Outside of Wales, the series is represented by different regional names:
*   **Shropshire:** The Caradoc and Chirbury Series.
*   **Southern Scotland:** The Hartfell and Ardmillan Series.
*   **Ireland:** Similar rocks are present in this region.

## Composition of the Bala Series
In its type area, the series includes the Hirnant limestone, which is described as a thin and inconstant bed. This limestone is separated from the Bala limestone by 1400 feet of slates. Below these layers are further slates and volcanic rocks. These volcanic deposits consist of felsitic lava and tuff, which reach thicknesses of several thousand feet in the Snowdon District.

## Paleontology
The Bala Series is characterized by typical graptolites. The lower part of the series contains *Dicranograptus Clingani* and *Pleurograptus linearis*, while the upper part contains *Dicellograptus complanatus* and *Dicellograptus anceps*.
