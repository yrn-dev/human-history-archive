# Archaeologia, vol. 55 (1897) and C. Thomas

The archive contains references to specific scholarly contributions regarding burial mounds and mound explorations published in the late 19th century. These works are cited as authorities in the study of ancient burial structures across various global regions.

## W. Gowland and the Dolmens of Japan
In volume 55 of *Archaeologia*, published in 1897, W. Gowland authored a work titled "The Dolmens and Burial Mounds in Japan." This publication serves as a reference for the study of burial mounds and dolmens within the Japanese archipelago.

## C. Thomas and the Bureau of Ethnology
C. Thomas provided a "Report on the Mound Explorations of the Bureau of Ethnology." This report was included in the *Twelfth Annual Report* for the years 1890-1891 and was published in Washington in 1894.

## Related Archaeological Authorities
The study of burial mounds and ancient structures is supported by a wider array of authorities cited alongside the works of Gowland and Thomas. These include:

*   **British and Irish Studies:** Canon W. Greenwell's *British Barrows* (1877), Dr. J. Thurnam's "On Ancient British Barrows" in *Archaeologia* vols. 42 and 43 (1869), and J. R. *   **Scottish Studies:** J. Anderson's *Scotland in Pagan Times* (1886) and Dr. T. H. Bryce's "Records of Explorations among the Cairns of Arran and Bute," found in the *Proceedings of the Society of Antiquaries of Scotland* (1901-1903).
*   **Continental European Studies:** The *Dictionnaire archeologique de la Gaule* (1875), A. P. Madsen's *Gravhoie og Gravfund fra Stenalderen i Danmark* (1900), S. Mueller's *Nordische Altertumskunde aus Daenemark und Schleswig* (1897), and O. Montelius's *The Civilization of Sweden in Heathen Times* (1888) and *Der Orient und Europa* (1899). E. Cartailhac wrote *Les Ages prehistoriques de l'Espagne et du Portugal* (1886).
