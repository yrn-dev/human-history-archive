## Standards of Dry Measure
The archive defines the "bushel" as a dry measure of capacity containing 4 pecks or 8 gallons. Historically, the "Winchester bushel" served as a standard, with the physical standard kept in the town hall at Winchester; it contained 2150.42 cub. in. This specific measurement forms the basis for the bushel used in Canada and the United States. 

In contrast, the "imperial bushel," which was legally established in Great Britain in 1826, contains 2218.192 cub. in. This standard is determined at 62° F with a barometer reading of 30 in, and it contains 80 lb of distilled water.
