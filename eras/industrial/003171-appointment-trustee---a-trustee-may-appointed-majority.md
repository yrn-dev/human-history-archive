# Appointment and Administration of Trustees

The appointment and oversight of trustees vary significantly depending on the legal or institutional context, ranging from bankruptcy proceedings in different jurisdictions to the management of educational endowments and university governance.

## Trustees in Bankruptcy Proceedings

### Appointment and Confirmation
In certain bankruptcy frameworks, a trustee may be appointed by a majority in value of the creditors who vote, either at the first meeting or any subsequent meeting. Alternatively, the creditors may leave the appointment to the committee of inspection. Regardless of the method, the appointment must be confirmed by the Board of Trade. The Board of Trade may object to an appointment if it believes the creditors did not act in good faith for the general body's interests, or if the appointee is unfit, lacks impartiality due to their relationship with the debtor, creditors, or estate, or has previously been removed for misconduct or failure to render accounts for audit.

If the Board of Trade objects, a majority in value of the creditors may appeal to the High Court; otherwise, the objection is fatal to the appointment. Additionally, the trustee-elect must provide security to the satisfaction of the Board of Trade, which must be maintained throughout the tenure of the trusteeship to avoid removal. If creditors fail to appoint a trustee, the Board of Trade may do so, though creditors retain the power to supersede such an appointment.

### Role of the Official Receiver
The official receiver serves as a receiver prior to the first meeting of creditors and becomes a trustee by operation of law upon an order of adjudication. This office is vacated once a trustee appointed by the creditors is certified by the Board of Trade. However, the official receiver resumes the role of trustee if the creditors' trustee dies, resigns, is removed, or is released. Because the bankrupt's property vests in the trustee and passes between trustees by operation of law without formal conveyance, continuity of the office is maintained. In cases of summary administration—where the court is satisfied the property is unlikely to realize more than £300—the official receiver typically remains the trustee. In such instances, the Board of Trade serves as the committee of inspection. However, creditors may still appoint a different trustee via a resolution passed by a majority in number and three-fourths in value of those voting.

### Control and Removal
The trustee's administration of the estate is subject to control by the court, the creditors, the Board of Trade, and a committee of inspection. The committee, appointed by creditors, consists of three to five creditors or their authorized representatives and generally meets monthly. While the committee's directions are binding if they do not conflict with the act, they can be overruled by the court or the creditors. The court's directions override those of the creditors.

The Board of Trade monitors the conduct of trustees, investigates creditor complaints, and may remove a trustee for failing to perform duties faithfully. It can order local investigations of accounts, require answers to inquiries, or apply for the trustee to be examined on oath. If the trustee's neglect, omission, or misfeasance causes loss to the estate, the Board of Trade may require the trustee to make it good.

A trustee may be removed by the creditors at a meeting without needing to assign a reason. The Board of Trade may remove a trustee for incapacity, misconduct, failure to perform duties, or the same personal grounds used to object to an appointment. A trustee also vacates office if a receiving order is made against them. Resignation is permitted with the consent of a general meeting of creditors, though this does not release the trustee from the liability to account for their administration.

## International and Other Bankruptcy Systems

### Germany
Under a code passed in 1877, Germany distinguishes between simple bankruptcy (resulting from extravagance, gambling, or disorderly book-keeping) and fraudulent bankruptcy (involving the falsifying of books or concealment of property). In German proceedings, creditors are convened within one month of adjudication and may appoint a committee to advise the trustee. The court is responsible for ensuring the trustee performs their functions.

### Other Jurisdictions
In other systems, the administration of bankruptcy law is entrusted to district courts and exercised through officers called referees. In these cases, the creditors appoint the trustee or trustees of the estate.

## Institutional and Educational Trustees

### The Lowell Institute
The Lowell Institute in Boston, established by a bequest of $237,000 from John Lowell, junior, is managed by a trustee who is authorized to select his own successor. The will stipulates that the trustee must prefer a competent male descendant of John Lowell (grandfather) of the name of Lowell. The sole trustee holds the authority to select both the subjects and the lecturers.

### Cornell University
The affairs of Cornell University are administered by a board of forty trustees. Ten of these are elected by alumni, while others serve as *ex officio* members, including the university president, the governor and lieutenant-governor of the state, the speaker of the state assembly, the librarian of the Cornell Library, and the state commissioners of agriculture and education. To maintain a non-sectarian character, the university charter mandates that no majority of the board may belong to one religious sect or to no religious sect.
