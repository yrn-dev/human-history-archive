# Rev. H. Christmas and the Autograph of Bale

## Rev. H. Christmas and the Parker Society
In 1849, the Rev. H. Christmas served as an editor for the Parker Society. In this capacity, he edited the work titled *The Examinations of Lord Cobham, William Thorpe and Anne Askewe, &c.*

## The Autograph Note-book of Bale
The autograph note-book of Bale is preserved within the Selden Collection of the Bodleian Library in Oxford. This notebook contains materials that Bale collected for his two published catalogues. The contents are arranged alphabetically and are characterized by a lack of ornament. Notably, the notebook does not contain the personalities that are found in Bale's completed work. In most instances, the notebook also specifies the sources from which Bale derived his information.

## Publication of the Index
The materials from Bale's autograph note-book were prepared for publication as *Index Britanniae Scriptorum quos ... collegit Ioannes Baleus*. This publication was produced by the Clarendon Press in 1902 and constitutes part ix. of *Anecdota Oxoniensia*. The preparation of this volume included notes by Dr. R. Lane Poole, who was assisted by Miss Mary Bateson.

## Related Bibliographic Records
A comprehensive list of the works produced by Bale can be found in *Athenae Cantabrigienses* (vol. i. pp. 227 et seq.). Additionally, Bale's two tracts, which were printed in January 1547 and November 1546 at Marburg, served as the basis for the account provided by Foxe.
