# George Abraham Grierson

George Abraham Grierson, C.I.E., Ph.D., D.Litt. (Dublin), was a scholar and administrator who served as a member of the Indian Civil Service from 1873 to 1903. He is noted for his extensive work in linguistics and his contributions to the study of the languages of India.

## Professional Career and Administration
Grierson's tenure in the Indian Civil Service spanned three decades, beginning in 1873 and concluding in 1903. During this period, he held a significant administrative and scholarly role as the person in charge of the Linguistic Survey of India from 1898 to 1902.

## Linguistic Contributions and Publications
Grierson authored several major works focusing on the diverse languages of the Indian subcontinent. His primary publication is titled *The Languages of India*. His research extended into specific regional languages and dialects, including:

*   **Bihari:** Grierson produced *Seven Grammars of the Dialects and Sub-dialects of the Bihari Language* (published in parts between 1883 and 1887), which addressed every form of the Bihari language with the exception of standard Maithili.
*   **Maithili:** He authored *An Introduction to the Maithili Language of North Bihar*, a work containing a vocabulary, chrestomathy, and grammar. The grammar portion was first published in Calcutta in 1881, with a second edition released in 1909.
*   **Marathi:** Grierson provided a detailed account of the Marathi language in volume vii of the *Linguistic Survey of India*, published in Calcutta in 1905.

Additionally, the *Linguistic Survey of India* (vol. v. part ii., 1903) provides a complete conspectus of Bihari, including its various sub-dialects and dialects.

## Academic Honors and Affiliations
Grierson held several prestigious academic and honorary positions. He was formerly a Fellow of Calcutta University and served as the Vice-President of the Royal Asiatic Society. In 1909, he was recognized as a Gold Medallist by the Royal Asiatic Society (also cited as the Asiatic Society).
