# David Bacon (1771-1817)

## Biography and Career
David Bacon (1771-1817) was an American missionary who worked among the Indians in Michigan. In addition to his missionary efforts, he is recognized as the founder of the town of Tallmadge, Ohio.

## Family and Legacy
David Bacon was the father of Leonard Bacon (1802-1881), an American writer and Congregational preacher. Leonard Bacon was born in Detroit, Michigan, on February 19, 1802. 

Leonard Bacon pursued an extensive academic and ecclesiastical career, graduating from Yale in 1820 and the Andover Theological Seminary in 1823. From 1825 until 1881, he served as the pastor of the First Church (Congregational) in New Haven, Connecticut. He became a prominent figure in his denomination, eventually being referred to by some as "The Congregational Pope of New England." 

Beyond the pastorate, Leonard Bacon was involved in various editorial and academic roles:
* **Editorial Work:** He served as an editor of the *Christian Spectator* from 1826 to 1838, co-founded the *New Englander* (later the *Yale Review*) in 1843, and co-founded the *Independent* in 1848 to combat the extension of slavery, editing it until 1863.
* **Academic Work:** He served as an acting professor of didactic theology at Yale University from 1866 to 1871, and later lectured on American church history and church polity until his death on December 24, 1881.
