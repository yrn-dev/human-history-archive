## Ecclesiastical and Educational Efforts in India

Buchanan focused on advancing native education and Christianity, specifically through the organization of systematic translations of the Scriptures. He published *Christian Researches in Asia* in 1811, detailing his travels in western and southern India. After returning to England in 1808, he continued his involvement in Indian affairs. His 1813 book, *Colonial Ecclesiastical Establishment*, helped resolve a controversy that led to the establishment of the Indian episcopate.
