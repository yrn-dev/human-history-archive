Queen Louise died on July 19, 1810, while visiting her father in Strelitz. She was buried in the garden of the palace at Charlottenburg, where a mausoleum was constructed featuring a recumbent statue by Rauch. Later, in 1880, a statue of the queen was erected in Berlin's Thiergarten.
