# Johann Christian Felix Baehr

## Early Life and Education
Johann Christian Felix Baehr was a German philologist born in Darmstadt on June 13, 1798. He pursued his higher education at the university of Heidelberg.

## Academic Career
Baehr's professional career was centered at the university of Heidelberg, where he held several prominent positions. In 1823, he was appointed as a professor of classical philology. He later expanded his administrative roles within the university, becoming the chief librarian in 1832. Following the retirement of G. F. Creuzer, Baehr assumed the role of director of the philological seminary. He remained active in these academic circles until his death in Heidelberg on November 29, 1872.

## Published Works
Baehr's early scholarly contributions focused on the production of editions of classical texts. Between 1822 and 1835, he published editions of the following:
* Plutarch's *Alcibiades* (1822)
* Plutarch's *Philopoemen, Flamininus, Pyrrhus* (1826)
* The fragments of Ctesias (1824)
* Herodotus (published in segments from 1830-1835 and 1855-1862)

## Research on Roman Literature and the Middle Ages
The most significant portion of Baehr's bibliography concerns humanistic studies in the middle ages and Roman literature. His major works in these fields include:
* *Geschichte der roemischen Litteratur* (the 4th edition of which was published between 1868 and 1870)
* *Die christlich-roemische Theologie* (1837)
* *Geschichte der roemischen Litteratur im karolingischen Zeitalter* (1840)
* *Die christlichen Dichter und Geschichtschreiber Roms* (the 2nd edition of which was published in 1872), which served as supplementary volumes to his history of Roman literature.
