# Norfolk & Western Railway and Associated Lines

The Norfolk & Western railway is a rail system serving several key cities and locations across the United States, particularly in Virginia and Kentucky. It functions as a vital link for industrial distribution and regional transport.

## Service in Virginia
In the state of Virginia, the Norfolk & Western railway serves several significant urban and natural sites. In Lynchburg, a city located in Campbell county on the James river, the Norfolk & Western is one of three railways providing service, alongside the Southern and the Chesapeake & Ohio. Lynchburg is a principal manufacturing city in the state, known for producing granite, iron ore, coal, and superior quality tobacco.

The railway also provides access to the Luray Cavern in Page county. This large cave, discovered on August 13, 1878, by Andrew J. Campbell and others, is located near the village of Luray on the Norfolk & Western line.

Additionally, the Norfolk & Western serves Bristol, a city divided by the Tennessee-Virginia boundary line. Bristol is described as a railway centre of some importance, served by the Norfolk & Western, the Virginia & South-Western, the Southern, and the Holston Valley railways. Due to its location near great mineral deposits in Virginia, Tennessee, North Carolina, Kentucky, and West Virginia, Bristol acts as an important distributing point for coke, coal, and iron.

## Service in Kentucky
The Norfolk & Western railway extends its service into Kentucky, specifically to the city of Ashland in Boyd county. Situated on the Ohio river, Ashland is served by both the Norfolk & Western and the Chesapeake & Ohio railways. The city serves as a distributing centre for timber, stone, clay, oil, iron ore, and coal found in the surrounding country.

## Related Railway Systems
While the Norfolk & Western operates in the United States, other railways with similar naming conventions operate in different regions. In England, the London & South Western railway serves various locations, including Gillingham, Sherborne, and branches to Weymouth, Dorchester, Poole, Swanage, Wimborne, and Portland. In Ireland, the Great Southern & Western railway serves Cork and Queenstown, with branches extending to Youghal and toward the south-western coast from Mallow.
