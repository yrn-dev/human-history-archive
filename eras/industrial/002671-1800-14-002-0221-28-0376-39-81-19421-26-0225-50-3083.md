## Historical Figures

His father, Zachary Macaulay, served as the secretary to the chartered company that founded the colony of Sierra Leone in 1800; Zachary Macaulay had previously been the governor of that colony and was a philanthropist who worked toward the abolition of the slave trade.

In his youth, Thomas Babington Macaulay demonstrated a strong interest in literature. Before reaching the age of eight, he authored a romance titled *The Battle of Cheviot* in three cantos, as well as a *Compendium of Universal History*, which provided a connected overview of major events from the creation of the world up to the year 1800.
