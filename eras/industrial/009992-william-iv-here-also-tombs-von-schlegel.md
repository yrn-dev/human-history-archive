# William IV

## Frederick William IV and Bonn
Frederick William IV is noted for his contribution to the commemorative landscape of Bonn. In the cemetery located outside of the Sterntor, he placed a monument to the memory of B.G. Niebuhr, who is buried there. This cemetery also serves as the final resting place for several other figures, including the diplomatist Christian Karl von Bunsen, Robert Schumann, Karl Simrock, E.M. Arndt, A.W. von Schlegel, and the wife of Schiller.

## Other Figures Named William
The archive contains records of several other individuals named William across different historical and geographical contexts:

### Prussian and German Figures
*   **William I:** The King of Prussia, who crowned himself in 1861 in a chapel built in 1592 within the Schloss in Königsberg. He is commemorated with a monument in the grounds of the Schloss. Records also place him at Sedan and Ems, where a statue of him is located.
*   **William II:** The Kaiser, who is documented as having visited Metz and Douai, and having visited French prisoners. He is noted for his piety, his jubilee, and his efforts to avert war. He is also credited with the creation of a Navy.
*   **William, Landgrave of Hesse:** He was granted the abbey of Fulda as a principality by Gustavus Adolphus, though his rule over it lasted for only ten years.
