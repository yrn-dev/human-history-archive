# Edward Baines (1800-1890)

Sir Edward Baines was an English editor, proprietor, and politician who is noted for his professional contributions to journalism and his service in the British Parliament. He was the son of Edward Baines (1774-1848), a prominent newspaper proprietor and politician from Walton-le-Dale, Lancashire.

## Professional Career and Journalism
Sir Edward Baines served as the editor and later became the proprietor of the *Leeds Mercury*. This publication had been acquired by his father in 1801. Under the elder Baines, the *Mercury* had been transformed into a powerful organ of Liberal opinion in Leeds through the introduction of political articles and an improved system of reporting. The Baines family maintained a powerful influence through their connection with the paper for many years.

In addition to his work in journalism, Sir Edward was an author. His work *History of the Cotton Manufacture*, published in 1835, served as a standard authority for a long period.

## Political Life and Public Service
Baines entered politics as a Member of Parliament (M.P.) for Leeds, serving from 1859 to 1874. His political views aligned with those of his father, specifically in his strong advocacy for the separation of church and state. He also opposed government interference in national education. For his services, he was knighted in 1880.

## Family Background
Sir Edward was born into a family with significant political and legal involvement. His father, Edward Baines, had been an ardent advocate for parliamentary reform and served as a member of parliament from 1834 to 1841, having succeeded Macaulay in the seat for Leeds. 

Sir Edward had a brother, Matthew Talbot Baines (1790-1860), who pursued a legal career. Matthew Talbot Baines became the recorder of Hull in 1837 and served as M.P. for Hull starting in 1847. He held the position of president of the Poor Law Board twice: first in 1849 and again from 1852 until 1855, after being returned for Leeds. In 1856, he entered the cabinet as the chancellor of the duchy of Lancaster.

## Literary Contributions
In 1861, Sir Edward Baines wrote *Life*, a biographical work concerning his father.
