# North-Western and Lancashire & Yorkshire Railways

The London & North-Western and the Lancashire & Yorkshire railways are primary rail systems serving various municipal boroughs, market towns, and urban districts across Lancashire and the West Riding of Yorkshire in England. These lines provide critical infrastructure for industrial centers specializing in cotton, coal, and machinery, as well as for seaside resorts.

## Service in Lancashire

In Lancashire, the London & North-Western and Lancashire & Yorkshire railways frequently serve the same urban centers, sometimes through joint operations or separate stations.

### Ashton-under-Lyne and Bolton
Ashton-under-Lyne, a market town and parliamentary borough on the river Tame, is served by both the London & North-Western and the Lancashire & Yorkshire railways; the latter operates the Charlestown station. The town is a center for iron-founding, machinery works, hat-making, and cotton-spinning, with large collieries nearby.

Bolton (also known as Bolton-le-Moors) is a municipal, county, and parliamentary borough that maintains stations on both the London & North-Western and the Lancashire & Yorkshire railways. Additionally, the Midland railway possesses running powers in Bolton. The town's economy is heavily reliant on cotton mills, specifically fine spinning, as well as iron and steel works, collieries, and the manufacture of spinning and weaving machines.

### Lytham
The town of Lytham, an urban district and seaside watering-place on the north shore of the Ribble estuary, is located 13½ miles west of Preston. It is served by a joint line operated by the London & North-Western and Lancashire & Yorkshire railways.

### Other Lancashire Locations
Several other Lancashire towns are served specifically by the Lancashire & Yorkshire railway:
*   **Bury:** A market town and county borough on the river Irwell, where the railway operates alongside the Manchester & Bolton canal.
*   **Bacup:** A municipal borough in the Rossendale parliamentary division, situated in a narrow valley on the river Irwell.
*   **Darwen:** A municipal borough and center of the cotton trade, located 20 miles northwest of Manchester.

Additionally, the London & North-Western railway serves Denton, an urban district in the Gorton parliamentary division known for coal mining and the manufacture of felt hats.

## Service in the West Riding of Yorkshire

The Lancashire & Yorkshire railway extends its reach into the West Riding of Yorkshire, connecting several manufacturing and urban districts.

### Brighouse and Castleford
In the Elland parliamentary division, the Lancashire & Yorkshire railway serves Brighouse, a municipal borough on the river Calder. Brighouse is situated in the heart of the West Riding manufacturing district and contains large factories for worsted and woollen goods, as well as soap, machinery, and carpets.

In the Osgoldcross parliamentary division, the Lancashire & Yorkshire railway serves Castleford, an urban district on the river Aire. This location is also served by the North-Eastern railway. Castleford's economy is supported by chemical works, earthenware-jar and glass-bottle works, and nearby collieries.

### Doncaster
Doncaster, a market town and municipal borough on the river Don, serves as a major railway hub. While it is an important station for the Great Northern railway—which maintains its principal carriage and locomotive works there—it is also served by the Lancashire & Yorkshire railway, as well as the Midland, Great Central, Great Eastern, and North Eastern railways.
