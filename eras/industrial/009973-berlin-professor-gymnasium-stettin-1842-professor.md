# Hermann Bonitz

Hermann Bonitz (1814–1888) was a German scholar who achieved international recognition primarily as a commentator on the works of Aristotle and Plato. Born on July 29, 1814, in Langensalza, Saxony, he pursued his higher education at Leipzig, where he studied under G. Hermann, and at Berlin, where he studied under Lachmann and Bockh.

## Academic and Professional Career

Bonitz's professional life was characterized by a series of teaching and administrative roles across various German institutions. He began his career in 1836 as a teacher at the Blochmann institute in Dresden. He then moved to Berlin, where he served as an Oberlehrer at the Graues Kloster in 1840 and at the Friedrich-Wilhelms gymnasium in 1838. 

In 1842, he became a professor at the gymnasium at Stettin. By 1849, he had transitioned to higher education as a professor at the university of Vienna. His professional ascent continued with his appointment as a member of the imperial academy in 1854 and his appointment to the council of education in 1864. In 1867, he returned to the Graues Kloster gymnasium to serve as its director. Bonitz retired in 1888 and died in Berlin on July 25 of that same year.

## Contributions to Education

Beyond his scholarly research, Bonitz maintained a significant interest in the administration of higher education. He was the individual chiefly responsible for the system of examination and teaching that was implemented in the high schools of Prussia after 1882. Additionally, he contributed a large volume of writing on educational and classical subjects, primarily for the *Zeitschrift fur die osterreichischen Gymnasien*.

## Scholarly Works

Bonitz is best known outside of Germany for his critical commentaries on classical philosophy. His work on Plato includes *Disputationes Platonicae Duae* (1837) and *Platonische Studien*, the third edition of which was published in 1886.

His extensive research on Aristotle is evidenced by a wide array of publications, including:
* *Observations Criticae in Aristotelis Libros Metaphysicos* (1842)
* *Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis quae feruntur Magna Moralia et Ethica Eudemia* (1844)
* *Alexandri Aphrodisiensis Commentarius in Libras Metaphysicos Aristotelis* (1847)
* *Aristotelis Metaphysica* (1848–1849)
* *Uber die Kategorien des A.* (1853)
* *Aristotelische Studien* (1862–1867)
* *Index Aristotelicus* (1870)

In addition to his philosophical studies, Bonitz wrote on other classical topics, such as *Beitrage zur Erklarung des Thukydides* (1854), *des Sophokles* (1856–1857), and *Uber den Ursprung der homerischen Gedichte*, which reached its fifth edition in 1881.
