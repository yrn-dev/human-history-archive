# Schlueter--Baader's saemmtliche Werke

## Overview and Publication
*Baader's saemmtliche Werke* is a collection of the works of Franz Baader, published between 1851 and 1860. The collection consists of 16 volumes and is described as valuable. The publication was made possible through the efforts of a number of Baader's adherents who served as editors, including Schlueter, F. Hoffman, J. Hamberger, E. v. Schaden, Lutterbeck, and von Osten-Sacken.

## Volume Contents and Structure
The collection is structured to provide both the primary texts and critical scholarly apparatus. The editors prefixed several of the volumes with valuable introductions. Specific volumes contain the following specialized materials:
* **Volume XV**: Contains a full biography of the subject.
* **Volume XVI**: Contains an index and an able sketch of the whole system provided by Lutterbeck.

## Related Works and Surveys
Beyond the *saemmtliche Werke*, other significant publications and surveys regarding Baader's philosophy and life exist. F. Hoffmann published *Vorhalle zur spekulativen Lehre Baader's* (1836), *Grundzuege der Societaets-Philosophie Franz Baader's* (1837), *Philosophische Schriften* in three volumes (1868-1872), *Die Weltalter* (1868), and *Biographie und Briefwechsel* (Leipzig, 1887). 

Other contributors to the study of Baader include J. Hamberger, who wrote *Cardinalpunkte der Baaderschen Philosophie* (1855) and *Fundamentalbegriffe von F. B.'s Ethik, Politik, u. Religions-Philosophie* (1858), and J. A. B. Lutterbeck, who authored *Philosophische Standpunkte Baaders* (1854) and *Baaders Lehre vom Weltgebaeude* (1866).
