# Port of Buenos Aires and the Riachuelo Improvements

The port of Buenos Aires is characterized by its significant infrastructure developments, most notably the Madero docks, which were constructed to improve and enlarge the city's shipping facilities.

## Early Improvements at the Riachuelo
Initial efforts to enhance the port began in 1872 at the "Boca," which is the name given to the port located on the Riachuelo. During this phase of development, nearly £1,500,000 was expended on the construction of landing facilities and the dredging of a channel to deep water, which measured 12 meters in length.

## The Madero Docks
The improvements at the Riachuelo were eventually found to be insufficient for the port's needs. Consequently, work began in 1887 based on plans executed by Sir John Hawkshaw. This project involved the creation of two basins and a series of four docks situated in front of the city. These works occupied 3 meters of reclaimed shore-line and utilized two dredged channels to connect the facilities with deep water.

The infrastructure of the Madero docks includes:
*   Two dry docks located in the north basin.
*   24 warehouses.
*   Hydraulic cranes.
*   28 meters of railway sidings and connections.

## Costs and Financials
The total expenditure for the new port works up to the year 1908 was approximately £8,000,000 sterling, equivalent to $40,000,000 gold. Due to further required extensions, congress decided in September 1908 to borrow an additional £5,000,000. 

Maintenance is a significant ongoing expense because the river deposits a great quantity of silt, necessitating constant dredging of the channels to deep water. In 1908, an expenditure of £560,000 was voted for dredging and allied purposes.

## Port Traffic and Revenue
In 1907, the port recorded 29,178 shipping entries with an aggregate tonnage of 13,335,737. The movement of merchandise during that year consisted of 4,360,000 tons of imports and 2,900,000 tons of produce exports.

Financially, the port generated revenues of $5,452,000 gold in 1907. With working expenses totaling $2,213,000 gold, the resulting profit was $3,229,000, which represented approximately 8% of the construction cost.
