# Grosvenor

## Overview and Purpose
The Grosvenor is a reference library located in the city of Buffalo. Established in 1859, it serves as one of the city's important libraries, specifically designated for reference purposes.

## Collection Size
The library maintains a substantial collection of materials to support its reference function. Its holdings consist of approximately 75,000 volumes and 7,000 pamphlets.

## Context within Buffalo's Libraries
The Grosvenor is part of a broader network of libraries and educational resources in Buffalo. It exists alongside the Buffalo public library, which was founded in 1859 and contains about 300,000 books and pamphlets in a building valued at $1,000,000. Other notable libraries in the city include:
* The library of the Buffalo Historical Society (founded 1862), which holds 26,600 volumes and is located in Delaware Park.
* The John C. Lord library, which contains 10,620 volumes and is housed in the Historical Society building.
* The Law library (8th judicial district), containing 17,000 volumes.
* The Catholic Institute library, containing 12,000 volumes.

## Educational Environment
The presence of the Grosvenor library complements the city's extensive educational infrastructure. Buffalo supports various institutions, including the university of Buffalo (organized in 1845), which features schools of pharmacy (1886), law (1887), and dentistry (1892). Other academic institutions include Canisius College, a Jesuit institution for men established in 1870, and the Martin Luther Seminary, a theological seminary of the Evangelical Lutheran Church founded in 1854. The city also provides a state normal school, a city training school for teachers, and a system of kindergartens and night schools.
