# Prince's Dock

Prince's dock is a maritime facility located in Bombay. The foundation-stone for the dock was laid by the prince of Wales in 1875, and the facility was subsequently opened in 1879.

## Dimensions and Capacity
The dock measures 1460 feet in length and 1000 feet in breadth. It possesses a water area of 30 acres.

## Regional Context and Related Facilities
Prince's dock is part of the broader dockyard infrastructure in Bombay, which was originally constructed in 1736. The dockyard encompasses an area of approximately 200 acres and features a sea-face extending nearly 700 yards. The facility is equipped with electricity, allowing for operations to continue during both day and night.

The dockyard includes four building slips located on the south-east side of the enclosure, opposite the Apollo Bandar landing-place. Additionally, there are five graving docks; two of these combine to form a single dock 582 feet long, while the other three together create a large dock 648 feet long.

Other notable docks in the vicinity include:
* **Victoria dock**: Completed and opened between 1887 and 1888, with a water area of 25 acres.
* **Alexandra dock**: Located south of the Victoria dock, this is the largest dock in India. Its foundation-stone was laid by the prince of Wales in 1905.

## Geographical Advantages
Bombay is identified as the only important coastal location in India where the rise of the tide is sufficient to allow for the construction of docks on the largest scale. While the average tide is 14 feet, the highest spring tides in the area reach 17 feet.
