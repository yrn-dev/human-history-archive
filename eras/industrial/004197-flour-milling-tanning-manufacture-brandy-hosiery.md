# Flour-milling, Tanning, and the Manufacture of Brandy and Hosiery

The industrial activities of flour-milling, tanning, the production of brandy, and the manufacture of hosiery are distributed across various global commercial centers, ranging from the towns of France and Spain to the industrial hubs of England and Russia.

## Flour-milling
Flour-milling is a widespread industry appearing in numerous European regions. In France, it is a noted activity in the department of Aube, specifically in the towns of Bar-sur-Aube and Bar-sur-Seine, as well as in the department of Nievre in the town of Cosne. In the department of Marne, flour-mills are listed among the most important industries. In Spain, the town of Don Benito is involved in the manufacture of flour.

Other regions utilize different milling technologies or focus on specific grains. In Russia, the town of Kremenchug employs steam flour-mills and engages in the trade of rye-flour, as well as wheat, oats, buckwheat, and rye. In Scotland, the city of Dundee maintains corn and flour mills. In northern Europe, the city of Danzig has focused on the development of flour and oil mills as part of an effort to establish itself as a large-scale industrial center.

## Tanning and Leather Work
Tanning and related leather-working industries are prevalent in several European towns. In France, tanning is carried out in Cosne, Bar-sur-Aube, and Bar-sur-Seine. The department of Marne also contains tan-yards alongside establishments for leather-dressing and currying.

In the United Kingdom, tanneries are found in the city of Derby and the city of Dundee. In Dundee, tanning is complemented by the manufacture of boots, shoes, and other leather goods.

## Manufacture of Brandy
The production and trade of brandy are centered in several distinct regions. In France, the manufacture of brandy is carried on in Bar-sur-Aube and Bar-sur-Seine. In Spain, Don Benito is involved in the manufacture of brandy.

In northern Europe, the city of Danzig is famous for its brandy and liqueurs, and has fostered the development of distilleries. In Russia, the town of Kremenchug serves as a major commercial hub for brandy manufactured in the government of Kharkov, concentrating the trade for brandy destined for the governments of Taurida and Ekaterinoslav.

## Manufacture of Hosiery
The manufacture of hosiery is particularly prominent in the textile regions of England and France. In England, the city of Derby is noted for the manufacture of hosiery, an industry that benefited significantly from the inventions of Jedediah Strutt around 1750. Within the broader county of Derbyshire, hosiery and tape manufactories are located in various parts, including the county town and its neighborhood.

In France, the manufacture of hosiery is carried on in Bar-sur-Aube. Additionally, the neighborhood of Reims is noted for the manufacture of both cotton and woollen hosiery.
