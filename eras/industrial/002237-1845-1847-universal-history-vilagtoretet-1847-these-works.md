# The Works of Joseph Bajza (1843-1847)

The *Historical Library* (*Tortereti Konyvtar*), *Modern Plutarch* (*Uj Plutarch*), and *Universal History* (*Vilagtoretet*) are a series of historical publications authored by the Hungarian poet and critic Joseph Bajza. These works were produced during a period in Bajza's career when he devoted himself to historical writing.

## Authorship and Context
Joseph Bajza (1804-1858) was born in Szucsi. Before turning to history, he was established as a critic and poet, serving as the editor of Kisfaludy's *Aurora* from 1830 to 1837. He contributed to several publications, including the *Athenaeum*, the *Kritische Blaetter*, and the *Figyelmezo* (or *Observer*), and was recognized for his criticisms of dramatic art. In 1837, he was appointed director of the national theatre at Pest, after which he transitioned into the writing of historical texts.

## The Historical Works
Between 1843 and 1847, Bajza published three major historical projects:

*   **Historical Library (*Tortereti Konyvtar*)**: Published in six volumes between 1843 and 1845.
*   **Modern Plutarch (*Uj Plutarch*)**: Published between 1845 and 1847.
*   **Universal History (*Vilagtoretet*)**: Published in 1847.

These three works were, to some extent, translations from authors who wrote in German.

## Subsequent Career of the Author
Following the publication of the *Universal History* in 1847, Bajza's professional focus shifted toward political journalism. In 1847, he edited the opposition journal *Ellenoer* in Leipzig. By March 1848, he was appointed by Kossuth as the editor of the *Kossuth Hirlapja*. Bajza's later years were marked by illness; he was attacked by brain disease in 1850 and died in 1858.
