# Gold Exports of the Gold Coast (1902 and Following)

## Primary Source of Gold
In the years following 1902, the majority of the gold exported from the Gold Coast originated from the Obuassi mines. Gold deposits are located within the western districts of the country, where several companies were established to operate the mines between 1895 and 1901.

## Production and Value in Ashanti
Ashanti, a British possession in West Africa, is bounded by the river Volta to the east, the French Ivory Coast colony to the west, and the British Protectorate of the Northern Territories of the Gold Coast to the north. Its southern frontier meets the northern frontier of the Gold Coast colony.

In 1905, the gold output from Ashanti reached 68,259 oz., which held a value of L254,790. The region's revenue is derived primarily from contributions from gold-mining companies, as well as rents from government land, liquor licences, and caravan taxes.

## Infrastructure and Trade
The transport of minerals and other goods was aided by the construction of a railway from Sekondi to Kumasi, which was completed in 1903. This railway line passes through the auriferous region. Kumasi serves as the distributing centre for the hinterland and the entirety of Ashanti.

While Ashanti maintains considerable trade with its neighbours in Germany and France, as well as the Niger countries to the north, any trade moving southward through British territory is included in the figures for the Gold Coast. Consequently, the expenditure and revenue of Ashanti are also included in those of the Gold Coast.

## Other Commercial Exports
Beyond gold, the region exports various vegetable products. Gum copal is exported, and the nut of the kola tree is in high demand. Since 1905, cocoa plantations have been established, particularly in the eastern districts. In the northern regions, tobacco is cultivated. Additionally, the rubber tree (_Funtumia elastica_) is a commercially important product.
