# Essai sur la monnaie parisis and Note sur l'origine de la monnaie

## Overview
*Essai sur la monnaie parisis* (1874) and *Note sur l'origine de la monnaie tournoise* (1896) are numismatical works authored by Anatole Jean-Baptiste Antoine de Barthelemy. These publications contributed to Barthelemy's professional reputation as a numismatist and archaeologist.

## Author Biography
Anatole Jean-Baptiste Antoine de Barthelemy was born in Reims on July 1, 1821, and passed away on June 27, 1904, in Ville d'Avray. Throughout his career, he engaged in various historical and archaeological studies. In 1858, he collaborated with J. Geslin de Bourgogne to publish *Etudes sur la revolution en Bretagne*. Between 1855 and 1879, he produced an exhaustive six-volume work titled *Anciens eveches de Bretagne; histoire et monuments*. He also authored *Choix de documents inedits sur l'histoire de la ligue en Bretagne*, which was published in 1880.

## Numismatical Contributions
While Barthelemy produced various historical works, his reputation was primarily established through his numismatical research. This body of work included several popular publications and a variety of articles and monographs in technical reviews. His major contributions to the field include:

*   **General Manuals:** He wrote the *Nouveau manuel complet de numismatique ancienne* (first published in 1851, with a revised second edition in 1890) and the *Nouveau manuel complet de numismatique du moyen age et moderne* (1853), the latter of which was revised by Adrien Planchet.
*   **Specialized Monographs:** His specific studies on currency and coinage include *Numismatique merovingienne* (1865), the *Essai sur la monnaie parisis* (1874), and the *Note sur l'origine de la monnaie tournoise* (1896).
*   **Editorial Work:** In 1891, he edited the volume on *La Numismatique de la France* for the series of instructions issued by the *Comite des travaux historiques et scientifiques*.

## Professional Recognition
In recognition of his scholarly contributions, Barthelemy was elected as a member of the Academie des Inscriptions et Belles-lettres in 1897.
