# Museums and Cultural Institutions of Bonn

The city of Bonn contains several specialized museums and monuments that preserve its musical, artistic, and regional history. These institutions range from municipal collections to academic galleries and memorials dedicated to the city's most famous residents.

## Art and Provincial Museums
Bonn is home to an academic art museum established in 1884, which was a creation of F.G. Welcker and houses several classic originals. Additionally, the city maintains a municipal museum that has been arranged in a private house since 1882. 

For those interested in regional history and archaeology, the provincial museum is located near the railway station. This institution contains a collection of works of art and medieval stone monuments, and it also features a small picture gallery.

## Beethoven Memorials
Because the composer Beethoven was born in Bonn, the city has dedicated several sites to his memory. A statue of Beethoven was erected in the Munster-platz in 1845. Furthermore, in 1889, a museum of Beethoven relics was opened within the house where the composer was born.

## Other Monuments and Landmarks
The city's landscape is marked by various other monuments and historical structures:
* **Commemorative Statues:** The town features a statue of the Old Catholic bishop Reinkens and a marble monument commemorating the war of 1870-71.
* **Civic Features:** Bonn is adorned with a handsome fountain.
* **Religious Sites:** One of the most prominent features of the city when viewed from the river is the pilgrimage (monastic) church of Kreuzberg, built in 1627. Located above and behind Poppelsdorf, the church is known for a flight of 28 steps that pilgrims previously ascended on their knees.
* **Fortifications:** "Der alte Zoll" is the only remaining bulwark of the city's old fortifications and offers a magnificent view of the Siebengebirge. The Sterntor was removed to improve communication with the terminus of the light railway to Cologne and the growing western suburbs.

## Notable Burials
The cemetery outside of the Sterntor serves as the resting place for several prominent figures. This includes B.G. Niebuhr, for whom Frederick William IV placed a monument. Other tombs located here belong to:
* Robert Schumann
* A.W. von Schlegel
* Christian Karl von Bunsen (diplomatist)
* Karl Simrock
* E.M. Arndt
* The wife of Schiller
