# Instrumentenmacher

The term *Instrumentenmacher* refers to instrument makers, a subject addressed in technical literature from the early 19th century. Specifically, the archive contains references to instructional materials intended for these craftsmen, particularly regarding the development and construction of woodwind instruments.

## Technical Instructions for Instrument Makers
In 1826, Iwan Mueller published a work in Leipzig through the publisher Freidrich Hofmeister titled *Anweisung zu der neuen Clarinette und der Clarinette-alto, nebst einigen Bemerkungen fuer Instrumentenmacher*. This publication included illustrations and provided specific remarks and instructions directed toward instrument makers (*Instrumentenmacher*) concerning the new clarinet and the clarinet-alto.

## Related Woodwind Literature and Construction
The study of instrument making and the technical evolution of woodwinds is supported by several other historical texts. J. G. H. Backofen's *Anweisung zur Klarinette, nebst einer kurzen Abhandlung ueber das Basset-Horn*, published in Leipzig by Breitkopf & Haertel in 1803, provided illustrations and guidance on the clarinet and basset horn. Further technical and acoustic analysis of the clarinet's origin and development up to the modern era was documented by Wilhelm Altenburg in *Die Clarinette, ihre Entstehung und Entwickelung bis zur Jetztzeit in akustischer, technischer u. musikalischer Beziehung* (Heilbronn, 1904).

Additional theoretical perspectives on these instruments were provided by Gottfried Weber in "Ueber Clarinette und Bassethorn," published in *Cacilia* (Mainz, 1834).
