# Hermann Bonitz

Hermann Bonitz (1814–1888) was a German scholar and educator who gained international recognition as a commentator on the works of Aristotle and Plato. Born on July 29, 1814, in Langensalza, Saxony, he pursued his early studies at Berlin under Lachmann and Bockh, as well as at Leipzig under G. Hermann.

## Academic and Administrative Career

Bonitz held a variety of teaching and administrative positions throughout his professional life. His career began in 1836 as a teacher at the Blochmann institute in Dresden. He subsequently moved to Berlin, where he served as an Oberlehrer at the Graues Kloster in 1840 and at the Friedrich-Wilhelms gymnasium in 1838. In 1842, he was appointed professor at the gymnasium at Stettin.

His career later transitioned into higher education and governance. He became a professor at the university of Vienna in 1849 and was elected a member of the imperial academy in 1854. By 1864, he was a member of the council of education. He returned to the Graues Kloster gymnasium, serving as its director in 1867. Bonitz retired in 1888 and died in Berlin on July 25 of that same year.

## Contributions to Education

Beyond his scholarly writing, Bonitz maintained a significant interest in higher education. He was primarily responsible for the system of examination and teaching that was implemented in the high schools of Prussia after 1882. Additionally, he contributed extensively to the *Zeitschrift fur die osterreichischen Gymnasien*, writing on educational and classical subjects.

## Scholarly Works

While Bonitz wrote on various topics, he is best known outside of Germany for his commentaries on Plato and Aristotle.

### Works on Aristotle
Bonitz produced several critical studies on Aristotelian texts, including:
* *Observations Criticae in Aristotelis Libros Metaphysicos* (1842)
* *Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis quae feruntur Magna Moralia et Ethica Eudemia* (1844)
* *Alexandri Aphrodisiensis Commentarius in Libras Metaphysicos Aristotelis* (1847)
* *Aristotelis Metaphysica* (1848–1849)
* *Uber die Kategorien des A.* (1853)
* *Aristotelische Studien* (1862–1867)
* *Index Aristotelicus* (1870)

### Works on Plato and Other Subjects
His contributions to Platonism include *Disputationes Platonicae Duae* (1837) and *Platonische Studien*, the third edition of which was published in 1886. He also authored works on other classical subjects, such as *Beitrage zur Erklarung des Thukydides* (1854), *des Sophokles* (1856–1857), and *Uber den Ursprung der homerischen Gedichte*, which saw a fifth edition in 1881.
