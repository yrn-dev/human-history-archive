# Economizer

In the context of marine engineering, specifically regarding the Belleville water-tube boiler used in the British navy, the economizer is one of two primary components of the boiler system. The other component is the boiler proper.

## Design and Construction
The economizer is constructed similarly to the boiler proper, consisting of several sets of elements placed side by side. These elements are located in the uptake above the boiler. The space between the boiler proper and the economizer is designed to function as a combustion chamber.

Each element within the economizer is composed of a number of straight tubes. These tubes are made of seamless steel and are generally smaller and shorter than those found in the boiler proper, with an external diameter of approximately 2-3/4 inches.

## Assembly and Connectivity
The tubes of the economizer elements are connected at their ends via screwed joints to junction-boxes. These boxes are manufactured from malleable cast iron and are arranged vertically. Except for the boxes at the very top and bottom of the front of the boiler, each box connects the upper end of one tube to the lower end of the subsequent tube in the element.

The connectivity of the economizer is organized as follows:
* **Lower Boxes:** The lower boxes of the economizer elements are connected to a horizontal feed pipe.
* **Water Supply:** This horizontal feed pipe is kept supplied with water by a feed-pumping engine.
