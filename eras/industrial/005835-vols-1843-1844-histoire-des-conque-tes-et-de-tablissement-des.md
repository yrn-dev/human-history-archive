# Nouvelles recherches historiques sur la principauté française de Morée et ses hautes baronnies

## Overview
*Nouvelles recherches historiques sur la principauté française de Morée et ses hautes baronnies* is a two-volume historical work published between 1843 and 1844. It was authored by Jean Alexandre Buchon, a French scholar and ardent Liberal who was active in party struggles during the Restoration.

## Author and Research Context
Jean Alexandre Buchon (1791–1849) was born on May 21, 1791, in Menetou-Salon (Cher) and died on August 29, 1849. Buchon was deeply involved in the historical regeneration of his era. His academic interests were significantly shaped by his travels in the East and southern Italy, which led him to investigate medieval French settlements in those regions.

Buchon's work on the French presence in the East is characterized as having the value of original research, although his numerous publications are noted as not being thoroughly scholarly.

## Related Works on French Settlements
*Nouvelles recherches historiques sur la principauté française de Morée et ses hautes baronnies* is part of a broader body of work by Buchon concerning French domination and establishment in the East. Other related titles include:

*   *Recherches et matériaux pour servir a une histoire de la domination française dans les provinces démembrées de l'empire grec* (1840).
*   *Histoire des conquêtes et de l'établissement des Français dans les états de l'ancienne Grèce sous es Villehardouin* (1846), a work that remained unfinished.

## Other Contributions of Jean Alexandre Buchon
Beyond his research into the East, Buchon was a prolific publisher and scholar. Between 1822 and the following years, he traveled across Europe to gather materials for his 47-volume *Collection des chroniques nationales françaises écrites en langue vulgaire du XIII^e au XVI^e siècle* (1824–1829). 

Following the revolution of 1830, he founded the *Panthéon litteraire*, through which he published several collections, including:
*   *Choix de chroniques et mémoires sur l'histoire de France* (1836–1841).
*   *Choix des historiens grecs* (1837).
*   *Choix de monuments primitifs de l'église chrétienne* (1837).
*   *Chroniques étrangères relatives aux expéditions françaises pendant le XIII^e siècle* (1840).
*   *Choix d'ouvrages mystiques* (1843).
