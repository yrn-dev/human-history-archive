## Business Accounting and Financial Position
In the context of business management, a statement of affairs—such as a balance sheet—is used to determine the financial position of an entity. This is achieved by utilizing both personal accounts and real accounts. While personal accounts track transactions, real accounts record the acquisition and disposal of different classes of property. In these accounts, expenditure is recorded on the debit (Dr.) side and receipts on the credit (Cr.) side; the excess of debit entries over credit entries represents the value of the assets remaining as the property of the business.
