# Jaime Luciano Balmes

## Life and Political Activity
Jaime Luciano Balmes (1810–1848) was a Spanish philosopher and ecclesiastic who was born in Vich, Catalonia, on August 28, 1810. He passed away in the same location on July 9, 1848. 

Balmes was active in the political sphere of his time, where he attacked the regent Espartero. This conflict led to his exile. Following his return, he founded and served as the editor of *El Pensamiento de la Nacion*, a weekly publication characterized as Conservative and Catholic.

## Philosophical and Literary Works
Balmes is recognized as an eminent political writer and philosopher. His most significant fame is derived from his work *El Protestantismo comparado con el Catolicismo en sus relaciones con la Civilisacion Europea*, published in three volumes between 1842 and 1844 (with a 6th edition appearing in 1879 and an English translation published in London in 1849). In this text, Balmes provides a defense of Catholicism, arguing that it represents a spirit of order and obedience, which he contrasts with Protestantism, which he characterizes as a spirit of anarchy or revolt. Despite its argumentative strength, the work is noted to be of little value from a historical perspective.

In addition to his political writings, Balmes produced philosophical works that served as clear expositions of the scholastic system of thought. These include:
*   *Filosofia Fondamental* (4 vols., 1846), which was translated into English in two volumes by H. F. Brownson in New York in 1856.
*   *Curso de Filosofia Elemental* (4 vols., 1847), a work that Balmes translated into Latin for use within seminaries.
