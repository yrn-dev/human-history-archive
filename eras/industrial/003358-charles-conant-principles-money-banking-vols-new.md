# The Principles of Money and Banking

*The Principles of Money and Banking* is a two-volume work published in New York in 1905. It was authored by Charles Arthur Conant, a financial professional and writer who served as the Treasurer of the Morton Trust Co. in New York from 1902 to 1906.

## Author Biography
Charles Arthur Conant was an expert in banking and international finance. In 1903, he served as a member of the Commission on International Exchange of the United States. In addition to *The Principles of Money and Banking*, Conant authored another significant work titled *A History of Modern Banks of Issue*, which was published in New York in 1896.
