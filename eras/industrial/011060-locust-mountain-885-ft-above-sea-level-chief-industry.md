# Locust Mountain

## Geography and Location
Locust Mountain is a geographical feature located in Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania, in the United States. The mountain reaches an elevation of approximately 885 feet above sea-level. Built upon the slope of this mountain is the borough of Ashland.

## The Borough of Ashland
Ashland is situated roughly 100 miles northwest of Philadelphia and about 50 miles northeast of Harrisburg. The town was laid out in 1847 and was named in honor of the home of Henry Clay, located at Lexington, Kentucky. The municipality was later incorporated in 1857.

The borough's population has fluctuated over the early 20th century, recorded at 7,346 in 1890, 6,438 in 1900 (which included 969 foreign-born residents), and 6,855 in 1910. The municipality is responsible for the ownership and operation of its own waterworks.

## Industry and Infrastructure
The chief industry of the area is the mining of anthracite coal, which is conducted at several collieries located in the vicinity of the borough. 

Ashland is served by several transportation networks, including:
* The Philadelphia & Reading railways
* The Lehigh Valley railways
* The Shamokin & Mount Carmel Transit Company electric lines
* The Schuylkill Railway Company electric lines

## Regional Healthcare
Located one mile southeast of Ashland at Fountain Springs is a state hospital. Opened in 1883, this facility serves injured persons from the Anthracite Coal Region of Pennsylvania.
