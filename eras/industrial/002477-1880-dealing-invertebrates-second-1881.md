# Scientific and Academic Publications of 1880-1881

The years 1880 and 1881 marked the publication of several significant works across the fields of biology, zoology, archaeology, and oriental studies, as well as the release of various historical and religious translations.

## Biological and Zoological Research
Francis Maitland Balfour, a British biologist born in Edinburgh in 1851, published a major two-volume treatise titled *Comparative Embryology* during this period. The first volume, released in 1880, focused on Invertebrates, while the second volume, published in 1881, dealt with Vertebrates. This work was recognized as an original research contribution and an admirable digest of animal development observations from the preceding twenty-five years. Balfour's scientific approach was characterized by a logical sense that strictly separated hypothesis from proved fact.

In the field of zoology, Francis Trevelyan Buckland, an English zoologist and former government inspector of fisheries, published *Natural History of British Fishes* in 1881. Buckland, who had previously written *Fish Hatching* (1863) and *Logbook of a Fisherman* (1875), died on December 19, 1880.

## Archaeology and Ethnology
Adolph Francis Alphonse Bandelier, an American archaeologist born in Switzerland, began devoting himself to ethnological and archaeological work among the Indians of Mexico, South America, and the south-western United States after 1880. His research in this region, conducted primarily between 1880 and 1885, resulted in several publications. In 1881, he published a *Report of an Archaeological Tour in Mexico* (later published in 1884) and a *Historical Introduction to Studies among the Sedentary Indians of New Mexico, and Report on the Ruins of the Pueblo of Pecos*.

## Orientalism and Religious Texts
Joseph Derenbourg, a Franco-German orientalist, published *Opuscules et traités d'Abou-'l-Walîd* in 1880 in collaboration with his son, Hartwig. In 1881, they published *Deux Versions hébraïques du livre de Kalilâh et Dimnah*. Derenbourg was also noted for his contributions to the knowledge of Saadia and his work on the history and geography of Palestine.

The period also saw the release of several Buddhist translations within the "Sacred Books of the East" series. In 1880, Rhys Davids published volume I of the *Jataka* under the title *Buddhist Birth Stories*. The year 1881 saw the publication of *Buddhist Suttas* by Rhys Davids, the *Dhammapada* by Max Müller, and the *Sutta Nipata* by Fausböll. Additionally, the *Vinaya Texts* by Oldenberg and Rhys Davids began publication in 1881, spanning three volumes through 1885.

## Other Academic and Historical Works
Several other scholarly works were published during this timeframe:
* **Theology and Philosophy:** C.R. Hagenbach published *History of Doctrines* (vol. ii) in 1880. In 1881, publications regarding Duns Scotus included J. Muller's *Biographisches uber Duns Scotus*, W.J. Townsend's *The Great Schoolmen*, and K. Werner's *Die Scholastik des spateren Mittelalters* (vol. i).
* **History:** Otto Mejer published *Febronius, Weihbischof Johann Nikolaus von Hontheim und sein Widerruf* in 1880. A biography of the same subject by F. X. Kraus appeared in the *Allgemeine deutsche Biographie* in 1881.
* **Natural History:** Ernest Ingersoll published *Nests and Eggs of North American Birds* between 1880 and 1881.
