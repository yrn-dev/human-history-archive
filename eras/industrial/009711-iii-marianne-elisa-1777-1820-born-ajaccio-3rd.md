# MARIANNE ELISA (1777-1820)

## Early Life and Education
Marianne Elisa was born in Ajaccio on January 3, 1777. Through the efforts of her brothers, she was admitted to the establishment of St Cyr, located near Paris, where she studied as a "king's scholar." This period of her education ended in 1792 when the establishment was disrupted by revolutionists. Following this event, Napoleon took charge of her and returned her to Ajaccio.

## Marriage and Political Rise
Marianne Elisa remained in the south of France, sharing the fortunes of her family. On May 5, 1797, she married Felix Bacciochi, a Corsican who was well-connected. 

Following the foundation of the French empire in 1805, Napoleon granted her the principality of Piombino and, shortly thereafter, Lucca. In 1808, she acquired the grand duchy of Tuscany through her own importunities. During her tenure in these regions, her pride and ability allowed her to exert a significant influence over Italian affairs in general and the administration of her territories, particularly as her husband, Bacciochi, was considered almost a nullity.

## Later Years and Exile
The relationship between Marianne Elisa and Napoleon was frequently strained. Between 1813 and 1814, she provided abetment to Murat in his enterprises. After the fall of her brother, she retired from public life under the title of countess of Compignano. 

She first resided in Bologna and later moved to Santo Andrea, located near Trieste. She died in Santo Andrea on August 6, 1820.

## Family Connections
Marianne Elisa was one of several sisters. Her sister, Marie Pauline (1780-1825), was described as the most beautiful and gay member of the family; she married General Leclerc and later Prince Camillo Borghese, eventually receiving the title of duchess of Guastalla in 1806. Another sister, Maria Annunciata Caroline (1782-1839), married Joachim Murat in 1800. Caroline, who shared a strained relationship with Napoleon and associated herself with her husband's movements in 1814-1815, eventually lived in Trieste with Elisa before her own death.
