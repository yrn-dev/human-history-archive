# Sekondi and the Kumasi Railway

## Infrastructure and Geography
The railway connecting Sekondi to Kumasi was completed in 1903. This rail line is noted for passing through the auriferous region of the country. 

## Gold Mining and Production
Gold deposits are located in the western districts of the country. Between 1895 and 1901, several companies were established to operate the mines in this area. In 1902 and the years that followed, the majority of the gold exported from the Gold Coast originated from the Obuassi mines. By 1905, the gold output from Ashanti reached 68,259 oz., which held a value of L254,790.

## Economy and Trade of Ashanti
Kumasi serves as the distributing center for the hinterland and the entirety of Ashanti. While some Ashanti trade had previously been diverted to Assini, a French port, due to wars between England and the Ashanti, measures were implemented to improve trade between Cape Coast and Kumasi following the suppression of the 1900 revolt.

Ashanti maintains significant trade with its neighbors in Germany and France, as well as with the Niger countries to the north. Trade moving southward through British territory is included in the figures for the Gold Coast. Similarly, the expenditure and revenue of Ashanti are included in those of the Gold Coast. Revenue is primarily generated from the following sources:
* Contributions from gold-mining companies
* Rents from government land
* Liquor licences
* Caravan taxes

## Agricultural Products
The vegetable products of the region are similar to those found on the Gold Coast. The rubber tree (_Funtumia elastica_) is the most commercially important product. Other notable agricultural activities include:
* **Cocoa:** Many plantations were established starting in 1905, particularly in the eastern districts.
* **Kola:** The nut of the kola tree is in great demand.
* **Tobacco:** This is cultivated within the northern regions.
* **Gum copal:** This product is exported from the region.
