# BARTLETT, JOHN RUSSELL (1805-1886)

John Russell Bartlett was an American linguistic and historical student. He was born on October 23, 1805, in Providence, Rhode Island.

## Early Life and Professional Career
During the first eighteen years of his life, Bartlett resided in Kingston, Canada. Following this period, between 1824 and 1836, he held several positions in Providence, serving as a bank cashier, a book-keeper, and a clerk in a dry goods store. In 1836, he moved to New York City, where he worked as a bookseller for more than ten years before returning to Providence in 1850.

## Government Service and Explorations
From 1850 to 1853, Bartlett served as the United States commissioner for the survey of the boundary between Mexico and the United States. This work remained unfinished due to a lack of funds. Following the accession of President Franklin Pierce, Bartlett was superseded by another commissioner.

In 1851, Bartlett explored the locality of Casas Grandes, and his findings were later cited as a principal authority in volume IV of H.H. Bancroft's *The Native Races of the Pacific States of North America*. In 1854, he published a two-volume work titled *A Personal Narrative of Explorations and Incidents in Texas, New Mexico, California, Sonora and Chihuahua*, which provided significant historical and scientific material regarding the south-west.

## State Administration and Archival Work
Between 1855 and 1872, Bartlett held the office of secretary of state of Rhode Island. During his tenure in this role, he focused on the state's records, which he thoroughly classified and re-arranged. He also produced various compilations and bibliographies primarily concerning the history of Rhode Island.

## Linguistic Contributions
Bartlett is chiefly remembered for his *Dictionary of Americanisms*, published in 1848. This pioneer work remains his primary contribution to the subject of language. While later changes in dialect have deprived the dictionary of final authoritativeness or completeness, it continues to be of value to students of language.

John Russell Bartlett died in Providence on May 28, 1886.
