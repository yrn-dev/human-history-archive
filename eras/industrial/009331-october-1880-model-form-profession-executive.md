# Government of Bolivia

## Constitutional Framework
The government of Bolivia is established as a centralized or "unitarian" republic. While it is representative in form, it is described as being autocratic in several important particulars. The governing constitution in force as of 1908 was originally adopted on October 28, 1880, and is considered a model in its profession and form.

## The Executive Branch
The executive branch of the Bolivian government is presided over by a president and two vice-presidents. These officials are elected via direct popular vote for four-year terms. To prevent long-term consolidation of power, they are not eligible for re-election for the term immediately succeeding their own.

The president is supported by a cabinet consisting of five ministers of state. These ministers oversee the following portfolios:
* Foreign relations and worship
* Finance and industry
* Interior and fomento
* Justice and public instruction
* War and colonization

A system of accountability is integrated into the executive process; every executive act requires the countersignature of a minister of state. The signing minister is held responsible for the enforcement and character of the act and may be prosecuted before the supreme court if the act is found to be illegal or produces illegal effects.

## The Legislative Branch
Legislative power is held by a national congress composed of two houses: the Senate and the Chamber of Deputies.

* **The Senate:** This house consists of 16 members, with two representatives from each department. Senators are elected by direct popular vote for six-year terms, with one-third of the members retiring every two years.
* **The Chamber of Deputies:** This house consists of 72 members elected for four-year terms, with one-half of the members retiring every two years.

The two chambers possess separate and concurrent powers as defined by the constitution. Congress meets on an annual basis for sessions lasting sixty days, though these may be extended to ninety days. In the event of impeachment trials, the Chamber of Deputies acts as the prosecutor while the Senate serves as the court. Additionally, the Chamber of Deputies is responsible for electing the justices of the supreme court.

## Suffrage and Political Control
The right to vote is granted to male citizens based on age, literacy, and financial standing. To exercise suffrage, a citizen must be able to read and write and meet one of the following criteria:
* If single: twenty-one years of age or older.
* If married: eighteen years of age or older.
* Financial requirement: ownership of real estate or an annual income of 200 bolivianos (provided this income is not compensation for services as a servant).

Due to these requirements, the electoral body remains small. Consequently, the country is practically ruled by a political oligarchy, regardless of which political party holds power.
