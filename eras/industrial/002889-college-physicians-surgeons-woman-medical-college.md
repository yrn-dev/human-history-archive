## Professional Schools in Baltimore

## Medical Licensing and the College of Homoeopathic Physicians and Surgeons
In the Dominion of Canada, the licensing of physicians is generally controlled by the various provinces. A distinct system exists in the province of Quebec, which is the only province to maintain a separate homoeopathic board of examiners. This board is known as the College of Homoeopathic Physicians and Surgeons of Montreal and operates under the control of the Montreal Homoeopathic Association.

The association appoints three examiners annually. Those who successfully complete the examinations are granted a diploma from the college and are permitted to use the letters M.C.H.P.S. following their degree. Following the examination, a certificate of success is sent to the lieutenant-governor at Quebec. The lieutenant-governor may then grant a license to practice midwifery, physic, surgery, or any combination thereof, provided he is satisfied with the applicant's integrity, good morals, and loyalty. The provincial secretary has defined "loyalty" in this context to mean that the applicant must be a British subject. This represents the only government medical license currently issued within the British Empire; other licenses are issued by colleges of physicians and surgeons or provincial boards.
