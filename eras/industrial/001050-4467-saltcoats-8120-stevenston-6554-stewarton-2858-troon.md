# Saltcoats, Stevenston, Stewarton, and Troon

Saltcoats, Stevenston, Stewarton, and Troon are towns located within the south-western Scottish county of Ayrshire. This county is bounded by the Firth of Clyde to the west, Renfrewshire to the north, Lanarkshire and Dumfriesshire to the east, Kirkcudbrightshire to the south-east, and Wigtownshire to the south. Politically, the county is divided into North and South Ayrshire; the former comprises the district of Cunninghame, while the latter consists of Kyle and Carrick.

The broader county of Ayrshire had a population of 254,468 in 1901, with a density of 223 persons per square mile. Administratively, the county is a sheriffdom, with resident sheriffs-substitute located at Kilmarnock and Ayr. The region is under school-board jurisdiction, though it contains various voluntary schools. Secondary and technical education is supported by grants from borough and county councils, as well as contributions from the Kilmarnock Dairy School and the Glasgow and West of Scotland Technical College. Technical instruction is available at various local centres, covering subjects such as engineering, mining, gardening, plumbing, agriculture, and various arts and sciences.

## Industrial Activity
The towns of Saltcoats, Stevenston, Stewarton, and Troon contribute to the diverse industrial landscape of Ayrshire, which is the principal mining county in Scotland and possesses the second largest coalfield.

### Mining and Chemicals
Stevenston is identified as one of the chief coal districts of the county. Additionally, works have been established in the sandhills near Stevenston for the production of other explosives and dynamite.

### Manufacturing and Textiles
Stewarton is noted for the manufacture of hosiery, woollens, cotton, and other fabrics.

### Maritime Industry and Trade
Troon serves as one of the leading ports in the county. In addition to its role as a port, shipbuilding is conducted at Troon.
