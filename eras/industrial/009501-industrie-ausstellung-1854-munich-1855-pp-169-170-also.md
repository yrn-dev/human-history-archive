# Industrie-Ausstellung, 1854

The *Allgemeine deutsche Industrie-Ausstellung* (General German Industrial Exhibition) of 1854 was an event that produced significant documentation regarding the state of industry and technical arts, with official reports published in Munich in 1855.

## Musical Instruments and Acoustics
A primary component of the exhibition's records is the *Bericht der Beurtheilungscommission bei der Allg. deutschen Industrie-Ausstellung* (Report of the Judging Commission). Within this report, specifically in section 4 (or section iv), Dr. Emil Schafhautl authored an article focusing on musical instruments. This work, appearing on pages 169-170 of the 1855 Munich publication, serves as a reference for the study of musical instruments and their relationship to the laws of acoustics.

Research into these instruments also extended to the use of crooks, with V. Mahillon providing tables of crook lengths measured in metres.

## Brass Instruments and Regional Variations
The industrial and musical landscape of the mid-19th century showed distinct regional differences in the construction and use of brass instruments. The bombardon was a standard instrument used within the military bands of Austria. However, in Germany, the bombardon was superseded by the bass tuba. 

The German bass tuba differed slightly in its construction and form when compared to the bass tubas and bombardons utilized in other European nations, specifically in France, Belgium, Austria, and England.

## Related Academic Context
The period surrounding the 1854 exhibition saw a broader interest in the intersection of physics and music. Contemporary works, such as those by Friedr. Zamminer (1855), explored the relationship between musical instruments and acoustic laws. Other academic discourse of the time addressed the mechanics of instrument play; for example, some treatises claimed that different notes in the horn were produced by altering the distance of the lips, though such statements were characterized by some critics as vague and misleading.
