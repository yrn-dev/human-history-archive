# Paninis Grammatik mit Ubersetzung

## Overview and Publication
*Paninis Grammatik mit Ubersetzung* is a scholarly work published in Leipzig in 1887. It was authored by Otto von Bohtlingk (1815–1904), a distinguished German Sanskrit scholar of the 19th century whose contributions are considered of pre-eminent value to the fields of comparative philology and Indian philology.

## Development and Relation to Earlier Works
The 1887 publication was a republication of Bohtlingk's first major work, *Acht Bucher grammatischer Regeln*, which was originally published in Bonn between 1839 and 1840. The original edition served as a criticism of the philological methods employed by Franz Bopp. Bohtlingk returned to this work forty-seven years after its initial release to produce the Leipzig edition, which expanded the original text by including a complete translation.

## About the Author
Otto von Bohtlingk was born on May 30 (June 11 O.S.) 1815 in St Petersburg. His academic foundation began at the university of St Petersburg from 1833 to 1835, where he studied Oriental languages, with a particular focus on Sanskrit, Persian, and Arabic. He furthered his education in Germany, studying in Berlin and later in Bonn from 1839 to 1842.

Upon returning to St Petersburg in 1842, Bohtlingk became attached to the Royal Academy of Sciences and was elected an ordinary member of the society in 1855. His professional ascent included appointments as "Russian state councillor" in 1860 and later "privy councillor," a role that carried a title of nobility. In 1868, he moved to Jena, and in 1885, he relocated to Leipzig, where he lived until his death on April 1, 1904.

## Other Works by Bohtlingk
In addition to *Paninis Grammatik mit Ubersetzung*, Bohtlingk produced a wide array of philological texts:

*   **Dictionaries:** His *magnum opus* is the *Sanskrit-Worterbuch*. This massive project was completed over twenty-three years with the assistance of Albrecht Weber (born 1825) and Rudolf Roth (1821–1895). It was first published in seven volumes in St Petersburg between 1853 and 1875, with a new seven-volume edition appearing between 1879 and 1889.
*   **Grammars and Linguistic Studies:** He authored *Vopadevas Grammatik* (St Petersburg, 1847), *Uber die Sprache der Jakuten* (St Petersburg, 1851), and a treatise on Sanskrit accents titled *Uber den Accent im Sanskrit* (1843).
*   **Translations and Collections:** He published *Indische Spruche* (second edition in three parts, St Petersburg, 1870–1873), for which Blau published an index in Leipzig in 1893. In 1889, he produced both a translation of the *Brihadaranyaka-upanishad* and a translation and critical examination of the *Chhandogya-upanishad* in St Petersburg.
