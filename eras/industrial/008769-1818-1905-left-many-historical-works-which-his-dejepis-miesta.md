# Wenceslas Tomek

## Overview
Wenceslas Tomek (1818-1905) was a historian from Bohemia. He is recognized as one of the fruitful historic writers produced by the region.

## Major Works
Tomek authored numerous historical works throughout his career. Among these, his most significant contribution is the *Dejepis miesta Prahy*, which provides a history of the town of Prague.

## Context of Bohemian Historiography
Tomek's work exists within a broader tradition of Bohemian historical research. Other notable figures in the field include:

*   **Jaroslav Goll (b. 1846):** An author of many historical works, with a particular focus on the community of the Bohemian Brethren. Goll also co-founded the *Cesky Casopis Historicky* (Bohemian Historical Review) in 1895 alongside Dr. Anton Rezek.
*   **Professor Joseph Kalousek:** A writer on the early history of Bohemia and the author of a study on the ancient constitution of Bohemia, titled *Statni pravo*.
*   **Dr. Anton Rezek:** An author of important historical studies, many of which were published in the *Cesky Casopis Historicky* and the Journal of the Bohemian Museum.
*   **Dr. Vaclav Flajshans:** A publisher of studies concerning the writings and life of John Huss.
*   **Professors Pic and Niederle:** Authors of learned archaeological studies focusing on the earliest period of Bohemian history.

Other significant contributions to the history of Bohemia include Frantisek Palacky's *Geschichte von Bohmen* (1844-1867), which covers the period up to 1526, and Professor Adolf Bachmann's *Geschichte Bohmens* (vol. i in 1899, vol. ii in 1905), which also covers history up to 1526 but is noted for having a strongly marked German tendency. For the period following 1526, the works of Gindely and Rezek are cited. Additionally, Professor Ernest Denis provided a continuous account of Bohemian history starting from the beginning of the 15th century through several volumes, including *Jean Hus, et la guerre des Hussites* (1878).
