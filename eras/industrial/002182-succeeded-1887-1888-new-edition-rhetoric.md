# Publications and Works of 1887 and 1888

The years 1887 and 1888 saw a diverse array of literary, academic, and historical publications across various fields, including rhetoric, biography, classical studies, and political history.

## Academic and Rhetorical Works
During this period, Bain, who had resigned his professorship in 1880, continued to pursue a scheme of work mapped out in his earlier years. In 1887 and 1888, he released a new edition of the *Rhetoric*. Alongside this edition, he published *On Teaching English*, a book designed for the use of teachers that provided an exhaustive application of rhetorical principles to the criticism of style.

In the field of educational and philosophical writing, John Bascom served as the president of the University of Wisconsin and professor of mental and moral philosophy until 1887. Following this tenure, from 1887 to 1891, he served as a lecturer in sociology.

## Biographies and Historical Accounts
The late 1880s were marked by several significant biographical publications. In 1888, a biography of Henry Ward Beecher was published, authored by William C. Beecher, Rev. Samuel Scoville, and Mrs. H.W. Beecher. This followed a series of other lives of Beecher published in 1887, including works by Thomas W. Knox, Frank S. Child, Joseph Howard, Jr., T.W. Hanford, and a sketch of his career by Lyman Abbott and S.B. Halliday.

In the realm of political history, American journalist and diplomat John Bigelow published *France and the Confederate Navy* in 1888. This work provided an account of Bigelow's efforts to thwart a scheme in 1863 involving a French shipbuilder and Napoleon III, who were constructing corvettes and iron-clads for the Confederacy. Additionally, Bigelow produced annotated editions of Franklin's autobiographical writings and complete works between 1868 and 1888; the complete works were published in ten octavo volumes from 1887 to 1889.

Other historical works from this period include E. Dümmler's *Geschichte des ostfränkischen Reiches* (1887-1888) and O. Chenavas's *La Revolution de 1788 en Dauphine* (1888). In France, A. Prudhomme published *Histoire de Grenoble* in 1888, and J. Roman released a *Repertoire archeologique du departement des Hautes-Alpes* that same year.

## Classical and Literary Editions
The study of classical oratory and literature produced several editions during these years. Regarding the works of Lysias, editions of select speeches were published by A. Weidner in 1888 and by A. Westermann and W. Binder between 1887 and 1890. Additionally, a second edition of *Selections from the Attic Orators* by Jebb was released in 1888, and F. Blass published the second edition of *Die Attische Beredsamkeit* between 1887 and 1898.

In the field of drama and poetry, Norman MacColl edited *Select Plays of Calderón*, published in London in 1888, and E. Günther published the two-volume *Calderon und seine Werke* in Freiburg i. B. in 1888. In German literature, selected works of Immermann, edited by M. Koch in four volumes, were published between 1887 and 1888, while R. Fellner published *Geschichte einer deutschen Musterbühne* in 1888.
