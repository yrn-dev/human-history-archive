## Banking and Finance

## Religious Developments
The religion of Babiism was founded in Persia during 1844–1845 by Mirza 'Ali Muhammad of Shiraz, a young Sayyid under twenty-five years of age. In the Persian *Bayan*, the date of his "manifestation" (*zuhur*) is given as May 23, 1844. Mirza 'Ali Muhammad had previously been a disciple of Sayyid Kazim of Rasht, leader of the Shaykhis. This sect held the doctrine of *Rukn-i-rabi* ("the fourth support"), which posited that an intermediary—the "perfect Shi'ite"—must always exist between the faithful followers and the twelfth Imam. 

Mirza 'Ali Muhammad assumed the title *Bab* in this specific sense, referring to the "gates" through which the twelfth Imam communicated during the "minor occultation" (*Ghaybat-i-sughra*) between A.D. 874 and 940. He later assumed the higher title of *Nuqta* ("Point"), while the title of *Bab* was transferred to his disciple, Mulla Husayn of Bushrawayh.
