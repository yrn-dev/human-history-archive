# 1870

The year 1870 is associated with various developments across different fields, including architecture, agricultural science, mineralogy, the arts, and geopolitical conflict.

## Architecture and Infrastructure
In England, the town of Bolton (Bolton-le-Moors) in Lancashire saw the construction of its existing parish church at the site of St Peter, where a church had been present since the 12th century or earlier. The current building dates from 1870.

## Agricultural Science
In the field of bird incubation, John Champion of Berwick-on-Tweed is credited with instituting a system in 1870 that was viewed as a potential future standard for the practice. Champion's method involved using a large room featuring two heated flues, with eggs placed on a central table. The temperature of the room was managed through the personal supervision of the fire. This system was later refined and adopted in certain parts of America, intended to rival the success and scale of the "Mamals" system of incubation.

## Mineralogy and Natural History
In 1870, Nordenskiold discovered native iron at Ovifak on Disco Island. This material was brought to Sweden in 1871 as meteorites, with the heaviest nodule weighing more than 20 tons. This native iron frequently displayed Widmannstatten figures, similar to those found in iron meteorites, though it was determined to be of telluric origin.

## Arts and Literature
In Japan, the painter Kyosai, Sho-fu (1831–1889), produced the illustrated work *Yehon Taka-kagami* (Illustrations of Hawks), a five-volume set published in 1870 and subsequent years. Kyosai is recognized as the first political caricaturist of Japan and a significant successor to Hokusai.

## Conflict and Commemoration
The Franco-German War of 1870-71 is commemorated in the German village and summer resort of Kösen, located in the Prussian province of Saxony. On the adjacent Rudelsburg, which features a ruined castle, German students erected a monument to their comrades who died during this conflict. This site also contains memorials to Emperor William I and Bismarck.

## Mining and Discovery
The year 1870 marked the beginning of the discovery of chief mines in South Africa. Specifically, the Dutoitspan mine was discovered in September 1870, followed by the Bultfontein mine in November 1870. These discoveries preceded the finding of the Colesberg Kop (Kimberley) and De Beers mines in 1871.
