# Statistical Institutions and Applications

## The Budapest Communal Bureau of Statistics
The official publications of the Budapest Communal Bureau of Statistics have earned a reputation across Europe for their completeness. The bureau is noted for its fearless exposure of shortcomings, which has contributed to the progress of the town. Dr. Joseph de Körösy, the director of the institution, has produced separate works that have gained recognition in England, specifically for his discovery of the law of marital fertility, published by the Royal Society. Dr. de Körösy has also worked on the development of comparative international statistics, providing valuable comparative data in his publications *Bulletin annuel des finances des grandes villes* and *Statistique Internationale des grandes villes*.

## Statistical Applications in Book-keeping
In the field of book-keeping, the ledger serves as the center of all systems. While transactions are typically recorded first in "books of entry" or "subsidiary books" before being classified into the ledger, some subsidiary books are used exclusively for statistical purposes. These specific records are referred to as "auxiliary books" or "statistical books" and do not form the basis for subsequent entries into the ledger.

## Social Statistics and Illegitimacy
Statistical data has been used to examine the causes and rates of illegitimacy in European countries. Data indicates that the rate of illegitimacy is high in European countries where elementary education is most common, while it remains low in more illiterate regions, such as Brittany and Ireland.

## The Statistical Society and Charles Babbage
Charles Babbage (1792-1871), an English mathematician and mechanician, was instrumental in the founding of the Statistical Society in 1834. Babbage, who was the Lucasian professor of mathematics at Cambridge from 1828 to 1839, was deeply concerned with the errors introduced into astronomical and other calculations due to inaccuracies in the computation of tables. This interest led him to develop the principles of a calculating engine, a project to which he devoted many years and a portion of his private fortune.

## Other Statistical Records
In modern legal and enforcement contexts, statistics are utilized to track specific populations. For instance, the U.S. Customs and Border Protection maintains "CBP Enforcement Statistics," which include "Criminal Alien Statistics." In these records, a "criminal alien" is defined as an alien convicted of one or more crimes, whether abroad or in the United States, prior to being interdicted by the U.S. Border Patrol.
