## Graziadio Isaia Ascoli
Graziadio Isaia Ascoli (1820–1907) was an Italian philologist born in Gorz to a family of Jewish origin. From an early age, he demonstrated a significant talent for linguistics. His professional contributions began with the publication of *Studii orientali e linguistici* in 1854, and by 1860, he had been appointed as a professor of philology at Milan.

Ascoli's academic work spanned several linguistic fields, including the study of Semitic and Indo-European languages, as well as the gipsy language. However, his primary area of expertise was the study of Italian dialects. He was widely regarded as the preeminent authority on Italian linguistics, and his contribution to the 9th edition of the *Encyclopaedia Britannica* (revised for the 11th edition) served as the classic English exposition on the subject.

## The Archivio Glottologico Italiano
In 1873, Ascoli founded the *Archivio glottologico italiano*. This publication served as a vehicle for his own research and a broader scholarly community. Within this archive, he published his *Saggi Ladini*. In the years following its inception, the *Archivio glottologico italiano* became the primary organ for original scholarship regarding this subject.
