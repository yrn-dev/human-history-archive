# Zur Entwicklungsgeschichte der deutschen Grossbanken mit besonderer Rucksicht auf die Konzentrationsbestrebungen

## Overview
*Zur Entwicklungsgeschichte der deutschen Grossbanken mit besonderer Rucksicht auf die Konzentrationsbestrebungen* is a work authored by Dr. Riesser. Published in 1905, the text serves as a resource for the study of German banking.

## Context within German Banking Literature
The work by Dr. Riesser is situated among several key academic and historical texts concerning the banking systems of Germany and other nations. Other notable German banking authorities from the same era include:

*   **Dr. W. Scharling**, who wrote *Bank Politik* (1900) and a history and description of the operations of the Reichsbank covering the years 1876 to 1900.

*   **Dr. Felix Hecht**, who documented the Mannheimer banks from 1870 to 1900 in *Die Mannheimer Banken, 1870 bis 1900* (1902).
*   **Siegfried Buff**, author of *Das Kontokurrentgeschaft im deutschen Bankwerbe* (1904).
*   **G. M. Boissevain**, who wrote *Duitsche en Engelsche Deposito-Banken* (1905).
