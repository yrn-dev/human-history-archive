The Woburn Experimental Fruit Farm was established in 1894 at Ridgmont, near Woburn, Beds. Founded by Mr. Spencer U. Pickering and the Duke of Bedford, this 20-acre station aimed to improve the practice of the fruit industry and ascertain facts regarding fruit culture.
