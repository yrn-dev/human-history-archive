# Louis Philippe, comte de Bombelles

Louis Philippe, comte de Bombelles (1780–1843), was a diplomat who spent his professional life in the service of Austria. He was the son of Marc Marie, Marquis de Bombelles, a French ecclesiastic and diplomat who had served as the ambassador of France to Portugal and later became the bishop of Oberglogau in Prussia and the bishop of Amiens.

## Early Life and Career
Louis Philippe was born at Regensburg. He entered the diplomatic service of Austria, where he held several high-ranking positions. In 1814, he was appointed as the Austrian ambassador to Denmark. Following this tenure, he filled a similar diplomatic position at Dresden in 1816.

## Distinction from Other Historical Figures
The archive contains records of other individuals named Louis Philippe, most notably Louis Philippe I, King of the French (1773–1850). While the comte de Bombelles served the Austrian empire, Louis Philippe I was the eldest son of Louis Philip Joseph, duke of Orleans, and Louise Marie Adelaide de Bourbon. 

Louis Philippe I's life was marked by his involvement in the French Revolution, his command in the army of the North as a lieutenant-general, and his eventual ascent to the throne. He was the subject of a claim by Maria Stella, who alleged that he was not the son of the duke of Orleans but the child of an Italian constable named Lorenzo Chiappini. Louis Philippe I treated these claims with amused contempt.
