## Religious Teachings: Al-Balad (The City)
The archive contains sacred texts from the Qur'an, specifically Chapter 90, titled *Al-Balad* (The City, This Countryside), revealed at Makka. The text begins with an oath by the city and mentions the creation of man into "toil and struggle" or "distress."

The text presents a vision of life involving a choice between two paths. It describes a "path that is steep" (the Ascent), which consists of:
*   Setting a slave free.
*   Providing food during a day of hunger, specifically to an orphan with kinship ties or to a poor person lying in the dust.

Those who follow this path and believe, while exhorting others toward patience, compassion, and deeds of kindness, are described as the "Companions of the Right Hand." Conversely, those who reject the revelations and signs are termed the "unhappy Companions of the Left Hand," for whom fire will serve as an awning or be closed over them.
