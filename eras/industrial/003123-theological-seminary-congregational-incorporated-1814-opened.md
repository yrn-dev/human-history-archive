# Bangor Theological Seminary

The Bangor Theological Seminary is a Congregational institution located in Bangor, Maine, United States. It is one of the state institutions situated within the city, which also serves as the seat for the law school of the University of Maine and the Eastern Maine insane hospital and general hospital.

## History and Development
The seminary was incorporated in 1814. It first opened its doors at Hampden in 1816 before being removed to the city of Bangor in 1819. Over time, the institution expanded its academic authority, and in 1905, it was empowered to confer degrees in divinity.

## Location and Context
The seminary is located in Bangor, a city and port of entry that serves as the county-seat of Penobscot county. The city is positioned at the head of navigation on the Penobscot river and at the confluence of the Kenduskeag stream. Bangor is a significant trade center for an extensive agricultural district and is recognized as one of the largest lumber depots in the United States, while also shipping considerable quantities of ice.

The city's infrastructure includes the Maine Central railway and several important branches, as well as the Eastern Steamship line. In addition to the Bangor Theological Seminary, the city maintains various public facilities, including a public library, a city hall, an opera house, and a Federal building. It also hosts several charitable institutions, such as homes for aged men and women, a children's home, and a deaconesses' home.
