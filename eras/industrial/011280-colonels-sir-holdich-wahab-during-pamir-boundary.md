# Colonels Sir T.H. Holdich and R.A. Wahab during the Pamir Boundary

Colonel Sir T.H. Holdich and Colonel R.A. Wahab were British officers involved in significant geographical surveys and boundary commissions across Asia during the late 19th century. Their work in the Pamir region contributed to the triangulation and mapping of the highlands and surrounding territories.

## The Pamir Boundary Commission of 1895
During the Pamir Boundary Commission of 1895, Colonels Sir T.H. Holdich and R.A. Wahab were responsible for carrying the Pamir triangulation across the Hindu Kush. This technical geographical work provided a foundation for further exploration; subsequently, Captain H. Deasy of the 16th Lancers continued this triangulation into the sources of the Zarafshan and the plains of Kashgar.

## Other Geographical Contributions of Sir T.H. Holdich
Sir Thomas Hungerford Holdich, a Colonel in the Royal Engineers, held several prominent positions and conducted extensive surveys. From 1892 to 1898, he served as the Superintendent of Frontier Surveys in India. His career included serving as the H.M. Commissioner for the Persa-Beluch Boundary in 1896.

Holdich's mapping and research efforts extended across various regions:
* **Central Asia:** Between 1884 and 1886, during the Russo-Afghan Boundary Commission, Holdich, along with Sir Adelbert Talbot and Colonel St George Gore, mapped the Oxus regions and Afghan Turkestan.
* **Persia:** He produced a map of Persia (1:1,014,000) published between 1897 and 1899.
* **Makran and Baluchistan:** Holdich authored several survey reports and articles, including "Ethnographic and Historical Notes on Makran" (1892), "Antiquities, Ethnography, &c., of Las Bela and Makran" (1894), and "Ancient and Medieval Makran" (1896).
* **South America:** Beyond Asia, Sir Thomas Holdich conducted the surveys that led to the settlement of boundary disputes with Argentina, which were resolved by arbitration in 1902 and 1899.

Holdich was a Gold Medallist of the R.G.S. (London) in 1887 and authored works such as *The Gates of India* and *The Indian Borderland*.

## Geographical Contributions of Colonel R.A. Wahab
In addition to his work with the Pamir Boundary Commission, Colonel Wahab conducted surveys in Arabia. These surveys were carried out in connection with the determination of the boundary in the Aden hinterland. These efforts resulted in the acquisition of more exact geographical knowledge within that specific limited area.
