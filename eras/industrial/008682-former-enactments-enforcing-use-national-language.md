# National Language Enactments in Bohemia

## The General Diet of 1615
In 1615, Matthias convoked a general diet. This assembly was distinguished from standard meetings by the inclusion of representatives from the "lands of the Bohemian crown" in addition to the Bohemian representatives. During this meeting, the issue of nationality became the predominant concern, having previously receded to the background due to constant religious controversies.

## Reaffirmation of Language Laws
At the 1615 diet, former enactments that enforced the use of the national language were reaffirmed. Consequently, it was decreed that Bohemian was to be the "authorized" language of the country, meaning it served as the official language.

## Shift in Language Status
The linguistic landscape shifted following the battle of the White Hill and the subsequent execution of the leaders of the national movement on June 21, 1621. This period saw wholesale confiscation of lands from those who participated in the national movement, leading to the exile of nearly the entire ancient Bohemian nobility. The lands were instead acquired by adventurers from various countries, most of whom had served in the imperial army and were unfamiliar with the national language.

Due to these demographic changes, it became a necessity to alter the legal status of the language. It was eventually decreed that the German language would be granted equal right with the Bohemian language within all law-courts and government offices of the kingdom.
