# Carlsbad

## Overview and Mineral Springs
Carlsbad is located in Bohemia, a region that is one of the greatest manufacturing centres of Europe and ranks first among the Austrian provinces from an industrial perspective. Bohemia possesses more than two hundred mineral springs, though only a small number of these are utilized for medicinal purposes. Carlsbad is recognized as one of the most celebrated mineral springs in the world, alongside other notable springs such as Franzensbad, Marienbad, Teplitz-Schonau, and Bilin. Additionally, the spring of Giesshubl is situated near Carlsbad.

## Industrial Activity
The industrial landscape of Bohemia was characterized by enormous strides during the second half of the 19th century, aided by abundant water supplies, iron, and coal. Within this industrial framework, Carlsbad serves as a primary hub for the porcelain industry, which is concentrated in and around the town.

While Carlsbad is a center for porcelain, other industrial specializations are distributed across the Bohemian region. The glass industry, introduced from Venice in the 13th century, is located near the mountains to utilize fuel and minerals like silica; crystal-glass is specifically produced around Steinschonau and Haida. 

## Regional Textile Industry
The textile industry in Bohemia is considered to be in the front rank. This sector is primarily concentrated in the valley of the Lower Elbe and the north-east corner of Bohemia around Reichenberg. The specific distribution of textile manufacturing is as follows:
*   **Cloth:** Manufactured at Reichenberg.
*   **Linen:** Centered at Trautenau and Rumburg.
*   **Woollen Yarns:** Produced at Asch and Aussig.
*   **Lace:** Pursued as a home-industry in the Erzgebirge region, with its main center at Weipert.

Other specialized manufactures in the region include red fezes (Turkish caps) produced at Strakonitz.

## Other Bohemian Industries
Beyond porcelain and textiles, Bohemia maintains several other significant industrial branches:
*   **Metallurgy:** Concentrated around mines due to the abundance of iron and coal.
*   **Machinery:** Industrial and agricultural machinery are manufactured in Prague, Pilsen, and Reichenberg. Prague also hosts a large establishment for the production of railway rolling-stock.
*   **Chemicals:** Produced at Aussig and Prague.
*   **Miscellaneous:** Pencils are manufactured at Budweis, while musical instruments are produced at Schonbach and Graslitz. The region also engages in the production of leather, paper, calico-printing, and dyeing.
*   **Food and Beverage:** Sugar refining has seen great development, and the breweries produce beer that is appreciated globally.

The commercial development of these industries is supported by a system of good roads and an extensive railway network.
