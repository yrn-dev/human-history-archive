# Architectural Antiquities of Normandy and Related Works

## Architectural Antiquities of Normandy
The *Architectural Antiquities of Normandy* is a work published between 1825 and 1827. It featured illustrations provided by Pugin and was associated with Britton, who served as either the author or the editor of the publication.

## Associated Works of Britton
Britton was involved in the production of several other historical and architectural texts. These include the *Historical Account of Redcliffe Church, Bristol*, published in 1813, and the *Illustrations of Fonthill Abbey* from 1823. In 1830, he was associated with *Picturesque Antiquities of English Cities*. Additionally, between 1834 and 1836, Britton and Brayley jointly produced the *History of the Palace and Houses of Parliament at Westminster*. Beyond these books, Britton contributed extensively to other periodicals and the *Gentleman's Magazine*. His *Autobiography* was released in 1850, and a *Descriptive Account of his Literary Works* was later published by T.E. Jones, who served as his assistant.

## Pugin's Contributions to Carpentry
In addition to his illustrative work for the archive's records on Normandy, A.W. Pugin authored a publication titled *Details of Ancient Timber Houses*. This work is cited as one of the principal publications on the subject of carpentry.

## Other Works Concerning Normandy
The archive contains other references to Normandy across different disciplines. In the field of natural history, Jacques Amand Eudes Deslongchamps (1794-1867) was a French palaeontologist born in Caen, Normandy. Deslongchamps was a founder of the museum of natural history at Caen and the *Sociétié linnéenne de Normandie*, established in 1823. His research included papers on Jurassic mollusca, brachiopoda, *Poekilopleuron* (*Megalosaurus*), and *Teleosaurus*, the remains of which he discovered in a Caen quarry.

In the study of national costumes, the archive notes *La Normandie illustree*, a two-volume folio published in Nantes in 1854 by Felix Benoist. This work contains coloured lithographs of costumes created by Hyppolite Lalaine.
