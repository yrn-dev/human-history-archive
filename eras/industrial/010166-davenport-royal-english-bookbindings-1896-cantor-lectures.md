# C.J. Davenport and Bookbinding Literature

## Overview of C.J. Davenport's Works
C.J. Davenport is an author of several specialized texts concerning the history and art of bookbinding. His published contributions to the field include *Royal English Bookbindings*, published in 1896, and the *Cantor Lectures on Bookbinding*, published in 1898. Additionally, Davenport authored *English Embroidered Bookbindings* in 1899, as well as biographical works including the *Life of Thomas Berthelet* (1901) and the *Life of Samuel Mearne* (1906).

## Context within Bookbinding Authorities
The works of C.J. Davenport are cited as authorities in the study of bookbinding alongside various other historical and technical texts. These include works by W.Y. Fletcher, such as *English Bookbindings in the British Museum* (1895) and *Foreign Bookbindings in the British Museum* (1896), as well as H.B. Wheatley's *Remarkable Bindings in the British Museum* (1889). 

Other recognized authorities in the field include S.T. Prideaux, who wrote *Historical Sketch of Bookbinding* (1893), and H.P. Horne, author of *The Binding of Books* (1894). Technical and regional perspectives are provided by J.W. Zaehnsdorf in *The Art of Bookbinding* (1880), L. Gruel in *Manuel de l'amateur de relieures* (1887), O. Uzanne in *La Relieure moderne* (1887), and E. Thoinan in *Les Relieurs francais* (1893).
