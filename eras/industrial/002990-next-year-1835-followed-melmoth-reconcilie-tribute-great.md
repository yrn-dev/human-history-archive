# Events and Figures of 1835

The year 1835 was marked by significant developments in the arts, journalism, and colonial administration, as well as the passing of a prominent figure in the Italian operatic school.

## Operatic Developments and the Death of Bellini
In 1835, the Italian operatic scene saw the production of *I Puritani*, written by Vincenzo Bellini for the Italian opera in Paris. The work was created under some influence from French music. During this same period, the composer Donizetti traveled to Paris for the first time. His work *Marino Faliero* was produced there, but it failed to maintain its position against Bellini's *I Puritani*, which had been recently produced at the Theatre Italien. Following this failure in Paris, Donizetti moved to Naples, where he found consolation in the enormous success of *Lucia di Lammermoor*.

The year also marked the end of Vincenzo Bellini's life. Born in Catania, Sicily, in 1801, Bellini had established a European reputation early in his career with *Il Pirata* (1827). In 1833, he had traveled to England to accompany the singer Pasta. By 1834, he had accepted an invitation to write an opera for the national grand opera in Paris. While studying the cadence of French verse and the French language for this project, he was struck by a sudden illness. Bellini died at his villa in Puteaux, near Paris, on September 24, 1835.

## Journalism and the New York Herald
In the United States, 1835 saw the launch of a significant journalistic venture. On May 6, 1835, James Gordon Bennett published the first issue of the *New York Herald*. The paper was a small one-cent publication issued from a cellar, where Bennett served as the editor, proprietor, and salesman. Bennett operated the paper with a disclaimer of all politics, party, and principle.

The *New York Herald* achieved commercial success through Bennett's industry and the inclusion of personal gossip, scandal, and "spicy" correspondence. Bennett is credited with introducing many methods of the modern American reporter. On June 13, 1835, the paper published the first Wall Street financial article to appear in any American newspaper. Later that year, in December, the *Herald* printed a detailed and vivid account of a great fire in New York.

## Colonial Annexations and Scholarship
In 1835, the British expanded their holdings in the region of the Khasi Hills. While the Khasi Hills themselves had been conquered in 1833, the Jaintia Hills—which had previously been a petty Hindu principality—were annexed in 1835. The inhabitants of this area, known as Syntengs, were a cognate tribe to the Khasis and were subjected to a moderate income tax following the annexation.

In the field of oriental studies, the French scholar Eugène Burnouf was engaged in the publication of his *Commentaire sur le Yaçna*, one of the liturgical books of the Parses. This work was published between 1833 and 1835.
