## Biblical Chronology and Ancient History
The archive preserves calculations regarding the timelines of the Babylonians and the Israelites. According to Berossus, the Babylonians believed ten antediluvian kings reigned for 432,000 years. This period is equivalent to 86,400 *lustra* (units of 5 years). In contrast, the Hebrew date of the Flood is 1656 years, which equals 86,400 weeks.

Regarding the Israelites, the archive details the period from the Call of Abraham to the Exodus based on Hebrew texts:
*   **Call of Abraham to the birth of Isaac:** 25 years (Abraham was 100).
*   **Birth of Isaac to the birth of Esau and Jacob:** 60 years.
*   **Age of Jacob when entering Egypt:** 130 years.
*   **Sojourn of the Patriarchs in Canaan:** 215 years.
*   **Sojourn of the Israelites in Egypt:** 430 years (per Ex. xii. 40, 41).

The total period from the Call of Abraham to the Exodus is 645 years. When combined with the period from the Flood to the Call of Abraham (365 years) and the Creation of Man to the Flood (1656 years), the total time from the Creation of Man to the Exodus is 2666 years.

## Engineering and Resource Extraction
In the field of bridge erection, the archive notes that the Kuilenburg bridge in Holland, which has a span of 492 ft, was built using a timber staging that contained 5 tons of bolts and 81,000 cub. ft. of timber. The bridge superstructure itself weighed 2150 tons.

Regarding mineral resources in Louisiana, the archive records significant salt and sulphur deposits. Rock salt on Petite Anse Island is in places 1000 ft. thick, while deposits on Orange Island reach at least 1800 ft. In 1907, Louisiana's salt output was valued at $226,892. Sulphur deposits, found four to six hundred feet underground, were exploited using superheated water and hot air pressure. The value of Louisiana sulphur in 1907 was slightly over $5,000,000.

## Demographics and Governance
The archive contains demographic data for Belgium between 1880 and 1904. Total births rose from 171,864 in 1880 to 191,721 in 1904. During 1904, 12,887 births (6.7%) were illegitimate. The population increased from 5.5 million to 7 million in this period. Belgian residents abroad include an estimated 400,000 in France, 15,000 in Holland, 12,000 in Germany, and 4600 in Great Britain; in 1904, 1505 Belgians lived in the Congo State.

In Louisiana, the state board of health was organized in 1855. The state assessment grew from $301,215,222 in 1901 to $508,000,000 in 1907. As of April 1, 1908, the bonded debt of the state was $11,108,300.
