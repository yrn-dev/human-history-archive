# Thoinan and Uzanne

The archive contains references to the works of E. Thoinan and O. Uzanne, primarily within the context of bibliographic authorities for bookbinding, music history, and art.

## E. Thoinan
E. Thoinan is cited as an authority on the history of French bookbinding, specifically through the work *Les Relieurs francais*, published in 1893. 

Beyond the field of bookbinding, Ernest Thoinan has authored works concerning French musical history and instrument makers. In 1864, he published *Les Origines de la chapelle musique des souverains de France*. Additionally, he wrote a work regarding the Hotteterre and Chedeville families, described as celebrated makers of bassoons, hautbois, flutes, and musettes (*Les Hotteterre et les Chedeville, celebres facteurs de flutes, hautbois, bassons et musettes*, published in Paris in 1894). 

Regarding the latter work, archive notes suggest a point of scholarly contention concerning Thoinan's use of the term *musette*. It is noted that Thoinan may not have considered that the word *musette* could refer to the *dessus de bombarde* (also written as *musele*, *muset*, or *muse*), which is a small rustic hautbois appearing in ballads from the 14th, 15th, and 13th centuries.

## O. Uzanne
O. Uzanne is recognized in the archive's records as an author contributing to the study of bookbinding and art. In 1887, Uzanne published *La Relieure moderne*, which is listed as an authority for the study of bookbindings.

Uzanne also collaborated with Henri Frantz on a volume titled *Daumier and Gavarni*, published in London by *The Studio* in 1904. This work features a large selection of the artists' work and serves as a resource for the study of Daumier.
