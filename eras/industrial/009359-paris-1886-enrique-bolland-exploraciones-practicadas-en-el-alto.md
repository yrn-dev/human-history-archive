# Paris (1886) and Enrique Bolland

The year 1886 was marked by various intellectual, artistic, and literary publications in Paris, as well as the death of notable figures in the French arts. ## Artistic and Cultural Events
In 1886, the Impressionist group continued their series of exhibitions. These exhibitions, which began in 1874 at Nadar's on the Boulevard des Capucines, were subsequently held by the dealer Durand-Ruel in various galleries. The exhibition of 1886 is documented in the work *Les Impressionistes en 1886*, written by Félix-Fénéon and published in Paris that same year.

The city also saw the passing of Jean Baptiste Prosper Bressant on January 23, 1886. Bressant was a French actor born in 1815 who had a distinguished career beginning at the Variétés in Paris in 1833. After spending eight years at the French theatre in St Petersburg, he returned to Paris in 1846 to find success at the Gymnase, eventually becoming a *sociétaire* at the Comédie Française in 1854. He was known for roles in the classical répertoire and modern plays, including *Tartuffe*, *Don Juan*, and *Le Misanthrope*. He also served as a professor at the Conservatoire, where Mounet-Sully was one of his pupils.

## Enrique Bolland and South American Exploration
While Paris was a hub for the publication of exploration narratives, other researchers documented the geography of South America. Enrique Bolland authored the work *Exploraciones practicadas en el Alto Paraguay y en la Laguna Gaiba*, which was published in Buenos Aires in 1901. His work contributes to the broader body of geographical and industrial knowledge regarding the region, alongside other authorities such as M.V. Ballivian and G.E. Church.
