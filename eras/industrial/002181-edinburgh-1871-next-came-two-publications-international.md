# Edinburgh in 1871

During the year 1871, Edinburgh served as a center for academic recognition, scientific gathering, and the publication of historical and medical texts.

## Academic and Scientific Activity
The University of Edinburgh recognized the contributions of Bain to Scottish education in 1871 by conferring upon him the honorary degree of doctor of laws. Bain had previously been appointed to the chair of logic and English at the University of Aberdeen in 1860, where he worked to address deficiencies in those subjects and established a school of philosophy.

In August 1871, Edinburgh hosted a meeting of the British Association. During this event, Yule served as the President of the Geographical Section. Yule had arrived in the city following a period in Paris immediately after the suppression of the Commune.
