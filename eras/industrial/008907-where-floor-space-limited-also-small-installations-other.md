# Cylindrical Boilers for Limited Floor Space

In industrial and maritime applications where floor space is limited or for installations of a small size, specific forms of cylindrical boilers are utilized. These are predominantly of the vertical type.

## The Simple Vertical Boiler
The most commonly used variety is the simple vertical boiler. This design features a plain vertical fire-box and an internal smoke stack that traverses the steam space. The fire-box is constructed with a diameter that tapers slightly, and the area between the fire-box and the shell is filled with water.

In most versions, except for those of the smallest sizes, cross tubes are installed. These tubes are approximately 9 inches in diameter and are made from 3/8-inch plate. To allow them to be riveted to the fire-box plates, they are flanged at each end. These tubes are typically fitted with a slight inclination to help facilitate the circulation of water. For cleaning purposes, a hand-hole closed by a suitable door is located in the outer shell opposite each tube. This specific boiler design is frequently used for auxiliary purposes on board ships.

## Advanced Vertical Boiler Types
When a greater heating surface is required than what a cross-tube boiler can provide, other vertical boiler types are employed:

*   **The "Tyne" Boiler:** This model utilizes a hemispherical furnace. The products of combustion are directed into an upper combustion chamber. This chamber is traversed by several vertical water-tubes of a smaller diameter and four or more inclined water-tubes measuring about 9 inches in diameter.
*   **The "Victoria" Boiler:** Manufactured by Messrs Clarke, Chapman & Co., this boiler also features a hemispherical furnace. The furnace gases are led to an internal combustion chamber and then passed through numerous horizontal smoke-tubes into a smoke-box positioned on the side of the boiler.
*   **The "Cochran" Boiler:** Similar to the Victoria, the Cochran boiler features a combustion chamber with a "dry" back. Rather than having a water space at the back of the chamber, it is fitted with doors lined with firebrick, which allow for easy access to the ends of the tubes.
