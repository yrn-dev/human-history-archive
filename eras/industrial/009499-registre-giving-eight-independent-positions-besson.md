# Besson Registre

The Besson *Registre* is a musical instrument system designed to provide eight independent positions. It was developed as a solution to inherent defects in the construction of valve instruments, specifically regarding the accuracy of intonation.

## Technical Context and Purpose
In instruments such as the bombardon, a complete chromatic scale across the compass requires seven different positions or lengths of tubing, each with its own harmonic series. On a standard bombardon, these lengths are achieved through combinations of pistons. For example, using pistons No. 2 and 3 lowers the pitch by two tones, while using Nos. 1, 2, and 3 lowers it by three tones, and the combination of Nos. 1, 2, 3, and 4 lowers it by five and a half tones.

However, the use of piston combinations fails to produce intervals with absolutely correct intonation. This occurs because the length of tubing opened by a combination is not the theoretical length required to produce the specific interval. This defect is also present in the cornet. For a B[flat] cornet with a tube approximately 4 feet long, a semitone lowering requires an additional length of about 3 inches. If the instrument has already been lowered one tone to the key of A[flat], the tube length increases by some 6 inches; consequently, the 3-inch semitone piston no longer provides sufficient tubing to maintain correct intonation. In such cases, performers must vary lip tension to conceal these shortcomings.

## The Registre System
To remedy these intonation issues, various instrument makers developed ingenious contrivances. The Besson *Registre* is one such improvement, distinguished by providing eight independent positions. This approach is similar in intent to the six-valve independent system designed by Adolphe Sax around 1850, which used a separate valve for each position to avoid the need for piston combinations. While Sax's system was theoretically perfect, it was eventually abandoned due to practical difficulties, as its valves were ascending rather than descending and cut off lengths of the wind-way rather than adding to them.

## Related Innovations
The Besson *Registre* exists alongside other efforts to improve valve instrument intonation, including:
*   The Besson compensating system *Transpositeur*.
*   The Besson enharmonic valve system, which utilizes three pistons and six independent tuning slides.
*   The Boosey automatic compensating piston, invented by D.J. Blaikley.
*   V. Mahillon's automatic regulating pistons.
*   The bore developed by Rudall, Carte & Company (Klussmann's patent), which is conical throughout the open tube and includes additional lengths.
