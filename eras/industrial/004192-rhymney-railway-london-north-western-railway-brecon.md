# Rhymney Railway, London & North-Western Railway, and Brecon & Merthyr Railway

## The Rhymney Railway
The Rhymney railway was opened in 1858, establishing a route from Rhymney to Caerphilly and continuing on to Taff's Well. From Taff's Well, the railway utilized running powers over the Taff Vale railway to reach Cardiff. In 1871, the connectivity between Caerphilly and Cardiff was improved through the provision of a direct line, which included a tunnel under Cefn Onn measuring approximately 2000 yards in length. Further expansion occurred in 1894 with the opening of a 4-mile branch line to Senghenydd. 

In Caerphilly, the Rhymney railway shares a joint station with the Pontypridd and Newport railway, the latter of which was constructed in 1887. Additionally, a branch from the main line at Tyn-y-caeau provides a connection to the Rhymney railway.

## The London & North-Western Railway
The London & North-Western railway is an extensive system with operations spanning multiple regions and serving various industrial and commercial purposes.

### Regional Connectivity and Infrastructure
In England, the main line of the London & North-Western railway crosses the north-east part of Buckinghamshire, where Bletchley serves as an important junction. From Bletchley, branches diverge west to Banbury and Oxford, and east to Cambridge, Bedford, and Fenny Stratford; the western branch also serves Buckingham. Another branch exists from Cheddington to Aylesbury. The railway also serves Denton, an urban district in Lancashire located 4½ miles north-east of Stockport.

In the Midlands, the London & North-Western railway provides a route to Birmingham, which is situated 113 miles north-west of London on the loop line between Stafford and Rugby.

The railway's reach extends to Ireland, where it owns a line connecting the port of Greenore with the town of Dundalk. From Greenore, the company operates regular passenger steamers to Holyhead.

### Commercial Operations
The London & North-Western railway plays a significant role in the distribution of perishable goods to the metropolis of London. Due to favorable rates and railway facilities, milk can be transported from considerable distances to be delivered to retailers at a lower price than local production. At the beginning of the 20th century, the London & North-Western railway delivered 9,000,000 gallons of milk annually to London.

## The Brecon & Merthyr Railway
The Brecon & Merthyr railway operates in Wales, with a station located at Bedwas, approximately 2 miles east of Caerphilly. Like the Rhymney and London & North-Western railways, the Brecon & Merthyr railway is connected via a branch from the main line at Tyn-y-caeau.
