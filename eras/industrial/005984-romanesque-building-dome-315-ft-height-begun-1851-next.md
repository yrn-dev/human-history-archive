# Leopoldstadt Basilica

The Leopoldstadt Basilica is located in Pest and is described as the most magnificent church in that city. It is a Romanesque building characterized by a dome that reaches a height of 315 ft. Construction of the basilica began in 1851.

## Related Architecture in Pest
Following the Leopoldstadt Basilica in significance is the Franzstadt church. Like the basilica, the Franzstadt church is a Romanesque building, though it was erected later, in 1874. 

Budapest also contains several other notable religious structures. These include a beautiful synagogue built in the Moorish style in 1861, and another synagogue constructed in 1872 in the Moorish-Byzantine style. Additionally, the construction of a much larger synagogue was initiated in 1901.

## Other Ecclesiastical Buildings in the Region
In Buda, the oldest church is the Matthias church. Begun by King Bela IV in the 13th century, it was completed in the 15th century and underwent restoration between 1890 and 1896. During the Turkish occupation, this building served as a mosque; later, in 1867, it was the site of the coronation of Franz Joseph as king of Hungary. Other significant ecclesiastical buildings in Buda include the Reformed church, which was finished in 1898, and the garrison church, a 13th-century Gothic building.

Additionally, Buda contains a small octagonal Turkish mosque located near the Margaret bridge and the Kaiserbad. This monument features a dome 25 ft. high and houses the grave of a Turkish monk. The emperor of Austria committed to the preservation of this mosque through a special article in the treaty of Karlowitz of 1699.

In the inner town of Pest, the parish church situated in the Eskü-Ter (Schwur-Platz) is the oldest church in that area. Built in 1500 in the Gothic style, it was restored in 1890.
