# Franz von Baader's Works and Scholarship

The intellectual output of Franz von Baader has been preserved through extensive collections published by his adherents and analyzed by various historians of philosophy. His writings encompass a wide range of philosophical, ethical, and political inquiries, often categorized under the umbrella of "societaets-philosophie" (society philosophy).

## Collected Works and Biographies
The primary collection of Baader's writings, titled *Baader's saemmtliche Werke*, consists of 16 volumes published between 1851 and 1860. This collection was brought to publication by a group of his adherents, including F. Hoffman, J. Hamberger, E. v. Schaden, Lutterbeck, von Osten-Sacken, and Schlueter. These volumes are noted for containing valuable introductions provided by the editors. 

Specific volumes within the *saemmtliche Werke* serve specialized purposes: Volume XV provides a full biography of Baader, while Volume XVI includes an index and a sketch of his entire system authored by Lutterbeck. Additionally, a work titled *Biographie und Briefwechsel* was published in Leipzig in 1887.

## Major Philosophical Writings
Baader's bibliography includes several significant standalone works and thematic studies. Among these are:
*   *Die Weltalter* (1868)
*   *Philosophische Schriften*, published in three volumes between 1868 and 1872.
*   *Grundzuege der Societaets-Philosophie Franz Baader's* (1837)
*   *Vorhalle zur spekulativen Lehre Baader's* (1836), written by F. Hoffmann.

## Specialized Studies and Surveys
Various scholars have produced focused analyses of Baader's specific philosophical leanings. J. Hamberger authored *Cardinalpunkte der Baaderschen Philosophie* (1855) and *Fundamentalbegriffe von F. B.'s Ethik, Politik, u. Religions-Philosophie* (1858). J. A. B. Lutterbeck contributed *Philosophische Standpunkte Baaders* (1854) and *Baaders Lehre vom Weltgebaeude* (1866).

Further academic exploration of his work is found in the writings of Reichel, who wrote *Die Sozietaetsphilosophie Franz v. Baaders* (Tuebingen, 1901), and J. Claassen, who published *Franz von Baaders Leben und theosophische Werke* (Stuttgart, 1886-1887) and *Franz von Baaders Gedanken ueber Staat und Gesellschaft* (Guetersloh, 1890).

## Historical and Critical Overviews
Baader's place in the history of philosophy has been documented in several surveys. Notable accounts include:
*   **Erdmann**: Provided a survey in *Versuch einer Gesch. d. neuern Phil.* (iii. 2, pp. 583-636).
*   **Otto Pfleiderer**: Discussed Baader in *Philosophy of Religion* (vol. ii., English translation 1887).
*   **R. Falckenberg**: Included Baader in *History of Philosophy* (pp. 472-475, translated by A. C. Armstrong, New York, 1893).
*   **Kuno Fischer**: Wrote *Zur hundertjaehrigen Geburtstagfeier Baaders* (Erlangen, 1865).
