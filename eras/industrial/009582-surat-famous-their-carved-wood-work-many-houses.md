# Wood-Carving in Surat and Related Industries

Surat is recognized as a center of fame for its carved wood-work. This artistic tradition is part of a broader regional expertise in the area, as excellent examples of wood-carving also exist in Baroda, Broach, Nasik, and Yeola. In the neighboring city of Ahmedabad, the tradition is particularly visible in the architecture, where many houses are covered with elaborate wood-carving.

## Bombay Blackwood Furniture
Surat, along with Ahmedabad and the city of Bombay, is a primary site for the manufacture of "Bombay blackwood furniture." This extensive class of articles is crafted from Shisham or blackwood (*Dalbergia*), a dark-colored, hard-grained timber that can achieve a beautiful natural polish when treated properly.

The quality and style of this furniture vary. Some pieces are considered clumsy or inelegant in form, a characteristic experts such as Sir George Birdwood suggest is due to the use of original Dutch models. However, smaller items—including ornamental stands, small tables, and flower stands—are noted for their exceedingly graceful contours and are highly prized by collectors.

At its highest level, the carving found in this furniture is described as lace-like. The work is characterized by the ingenuity of the craftsmen, who adapt their detailed designs to the specific purpose of the article being fashioned.

## Craftsmanship and Traditions
The production of the most artistic Bombay furniture is carried out by a special class of workmen who rely on inherited traditions. These skills are passed down through generations; frequently, a craftsman knows only one specific design, which was transmitted to him by his father, who in turn received it from his own father.

While conventional patterns are still largely adhered to, there have been efforts under European auspices in recent years to modernize the industry. These efforts have seen a certain measure of success by integrating portions of native work into furniture of Western design.

## Related Wood-Work and Inlay
Beyond carved furniture, the region produces "Bombay boxes," which feature inlay in geometrical patterns. The materials used for this distinctive and peculiar effect include:
* Ebony
* Sandal wood
* Sapan wood
* Ivory
* Stags' horns
* Wire

## Other Local Manufactures
In addition to its fame for wood-carving, Surat is a center for the manufacture of silk goods. These materials are often decorated with woven or printed designs, though this branch of industry, along with others, has faced decline due to competition from European goods.
