# Elizabethan Library (1894)

The *Elizabethan Library* is a publication released in 1894 containing a small selection of the works of Fulke Greville. This selection was curated and published by Dr. A.B. Grosart, who had previously edited the complete works of Greville for the *Fuller Worthies Library* in 1870.

## Contents of the Volumes
The volumes within the *Elizabethan Library* comprise a diverse range of Greville's writings, including theoretical treatises, poetry, and personal correspondence. The included works are as follows:

*   **Treatises and Essays:** The collection features *Poems of Monarchy*, *A Treatise of Religion*, *A Treatie of Humane Learning*, *An Inquisition upon Fame and Honour*, and *A Treatie of Warres*.
*   **Poetry:** The library includes *Caelica in CX Sonnets*, which is a collection of lyrics written in various forms, as well as other minor poems.
*   **Correspondence and Speeches:** The volumes contain a letter addressed to an "Honourable Lady" and a letter sent to Grevill Varney in France. Additionally, it includes a short speech that was delivered on behalf of Francis Bacon.
*   **Introductory Material:** The publication features an introduction that incorporates some of the author's letters.
