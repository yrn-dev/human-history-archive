## Publications in Stuttgart (1886-1887)

In the years 1886 and 1887, Stuttgart was the location of publication for two distinct works. The first was *Franz von Baaders Leben und theosophische Werke*, written by J. Claassen. This work is noted as one of the most satisfactory surveys regarding the life and theosophical writings of Franz von Baader.

The second work published in Stuttgart during this timeframe was *Briefwechsel der Konigin Katharina und des Konigs Jerome von Westfalen mit Konig Friedrich von Wurttemberg*, authored by August von Schlossberger. ## Franz von Baader: Life and Philosophy

Franz Xaver von Baader (1765-1841) was a German theologian and philosopher born in Munich on March 27, 1765. He was the third son of F. P. Baader, who served as the court physician to the elector of Bavaria. Baader's siblings were also distinguished; his elder brother Clemens was an author, and his second brother, Joseph (1763-1835), was an engineer.

Baader's early academic pursuits were in medicine, which he studied in Vienna and Ingolstadt. After a brief period assisting his father in medical practice, he found the profession uncongenial and transitioned to the study of mining engineering under Abraham Gottlob Werner at Freiberg. This path led him to travel through mining districts in north Germany and reside in England from 1792 to 1796.

During his time in England, Baader encountered the ideas of Godwin, Hartley, and Hume, all of which he found extremely distasteful. Conversely, he found harmony in the mystical speculations of Saint Martin, Meister Eckhart, and especially Jakob Boehme. Upon returning to Germany in 1796, he settled in Hamburg and formed a long-term friendship with F. H. Jacobi.

Baader's later intellectual development was influenced by Schelling, though he is not considered a disciple of the philosopher. Their friendship lasted until approximately 1822, ending when Baader wrote a letter to Emperor Alexander I. of Russia denouncing modern philosophy, an act that alienated Schelling.

## Works and Legacy of Franz von Baader

The intellectual output of Franz von Baader was preserved and organized by several adherents, including Schlueter, von Osten-Sacken, Lutterbeck, E. v. Schaden, J. Hamberger, and F. Hoffman. Together, they published *Baader's saemmtliche Werke*, a 16-volume collection released between 1851 and 1860. This collection includes a full biography in volume xv, an index in volume xvi, and a sketch of Baader's entire system provided by Lutterbeck.

Other significant writings and analyses of his work include:
*   *Philosophische Schriften* (3 volumes, 1868-1872)
*   *Die Weltalter* (1868)
*   *Biographie und Briefwechsel* (Leipzig, 1887)
*   *Vorhalle zur spekulativen Lehre Baader's* (1836) and *Grundzuege der Societaets-Philosophie Franz Baader's* (1837) by F. Hoffmann.
*   *Cardinalpunkte der Baaderschen Philosophie* (1855) and *Fundamentalbegriffe von F. B.'s Ethik, Politik, u. Religions-Philosophie* (1858) by J. Hamberger.
*   *Philosophische Standpunkte Baaders* (1854) and *Baaders Lehre vom Weltgebaeude* (1866) by J. A. B. Lutterbeck.

## Gedanken ueber Staat und Gesellschaft

In addition to the works published in Stuttgart, J. Claassen authored another significant study on the philosopher titled *Franz von Baaders Gedanken ueber Staat und Gesellschaft*. This specific work was published in Guetersloh in 1890.
