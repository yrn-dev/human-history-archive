## Taxation and Trade
### Colonial Income Tax
In the Cape Colony, an income tax system was established by an act on May 31, 1904. Incomes up to £1000 per annum were exempt. Those between £1000 and £2000 were taxed 6d. in the pound on the amount exceeding £1000. For incomes between £2000 and £5000, the first £1000 was exempt, the next £1000 was taxed at 6d. in the pound, and the remainder at 9d. in the pound. For incomes exceeding £5000, the second £1000 was taxed at 6d., the next £3000 at 9d., and the remainder at 1s. in the pound.

In New South Wales, income tax began on January 1, 1896, with a rate of 6d. in the pound. Incomes of £200 per annum and under were exempt.
