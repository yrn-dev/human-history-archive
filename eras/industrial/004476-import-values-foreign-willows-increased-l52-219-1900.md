## Manufacturing Centres
Within England, the primary centres for basket manufacture outside of London are Grantham, Basford (near Nottingham), and Thurmaston (near Leicester). 

International production is also significant. Large quantities of light basket-work are produced in France, specifically in other parts of the country and in Verdun, located in the department of the Aisne, though these quantities for the English market have been decreasing. Furthermore, Germany, the Netherlands, and Belgium produce great quantities of other work and fancy work. Notable German production centres include Sonnefeld in Saxony, the Black Forest, and Lichtenfels in Bavaria.
