# Annual Reports and Statistical Documentation

Annual reports serve as primary repositories for statistical information across various governmental, financial, and administrative sectors. These documents provide detailed data on economic resources, population movements, fiscal policies, and industrial conditions.

## Financial and Trade Statistics
In the United States, the annual reports of the comptroller of the currency, published in Washington, contain significant statistical information. Similarly, in England and Wales, the annual reports of the Board of Trade provide elaborate statistical tables. These reports offer insights into the causes and features of current insolvency and the operation of the bankruptcy system. 

Data from the Board of Trade reports indicate that for the ten years ending December 31, 1905, there were 43,141 insolvencies handled under bankruptcy acts, with estimated assets of £26,001,417 and estimated liabilities of £61,685,678. This represents a decrease in average annual liabilities compared to the ten years ending 1883, which averaged £22,380,000 per annum, whereas the period ending 1905 averaged £6,168,567 per annum. Additionally, during the latter period, an annual average of 3,426 private arrangements were made outside of the Bankruptcy Acts, involving an estimated annual liability of £4,166,354. Official figures also show a considerable decrease in the cost of bankruptcy administration compared to the system under the act of 1869.

## Taxation and Revenue
Annual reports are essential for tracking taxation yields and rates. In Victoria, the rate of income tax is fixed annually by act. For 1905, the rate for income derived from personal exertion was 3d. in the pound for the first £500, 4d. for the second £500, 5d. for the third £500, and 6d. for incomes exceeding £1500. Income from property was charged double these rates. Companies were generally charged 7d. in the pound, while life insurance companies were charged 8d. Full statistics on the yield of these taxes are found in the annually published Reports of the Commissioners of His Majesty's Inland Revenue, with the 1870 and 1885 issues noted as especially interesting.

## Demography and Population Registers
The science of demography deals with the statistics of mortality, marriages, and births, as well as the physical, intellectual, physiological, and economical aspects of these events. While early definitions by Achille Guillard in 1855 focused on the progress and movement of populations in civilized countries based on registration reports and censuses, the term now broadly refers to the branch of statistics dealing with the life-conditions of peoples.

Historical population data has been derived from original registers of births, deaths, and marriages. For example, registers deposited in the Contrôlee General at Paris showed that between 1770 and 1774, the annual average of births in the kingdom of France was 928,918 (479,649 boys and 449,269 girls). In the province of French Hainault, an actual enumeration repeated annually from 1773 to 1776 showed an average of 257,097 inhabitants and 9,906 births. Using these figures, analogies suggested the kingdom of France contained between 23,222,950 and 24,151,868 persons.

## Regional and Resource Reports
Annual and biennial reports are used to document the physical and economic state of specific regions:

*   **Idaho:** Information on economic resources and physical features is found in the 18th and 20th Annual Reports of the U.S. Geological Survey, as well as reports from various state executive officers.
*   **Illinois:** Economic conditions are detailed in the Twelfth Census of the United States, while the U.S. Geological Survey's Annual Report (xviii) describes water resources. Valuable data is also provided by the reports of the Commissioners of Charity, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, and the Railroad and Ware House Commission.
*   **Louisiana:** The Annual Report of the Commissioners of Agriculture of the State of Louisiana (biennial until 1899) and the Annual Reports of the Railroad Commissioners of Louisiana provide data on agriculture and commerce. Additionally, the Annual Reports of the Chief of Engineers of the U.S. Army contain extensive material on river improvements and navigation of the Mississippi.
*   **Kentucky:** The state utilizes the Biennial Reports of the Bureau of Agriculture, Labor and Statistics, along with the Annual Reports of state boards and officers, to document its administration and resources.
*   **Honduras:** Official documents published in Spanish at Tegucigalpa include the annual presidential message and reports of the ministries. International data is provided by the annual British Foreign Office reports and monthly United States consular reports.
