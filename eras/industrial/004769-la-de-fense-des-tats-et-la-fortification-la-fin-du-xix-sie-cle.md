# La Défense des états et la fortification à la fin du XIX^e siècle

## Overview and Authorship
*La Défense des états et la fortification à la fin du XIX^e siècle* is a work published in Brussels in 1895. It was written by Brialmont, an engineer and the inspector-general who was responsible for drafting and executing the entire scheme for the defenses of Belgium. Brialmont was a prolific author in the field of military engineering, producing forty-nine pamphlets and twenty-three important works, in addition to various reviews, essays, and papers. In 1850, he also founded the *Journal de l'armée Belge*.

## Evolution of Fortification Theory
Brialmont's approach to fortification evolved significantly over the course of his career. In the initial stage, his engineering plans were closely aligned with the ideas of Vauban. This was evident in his original scheme for the fortification of Antwerp, which utilized a bastioned trace for both the forts and the enceinte.

By 1859, during the implementation of the great entrenched camp at Antwerp, Brialmont shifted his methodology. He moved away from Vauban's influence and adopted the ideas of Montalembert and the school of polygonal fortification.

## Impact of Technological Advancements
Approximately twenty years after his shift to polygonal fortification, Brialmont developed his own distinct types and plans. This development occurred during a period of general confusion regarding fortification, which was caused by two primary factors: the events of 1870-71 and the introduction of long-range guns.

Brialmont's final principles for fortification were applied to the fortifications on the Meuse at Namur and Liége, as well as the extreme detached forts of the Antwerp region. These final principles were characterized by three main elements:
* The multiplication of intermediate batteries.
* The suppression of all artillery positions that were open to overhead fire.
* The lavish use of armour to protect the artillery located inside the forts.

## Related Works by Brialmont
The publication of *La Défense des états et la fortification à la fin du XIX^e siècle* was part of a broader body of technical literature by Brialmont. Other significant publications include:
* *La Fortification du temps présent* (Brussels, 1885)
* *Influence du tir plongeant et des obus-torpilles sur la fortification* (Brussels, 1888)
* *Les Régions fortifiées* (Brussels, 1890)
* *Progrès de la défense des états et de la fortification permanente depuis Vauban* (Brussels, 1898)
