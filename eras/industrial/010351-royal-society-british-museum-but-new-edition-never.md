# Royal Society and the British Museum

The Royal Society and the British Museum serve as significant centers for research, the preservation of historical manuscripts, and the administration of scientific and cultural collections.

## The British Museum: Collections and Administration
The British Museum maintains an extensive array of historical documents, religious texts, and scientific specimens. Its administration has included figures such as Sir Edward Maunde Thompson, who served as the Director and Principal Librarian from 1888 to 1909. Thompson was also a Fellow of the British Academy and a corresponding member of the Royal Prussian Academy of Sciences and the Institute of France.

The museum's holdings include a vast collection of biblical manuscripts and early printed Bibles, such as:
* The *Codex Alexandrinus* and a probable Northumbrian copy of the *Codex Amiatinus*.
* A 9th-century Hebrew *Pentateuch*.
* A Vulgate manuscript written in A.D. 1148 for the monastery of Ste Marie de Parco, Louvain.
* A 13th-century Latin Bible previously belonging to Robert de Bello, abbot of St Augustine's, Canterbury.
* An early Wycliffite Version from the end of the 14th century, once belonging to Thomas of Woodstock, duke of Gloucester.
* The 42-Line Bible, printed at Mainz between 1452 and 1456.
* Tyndale's Quarto Edition of the New Testament (1525), of which only a fragment remains.
* The first printed English Bible (1535) and the first edition of the Authorized Version (1611).

Beyond religious texts, the museum preserves the papers of Samuel Butler (Addit. MSS. 32,625-6) and part of the correspondence of the French writer Pierre Desmaiseaux. It also holds manuscripts relevant to the poems of Dunbar, specifically Arundel 285 and MS. Cotton Vitellius A. xvi. (an appendix to Royal MSS. No. 58). Additionally, the *British Museum Catalogue* lists several works edited by De Morgan.

## Departmental Specializations
The British Museum houses specialized departments for various scientific disciplines. In the Department of Zoology, George A. Boulenger, an F.R.S. and Vice-President of the Zoological Society of London, was in charge of the Collections of Fishes and Reptiles.

## The Royal Society
The Royal Society is a learned society to which various scholars and savants have been elected. Pierre Desmaiseaux, a French writer and correspondent of continental savants, was elected as a fellow of the Royal Society in 1720. 

The society has also been the subject of literary satire; for instance, the work *The Elephant in the Moon*, written by Samuel Butler and later edited by Robert Thyer, is a satire directed at the Royal Society.

## Use of Libraries for Research
The libraries of both the Royal Society and the British Museum have been utilized by researchers to advance scientific treatises. Boole spent part of his final vacation in these libraries while attempting to produce a more complete second edition of his work, *Differential Equations*. However, this edition was never completed. Following his death, the manuscripts were found to be too incomplete for use in a second edition of the original treatise; consequently, Todhunter printed them in a supplementary volume in 1865.
