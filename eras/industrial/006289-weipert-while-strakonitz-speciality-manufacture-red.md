# Industrial Specializations of Bohemia

## Overview of Bohemian Industry
From an industrial perspective, Bohemia is one of Europe's greatest manufacturing centers and holds the first rank among the Austrian provinces. The region's industrial growth was accelerated during the last half of the 19th century, driven by an abundance of water supplies for motive power as well as rich deposits of iron and coal. These natural advantages allowed the inhabitants to make enormous strides in various sectors of production.

## Textile and Lace Manufacture
The textile industry is a leading sector in Bohemia, primarily concentrated in the valley of the Lower Elbe and the north-east corner of the region around Reichenberg. Different towns have developed specific specializations within this field:
*   **Reichenberg:** The center for cloth manufacture.
*   **Rumburg and Trautenau:** The centers for the linen industry.
*   **Aussig and Asch:** Locations where woollen yarns are produced.
*   **Weipert:** The principal center for lace, which is pursued as a home-industry in the Erzgebirge region.

Additionally, the town of Strakonitz possesses a specific specialty in the manufacture of red fezes, which are Turkish caps.

## Glass and Porcelain Production
The glass industry was introduced to Bohemia from Venice during the 13th century and grew to attain vast importance. Factories are typically situated near the mountains to utilize plentiful fuel and minerals, specifically silica. The finest crystal-glass is produced around Steinschonau and Haida. Parallel to this, a very extensive porcelain industry is concentrated in and around Carlsbad.

## Metallurgy and Machinery
Favored by the availability of coal and iron, metallurgic industries are concentrated around the mines. The production of industrial and agricultural machinery takes place in Prague, Pilsen, and Reichenberg. Prague also hosts a large establishment dedicated to the production of railway rolling-stock.

## Other Industrial Branches
Bohemia maintains several other significant industrial sectors:
*   **Chemicals:** Manufactured at Aussig and Prague.
*   **Pencils:** Produced at Budweis.
*   **Musical Instruments:** Manufactured at Schonbach and Graslitz.
*   **Sugar Refining:** A more recent industry that has seen very great development.
*   **Brewing:** The breweries produce a beer that is appreciated worldwide.
*   **Miscellaneous:** Other important branches include dyeing, leather, paper, and calico-printing.

## Commercial Infrastructure and Administration
The commercial development of the region has been stimulated by a system of good roads and an extensive railway system. Administratively, Bohemia is divided into ninety-four districts and two autonomous municipalities: Reichenberg and the capital, Prague. Other notable towns include Budweis, Pilsen, Aussig, and Weipert.
