The league was originally founded in 1888 by delegates from six Lutheran church societies in New York City. Its stated goals include encouraging the formation of young people's societies in Lutheran congregations, urging affiliation with state or territorial leagues, fostering loyalty to the church, and stimulating Christian activity. By 1906, the league reported 70,000 members, a figure that grew to over 100,000 by 1910.
