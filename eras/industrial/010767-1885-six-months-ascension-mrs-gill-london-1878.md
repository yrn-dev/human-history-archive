## Literary Publications and Authorship
In the field of English literature and stage history, 1885 marked the posthumous publication of *In and About Drury Lane*. This work served as a supplement to *Their Majesties' Servants*, a history of the English stage from Betterton to Kean published in 1860. Both works were authored by John Doran (1807–1878), a London-born author of Irish parentage who had previously served as the editor of *Notes and Queries* and a contributor to *The Athenaeum*.

## Academic and Professional Appointments
In the realm of electrical engineering and physics, 1885 was a year of personal transition for William Edward Ayrton (1847–1908). Having previously served as a professor of physics and telegraphy in Tokio and later as a professor of applied physics at the Finsbury College of the City and Guilds of London Technical Institute, Ayrton married Mrs. Hertha Ayrton in 1885. Hertha Ayrton would later become known for her own scientific research on the electric arc, for which she received a Royal medal from the Royal Society in 1906.
