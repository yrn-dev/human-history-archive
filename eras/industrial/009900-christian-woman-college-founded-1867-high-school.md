# Carlton College

## Overview and Affiliation
Carlton College is a woman's college located in Bonham, Texas. Founded in 1867, the institution is identified as Christian.

## Academic Standing
In addition to the college, the institution maintains a high school, which is noted as being one of the best in the state of Texas.

## Location and Setting
The college is situated in Bonham, which serves as the county-seat of Fannin county in the north-east part of Texas. The town is located approximately 70 miles north of Dallas and about 14 miles south of the Red river. 

Bonham functions as a shipping and trading center for a broad farming territory. This surrounding region is primarily dedicated to the growing of fruit, Indian corn, and cotton, as well as the raising of live-stock. The town's industrial infrastructure supports this agricultural activity through the presence of flour mills, large cotton compresses and gins, a large cotton mill, ice and canning factories, carriage works, planing mills, and railway repair shops.

## Town History and Infrastructure
The town of Bonham was first settled in 1836 and was incorporated in 1850, with a subsequent re-incorporation occurring in 1886. It was named in honor of J.B. Bonham, a native of South Carolina who was killed in the Alamo. 

The town is served by two railways: the Texas & Pacific and the Missouri, Kansas & Texas. Population records indicate that in 1890, the population was 3,361. By 1900, this number grew to 5,042 (including 1,223 negroes), before shifting to 4,844 in 1910.
