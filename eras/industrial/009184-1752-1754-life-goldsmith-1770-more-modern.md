## Academic and Scientific Publications of 1752 and 1754

The years 1752 and 1754 were productive for several scholars and writers:

**1752**

*   **Geography:** Anton Friedrich Büsching, a German theologian and geographer, published his *Description of the Counties of Schleswig and Holstein*.
*   **History:** Thomas Carte, an English historian and non-juror, published the third volume of his *Central History of England*.

**1754**
*   **Botany and Psychology:** Bonnet published two significant works in 1754. The first, *Recherches sur l'usage des feuilles dans les plantes*, explored the use of leaves and suggested that plants possess powers of discernment and sensation. The second, *Essai de psychologie*, was published anonymously in London.
*   **History:** Thomas Carte continued his work on the *Central History of England*, though the fourth volume of this series did not appear until 1755.
*   **Geography:** Anton Friedrich Büsching moved to Göttingen in 1754.
