## Athletics and Organized Sports
In the realm of amateur base-ball, 1887 marked the formation of the Eastern College League. This organization included Yale, Princeton, Harvard, and Columbia. ### Dairy Products
The period between 1887 and 1896 saw a rapid expansion in the import of butter, with the quantity doubling over those nine years. In 1887, the imports of cheese exceeded those of butter, a trend that continued in 1886, 1888, 1890, and 1892, before butter imports became the larger of the two.
