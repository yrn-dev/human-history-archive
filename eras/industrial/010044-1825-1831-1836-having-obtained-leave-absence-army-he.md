# 1825

The year 1825 is associated with various developments across military, academic, financial, and professional sectors, as well as specific events in the lives of notable historical figures.

## Military and Exploration
In the United States, Benjamin L.E. Bonneville, a military engineer born in France around 1795 and a graduate of West Point (1815), attained the rank of captain of infantry in 1825. Bonneville had previously been involved in the construction of military roads in the south-west. Later, between 1831 and 1836, he took a leave of absence from the army to lead an exploring expedition to the Rocky Mountains. This journey took him up the Platte river through areas that later became Wyoming and Colorado, extending into California and the Great Salt Lake basin. Following several years of isolation from civilization and the removal of his name from the army list, he published a two-volume account of his adventures in 1837, titled *The Rocky Mountains: or Scenes, Incidents, and Adventures in the Far West*, which was edited and amplified by Washington Irving.

## Academic and Professional Developments
In the field of mathematics, Augustus De Morgan, who had entered Trinity College, Cambridge, at the age of sixteen and a half, gained a Trinity scholarship in 1825. De Morgan later took fourth place in the mathematical tripos in 1827. Due to his conscientious objection to signing theological tests required for fellows and masters of arts at Cambridge, he was barred from obtaining a fellowship or an M.A. degree. This led him to enter Lincoln's Inn before becoming a professor of mathematics at the university of London (University College) at age twenty-two.

In the legal profession, the Incorporated Law Society was established in 1825 and the subsequent years to address the lack of professional organization in the education of solicitors. While statute had required examinations for attorneys and solicitors since 1729, these had become empty forms. The Law Society subsequently introduced lectures in 1833 and regular examinations in 1836.

## Finance and Botany
The year 1825 saw the establishment of two Irish joint-stock banks: the Provincial Bank and the Northern Banking Co. As of 1906, the Provincial Bank had a paid-up capital of L540,000 and a dividend rate of 20 per annum, while the Northern Banking Co. had a paid-up capital of L500,000 and a dividend rate of 18 1/2 per annum.

In the field of botany, the *Catalogue of Plants in the Glasgow Botanic Garden* was published in 1825.

## International Relations and Law
In Central America, the British government engaged in the drafting of treaties with New Granada in 1825. This was part of a broader effort to secure British positions following the independence of Central America (1819-1822), which also included a new treaty with Mexico in 1826 and drafts with the United States of Central America in 1831. These diplomatic efforts contributed to the eventual confirmation of British legal sovereignty over the colony of Belize.
