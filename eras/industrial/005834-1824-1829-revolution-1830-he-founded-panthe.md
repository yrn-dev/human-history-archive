# Jean Alexandre Buchon

Jean Alexandre Buchon (1791–1849) was a French scholar and ardent Liberal who contributed significantly to the historical regeneration of France during the early 19th century. Born on May 21, 1791, in Menetou-Salon (Cher), Buchon was actively involved in party struggles during the Restoration period.

## Historical Research and Publications
Beginning in 1822 and continuing for several years, Buchon traveled across Europe to gather materials for his extensive work, the *Collection des chroniques nationales françaises écrites en langue vulgaire du XIII^e au XVI^e siècle*. This massive project resulted in the publication of 47 volumes between 1824 and 1829.

Following the revolution of 1830, Buchon founded the *Panthéon littéraire*. Through this venue, he published a variety of specialized collections, including:
* *Choix de monuments primitifs de l'église chrétienne* (1837)
* *Choix des historiens grecs* (1837)
* *Choix de chroniques et mémoires sur l'histoire de France* (1836–1841)
* *Chroniques étrangères relatives aux expéditions françaises pendant le XIII^e siècle* (1840)
* *Choix d'ouvrages mystiques* (1843)

## Studies on French Settlements in the East
Buchon's travels in the East and southern Italy led him to investigate medieval French settlements in those regions. He produced several important works on this subject, focusing on French domination and establishment in the former Greek empire. These include *Recherches et matériaux pour servir a une histoire de la domination française dans les provinces démembrées de l'empire grec* (1840) and a two-volume work titled *Nouvelles recherches historiques sur la principauté française de Morée et ses hautes baronnies* (1843–1844). He also began a work titled *Histoire des conquêtes et de l'établissement des Français dans les états de l'ancienne Grèce sous es Villehardouin* (1846), though this remained unfinished.

## Scholarly Legacy
While the numerous publications produced by Buchon are not described as being thoroughly scholarly, they provided great service to the field of history. His research concerning the East is noted for having particular value as original research. Buchon died on August 29, 1849.
