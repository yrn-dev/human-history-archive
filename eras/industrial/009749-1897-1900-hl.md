## Trade in Dairy Products
During this timeframe, the United Kingdom experienced a continuous increase in the import of condensed milk. In 1897, imports stood at 756,243 cwt., rising to 817,274 cwt. in 1898, 824,599 cwt. in 1899, and reaching 986,741 cwt. by 1900. This growth contributed to a broader trend where imports in 1900 were nearly three times the amount seen in 1889. While the quantity of condensed milk trebled over the 1889-1900 period, its value doubled, advancing from L704,849 to L1,405,033.

A portion of these imports was subsequently exported. Between 1897 and 1900, the quantity of imported condensed milk exported was 143,932 cwt. (L274,578) in 1897, 133,596 cwt. (L256,525) in 1898, 118,394 cwt. (L228,446) in 1899, and 164,602 cwt. (L309,460) in 1900. Simultaneously, the United Kingdom exported home-made condensed milk. These exports grew from 154,901 cwt. (L302,748) in 1897 to 209,447 cwt. (L390,559) in 1900.

## Agriculture and Horticulture
The extent of orchards in Great Britain grew steadily during this period. In 1897, there were 224,116 acres, increasing to 226,059 in 1898, 228,603 in 1899, and 232,129 in 1900. In England specifically, the area under orchards rose from 218,261 acres in 1897 to 226,164 acres in 1900. During the same years, Wales saw orchard areas fluctuate between 3,666 and 3,707 acres, while Scotland's orchard areas increased from 2,148 acres in 1897 to 2,270 acres in 1900.

In the hop-growing sector, acreage in several English counties showed a general decline or fluctuation. In Kent, acreage dropped from 31,661 in 1897 to 31,514 in 1900.
