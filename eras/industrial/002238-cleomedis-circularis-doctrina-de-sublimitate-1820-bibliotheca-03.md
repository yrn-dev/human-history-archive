# Cleomedis Circularis Doctrina de Sublimitate (1820)

## Authorship and Context
The text was authored by Jan Bake (1787–1864), who was born in Leiden on September 1, 1787. Bake served as a professor of Greek and Roman literature at the university from 1817 until 1854. He died on March 26, 1864. 

Bake's academic contributions were extensive, and his services to classical literature were later appreciated by L. Mueller in the 1869 work *Geschichte der klassischen Philologie in den Niederlanden*. A biography of Bake was also written in Dutch in 1865 by his pupil, Bakhuizen van der Brink.
