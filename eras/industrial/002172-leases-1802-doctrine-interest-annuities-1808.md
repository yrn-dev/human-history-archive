# Tables for the Purchasing and Renewing of Leases and The Doctrine of Interest and Annuities

## Overview
*Tables for the Purchasing and Renewing of Leases* (1802) and *The Doctrine of Interest and Annuities* (1808) are specialized publications authored by Francis Baily. These works, along with his 1810 publication *The Doctrine of Life-Annuities and Assurances*, established Baily's high reputation as a writer focusing on life-contingencies.

## Author Biography
Francis Baily was an English astronomer born on April 28, 1774, in Newbury, Berkshire. Early in his life, between 1796 and 1797, he conducted a tour of the unsettled regions of North America; his journal from this period was later edited by Augustus de Morgan in 1856. 

In 1799, Baily entered the London Stock Exchange. Through a combination of integrity and diligence, he amassed a fortune, which allowed him to retire from business in 1825. Following his retirement, he devoted himself entirely to the study of astronomy.

## Contributions to Astronomy
Baily was a significant figure in the astronomical community. In 1820, he played a leading role in founding the Royal Astronomical Society. In 1827, the society awarded him its gold medal for his work in preparing the Astronomical Society's Catalogue of 2881 stars.

His further contributions to the field include:
* **Nautical Almanac:** His protests in 1829 initiated the reform of this publication.
* **Star Catalogues:** In 1837, he recommended and largely executed the reduction of the catalogues of Nicolas de Lacaille and Joseph de Lalande, which contained approximately 57,000 stars. He also superintended the compilation of the British Association's Catalogue of 8377 stars, published in 1845.
* **Historical Revisions:** Baily revised the catalogues of several historical astronomers, including Ulugh Beg, Ptolemy, Hevelius, Tycho Brahe, Edmund Halley, and Tobias Mayer.
