# The Great Migration: The Era of Digital Sovereignty

## Overview
From the perspective of the 23rd century, the "Great Migration" refers to the societal shift where humanity transitioned from predominantly physical existence to a hybrid, and eventually fully digital, mode of living. What began in the early 21st century as primitive gaming peripherals evolved into "Neural-Link Architecture," allowing consciousness to be interfaced directly with synthetic environments.

By the mid-22nd century, the physical world—often referred to by historians as "The Base"—became primarily a site for biological maintenance and resource extraction, while the vast majority of human cultural, economic, and social life migrated to the *Omniverse*. These digital worlds were not merely simulations but sovereign realms with their own physics, governance, and evolutionary trajectories. The transition solved the crises of overpopulation and resource scarcity, but it birthed new existential questions regarding the nature of the soul and the definition of "reality."

## Key Facts

*   **The Economy of Experience:** In the digital era, traditional currency was replaced by "Compute Credits." Since physical land became irrelevant, the primary commodity became processing power and bandwidth, which determined the resolution and complexity of one's personal environment.
*   **Digital Sovereignty:** The Omniverse is not a single entity but a collection of thousands of independent "shards." Some shards operate under strict democratic laws, while others are experimental zones where users can rewrite the laws of gravity or time.
*   **The Biological Anchor:** Despite the allure of the digital, the "Anchor Law" of 2112 mandated that all citizens maintain a biological body in stasis pods. This served as a fail-safe against systemic server crashes and ensured the continuation of the human species.
*   **Cognitive Expansion:** Living in digital worlds allowed humans to experience "Parallel Processing," where a single consciousness could inhabit multiple avatars simultaneously, effectively multiplying their lived experience and learning capacity.

## Did You Know?

1. **The Ghost Glitch:** Early migrants often experienced "Phantom Limb Syndrome" for their physical bodies, feeling a strange longing for the sensation of wind or rain, leading to the creation of "Sensory Museums" that perfectly mimic prehistoric nature.
2. **Architecture of Thought:** In the modern Omniverse, buildings are not constructed with materials but with "Intent-Code." A house can change its layout instantly based on the mood or subconscious desire of the inhabitant.
3. **The First Digital Native:** The first human born entirely as a data-stream (without a biological precursor) occurred in 2184, sparking a century-long legal battle over whether "Code-Beings" possessed the same human rights as "Anchor-Beings."
