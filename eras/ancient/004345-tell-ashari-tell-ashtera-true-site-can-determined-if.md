# Tell Ashari and Tell 'Ashtera

Tell Ashari and Tell 'Ashtera are archaeological sites that have been proposed as the location of the city of Ashteroth Karnaim. 

## Identification with Ashteroth Karnaim
Ashteroth Karnaim, which is also referred to as Ashtaroth and Be-eshterah (as noted in Josh. xxi. 27), is one of the cities of Bashan enumerated by name. Because of the similarity of the names, researchers have sought the site of Ashteroth Karnaim at Tell Ashari and Tell 'Ashtera.

## Comparative Identifications
The identification of Ashteroth Karnaim is subject to debate, as are other cities of Bashan. For instance, Ashteroth Karnaim has also been identified with Busrah (Bostra). While Busrah contains very important Herodian ruins, there is currently no tangible evidence provided to prove that the history of that specific site extends to such remote antiquity.

Other nearby identifications include Edrei, which is identified with Ed-Dera'a. This comparison is considered perhaps the most satisfactory due to the presence of Greco-Roman remains and an extensive subterranean city of unknown date, though the antiquity of the latter remains *sub judice*. Additionally, Salecah is identified with Salkhat in the Hauran, a site inhabited by Druses, and Kenath has been identified by Porter as Kanawat.

## Determination of the True Site
The archive notes that identifications based solely on the outward similarity of names have frequently been sources of error. Consequently, the true site of Ashteroth Karnaim—whether it be Tell Ashari, Tell 'Ashtera, or another location—can be determined by excavation only, if it can be determined at all.
