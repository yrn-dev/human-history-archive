# The Return of Gotama to Kapilavastu

## The Return of the Wanderer
Suddh[=o]dana, the father of the prophet Gotama, had watched his son's career with anxiety. Upon hearing that his son had abandoned asceticism and appeared as a teacher, itinerant preacher, and Wanderer, Suddh[=o]dana sent word to him. He urged Gotama to return home so that he might see his son once more before his death.

Gotama traveled to Kapilavastu, following his custom by stopping in a grove outside the town. He was visited there by his father, his uncles, and others. However, the uncles were angry and refused to show him reverence. While it was the custom to invite such teachers and their disciples for a meal the following day, the group departed without extending this invitation.

## The Encounter in the City
The following day, Gotama set out at the usual hour with his bowl to beg for a meal. Although he hesitated and considered going directly to his father's house, he decided to adhere to his custom. Suddh[=o]dana soon learned that his son was walking through the streets begging. Startled by this news, Suddh[=o]dana rose, seized the end of his outer robe, and hurried to find Gotama.

Suddh[=o]dana questioned why Gotama was exposing the family to such shame by going from door to door for food, asserting that he was capable of supplying the needs of many mendicants. Gotama responded that this was the custom of his race. When his father argued that no single person of their illustrious line had ever acted so indecorously, Gotama clarified the distinction in their descent. He acknowledged that while his father and family could claim the privileges of Kshatriya descent, his own descent was from the prophets (Buddhas) of old, who had always acted in this manner.

## The Treasure of Dharma
Gotama explained to his father that the customs of the law (Dharma) are beneficial for both the current world and the world to come. He further stated that when a man finds a treasure, it is his duty to offer the most precious jewels to his father first. He invited Suddh[=o]dana to share in the treasure he had found. Following this exchange, an abashed Suddh[=o]dana took his son's bowl and led him back to his house.
