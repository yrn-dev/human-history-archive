## Historical and Demographic Records
In the history of Central Asia, the Tartars (also referred to as Tatars) are described as being descended from Tatar Khan, who was the brother of Mogul Khan. According to certain records, they once constituted a horde consisting of 70,000 families located on the borders of Kitay. These groups are noted for leading the vanguard during the great invasion of Europe in A.D. 1238. 

However, the archive notes a scholarly dispute regarding these origins. M. Klaproth suggests that the relationship between these figures is fabulous and was invented by Mahometan writers. ## Artistic and Religious Contributions

This artistic work consists of a Burial Scene and an *Ecce Homo* located at the top of the arch. The front of the Tomb features figures of Pierre de Baillon and his wife, Marguerite de la Morlière, depicted as kneeling at praying-desks.

## Biological Context
In the study of anatomy and biology, the term "basement" is used to describe the membrane upon which gland cells are placed. This basement membrane may be formed of thin flattened cells or appear as a homogeneous membrane. Some observers characterize it as a modified part of the cell's basal surface, while others view it as a distinct structure from the epithelium. In larger ducts, surface cells typically extend down to this basement membrane via a thinned-out stalk.
