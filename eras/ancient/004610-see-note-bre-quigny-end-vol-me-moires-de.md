# Louis George Oudard Feudrix de Bréquigny

Louis George Oudard Feudrix de Bréquigny was a French historian and researcher known for his extensive work with historical documents, diplomatic charters, and studies of French communes.

## Contributions to French History
Bréquigny conducted significant research into French communes, which can be found in the preface to volume xi of a series spanning volumes x through xiv. He also collaborated with Mouchet between 1769 and 1783 to contribute three volumes to the *Table chronologique des diplômes, chartes, lettres, et actes imprimés concernant l'histoire de France*.

Additionally, Bréquigny was tasked with the supervision of a large collection of documents regarding French history. This collection was considered analogous to Rymer's *Foedera*. In 1791, he published the first volume of this work, titled *Diplomatat. Chartae*.

## Works on Chinese History
Beginning in 1776, at the request of the minister Bertin, Bréquigny started a collection titled *Mémoires concernant l'histoire, les sciences, les lettres, et les arts des Chinois*. He had published fifteen volumes of this work before his progress was interrupted by the Revolution.
