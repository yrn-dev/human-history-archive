## Ancient Greek Political and Military Affairs

Historical records from the *Grote History of Greece* detail the strategic maneuvers and territorial conquests of Philip of Macedon. Philip's ascendency became an uncontested fact in Thessaly and in areas outside the straits of Thermopylæ, even while he was kept out of Southern Greece. This allowed the Phokians to reorganize themselves against Thebes.

The conquests attributed to Philip are consistently enumerated in a specific order: Amphipolis, Pydna, Potidæa, and Methônê. Evidence from a discourse pronounced in 355 B.C. provides confirmatory data regarding the date of the surrender of Potidæa and Pydna. While Diodorus indicates that Methônê was not captured until 353-354 B.C., Demosthenes later received a public vote of gratitude from the Athenian assembly for his contributions toward the relief of captives at Olynthus, Methônê, and Pydna.

Further geopolitical tensions involved the islands of Chios, Rhodes, and Cos, as well as Byzantium. Records indicate that Mausolus, while claiming to be a friend of the Rhodians, deprived them of their liberty. Additionally, there are references to the governor of Krithôtê reporting a formidable increase in force following the arrival of Charidemus.

## The Afterlife and Divine Reward

The Qur'an describes a stark contrast between different states in the afterlife. One state is characterized by a nourishment that neither fattens nor releases from hunger. In contrast, other faces on that day are described as joyful, calm, or happy, pleased because of their past striving.

These individuals reside in a high or lofty Garden where they hear no idle speech, vain talk, or recrimination; the only word heard is "Peace, Peace." This environment is characterized by:
* **Physical Comforts:** Raised couches or thrones of dignity, cushions set in rows, and silken or rich carpets spread out.
* **Natural Beauty:** Thornless lote-trees, clustered plantains or banana-trees with fruits piled one above another, and extended shade.
* **Provision:** Gushing or flowing springs, water flowing constantly, and abundant fruit that is neither forbidden nor out of reach.
* **Service:** Drinking-cups or goblets placed ready at hand.

The text notes that these "Companions of the Right Hand" have been brought into a new creation or growth.
