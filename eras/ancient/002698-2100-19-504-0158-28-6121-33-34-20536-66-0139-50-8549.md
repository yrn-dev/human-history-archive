## Babylonian Land Surveys
Archaeological findings in the British Museum include a series of circular clay tablets containing land surveys. These tablets are dated back to either 2300 B.C. or 2100 B.C. One such tablet provides a rough depiction of lower Babylonia, showing the region encircled by the "salt water river" known as Oceanus. This reflects a system of cadastral surveying used for taxation purposes, which was already in operation during the age of Sargon of Akkad in 3800 B.C.
