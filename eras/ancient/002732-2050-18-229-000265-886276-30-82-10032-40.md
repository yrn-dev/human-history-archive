According to summarized results of new information, the elimination of Dynasty II from the Babylonian throne suggests that Dynasty I rose no earlier than 2000 or 2050 B.C. Various historians and scholars have proposed differing timelines for the Babylonian dynasties. For instance, Hommel (1901) suggested a timeframe for Dynasty I ranging from 2050 to 1752 B.C. ## Bavarian Income Tax

For individuals with earned incomes between £1700 and £2050, the tax rate was set at £2 for every £50 earned. Those with incomes exceeding £2050 but not exceeding £2500 paid £2, 10s. on every £50.
