# Friedrich Jeremias

## Academic Contributions
Friedrich Jeremias was a scholar who contributed to the study of ancient Near Eastern religions. He authored a work titled *Die Babylonier und Assyrer*, which was published in 1905 as part of volume i of de la Saussaye's *Lehrbuch der Religionsgeschichte* (3rd edition), published in Tuebingen.

## Context within Religious Studies
Jeremias's work on the Babylonians and Assyrians is situated among a broader body of bibliographic research concerning the religions of these ancient civilizations. His contributions are listed alongside other significant scholars and texts in the field, including:
