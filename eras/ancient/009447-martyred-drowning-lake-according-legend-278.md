# Saint Christina of Bolsena

## Life and Martyrdom
Saint Christina is a figure associated with the town of Bolsena, located in the province of Rome, Italy. According to legend, she was martyred in the year 278. The tradition states that her death occurred by drowning in the lake of Bolsena.

## The Church of S. Christina
The town of Bolsena contains a church dedicated to S. Christina, which dates back to the 11th century. The structure features a fine Renaissance facade that was constructed around the year 1500 by Cardinal Giovanni de' Medici, who later became Pope Leo X. The interior of the church contains several frescoes, which are thought to be from the school of Giotto, as well as terra cottas produced by the Della Robbia.

## The Crypt and Tomb
Beneath the church of S. Christina are catacombs. In 1880, the tomb of the saint was discovered within these catacombs. 

## The Miracle of Bolsena
The crypt of the church is also the site of the miracle of Bolsena, which took place in 1263 at one of its altars. The event involved a Bohemian priest who held skeptical views regarding the doctrine of transubstantiation. The priest was convinced of the truth of the doctrine when drops of blood appeared on the host he was consecrating. 

In commemoration of this miracle, Pope Urban IV instituted the festival of Corpus Christi and ordered that the cathedral of Orvieto be erected. The miracle of Bolsena was later depicted in a celebrated fresco by Raphael located in the Vatican.
