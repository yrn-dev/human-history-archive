# The Great Pyramid of Giza: Engineering the Eternal

## Overview
The Great Pyramid of Giza, commissioned by the Pharaoh Khufu during the Fourth Dynasty of the Old Kingdom, stands as the sole survivor of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Built approximately 4,500 years ago, it represents the pinnacle of ancient Egyptian architectural ambition and mathematical precision. 

The construction remains one of history's most debated topics. While the pyramid consists of roughly 2.3 million stone blocks, the exact mechanism used to lift these massive limestone and granite slabs into place is still contested among historians and engineers. The prevailing academic consensus rejects the notion of slave labor, pointing instead to a highly organized workforce of skilled laborers and seasonal farmers who were paid in rations and housed in nearby purpose-built villages.

## Key Facts
*   **Composition:** The core is primarily local yellow limestone, while the exterior was originally encased in polished Tura limestone, which reflected the sun's light. The interior King's Chamber is constructed from massive red granite transported from Aswan, over 500 miles away.
*   **Alignment:** The pyramid is aligned to true north with an accuracy of within three-sixtieths of a degree, demonstrating a sophisticated understanding of stellar observation.
*   **The Ramp Debate:** Historians debate how the stones were raised. Theories include a **straight external ramp**, a **spiral ramp** wrapping around the exterior, or an **internal ramp** system (a theory popularized by architect Jean-Pierre Houdin).
*   **The Purpose:** While traditionally viewed as a tomb to protect the Pharaoh's *ka* (spirit) and facilitate his ascent to the heavens, some scholars examine its role as a symbol of state power and economic mobilization.

## Did You Know?
1. **Not Slaves:** Archaeological evidence from the "Lost City of the Pyramid Builders" reveals that workers ate high-protein diets of beef and sheep, suggesting they were respected laborers rather than enslaved people.
2. **Original Height:** Upon completion, the pyramid stood at approximately 146.6 meters (481 feet), making it the tallest man-made structure in the world for nearly 3,800 years.
3. **Precision Joints:** The casing stones were fitted together so tightly that, in some areas, a thin knife blade cannot be inserted between the joints.
