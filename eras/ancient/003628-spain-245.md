## Late Roman and Post-Roman Period
During the period of the Western Empire's decline, Spain was a site of conflict and political maneuvering. Theodoric provided a solemn and voluntary promise of fidelity that was recognized in both Spain and Gaul. 

The Suevi, a people who established a presence in the region, operated out of Bracara (Braga). The king of the Suevi utilized navigation from the ports of Gallicia to the Mediterranean; however, ships from Bracara typically steered cautiously along the coast to avoid losing themselves in the Atlantic. The conflicts involving the Suevi were documented by Idatius, the bishop of Iria Flavia, who witnessed and suffered during the Suevic war. Other historical accounts, such as those by Jornandes, describe the Gothic victory over the Suevi.
