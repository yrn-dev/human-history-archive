# American Buckthorns

American buckthorns comprise several species of shrubs and trees found in North America, primarily within the botanical order Rhamnaceae.

## Species and Distribution
The American buckthorns include *Rhamnus purshiana*, also known as *Cascara sagrada*, which is found on the Pacific coast. Another species is *Rhamnus caroliniana*, referred to as the alder-buckthorn. Additionally, *Bumelia lycioides* (or *Bumelia lanuginosa*) is popularly identified as the "southern buckthorn."

## Characteristics and Uses
The species *Rhamnus purshiana* (Cascara sagrada) is noted for producing cascara bark. While not specifically detailed for the American species, other members of the *Rhamnus* genus are known for various properties; for example, the fruit of *Rhamnus cathartica* possesses strong purgative properties and its bark is used to produce a yellow dye. Other species of *Rhamnus*, such as *R. infectoria*, *R. tinctoria*, and *R. davurica*, are used to obtain dyes, with the latter two producing the China green used in commerce.

## Related Botanical Classifications
The American buckthorns belong to the natural order Rhamnaceae. This order also includes the British buckthorn (*Rhamnus cathartica*) and the genus *Zizyphus*. *Zizyphus lotus* is a bush native to south Europe that produces fruits containing a mealy substance used for bread and fermented drinks.
