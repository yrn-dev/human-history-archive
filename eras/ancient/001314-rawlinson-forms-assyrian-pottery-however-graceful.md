## Library Collections and Bequests
In the history of book-collecting, the Rawlinson family is noted for the accumulation of immense libraries. Thomas Rawlinson, who died in 1725, amassed a vast collection of materials that later provided the basis for a series of auctions. His brother, Richard Rawlinson (d. 1755), took a different approach to his acquisitions; while he permitted the sale of Thomas's books, he bequeathed the best portions of his own collection to Oxford.

## Military History

By October 15, Rawlinson, commanding the 3rd Cavalry Division and the 7th Division, had positioned himself in the vicinity of Ypres. He is noted for having skillfully extricated his forces from large German armies, lingering at various points to avoid becoming seriously engaged.
