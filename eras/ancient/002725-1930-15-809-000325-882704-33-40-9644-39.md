## International Reparations

This commission consisted of representatives from the United States, Great Britain, France, Italy, and Belgium. In certain instances, a representative from Japan or Serbia could replace the Belgian representative if their specific interests were affected. Other allied powers were granted representation without voting power when their claims were being considered.

The commission, headquartered in Paris, served as the exclusive agency for the Allies to receive, hold, sell, and distribute reparation payments. While majority votes generally prevailed, unanimity was required for specific high-level decisions. These included matters of sovereignty for the Allies, the cancellation of German obligations, and the methods of negotiating bonds issued by Germany.
