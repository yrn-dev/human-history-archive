# Holder's Altkeltische Sprachschatz (1896-1908)

The *Altkeltische Sprachschatz*, published between 1896 and 1908 by A. Holder, serves as a significant reference work for the study of ancient authorities and linguistic materials.

## Content and Organization
The *Altkeltische Sprachschatz* is utilized as a primary resource for collecting chief passages related to early Britain from classical writers. These passages are organized alphabetically within the work. The classical writers referenced in these collections include:
* Julius Caesar
* Tacitus
* Strabo
* Diodorus
* The elder Pliny
* Ptolemy
* Cassius Dio

Additionally, the work is cited as a reference for information regarding the Boii, a people belonging almost certainly to the Early Iron Age.

## Relation to Roman Inscriptions and Other Sources
While Holder's *Altkeltische Sprachschatz* collects passages from classical writers, the Roman inscriptions themselves are documented in different collections. These include:
* Hübner's *Corpus Inscriptionum Latin.* vii. (1873).
* Supplements provided by Haverfield and Hübner in the periodical *Ephemeris epigraphica*.
* Hübner's *Inscript. Britann. Christianae* (1876), though this is noted as being out of date.
* J. Rhys's work on Pictish and other inscriptions in the *Proceedings Soc. Antiq. Scotland*, volumes xxvi and xxxii.

Other complementary sources for the study of early Britain and its authorities include Petrie's *Monumenta Hist. Britann.* (1848), J. Rhys's *Celtic Britain* (3rd ed., 1904), and the *Realencyclopädie* by Pauly-Wissowa (iii. pt. i., 1897).

## Other Works by A. Holder
Beyond the *Altkeltische Sprachschatz*, A. Holder has contributed to other scholarly fields:
* **Classical Commentaries:** Holder collected and edited the Porphyrio scholia in 1902. These scholia are ancient notes found in many manuscripts of Horace, derived from the commentaries of Porphyrio, who lived around A.D. 200.
* **Critical Editions:** Holder, along with Keller, produced a critical edition of Horace that continues to be a primary reference in the field.
