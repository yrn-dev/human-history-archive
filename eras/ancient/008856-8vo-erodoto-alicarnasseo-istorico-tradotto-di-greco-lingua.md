# Erodoto Alicarnasseo istorico, tradotto di Greco in Lingua Italiana

## Overview and Publication
*Erodoto Alicarnasseo istorico, tradotto di Greco in Lingua Italiana* is a work attributed to Boiardo. The text is a translation of the historian Herodotus of Halicarnassus from the Greek language into the Italian language. It was published in Venice in two separate editions, appearing in 1533 and 1538.

## Physical Format
The archive records indicate that both the 1533 and 1538 editions of this work were printed in the 8vo (octavo) format.
