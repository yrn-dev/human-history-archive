# Beiträge zur Erklärung des Thukydides

The *Beiträge zur Erklärung des Thukydides* is a scholarly work authored by F. W. Ullrich. Published in Hamburg in 1846, the text provides critical explanations and interpretations of the writings of the historian Thucydides.

## Interpretations of the Peloponnesian War
In the *Beiträge zur Erklärung des Thukydides*, Ullrich addresses the structure and timeline of Thucydides' accounts of the Peloponnesian War. Specifically, Ullrich analyzes the later books of Thucydides' work, which appear to be treated as a separate continuation of the earlier material and include a separate preface. 

Ullrich identifies a specific viewpoint held by Thucydides in this later portion: the perspective that the entire twenty-seven years of conflict constituted one continuous war, despite being nominally interrupted. This interpretation is supported by Ullrich's analysis on pages 85, 125, and 138 of the 1846 publication.

## Chronology and Terminology
The work is also utilized to clarify Thucydides' use of time and seasonal markers. Ullrich's writings (specifically on page 32) are cited to correct erroneous treatments of the "Thucydidean winter." While some scholars, such as Poppo, treated the winter as lasting only four months, Ullrich's work supports the view that Thucydides bisected the year into summer (*θέρος*) and winter (*χείμων*) based on the equinoxes, with each season representing one half of the year.

## Analysis of Specific Phrases
Ullrich's *Beiträge* provides linguistic and historical clarity regarding specific phrases used by Thucydides. In the context of disturbances in Korkyra mentioned by Diodorus, Ullrich (pp. 95-99) explains a particular phrase used by Thucydides as referring to the first ten years of the Peloponnesian War. This specific period is defined as the interval between the Peace of Nikias and the surprise of Platæa.
