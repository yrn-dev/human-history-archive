# The Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa and Related Historical Conflicts

## The Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa
In 1212, a significant engagement known as the battle of Las Navas de Tolosa took place in the neighborhood of Bailen, a town located in the province of Jaen in southern Spain. According to accounts provided by ancient chroniclers, the Castilians, led by Alphonso VIII, achieved a victory over the Moors. The chroniclers claimed that the Castilians slew 200,000 Moors while suffering only 25 casualties themselves. While this specific estimate is considered absurd, the victory for the Christians was complete.
