# GAVE D'ASPE

## Geography and Location
The Aspe is a river located in the south-western region of France, specifically within the department of Basses-Pyrenees. This department was formed in 1790, consisting of two-thirds from Bearn and the remainder from three districts of Gascony: Labourd, Soule, and Basse-Navarre. The Aspe river is situated in the southern portion of the department, which is occupied by the lower and western summits of the Pyrenees.

## Regional Topography and Borders
The river Aspe flows through a valley that serves as a strategic point of transit. The frontier between France and Spain generally follows the crest-line of the main mountain range. In the upper valleys of the Aspe, as well as the Nive, forts are positioned to guard important passes that lead into Spain.

The surrounding mountainous terrain varies in elevation. To the west, the peak of the Rhune reaches 2950 ft., and the peak of Orhy reaches 6618 ft. Further east, elevations increase, with the peak of Anie reaching 8215 ft. Other significant peaks in the region include the southern peak of Ossau at 9465 ft. and Mourrous, which stands at 9760 ft. on the border of the Hautes-Pyrenees.

## Hydrology of the Region
The general direction of the rivers in the Basses-Pyrenees department is toward the north-west. The Aspe is part of a network of streams that eventually meet in the Adour. This convergence occurs through several larger streams, including the Nive, the Bidouze, and the Gave de Pau. The Gave de Pau is noted as being a larger stream than the Adour itself.

The Adour eventually enters the sea a short distance below Bayonne. The mouth of the Adour is characterized by a shifting bar that has frequently altered its position.
