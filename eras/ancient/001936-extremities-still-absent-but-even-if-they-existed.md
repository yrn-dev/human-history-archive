## Geographic Extremities and Coastal Features
In the region of Latium, Italy, Astura is located at the south-east extremity of the Bay of Antium. Formerly an island and now a peninsula, it was the site of a villa favored by Cicero, who retired there following the death of his daughter Tullia in 453 B.C. The area was considered unhealthy during Roman times; Suetonius notes that both Tiberius and Augustus contracted the illnesses that proved fatal to them at this location. While a line of villas extends from Astura north-west toward Antium, remains are almost entirely absent to the south-east, suggesting that this portion of the coast was sparsely populated in both Roman and modern times.

In Asia, the country of Cathay is described as existing in the extremity of the continent. Its existence as a civilized land was reported to Europe by William Rubruquis in 1253 and Plano Carpini in 1246. Although these friars did not reach the frontiers of Cathay, they encountered its people at the Court of the Great Kaan in Mongolia. Rubruquis identified these people as the Seres of classic fame. Additionally, the town and territory of Phasis are located at the eastern extremity of the Euxine.

## Biological Absences and Characteristics
In the study of fungi, specifically Discomycetes, the presence of sexual organs varies by species. In *Lachnea stercorea*, *Humaria granulata*, and *Ascobolus furfuraceus*, the antheridium is either non-functional or entirely absent, though the ascogonium remains. In *Humaria rutilans*, both the antheridium and ascogonium are absent, and ascogenous hyphae arise apogamously from the mycelium hyphae. In *Exoascus*, sexual fusion is apparently absent altogether.

Regarding avian anatomy, the syrinx (the lower larynx) is a modification of the bronchi and the lower part of the trachea used for producing voice. However, this organ is absent in most storks, turkey buzzards (*Cathartes*), Steganopodes, and the majority of Ratitae, likely due to retrogression.
