# St Michael's and Regional Fauna

## Avian Population Control in St Michael's
In St Michael's, the local agricultural sector faced significant challenges due to the presence of various bird species. A multitude of green canaries, chaffinches, bullfinches, and blackbirds inflicted damage upon the crops. To combat this, a reward system was previously established to encourage the destruction of these birds. It is reported that between the years 1875 and 1885, over 400,000 birds were destroyed over several successive years.
