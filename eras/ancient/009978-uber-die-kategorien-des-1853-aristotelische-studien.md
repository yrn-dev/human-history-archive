# Uber die Kategorien des A. (1853) and Aristotelische Studien

## Overview
*Uber die Kategorien des A.* (1853) and *Aristotelische Studien* (1862-1867) are scholarly works authored by Hermann Bonitz, a German scholar recognized internationally as a commentator on the works of Aristotle and Plato. These texts form part of a broader body of research by Bonitz focused on classical philosophy and philology.

## Author Biography
Hermann Bonitz was born on July 29, 1814, in Langensalza, Saxony. His academic foundation was established through studies at Berlin under Lachmann and Bockh, as well as at Leipzig under G. Hermann. 

Bonitz held a variety of academic and administrative positions throughout his career:
* **Teaching and Gymnasium Roles:** He served as a teacher at the Blochmann institute in Dresden in 1836. In Berlin, he was an Oberlehrer at the Friedrich-Wilhelms gymnasium (1838) and the Graues Kloster (1840). He later became a professor at the gymnasium at Stettin in 1842 and eventually the director of the Graues Kloster gymnasium in 1867.
* **University and Academy Roles:** In 1849, he was appointed professor at the university of Vienna. He was elected a member of the imperial academy in 1854 and a member of the council of education in 1864.

Beyond his research, Bonitz was heavily involved in higher education, playing a primary role in the examination and teaching systems utilized in Prussian high schools after 1882. He retired in 1888 and died in Berlin on July 25 of that same year.

## Contributions to Aristotelian Scholarship
While Bonitz wrote on various classical and educational subjects—including contributions to the *Zeitschrift fur die osterreichischen Gymnasien* and works on Thukydides and Sophokles—he is most noted outside of Germany for his commentaries on Aristotle and Plato.

His work on Aristotle is extensive and includes several critical observations and systematic studies:
* **Early Critical Works:** He published *Observations Criticae in Aristotelis Libros Metaphysicos* in 1842 and *Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis quae feruntur Magna Moralia et Ethica Eudemia* in 1844.
* **Metaphysical Studies:** His research continued with *Alexandri Aphrodisiensis Commentarius in Libras Metaphysicos Aristotelis* (1847) and *Aristotelis Metaphysica* (1848-1849).
* **Categorical and General Studies:** In 1853, he produced *Uber die Kategorien des A.*, followed by the *Aristotelische Studien*, published between 1862 and 1867.
* **Indexing:** He concluded this trajectory of Aristotelian research with the *Index Aristotelicus* in 1870.

## Other Academic Works
In addition to his focus on Aristotle, Bonitz authored *Disputationes Platonicae Duae* (1837) and *Platonische Studien*, the third edition of which was released in 1886. His interests also extended to the origins of Homeric poetry, as seen in *Uber den Ursprung der homerischen Gedichte* (5th ed., 1881).
