## The Bag-pipe in the British Isles
The bag-pipe is an instrument that spread from England to Ireland and Caledonia. In these regions, it became deeply integrated into the life of the people and was highly regarded by the Celtic races as a military instrument. Its use was widespread across various social and ceremonial functions, including festivals, weddings, and funerals.

During the 18th century, the bag-pipe was also employed to lighten the burden of physical labor. An example of this occurred in 1786 in Skye, where parties of labourers engaged in roadmaking were accompanied by their own bag-pipers. Additionally, the instrument was utilized in old mysteries in Coventry as early as 1534.
