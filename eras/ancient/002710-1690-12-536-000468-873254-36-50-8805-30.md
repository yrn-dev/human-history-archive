## Military and Political Events
In Europe, 1690 saw military conflict in the Balkans. The Austrians routed the Turks at Gornja Tuzla. Following this engagement, the Franciscan friars, along with approximately 3,000 other Roman Catholics, were removed and relocated into Slavonia.

In naval history, a commander in the royal navy, who had previously received a full pardon, died on June 21, 1690. His death occurred while he was leading an assault on St Christopher's.

## Foundation of Calcutta
The history of Calcutta is recorded as practically dating from August 24, 1690. The city was founded by Job Charnock of the English East India Company. While the location had been noted as a rent-paying village in a survey of Bengal conducted under the command of Emperor Akbar in 1596, it did not emerge into history until Charnock's efforts. 

Prior to the permanent settlement in 1690, English merchants under Charnock's leadership had been forced to leave their factory due to a rupture with Mogul authorities. They had made temporary occupations of Sutanati, a village on the banks of the Hugli, in December 1686 and November 1687. The third attempt, on August 24, 1690, resulted in a permanent settlement. This center eventually expanded to include the village of Kalikata and other neighboring hamlets.

## Notable Figures
Several individuals of intellectual and political note are associated with this period:

*   **Nicholas Barbon**: An English economist born in London (c. 1640–1698) who studied medicine at Leiden and graduated M.D. at Utrecht in 1661. Barbon was involved in the rebuilding of London after the great fire of 1666 and is credited as the institutor of fire-insurance in England around 1680. In 1690, he served as M.P. for Bramber (a position he also held in 1695). He published *A Discourse of Trade* in 1690.
*   **Peter Dens**: A Belgian Roman Catholic theologian born in 1690 at Boom, near Antwerp. He spent the majority of his life at the archiepiscopal college of Malines, serving as president for forty years and as a reader in theology for twelve. He authored the *Theologia moralis et dogmatica*, a text-book compendium of Roman Catholic ethics and doctrine.
