# End of "the 22nd dynasty"

## The 22nd Dynasty in Egypt
In the broader history of Egypt, the 22nd Dynasty is identified as one of a series of changing dynasties. A notable figure associated with this dynasty was Shishak, who is recorded as having plundered the temple of Solomon circa 930 B.C. 

The Egyptian historical trajectory following the 22nd Dynasty eventually led to the establishment of the 25th Dynasty, founded by an Ethiopian conqueror from the Upper Nile. This foreign dynasty lasted until 670 B.C., when it fell to the new Assyrian Empire established by Sennacherib, Sargon II, and Tiglath Pileser III.
