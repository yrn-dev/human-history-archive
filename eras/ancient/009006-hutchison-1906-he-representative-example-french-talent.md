# Hutchison

## Literary Translation
W.G. Hutchison is identified as the English translator of the work *Tacite*. The original French text was published in 1903, and Hutchison's English translation followed in 1906. This translation was based on the work of a French author who was noted for applying lucidity and elegance to weighty literary matters and who focused primarily on the reconstruction of Roman society.

## Nutritional Analysis of the Banana
In the study of food-stuffs, Hutchison provided a detailed composition of banana flour. According to his findings, the flour consists of the following:
* Carbohydrates: 80%
* Water: 13%
* Salts: 2.5%
* Proteid: 4%
* Fat: 0.5%

Hutchison's analysis indicates that an average person would require approximately eighty bananas of average size to meet their daily energy requirements. To meet the necessary amount of proteid, double that number of bananas would be required. Hutchison links the undue abdominal development observed in populations that rely mainly on this diet to these nutritional proportions.

## Military History
In the history of Castlebar, a market town in Co. Mayo, Ireland, Luke Hutchison is recorded as an English commander. In 1798, the town was occupied for several weeks by French forces under General J.J. Humbert. During this period, General Humbert defeated the English forces led by Luke Hutchison in a conflict that has been jocularly referred to as the "Castlebar Races."
