## Institutional Conflicts in Coimbra
The tenure of the humanists at the university of Coimbra was marked by institutional instability. André de Gouvéa served as the rector, a position that was also coveted by his uncle, Diogo de Gouvéa, who had previously headed Sainte-Barbe. It is probable that Diogo urged the Inquisition to attack André and his staff before André's death at the end of 1547. 

Until the full publication of trial records in 1906, biographers of Buchanan had attributed this Inquisition attack to other influences, specifically the Jesuits, the Franciscans, or Cardinal Beaton, as the details of Buchanan's time in Portugal remained obscure.
