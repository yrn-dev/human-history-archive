# Etudes historiques (Paris, 1904) and J. C. W. Augusti, Denkwuerdigkeiten

The works *Etudes historiques* (Paris, 1904) and *Denkwuerdigkeiten* (Leipzig, 1829-1831) are identified in the archive's records as authorities within the study of baptism and early Christian rites.

## Etudes historiques (Paris, 1904)
*Etudes historiques*, published in Paris in 1904, was authored by Mgr. P. Batiffol. It is categorized among the modern literature regarding the sacrament of baptism. This work is listed alongside other exhaustive studies on the subject, such as those by W. F. Hoefling, Jos. Bingham, and W. Wall.

## J. C. W. Augusti, Denkwuerdigkeiten
*Denkwuerdigkeiten* is a work authored by J. C. W. Augusti. It was published in Leipzig between 1829 and 1831. Like Batiffol's *Etudes historiques*, it is cited as a recommended authority for those researching the history and practice of baptism.
