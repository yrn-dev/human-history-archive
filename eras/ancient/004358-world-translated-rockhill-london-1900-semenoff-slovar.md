## Geographical and Linguistic Contributions
Rockhill's work is utilized by historians and geographers to identify specific locations in Asia. For example, in the analysis of the city of Tenduc, Rockhill's *Diary* is used to identify the location as *Tou Ch’eng* (or *Toto Ch’eng*), known to the Mongols as *Togto* or *Tokto*. Rockhill records passing through this location and reaching Ho-k’ou (Dugus or Dugei in Mongol) on the Yellow River, situated five *li* to the south. He suggests that previous identifications of Tenduc with Kwei-hwa Ch’eng overlooked the existence of Togto, noting that Tou Ch’eng is located two days' march west of Kwei-hwa Ch’eng.

Furthermore, Rockhill provides linguistic translations for terms found in historical accounts. In the context of Rubruck's writings, Rockhill translates the term *veringal* as "basket," specifically in a passage describing the filling of a *veringal* with *biscocto* (twice-baked bread) and a platter with apples and other fruits.
