# WHAT BEFELL WHEN THE THREE KINGS RETURNED TO THEIR OWN

## The Visit to the Child
Before departing for their own land, the Three Kings arrived at the place where the Child was born. Upon their arrival, they entered to see the Child individually. The youngest king entered first and found the Child appeared to be of his own age, which caused him to leave the room marvelling greatly. The middle king entered next and experienced the same phenomenon, finding the Child seemingly of his own age, and he likewise departed in marvel. Finally, the eldest king entered and encountered the same result, leaving the room in a pensive state.

After the three kings rejoined one another and shared their individual experiences, their marvel increased. They agreed to enter together, and upon doing so, they beheld the Child with the appearance of its actual age, which was some thirteen days. The kings then adored the Child and presented offerings of Myrrh, Incense, and Gold. The Child accepted these three offerings and gave the kings a small closed box. Following this exchange, the kings departed to return to their own land.

## Descriptions of the Three Kings
According to the Collectanea ascribed to Bede, the three kings are characterized by their age and appearance: Melchior is described as a hoary old man, Balthazar is described as being in his prime with a beard, and Gaspar is described as young and beardless.
