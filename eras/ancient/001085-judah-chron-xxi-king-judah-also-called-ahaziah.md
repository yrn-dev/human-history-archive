# Ahaziah of Judah

Ahaziah was a King of Judah, the son of Jehoram. In historical records, he is also referred to by the names Jehoahaz and Ahaziah.

## Lineage and Family
Ahaziah was the son of King Jehoram. His father, Jehoram, had married Athaliah, who was the daughter of Ahab and Jezebel. Ahaziah's grandmother was the daughter of Ahab. Following the death of Ahaziah, his mother Athaliah usurped the throne of Judah and reigned for six years. During this period, Athaliah is said to have massacred all members of the royal house of Judah, though this account is contrasted with other notices in the archive. The only survivor of this royal line was Joash, who was hidden in the temple by his aunt Jehosheba, the wife of the priest Jehoiada.

## Reign and Relations
The reign of Ahaziah was characterized by his relations with Israel, for which he was denounced. His time on the throne occurred during a period of close intercourse between the kingdoms of Israel and Judah. ## Death and Succession
The end of Ahaziah's reign coincided with the rise of Jehu. Ahaziah and Jehoram of Israel both perished during the overthrow of Omri's dynasty by the usurper Jehu. Specifically, Ahaziah of Judah and Jehoram of Israel died in battle with Jehu. 

Following the death of Ahaziah, the throne was seized by Athaliah. This period of usurpation ended when Jehoiada the priest and Jehosheba organized a revolution in favor of Joash. This movement resulted in the death of Athaliah and her adherents, leading to the accession of Joash.
