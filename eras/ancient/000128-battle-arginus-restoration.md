## Naval Engagements and the Athenian Armament
During the period of the Peloponnesian War, the Athenian armament encountered three Chian men-of-war off Arginus while sailing around Corycus. The Athenians gave immediate chase, but a great storm emerged. The Chians managed to take refuge in a harbour with difficulty. However, the three Athenian vessels that were most forward in the pursuit were wrecked and thrown up near the city of Chios; the crews of these vessels were either taken as prisoners or slain. The remaining portion of the Athenian fleet sought refuge in a harbour known as Phoenicus, located under Mount Mimas. Following this, the fleet put into Lesbos to prepare for fortification work.
