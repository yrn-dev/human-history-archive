# The Great Pyramid of Giza: Engineering the Eternal

The Great Pyramid of Giza, constructed for the Pharaoh Khufu during the Fourth Dynasty of the Old Kingdom, remains one of the most formidable achievements of human engineering. As the only surviving member of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, it serves as a testament to the sophisticated organizational capacity and mathematical precision of ancient Egyptian society. For millennia, it stood as the tallest man-made structure on Earth, sparking endless debate among historians and archaeologists regarding its construction.

### The Chronology of Construction

While the exact dates are subject to slight academic debate, the general timeline of the pyramid's creation is as follows:

| Phase | Approximate Date | Key Activity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Planning** | c. 2580 BCE | Site selection on the Giza plateau and astronomical alignment. |
| **Core Build** | 2580 – 2560 BCE | Quarrying and placement of ~2.3 million limestone blocks. |
| **Finishing** | 2560 – 2550 BCE | Application of polished Tura limestone casing and pyramidion placement. |
| **Completion** | c. 2550 BCE | Final sealing of the entrance and interment of Khufu. |

### Construction Methods and Mysteries

The primary mystery of the Great Pyramid lies in the logistics of its assembly. The structure consists of roughly 2.3 million stone blocks, some weighing up to 80 tons. Historians generally agree that the Egyptians utilized a massive, organized workforce of skilled laborers—not slaves—who were housed in nearby purpose-built villages.

To move these behemoths, engineers likely employed a system of sledges and lubricated tracks of wet sand to reduce friction. The method of elevation remains the subject of intense study. The leading theories include the **External Ramp Theory** (a straight or zig-zagging ramp), the **Internal Spiral Ramp Theory** (proposed by architect Jean-Pierre Houdin), and the use of complex lever systems.

Beyond the physical build, the mathematical precision is staggering. The pyramid is aligned almost perfectly to true north, with an error of only a fraction of a degree. This suggests a profound understanding of stellar observation and geometry, as the base forms a near-perfect square.

### Key Facts
*   **Original Height:** Approximately 146.6 meters (481 feet).
*   **Material:** Primarily local limestone for the core, Tura limestone for the casing, and Aswan granite for the King's Chamber.
*   **Internal Layout:** Features a complex system of passages, including the Ascending Passage, the Grand Gallery, and three main chambers.
*   **Labor Force:** Estimated at 20,000 to 30,000 skilled workers.

### Did You Know?
*   **The Casing Stones:** The pyramid was originally covered in polished white limestone that reflected the sun, making the structure shine like a beacon across the desert.
*   **Temperature Control:** Despite the scorching Egyptian heat, the temperature inside the King's Chamber remains constant at approximately 20 degrees Celsius (68°F).
*   **The Eight Sides:** Although it appears to have four sides, the Great Pyramid actually has eight; the faces are slightly concave, a feature only visible from the air during the equinoxes.
