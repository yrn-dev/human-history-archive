## Boundary Surveys in North America
Between 1779 and 1780, commissioners representing North Carolina and Virginia conducted a survey of the southern boundary to the Tennessee river. Although the boundary was intended to be run along the parallel of latitude 36° 30´, it was actually run north of that parallel due to a mistake. This specific line from 1779-1780 was later formally adopted in 1820 by commissioners representing Tennessee and Kentucky as the boundary between the two states, in conjunction with a line from 1819.
