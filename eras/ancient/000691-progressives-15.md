## Religious Progress
The archive contains records regarding the progress of the Christian religion. These records include examinations of the sentiments, manners, condition, and numbers of primitive Christians. Further documentation details the conduct of the Roman government toward Christians from the reign of Nero until the reign of Constantine.

In other regions, the progress of Christianity varied by population. Among the Burmese, Christianity progressed slowly. In contrast, rapid progress was observed among the Karens, specifically the Sgaw Karens, where the number of spirit-worshippers was significantly smaller. The Baptist and Roman Catholic faiths are identified as the forms of Christianity that produced the most converts in Burma. Additionally, American Baptist missionaries achieved many conversions among the Lahu or Muhsö hill tribesmen.
