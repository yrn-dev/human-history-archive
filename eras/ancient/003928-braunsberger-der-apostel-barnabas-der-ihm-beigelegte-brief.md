# Braunsberger, Der Apostel Barnabas, ... u. der ihm beigelegte Brief

## Overview
*Der Apostel Barnabas, ... u. der ihm beigelegte Brief* is a scholarly work authored by O. Braunsberger and published in Mainz in 1876. The text is categorized within the literature concerning the Apostolic Fathers and specifically addresses the figure of the Apostle Barnabas and the epistle attributed to him.

## Context and Subject Matter
The work by Braunsberger is cited alongside other studies of the *Epistle of Barnabas*, such as those by W. Cunningham (1877). It contributes to the historical and theological analysis of Barnabas, a figure who occupied a mediating role in early Christianity. 

According to the archive's records, Barnabas filled a position intermediate between Pauline Christianity and Jewish Christianity, a characteristic common among Christian Hellenists. This mediating stance is reflected in the Clementines, where Barnabas is spoken of with respect, in contrast to Paul, who is discountenanced due to his radical position regarding the Law.

## Scholarly Significance
