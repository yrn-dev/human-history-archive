# Uber den Ursprung der homerischen Gedichte

*Uber den Ursprung der homerischen Gedichte* is a scholarly work authored by the German scholar Hermann Bonitz. The fifth edition of this text was published in 1881.

## Authorship and Context
The work was written by Hermann Bonitz (1814–1888), a scholar born on July 29, 1814, in Langensalza, Saxony. Bonitz was educated at Berlin under Lachmann and Bockh, and at Leipzig under G. Hermann. Throughout his career, he held numerous academic and administrative positions, including serving as a professor at the university of Vienna (1849) and as the director of the Graues Kloster gymnasium (1867). 

While Bonitz is most recognized outside of Germany for his contributions as a commentator on Aristotle and Plato—producing works such as *Aristotelische Studien* (1862–1867) and *Platonische Studien* (1886)—he also wrote extensively on classical and educational subjects. *Uber den Ursprung der homerischen Gedichte* represents part of his broader engagement with classical scholarship.

## The Homeric Question
The text exists within the wider academic discourse known as the "Homeric Question." This field of literary study practically began with the publication of Wolf's *Prolegomena* in 1795. The discourse involves various analyses of the origin and composition of the Homeric poems, with other significant contributions to the field including:

*   **Early Works:** Wood's *Essay on the Original Genius and Writings of Homer* and W. Muller's *Homerische Vorschule* (2nd ed., 1836), which popularized the views of Wolf.
*   **Dissertations and Papers:** G. Hermann's *De interpolationibus Homeri* (1832) and *De iteratis apua Homerum* (1840), as well as Lachmann's *Betrachtungen uber Homer's Ilias*.
*   **Compositional Studies:** Works focusing on specific poems, such as A. Kirchhoff's *Die Composition der Odyssee* (1869), E. Kammer's *Die Einheit der Odyssee* (1873), and O. Seeck's *Die Quellen der Odyssee* (1887).
*   **Critical Histories:** Friedlander's *Die homerische Kritik von Wolf bis Grote* (1853) and Volkmann's *Geschichte und Kritik der Wolf'schen Prolegomena* (1874).

## Related Classical Scholarship
In addition to his work on the origin of Homeric poems, Bonitz contributed to the understanding of other classical authors. He authored *Beitrage zur Erklarung des Thukydides* in 1854 and *Beitrage zur Erklarung des Sophokles* between 1856 and 1857. His academic output was often shared through the *Zeitschrift fur die osterreichischen Gymnasien*.
