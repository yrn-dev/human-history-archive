## Political and Diplomatic Events
In the Habsburg monarchy, 1731 was a year of strategic isolation for Charles. Due to this isolation, Charles became focused on securing the throne for his daughter, Maria Theresa, in accordance with the Pragmatic Sanction of April 19, 1713. This instrument pronounced the indivisibility of the monarchy and settled the succession on his daughter in the absence of a male heir. 

To secure adhesion to this sanction, Charles entered into agreements with Holland and Great Britain in 1731. In return for their respect of the Pragmatic Sanction, the cession of Guastalla, Piacenza, and Parma to Don Carlos was agreed upon. Despite these agreements, the Bourbon powers remained hostile, a situation that eventually contributed to the War of Polish Succession in 1733.
