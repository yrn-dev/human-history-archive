## René Descartes
René Descartes (1596–1650) was a French philosopher born on March 31, 1596, in La Haye, Touraine, located midway between Poitiers and Tours. His family was of Poitevin descent. His father, Joachim Descartes, served as a counsellor in the parlement of Rennes, which placed the family within the "demi-noblesse of the robe," a social rank situated between the high nobility and the bourgeoisie. René was one of three children; he had a brother who later succeeded their father in the parlement and a sister who married a M. du Crevis. His mother died following his birth. René Descartes died in Stockholm on February 11, 1650.
