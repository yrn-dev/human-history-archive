## Political Developments in France
During this period, Jean Paul Pierre Casimir-Perier held prominent roles in the French government. Having served as vice-president of the chamber from 1890 to 1892, he became president of the chamber in 1893. On December 3 of that year, he was appointed prime-minister and held the department of foreign affairs. He remained in this position until May 1894, at which point he resigned and was subsequently re-elected as president of the chamber.
