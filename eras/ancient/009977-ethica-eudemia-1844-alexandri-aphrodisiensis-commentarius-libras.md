# Ethica Eudemia (1844) and Alexandri Aphrodisiensis Commentarius in Libras

The *Ethica Eudemia* (1844) and the *Alexandri Aphrodisiensis Commentarius in Libras Metaphysicos Aristotelis* (1847) are scholarly works authored by the German scholar Hermann Bonitz. These texts are part of a broader body of research on the works of Plato and Aristotle, for which Bonitz is best known outside of Germany.

## Author Biography
Hermann Bonitz was born on July 29, 1814, in Langensalza, Saxony. His academic foundation was established through studies at Berlin under Lachmann and Bockh, as well as at Leipzig under G. Hermann. Throughout his career, Bonitz held various educational and academic positions:
* **Teaching and Administration:** He served as a teacher at the Blochmann institute in Dresden in 1836. In Berlin, he was an Oberlehrer at the Friedrich-Wilhelms gymnasium (1838) and the Graues Kloster (1840). He later became the director of the Graues Kloster gymnasium in 1867.
* **Professorships:** He held a professorship at the gymnasium at Stettin in 1842 and later became a professor at the university of Vienna in 1849.
* **Academic Appointments:** Bonitz was appointed a member of the imperial academy in 1854 and a member of the council of education in 1864.

Bonitz retired in 1888 and died in Berlin on July 25 of that same year. Beyond his commentaries on classical philosophers, he was a significant figure in Prussian education, bearing chief responsibility for the system of examination and teaching used in Prussian high schools after 1882.

## Commentaries on Aristotle
Bonitz produced several critical works focusing on the writings of Aristotle. In 1844, he published *Observationes Criticae in Aristotelis quae feruntur Magna Moralia et Ethica Eudemia*. This work specifically addresses the *Ethica Eudemia* and the *Magna Moralia*. 

Following this, in 1847, he published the *Alexandri Aphrodisiensis Commentarius in Libras Metaphysicos Aristotelis*, a commentary on the metaphysical books of Aristotle as interpreted by Alexander of Aphrodisias.

These publications were part of a lifelong engagement with Aristotelian thought, which also included:
* *Observations Criticae in Aristotelis Libros Metaphysicos* (1842)
* *Aristotelis Metaphysica* (1848-1849)
* *Uber die Kategorien des A.* (1853)
* *Aristotelische Studien* (1862-1867)
* *Index Aristotelicus* (1870)

## Other Scholarly Contributions
In addition to his work on Aristotle, Bonitz authored *Disputationes Platonicae Duae* (1837) and *Platonische Studien* (the third edition of which was released in 1886). His research extended into other classical areas, including contributions to the explanation of Sophokles (1856-1857) and Thukydides (1854), as well as a work on the origin of the Homeric poems (*Uber den Ursprung der homerischen Gedichte*), which saw a fifth edition in 1881. He also contributed frequently to the *Zeitschrift fur die osterreichischen Gymnasien* on educational and classical topics.
