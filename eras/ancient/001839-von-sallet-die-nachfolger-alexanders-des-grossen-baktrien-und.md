# A. von Sallet, Die Nachfolger Alexanders des Grossen in Baktrien und Indien

## Overview
*Die Nachfolger Alexanders des Grossen in Baktrien und Indien* is a historical work authored by A. von Sallet and published in 1879. The text serves as a scholarly resource for the study of the Graeco-Bactrian and Indo-Scythian kingdoms.

## Academic Context
Within the broader study of the successors of Alexander the Great, von Sallet's work is cited alongside other significant bibliographic contributions to the history of the region. These include H. H. Wilson's *Ariana Antiqua* (1841), P. Gardner's *The Coins of the Greek and Scythic Kings of India* (1886), and A. von Gutschmid's *Geschichte Irans und seiner Nachbarlaender von Alexander dem Grossen bis zum Untergang der Arsaciden* (1888). 

The study of these kingdoms often relies on a diverse array of sources. While Greek authors such as Strabo and Justin (including the prologues of Trogus) provide a few preserved notices, later information is derived from Byzantine authors as well as Armenian and Persian sources, such as the translation of Tabari by Th. Noeldeke. Additionally, Chinese sources have been analyzed by scholars including E. Specht, Sylvain Levi, and Deguignes.
