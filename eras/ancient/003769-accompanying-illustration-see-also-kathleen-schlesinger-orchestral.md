# Kathleen Schlesinger

## Overview
Kathleen Schlesinger is a musicologist and author recognized for her work on musical archaeology and orchestral instrumentation. She is the author of the work *The Instruments of the Orchestra* and serves as the editor of the *Portfolio of Musical Archaeology*. Her contributions to the archive's records include research on the evolution of string instruments and the origins of ancient musical devices.

## Research on Orchestral Instruments
Schlesinger's primary scholarly focus involves the history and development of orchestral instruments. In her work *The Instruments of the Orchestra*, specifically in Part II titled "The Precursors of the Violin Family" (published 1908-1909), she examines the ancestral forms of modern string instruments. Her research in this area is cited in discussions regarding the remote origins of the double bass and the violin, as well as the historical development of the violin family.
