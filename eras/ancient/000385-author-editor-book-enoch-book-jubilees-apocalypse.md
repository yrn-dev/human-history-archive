# Author and Editor of Book of Enoch, Book of Jubilees, and Apocalypse of Baruch

## Academic Editorship
The Reverend Robert Henry Charles, M.A., D.D., Litt.D. (Oxon.), a Fellow of the British Academy and former Senior Moderator of Trinity College, Dublin, served as the author and editor of several significant texts. These include the *Book of Enoch*, the *Book of Jubilees*, the *Apocalypse of Baruch*, the *Assumption of Moses*, the *Ascension of Isaiah*, and the *Testaments of XII. Patriarchs*.

## The Book of Enoch
The *Book of Enoch* (also referred to as the *Ethiopic Book of Enoch*) is not the product of a single author. Instead, it is a conglomerate of literary fragments that originally circulated under the names of Noah, Enoch, and possibly Methuselah. The work was compiled from documents written between 200 B.C. and 50 B.C. and is derived from various Pharisaic writers in Palestine.

### Internal Composition and Authorship
The text is highly composite and consists of various sections with differing dates and authors:
*   **Chapters vi.–xi.:** This section is an independent fragment of the Enoch Saga, compounded of the Azazel and Semjaza myths. Because it is presupposed by chapters lxxxviii.–lxxxix. 1, its present form dates before 166 B.C. These chapters appear to be from the *Book of Noah*, as they refer only to Noah and never to Enoch.
*   **Chapters xii.–xvi.:** These chapters belong to the *Book of Enoch* and primarily describe visions seen by Enoch.
*   **Chapters xvii.–xxxvi.:** This section details Enoch's journeys through heaven and earth escorted by angels. It contains doublets, where sections xvii.–xix. and xx.–xxxvi. deal with the same subjects.
*   **Chapters lxxii.–lxxxii.:** This is a distinct work focused on the law of heavenly bodies. It was written before the last third of the 2nd century B.C., as it was known to the author of *Jubilees*.
*   **Chapters xci.–civ.:** This section was written between 134 B.C. (the breach between the Pharisees and John Hyrcanus) and 95 B.C. (the massacres of the Pharisees by Jannaeus). It includes the "Ten-Weeks Apocalypse" (xciii. 1-10 and xci. 12-17), which is attributed to the same writer as the rest of this section. This author is characterized as a determined foe of the Maccabees and the Sadducean priesthood.

### The Final Editor
The *Book of Enoch* suffered various interventions by a final editor. In the section concerning the law of heavenly bodies (lxxii.–lxxxii.), the editor introduced interpolations and disarrangements, such as inserting ethical matters (lxxxi.) and geographical matters (lxxvi.–lxxvii.) into a work professing to deal only with luminaries. Additionally, the final editor altered the sequence of the Ten-Weeks Apocalypse, placing xci. 12-17 after xciii. 1-10.

## The Book of the Secrets of Enoch
Also known as *Slavonic Enoch*, this work is a separate fragment of Enochic literature. While the *Ethiopic Book of Enoch* was derived from Pharisaic writers in Palestine, the *Book of the Secrets of Enoch* was written for the most part by Hellenistic Jews in Egypt. It was likely written between 30 B.C. and A.D. 70. Evidence for this date includes the fact that the temple is still standing in chapter lix. 2, and the text makes use of the *Book of Wisdom*, the (Ethiopic) *Book of Enoch*, and Sirach. 

A large portion of this book was originally written in Greek, as evidenced by the derivation of Adam's name from the Greek words for the four quarters (East, West, North, and South), a derivation impossible in Semitic languages.

## Relationship to Other Works
The author of the *Book of Jubilees* clearly drew upon the *Book of Noah*, with subject matter in *Jubilees* (vii. 21-25) agreeing closely with the chapters in *Enoch* that are believed to be from the *Book of Noah*. 

Furthermore, the authors of the *Apocalypse of Baruch*, the *Ascension of Isaiah*, and the *Epistle of Barnabas* were likely acquainted with the *Book of the Secrets of Enoch*. This text is also cited in the *Sibylline Oracles*, the *Book of Adam and Eve*, and the *Apocalypses of Moses and Paul*.
