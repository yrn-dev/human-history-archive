# The Great Pyramid of Giza: Engineering Marvel of the Old Kingdom

## Overview
The Great Pyramid of Giza, built for the Pharaoh Khufu during the Fourth Dynasty of the Old Kingdom, remains one of the most studied structures in human history. Standing as the only surviving member of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, it represents the pinnacle of ancient Egyptian architectural ambition. 

The primary mystery surrounding the pyramid is not *why* it was built—as a monumental tomb to ensure the Pharaoh's transition to the afterlife—but *how* it was constructed. Comprising approximately 2.3 million stone blocks, some weighing up to 80 tons, the precision of its alignment to true north is staggering.

Historians and engineers continue to debate the specific methods of transport and elevation. While the use of copper tools, wooden sleds, and lubrication (water on sand) is well-documented, the exact mechanism for lifting massive blocks to great heights remains a subject of scholarly contention. The leading theories include the use of a long external ramp, a spiraling internal ramp, or a combination of both.

## Key Facts
*   **Composition:** The core consists primarily of local yellow limestone, while the interior chambers (like the King's Chamber) utilize massive red granite slabs transported from Aswan, over 500 miles away.
*   **Labor Force:** Contrary to the Greek historian Herodotus's claim that the pyramid was built by slaves, archaeological evidence from the "Workers' Village" suggests a highly organized workforce of paid laborers and skilled craftsmen who were well-fed and provided medical care.
*   **Precision:** The base of the pyramid is level to within a few centimeters, and the sides are aligned almost perfectly with the cardinal points of the compass.
*   **Original Appearance:** The pyramid was originally encased in polished Tura limestone, which would have reflected the sun, making the structure shine brilliantly white.

## Did You Know?
1. **The "Big Void":** In 2017, an international team using cosmic-ray muon radiography discovered a large, previously unknown void above the Grand Gallery, the purpose of which remains unknown.
2. **Not a Perfect Square:** The Great Pyramid is actually an eight-sided figure; the centers of the four faces are slightly concave, a detail only visible from the air during the equinoxes.
3. **The Weight:** It is estimated that the total mass of the pyramid is approximately 6 million tonnes.
