# L'Afrique romaine, promenades

*L'Afrique romaine, promenades archeologiques* is a work published in 1901. It is part of a broader body of scholarship focused on the reconstruction of the elements of Roman society.

## Authorship and Style
The work was written by a French author characterized as a representative example of the French talent for elegance and lucidity, qualities he applied with entire seriousness to weighty literary matters. While his primary scholarly theme was the reconstruction of Roman society, his bibliography also includes monographs on Saint-Simon (1892) and Madame de Sevigne (1887). The author died in June 1908.

## Related Works
*L'Afrique romaine, promenades archeologiques* follows a series of other archaeological studies by the same author. These include *Promenades archeologiques: Rome et Pompei*, which was published in 1880 with a second series appearing in 1886. His other publications include *Le Conjuration de Catilina* (1905), *Tacite* (1903), and a two-volume work titled *La Fin du paganisme* (1891).

## Context of Roman African Studies
The study of Roman Africa and Carthage during the late 19th and early 20th centuries involved various scholarly contributions. Other significant works on the region and its Roman influence include:

*   **Topography and Geography:** C. Tissot published *Geographie comparee de la province romaine d'Afrique* in two volumes between 1884 and 1888. Other topographical studies include those by Falbe (1833) and Dureau de la Malle (1835).
*   **Carthage:** Research on Carthage was conducted by several authors, including Beule (*Fouilles a Carthage*, 1861), E. de Sainte Marie (*Mission a Carthage*, 1884), E. Babelon (*Carthage*, 1896), and Aug. Audollent (*Carthage romaine*, 1901). A.J. Church and A. Gilman also published a work titled *Carthage* in 1886 as part of the "Story of the Nations" series.
*   **Military and Administration:** R. Cagnat wrote *L'Armee romaine d'Afrique* (1892), while Pallu de Lessert produced *Fastes des provinces africaines sous la domination romaine* (1896-1901) and *Vicaires et comtes d'Afrique* (1892).
*   **Literature and History:** Paul Monceaux authored *Les Africains, etude sur la litterature latine de l'Afrique; Les Paiens* (1898) and the three-volume *Histoire litteraire de l'Afrique chretienne* (1901-1909). C. Diehl provided a history of Byzantine domination in the region in *L'Afrique byzantine* (1896).
